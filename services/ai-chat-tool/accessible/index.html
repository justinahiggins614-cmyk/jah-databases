AI Chat Tool

A free local demo chat. Ask about the database and get answers from its records, in plain words. Hosted fact-checking is blocked; no key is ever asked for. No sign-in, no payment, free forever. Your words never leave the page.

Open the working tool (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Press a preset question, or write your own in plain words.
  2. Read the answer and the record it names.
  3. Open the record to check the answer against the whole file.
  4. Start a new conversation when the subject changes.

Local demo only. The demo answers only from the records stored on this page. It cannot look anything up live, cannot send mail, and cannot check a fact against the live web. Hosted fact-checking is blocked until the provider sign-in is given. No key is ever asked for, and there is nowhere on this page to type one.

The preset prompts

Five ready-made questions:

  • What is a slide deck?
  • What does an image editor do?
  • How does the local demo find an answer?
  • What is a database explorer?
  • When does the demo say it does not know?

When no record matches, the demo says: I do not know from the records in this database. It never invents an answer.
