The Tool — brew ratios

Tell it how much coffee you have and how strong you like it, and it tells you how much water to pour. It works the other way round too. The ratios come from the JAH Coffee Guide Database — pour over 1:16, french press 1:14, AeroPress 1:13, cold brew 1:8, Turkish 1:10, espresso 1:2.

Method shortcuts:
15 g of coffee at 1:16 needs 240 g of water.

Water is weighed in grams, so 1 g of water is 1 ml. The tool works in your browser only — your numbers never leave this page. It does not measure taste: if a cup is sour, grind finer; if bitter, grind coarser (JAH-COF-000029).

How it works

Weigh the coffee. Whole beans, ground fresh, weighed in grams — 15 g is one good mug of pour over.
Pick a ratio. The ratio is parts of water to one part of coffee. 1:16 is the archive's pour-over figure; 1:14 is french press; 1:2 is espresso.
Pour the water. Filtered water at 90–96 C (JAH-COF-000026). The tool gives the weight; pour slowly and evenly.
Wait, then taste. Each method has its time — pour over 3 minutes, french press 4, cold brew 12–24 hours. Adjust the grind, not the ratio, first.
