Brew ratios

The ratio is parts of water to one part of coffee. To find the water, multiply the coffee weight by the ratio number: 15 g of coffee at 1:16 needs 240 g of water. 15 g at 1:15 needs 225 g. 18 g at 1:2 (espresso) needs 36 g.

How it works

  1. Weigh the coffee — 15 g is one good mug of pour over.
  2. Pick a ratio — 1:16 pour over, 1:14 french press, 1:2 espresso.
  3. Pour the water — filtered, 90–96 C, slowly and evenly.
  4. Wait, then taste — pour over 3 minutes, french press 4, cold brew 12–24 hours.

Drinks and methods

  • Espresso — Espresso is pressure-extracted intensity: 18g in, 36g out, 25-30 seconds. The base of milk drinks. Full record JAH-COF-000001
  • Americano — Americano is espresso lengthened with hot water: clean and strong. Full record JAH-COF-000002
  • Cappuccino — Cappuccino is the classic thirds: dry foam cap, drinkable art. Full record JAH-COF-000003
  • Latte — Latte is the milky canvas: espresso softened under steamed milk. Full record JAH-COF-000004
  • Flat white — Flat white is the Australian intense latte: less foam, more coffee punch. Full record JAH-COF-000005
  • Macchiato — Macchiato is espresso stained with foam: small and strong. Full record JAH-COF-000006
  • Cortado — Cortado is the Spanish equal-parts: no foam, just balance. Full record JAH-COF-000007
  • Mocha — Mocha is dessert coffee: espresso meets chocolate. Full record JAH-COF-000008
  • Cold brew — Cold brew steeps coarse grounds cold for 12-24 hours: smooth, sweet, low acid. Full record JAH-COF-000009
  • Iced coffee — Iced coffee is hot coffee flash-chilled: brew strong over ice. Full record JAH-COF-000010
  • Pour over (V60) — Pour over is the clarity method: spiral pours, paper filter, bright cups. Full record JAH-COF-000011
  • Chemex — Chemex thick filters make the cleanest cup: tea-like and sweet. Full record JAH-COF-000012
  • French press — French press is full immersion: heavy body, simple ritual. Full record JAH-COF-000013
  • AeroPress — AeroPress is the pressure hack: fast, forgiving, endlessly recipe-tweakable. Full record JAH-COF-000014
  • Moka pot — Moka pot is stovetop espresso-ish: strong, nostalgic, Italian mornings. Full record JAH-COF-000015
  • Turkish coffee — Turkish coffee boils powder-fine grounds in a cezve: intense, with grounds settling. Full record JAH-COF-000016
