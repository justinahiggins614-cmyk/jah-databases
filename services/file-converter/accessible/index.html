How it works

  1. Paste your text, CSV, JSON, Markdown, or HTML in the box on the main page.
  2. Pick the conversion from the list: CSV to JSON, JSON to CSV, plain text to JSON, Markdown to HTML, HTML to plain text, JSON pretty-print or minify, or a case change.
  3. Press Convert. The result appears below, with its size. Download it or copy it.
  4. For pictures, pick a picture file, choose JPEG, WebP, or PNG, set the quality, and press Convert picture.

What it cannot do: it converts text and pictures in your browser only — it cannot open files from other websites, it cannot read password-locked files, and a JPEG made from a PNG cannot be turned back into the original PNG, because the JPEG has already thrown away fine detail. A conversion that fails says so plainly, with the reason.

Supported conversions

The tool converts between these formats, all in your browser:

FromToWhat happens
CSV tableJSONFirst row becomes field names; each later row becomes one record.
JSON listCSV tableField names become the first row; each record becomes one row.
Plain textJSONThe words become the value of a field called text.
MarkdownHTML# becomes a heading, - becomes a list item, **word** becomes bold.
HTMLPlain textTags are stripped; headings and paragraphs become their own lines.
JSONJSONPretty-print spreads it over many lines; minify squeezes it onto one line.
TextTextUpper case, lower case, title case, or sentence case.
PNG pictureJPEG or WebPRedrawn in the new format; JPEG loses fine detail, WebP keeps more.
JPEG picturePNGRedrawn as PNG; the detail the JPEG threw away does not come back.
