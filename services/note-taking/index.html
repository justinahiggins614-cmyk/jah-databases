How it works

Write a note One thing, in plain words, under a short title.
File it and tag it A folder for its kind, and tags for its subject.
Search your notes One word finds every note that holds it, in any folder.
Tick things off A checklist turns a large job into small steps that can be finished one at a time.

Notes stay in the browser unless you download them. The tool writes nothing to a server and keeps no record of who wrote what. When the page is closed, the notes are gone unless they were downloaded, so important notes should always be exported.
