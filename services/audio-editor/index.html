How it works

Look at the sample sound Bars show the wave: taller means louder, flat means silent.
Trim the silence Cut the flat lines at the start and the end, so the sound starts when it should.
Fade in and out A gentle start and a gentle end, instead of a jolt.
Set the volume and speed Louder or quieter, faster or slower — and read the new length at once.

This editor works on a sample sound on the page, to show how the tools work. It does not upload or keep your recordings. The database records explain trimming, fading, volume, speed, and saving as a sound file.
