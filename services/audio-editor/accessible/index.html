Open the working tool (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Look at the sample sound, drawn as bars.
  2. Trim the silence at the start and the end.
  3. Fade in gently at the start and fade out gently at the end.
  4. Set the volume and the speed, and read the new length.

This editor works on a sample sound on the page, to show how the tools work. It does not upload or keep your recordings. The database records explain trimming, fading, volume, speed, and saving as a sound file.

The sound rules

How the tools change a sound:

  • Trim — cuts the flat lines at the start and the end.
  • Fade in — the first 4 samples rise slowly from silence.
  • Fade out — the last 4 samples fall slowly to silence.
  • Volume — every sample is multiplied, and nothing goes past full loudness.
  • Speed — half speed doubles the length, double speed halves it.

Keep it true in WAV, share it small in MP3.
