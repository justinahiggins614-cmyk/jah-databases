The Tool

How it works

Load the demo clips Three clips appear on the timeline, in order, with their lengths.
Trim a clip Cut the dull start or the trailing end off the chosen clip.
Split a clip Cut one clip into two at the second you choose, and delete the dull middle.
Add a title Words on screen, long enough to read, placed where the picture is plain.
Export the plan Take the timeline as text: every clip, its start, its length, its title.

This editor plans a video from demo clips on the page. It does not upload or keep your recordings, and it does not export a finished video file. The database records explain trimming, splitting, titles, and exporting a finished video.
