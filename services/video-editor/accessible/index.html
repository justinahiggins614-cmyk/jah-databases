How it works

  1. Load the three demo clips onto the timeline.
  2. Trim the dull start or end off a clip.
  3. Split a clip to cut out a mistake in the middle.
  4. Add a title that can be read while the video plays.
  5. Export the plan as text.

This editor plans a video from demo clips on the page. It does not upload or keep your recordings, and it does not export a finished video file. The database records explain trimming, splitting, titles, and exporting a finished video.

The transitions

Three ways for one clip to hand over to the next:

  • cut — a straight jump from one clip to the next, with no fuss.
  • fade — one clip melts into the next over a second or two.
  • dissolve — the two clips overlap while one fades out and the other fades in.

Straight cuts suit most joins. Fades suit a change of place or time. The demo clips are Opening shot (12 seconds), Walk (20 seconds), and Market (15 seconds).
