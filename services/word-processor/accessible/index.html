Word Processor

A free word processor. Type your words, make them bold or italic, turn a line into a heading, see the word count live, and download your page as plain text or as a web page. No sign-in, no payment, free forever. Your words never leave the page.

Open the working word processor (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Write. Type or paste your words in the box. Your words stay on this page and are never sent anywhere.
  2. Format. Mark words and press B for bold, I for italic, or U for underline. Press Heading to turn a line into a big title line, or Paragraph to turn it back into plain text.
  3. Count and keep. The word count updates as you type. Download as text for a plain file any reader can open, or as a web page for a page that keeps your bold words and headings.

What it does not do: it does not check spelling, it does not save your work for you, and it does not open files from other word processors. Download your page before you leave, or your words will be gone when the page closes.

What it does

  • What a word processor is — a place to write and change text, where any word can be fixed without retyping the whole page.
  • Bold, italic and underline — three ways to make words stand out.
  • Headings and the shape of a page — big lines that name each part.
  • The word count — the number of words, counted live as you type.
  • Saving as plain text — keeping only the words, so any reader can open the file.
  • Saving as a web page — a page a browser can open, with headings and bold words kept.
  • Copy, cut and paste — moving words from one place to another in seconds.
  • Find and replace — finding every place a word appears, and changing them all at once.
  • Spelling as you type — wavy lines under words the checker does not know.
  • Margins and page size — the blank strips around a page and the size of the sheet.
  • Fonts and type sizes — the shape of the letters and how tall they are.
  • Lists: bullets and numbers — dots for items in no order, numbers for steps in order.
