How it works

Write. Type or paste your words in the box above. Your words stay on this page and are never sent anywhere.
Format. Mark words and press B for bold, I for italic, or U for underline. Press Heading to turn a line into a big title line, or Paragraph to turn it back into plain text.
Count and keep. The word count updates as you type. Press Download as text for a plain file any reader can open, or Download as web page for a page that keeps your bold words and headings.

What it does not do: it does not check spelling, it does not save your work for you, and it does not open files from other word processors. Download your page before you leave, or your words will be gone when the page closes.
