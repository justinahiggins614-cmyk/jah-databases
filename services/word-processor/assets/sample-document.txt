A letter home

Dear Ana,

The garden is full of late roses, and the kitchen smells of fresh bread. I have written three pages today, and the words came easily.

Tomorrow I will walk to the market early, before the sun is high, and bring back apples for a pie. The recipe is my grandmother's, and I have copied it out in full below so it is not lost.

Come and visit when the roses are still out. The spare room is ready, and the kettle is always on.

With love,
Mara
