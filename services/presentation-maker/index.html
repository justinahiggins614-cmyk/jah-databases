Presentation Maker

Slides for your talk — built here, shown anywhere.

This is a slide-deck maker that runs entirely in your browser. Your words never leave this page. Add slides, write bullets, move slides into order, pick a theme, and export the deck as text. Free forever — no sign-in, no payment

The Tool

Your deck will appear here. Start by adding a slide.

How it works

Add a slide Give it a title. One slide holds one idea.
Add bullets Short lines under the title. If it cannot be read from the back, it is too long.
Move slides into order The order is the talk. Move a slide earlier or later until the story flows.
Pick a theme One theme dresses the whole deck at once.
Export the deck Take the deck as text, and keep it with your notes.

The maker keeps your deck on this page only. It does not save files to a server, and it does not open decks made elsewhere — the database records explain how to open a deck from another maker.
