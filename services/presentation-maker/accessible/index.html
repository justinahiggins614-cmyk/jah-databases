Presentation Maker

A free slide-deck maker. Add slides, write bullets, reorder, pick a theme, and export the deck as text. No sign-in, no payment, free forever. Your words never leave the page.

Open the working tool (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Give each slide a title. One slide holds one idea.
  2. Add short bullets under the title.
  3. Move slides into order until the story flows.
  4. Pick one theme for the whole deck.
  5. Export the deck as text and keep it with your notes.

The maker keeps your deck on this page only. It does not save files to a server, and it does not open decks made elsewhere — the database records explain how to open a deck from another maker.

The five themes

Five themes dress a whole deck at once:

  • plain — dark words on a light ground.
  • sunrise — warm colours for a bright talk.
  • forest — calm greens for a long lesson.
  • ocean — cool blues for a report.
  • ink — light words on a dark ground.

One slide holds one idea, and body text should be readable from the back of the room.
