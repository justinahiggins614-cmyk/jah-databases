Spreadsheet

Type numbers in the grid. Write a formula, and the totals work themselves out.

This is a spreadsheet that runs entirely in your browser. Your numbers never leave this page. Type numbers and words into the grid, write a formula starting with =, and press Work it out. Change one number and every total that depends on it changes at once. Free forever — no sign-in, no payment

The Tool

Your grid — five columns (A to E) and six rows. Type a number, a word, or a formula like =SUM(A1:A3).

Formulas: =SUM(A1:A3) adds a range, =AVERAGE(A1:A3) averages it, =MAX and =MIN find the largest and smallest, =COUNT counts the numbers. You can also write plain sums like =A1+B1*2.

Worked-out answers

How it works

Type in the grid. Each box is a cell, named by its column letter and row number, such as B3. A cell holds one number, one word, or one formula.
Write a formula. Start with =, then name cells and functions: =SUM(A1:A3) adds the three cells, =AVERAGE(A1:A3) averages them, and =A1+B1*2 does plain arithmetic. Brackets set the order.
Press Work it out. The answers appear in the table below, with a total for each column. Change a number and press the button again — every total follows the change.

What it does not do: it does not save your sheet for you, it does not draw charts, and it does not look things up in another sheet. Download your sheet as a CSV file before you leave, or your numbers will be gone when the page closes.
