Image Editor

A free picture editor. Try filters on a sample picture, and work out crop, resize, and rotation sizes. No sign-in, no payment, free forever. Your words never leave the page.

Open the working tool (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Look at the sample picture of coloured squares.
  2. Press one filter at a time and see what it does.
  3. Read the average colour after each change.
  4. Type a picture's size to work out a crop, a resize, or a rotation.
  5. Keep the original picture, so a filter can be taken away.

This editor works on a sample picture on the page, to show how the tools work. It does not upload or keep your pictures. The database records explain cropping, resizing, layers, and saving as PNG or JPG.

The filters

Six filters, one change at a time:

  • Grey — every pixel becomes its grey value.
  • Invert — every colour becomes its opposite.
  • Sepia — warm brown tones, like an old photograph.
  • Brighten — every colour gains 30, up to white.
  • Darken — every colour loses 30, down to black.

Drawings, words, and sharp edges belong in PNG; photographs to share belong in JPG.
