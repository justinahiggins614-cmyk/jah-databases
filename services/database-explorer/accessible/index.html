Database Explorer

A free database explorer. Search a sample of the hub registry, filter by kind or letter, and open a full record. No sign-in, no payment, free forever. Your words never leave the page.

Open the working tool (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Search the sample registry for a word in a name or a kind.
  2. Filter by kind or by starting letter.
  3. Press a database to read its full entry.
  4. Follow the link to open the database itself.

This explorer searches a sample of the hub registry on the page, not the whole live manifest. The sample holds 18 of the 20 Wave 1 entries, the databases they expand, and a spread of live databases. The full registry is the live manifest, re-derived at merge time.
