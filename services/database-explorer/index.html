Database Explorer

The front door to the whole network — find it, open it, run it.

This is a database explorer that runs entirely in your browser. It searches a sample of the hub registry, filters by kind or letter, and opens a full record with its demo and its generator. The full registry is the live manifest. Free forever — no sign-in, no payment

The Tool

The sample registry will appear here.
Press a database to see its full entry.

How it works

Search the sample One word finds every database in the sample whose name or kind holds it.
Filter by kind or letter Narrow the sample to one kind of record, or one starting letter.
Press a database Read its full entry: its slug, its kind, and its record count.
Open the database Follow the link to the full database and read its records.

This explorer searches a sample of the hub registry on the page, not the whole live manifest. The sample holds 18 of the 20 Wave 1 entries, the databases they expand, and a spread of live databases. The full registry is the live manifest, re-derived at merge time.
