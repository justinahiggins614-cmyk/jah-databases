Unit Converter

Kilometres to miles, Celsius to Fahrenheit — worked out exactly.

This is a unit converter that runs entirely in your browser. Your numbers never leave this page. It converts length, weight, temperature, volume, speed, area, time, data size, pressure, energy, power, and angles — and it works from exact ratios, so every answer can be checked by hand. Free forever — no sign-in, no payment

The Tool

Money is not converted here. Money rates change by the hour, and no fixed table can hold a moving rate — this tool converts physical units only, where the ratios are fixed.

How it works

Pick the kind. Length, weight, temperature, volume, speed, area, and the rest — twelve kinds, each with its own units.
Type a number and pick the units. Choose what to convert from and what to convert to. The answer appears at once.
Check the factor. Every answer is worked out from an exact ratio, shown in the records below. Swap the units to convert back and check.

What it cannot do: it cannot convert money, because money rates change by the hour and this tool holds no live-rates feed; it cannot convert a cup of flour to grams without knowing what is in the cup, because a cup is a volume and a gram is a weight; and a month is not a fixed number of days, so months convert by the average month of 30.44 days, stated beside the answer. All other ratios here are exact and printed in the records below.

Good to know

Six things worth knowing before you convert anything.

1 mile = 1.609344 km

The mile is defined as 1,609.344 metres exactly, so every length conversion in this tool is exact, not rounded at the factor.

Read the full record →

0°C = 32°F, 100°C = 212°F

Temperature is a shift and a stretch: multiply by 9, divide by 5, add 32. The two scales meet only at -40.

Read the full record →

Two gallons

An imperial gallon is 4.54609 litres; an American gallon is 3.78541 litres. A gallon without a country is not a unit.

Read the full record →

A tonne is not a ton

A tonne is 1,000 kg. A long ton is 1,016.047 kg. A short ton is 907.18474 kg. Three tons, three weights.

Read the full record →

Data: 1,000, not 1,024

This tool uses the decimal scale: 1 MB is 1,000,000 bytes. The binary scale counts 1,024 — a different, larger number.

Read the full record →

Why money is not converted

Money rates move by the hour. A fixed table would be wrong within hours, so money stays out of this tool.

Read the full record →

Overview

The Unit Converter Service changes measurements from one unit to another — length, weight, volume, speed, area, time, and more — using fixed ratios. It covers physical units only, never money: there is no currency conversion here, and no live rates. The archive behind it, The Unit Converter Software Database, holds 20 records. A separate service database, The Unit Converter Service Database, holds 48 full records — 24 public records and 24 Signature records. The service is free forever: no sign-in, no payment, no tracking.
