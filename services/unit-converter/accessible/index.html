A free unit converter. Pick the kind, type a number, and read the answer. No sign-in, no payment, free forever. Your words never leave the page.

Open the working tool (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Pick the kind of unit: length, weight, temperature, volume, speed, area, time, data size, pressure, energy, power, or angle.
  2. Type a number, pick the unit to convert from and the unit to convert to.
  3. Press Convert. The answer appears with the exact ratio it was worked out from.
  4. Press Swap to convert back and check the answer.

What it cannot do: it cannot convert money, because money rates change by the hour and this tool holds no live-rates feed; it cannot convert a cup of flour to grams without knowing what is in the cup, because a cup is a volume and a gram is a weight; and a month is not a fixed number of days, so months convert by the average month of 30.44 days, stated beside the answer. All other ratios here are exact and printed in the records below.
