How it works

Make a PDF. Type a title and your lines, then press Make a PDF. The page writes a real PDF file, with the title at the top and your lines below it, and hands it to you to download.
Read a PDF. Drop a PDF in the box, or choose one. The page reads the file on this page only, counts its pages, and shows its title, its author if it has one, and its size.
Open it anywhere. A PDF looks the same on every computer, because the words and fonts travel inside the file. Open it in a browser or any reader.

What it cannot do: it cannot join two PDFs into one, and it cannot split one PDF into separate pages, because those jobs need to rebuild the file's inner structure, which a plain page in a browser cannot safely do. It also cannot fill in forms or add a signature. Those limits are stated here so no one expects more.
