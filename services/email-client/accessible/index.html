How it works

  1. Open the inbox and read the six sample messages.
  2. Press a message to choose it and read its words.
  3. Search the mailbox for a name or a word.
  4. Write a draft with an address, a subject, and your words.
  5. File the draft in the Sent folder. Nothing leaves the page.

Demo mailbox only. Live sending and delivery is a hosted service that this site does not provide, so no real mail is ever sent from here. The database records state the boundary in full.

The five folders

Five folders hold the demo mail:

  • inbox — where new mail arrives; six samples wait there.
  • sent — copies of messages filed as sent, samples only.
  • drafts — messages written but not filed yet.
  • trash — deleted messages, until the trash is emptied.
  • spam — mail that was not asked for.
