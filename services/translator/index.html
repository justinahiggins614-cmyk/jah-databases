The Tool

How it works

Type a phrase. Write English words in the box — a greeting, a question, a few plain words.
Pick a language. Choose Spanish, French, German, or Italian, then press Translate.
Read the answer. The tool looks each phrase up in its book and shows the translation. Words the book does not hold are marked plainly as not in this phrasebook — it never guesses.

What it covers: the phrases in its book — greetings, thanks, numbers, days, food, asking the way, at the shop, at the doctor, family, colours, weather, time, and emergency words — across four languages. What it does not do: it does not translate a sentence it has never seen, a joke, or a long speech. What it cannot translate, it says so in plain words.
