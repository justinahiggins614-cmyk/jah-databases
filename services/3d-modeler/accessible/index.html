3D Modeler

A free 3D modeler. Build from cubes, spheres, cylinders, and cones, move them, grow them, colour them, and orbit the view. No sign-in, no payment, free forever. Your words never leave the page.

Open the working tool (needs JavaScript)

How it works

  1. Add a shape: a cube, a sphere, a cylinder, or a cone.
  2. Move it along the three axes until it sits where it belongs.
  3. Grow it or shrink it, and give it a colour.
  4. Orbit the view and check the model from every side.

This modeler builds simple shapes on the page, to show how the tools work. It does not upload or keep your models, and it does not run a printer. The database records explain joining shapes, the ground plane, and checking a model before sharing.

The four primitives

Four starting shapes, and their volumes for a size of 2:

  • cube — side × side × side = 8.
  • sphere — 4/3 × pi × radius³ = 4.19.
  • cylinder — pi × radius² × height = 6.28.
  • cone — 1/3 × pi × radius² × height = 2.09.

The ground plane is 0. Every shape should rest on it, with its lowest part touching it.
