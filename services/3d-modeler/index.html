3D Modeler

Simple shapes, joined well, make real things.

This is a 3D modeler that runs entirely in your browser. Your models never leave this page. Build from cubes, spheres, cylinders, and cones, move them, grow them, colour them, and orbit the view. Free forever — no sign-in, no payment

The Tool

Your scene will appear here. Add a shape to begin.

How it works

Add a shape Pick a cube, a sphere, a cylinder, or a cone, and set its size.
Move it Slide it along the three axes until it sits where it belongs.
Grow it and colour it Double it or halve it, and give it a colour and a material of its own.
Orbit the view Turn the camera around the model and check it from every side.

This modeler builds simple shapes on the page, to show how the tools work. It does not upload or keep your models, and it does not run a printer. The database records explain joining shapes, the ground plane, and checking a model before sharing.
