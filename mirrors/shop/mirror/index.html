Section 1

Description and history

Signature Shop is a mirror of the shopping kind of website: a website where a visitor browses goods, reads prices, and builds a cart. Shopping websites began as plain lists of goods with prices, then grew catalogs you could search, carts that kept a running total, and checkout pages. This mirror follows the same shape — a catalog, a cart, and a total — rebuilt with Signature branding and the network's own data.

What this mirror sells is different from the kind it mirrors: it sells nothing. Its catalog is a sample set of Signature-made goods, made for this mirror, with prices set by the mirror itself. You can browse it and work out totals, but there is no checkout, no payment, and no delivery, because no real goods change hands here. The mirror exists to show how a shopping website works, using only the network's own data.

Section 2

Math proof (standard math + metamath)

The mirror's pricing rule is a deterministic formula. For an item with catalog price P and quantity Q, the line total is:

line total = P × Q

The cart total is the sum of every line total:

cart total = line total 1 + line total 2 + … + line total n

Worked example: a Signature Lantern costs 12 and a Signature Mug costs 8. Three lanterns give 12 × 3 = 36. Two mugs give 8 × 2 = 16. The cart total is 36 + 16 = 52. The same input always gives the same total, because the rule depends only on the catalog prices and the quantities you type.

Section 3

Science proof

The science behind this mirror is the arithmetic of trade: a price, a quantity, and a total. A shopping website keeps a cart — a list of chosen goods with quantities — and the total is the sum of price times quantity for each line. That is all the demo does, with the mirror's own sample prices.

Behind a real shopping website sits stock-keeping, payment, and delivery. None of that is on this page: the catalog is a sample, the cart lives only in the page, and no money moves. The mirror shows the counting part of shopping, honestly labeled as a sample, so you can check every number yourself.
