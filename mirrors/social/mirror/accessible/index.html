Section 1 — Description and history

Signature Social is a mirror of the social kind of website: a website where people share short posts, follow each other, and comment. Social websites began as plain lists of messages in time order, then grew follow lists, like counts, comment threads, and feeds ranked by score. This mirror follows the same shape — posts, a feed, and comments counted — rebuilt with Signature branding and the network's own data.

What this mirror shares is different from the kind it mirrors: it shares nothing outside the page. Its feed is a sample set of eight posts from sample names, made for this mirror, and any message you post joins the feed in the page only. There is no following, no account, and no sharing beyond the page, because the feed lives only here. The mirror exists to show how a feed works, using only the network's own data.

Section 2 — Math proof (standard math + metamath)

The mirror's feed rule is a deterministic score. For a post with L likes and C comments, the score is:

score = L + 2 × C

The feed lists your new posts first, then the sample posts in score order, highest first.

Worked example: the sample post by Rin has 14 likes and 3 comments. Its score is 14 + 2 × 3 = 14 + 6 = 20. The sample post by June has 30 likes and 7 comments, so its score is 30 + 2 × 7 = 30 + 14 = 44, and it ranks first among the sample posts. The same feed always gives the same scores, because the rule depends only on the fixed sample posts and the message you type.

Section 3 — Science proof

The science behind this mirror is the social feed: posts, counts, and ranking. A social website takes short messages from people, counts the likes and comments each one draws, and ranks the feed so the liveliest posts sit near the top. This mirror does that work in miniature: eight sample posts with fixed counts, a score rule from Section 2, and a feed that lists your new post first and the sample posts by score.

Following and commenting across accounts are the parts this mirror does not do: there are no accounts here, no one to follow, and no one else's feed to reach. The mirror says so plainly, and its sample posts are labeled as its own sample posts, never attributed to real people.
