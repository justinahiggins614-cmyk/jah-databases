Small-Shop Layout Rank 1 · simple · layout · beginner

A small-shop layout puts the workbench at the center, raw material storage near the door, and machines along the walls so that long boards can move through the shop without crossing a walkway. This guide lays out a 12 by 16 foot shop in four zones: bench, cutting, assembly, and storage, with a 42 inch main aisle kept clear at all times. Lumber enters at the door, goes to the cutting zone, then to the bench, then to assembly, so work flows in one direction and offcuts never pile up where people walk.

Dimensioned sketch of Small-Shop Layout: overall 144 x 192 x 96 in in, showing the plan/elevation outline with dimension labels and key features of the design.

Specifications

Dimensions: 144 x 192 x 96 in in. Materials: zone markers (painted floor tape, n/a). Fasteners: 2-1/2 in deck screws. Tolerances: Zone boundaries within 2 in; aisle clearance never below 36 in..

Layout spec

Zones: Bench 72 x 30 in; Cutting 96 x 48 in; Assembly 72 x 48 in; Storage 96 x 24 in; aisles (in): 42, 48.

Arithmetic note: Layout totals: 4 zones covering 87.0 sq ft; every aisle clearance is at least 36 in (minimum aisle 42 in), so walkways stay clear of the work zones.

How to make it

  1. Measure the room and mark the four zones on the floor with tape.
  2. Place the bench where daylight falls across the work surface.
  3. Put lumber storage by the door and machines along the walls.
  4. Mark the 42 inch main aisle and keep it clear.
  5. Walk a full board through the flow once before fixing anything down.

Tools required

tape measure, pencil, level, square, cordless drill/driver

Safety gear

safety glasses, hearing protection, dust mask

Safety notes: Wear eye and hearing protection whenever power tools run; keep blades guarded and hands clear of cutting paths; keep dust under control and walkways clear.

Working demo program

# Workshop layout checker: zone areas, aisle clearances, circuit loads
ZONES = [('Bench', 72, 30), ('Cutting', 96, 48), ('Assembly', 72, 48), ('Storage', 96, 24)]
AISLES = [42, 48]
CIRCUITS = [('General', 20, 8), ('Lighting', 15, 6)]
total = 0.0
for name, w, d in ZONES:
    area = w * d / 144.0
    total += area
    print("%s: %d x %d in = %.1f sq ft" % (name, w, d, area))
print("Total zone area: %.1f sq ft" % total)
for i, a in enumerate(AISLES, 1):
    print("Aisle %d: %d in clear - %s" % (i, a, "PASS (>=36 in)" if a >= 36 else "FAIL"))
for name, amps, load in CIRCUITS:
    print("%s: %d A load on %d A circuit = %.0f%%" % (name, load, amps, load / amps * 100.0))
