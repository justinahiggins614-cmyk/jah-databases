The capuchin monkey (Cebus capucinus and Sapajus apella) has its own mathematics, its own science, and its own court, the "Chirp" court. The capuchin monkey lives in the forests of Central and South America, eats fruit, insects, nuts, and small animals, and lives in a troop of about a dozen. It is about 2 to 4 kilograms and stands about 0.35 to 0.45 metres long in body. Its characteristic sound is the chirp call, a short sharp sound used between troop members.
