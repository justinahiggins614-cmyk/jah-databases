A Watch Linked to a Phone Calendar Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A wearable coordination website: a visitor picks a kind of link, such as a watch linked to a calendar, and the site shows the link in plain words, what each device does, and how the two agree. Every link is a generic study, and the site names no real device and never claims a link works on a live product. This record covers A Watch Linked to a Phone Calendar.

A watch that shows the next appointment from a phone calendar in plain words. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a watch linked to a phone calendar runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
