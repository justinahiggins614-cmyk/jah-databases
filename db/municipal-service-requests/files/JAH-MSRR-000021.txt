History
Towns have always taken requests from their residents: a permit, a collection, a repair, a question. As towns grew, the requests became numbered cases with deadlines, so that a resident could follow their request from the day it was made to the day it was done. This database is the specification for that system. The annual report is the town's yearly account of its requests: how many came in, how many were done on time, and which services were slowest.
