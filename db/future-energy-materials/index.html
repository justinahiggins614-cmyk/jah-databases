Advanced superconductors carry current with zero resistance at higher temperatures than classic superconductors..

Advanced superconductors carry current with zero resistance at higher temperatures than classic superconductors. High-temperature superconductors work above liquid-nitrogen temperature, and record materials work near room temperature only under extreme pressure. A practical high-temperature superconductor operates at about 77 kelvin, the temperature of liquid nitrogen. The open challenge is a material that superconducts at room temperature and normal pressure; none is confirmed. Maturity E4 (Experimental): tested in a controlled experiment.
