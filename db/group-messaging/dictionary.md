# Dictionary entries — The Group Messaging Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## group

A named set of people who talk together, where one message reaches everyone at once and every reply is seen by all.

## message

One written note sent to a group, carrying words and the time it was sent, kept in the order it was sent.

## member

One person in a group, who can read its messages, write new ones, and leave whenever they choose.

## delivery

The carrying of a message to a member, marked as sent, delivered, or read, so that the sender knows how far it reached.

## read receipt

A mark that a member has read a message, shown beside the message, which a member may turn off if they wish.

## thread

One line of talk inside a group, where replies to one message stay together, so that several talks can run side by side without mixing.

## mention

Naming one member inside a message, so that member is told directly while the rest of the group can still read it.

## moderator

A member trusted to keep the group in order: to remove messages that break its rules, to add and remove members, and to settle disputes.

## invite

An offer to join a group, sent to one person, which the person may take or leave, and which says who invited them.

## leaving

A member's choice to stop being in a group, after which they receive no more of its messages and their name leaves the list.

## attachment

A file sent with a message, such as a picture or a document, which every member can open and which is kept with the message.

## ledger

The group's own written record of every message, every member, and every change, kept in order.
