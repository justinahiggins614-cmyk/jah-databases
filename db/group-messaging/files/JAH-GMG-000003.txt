The Group Messaging Database holds the full record for Members. A member is one person in a group, who can read its messages, write new ones, and leave whenever they choose. No one is kept in a group against their will, and the list of members is open to every member. History
People have talked in groups for as long as there have been rooms: the town square, the kitchen table, the club. Online the room became a group chat, where one message reaches everyone at once and every reply is seen by all. The public standards for sending messages, and for proving they arrived, are published openly, and this database is built on them.
