# The Group Messaging Database

The Group Messaging Database is the group messaging record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full group messaging record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database software-messaging-apps, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
