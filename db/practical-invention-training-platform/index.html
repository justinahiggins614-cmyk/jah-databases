A Course in Building a Small Wooden Box Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A training platform website: a visitor picks a course, such as building a small wooden box, and the platform shows the course in plain words, the materials, the steps, and what the finished piece should do. Every course is a generic study, and the platform gives no instruction for any dangerous work and never claims a course runs in a real workshop. This record covers A Course in Building a Small Wooden Box.

A course that builds a small wooden box, step by step, with the finished box as proof of the lesson. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a course in building a small wooden box runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
