The Practical Invention Training Platform Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The practical invention training platform teaches by making: each course builds one small useful thing with the learner's own hands, a box, a pulley, a cold frame, and the finished piece is the proof of the lesson. Every course is a design study of a generic hands-on lesson, never a claim about a real workshop.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
