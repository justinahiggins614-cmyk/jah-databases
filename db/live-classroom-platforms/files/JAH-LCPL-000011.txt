History
Teachers have gathered learners in one room for as long as schools have stood, because a lesson taught face to face lets the teacher see who follows and who is lost. Live classroom platforms grew beside the network: a room held over distance, where the teacher speaks, the learners answer, and the lesson is shared at the same hour, so that a class can meet without sharing a building. From the chalked lesson in a village schoolroom to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the lesson down whole.
