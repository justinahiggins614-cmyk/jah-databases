A shopping list grouped by shop aisle lets a shopper walk the shop once: bread with bread, milk with milk, rice with rice, each group in the order the aisles run. The grouping needs the true aisle for every item, or the shopper walks back and forth. Without the grouping, the list is in the order the items were remembered, and the shopper crosses the shop three times for one list. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
