1. Description

An audio compressor lowers loud samples past a threshold, and the loud sample shrinks by the ratio. Set the threshold and the ratio, press Compress, and read the peak. A sample of 0.9 past a threshold of 0.5 at ratio 4 becomes 0.6. An excess of 0.4 at ratio 4 keeps 0.1. An input peak of 0.9 falls to 0.6: a reduction of 0.3. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
