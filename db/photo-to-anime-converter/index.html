1. Description and History

A photo-to-anime converter turns a photograph into an anime-style drawing: large expressive eyes, soft cel shading, clean lines, and painted-looking backgrounds. This database designs that converter one record at a time, from a single portrait to a whole series in one consistent style, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.

Software later learned to imitate those habits: enlarge the eyes, shade in flat bands, and paint the background softly.
