3. The ranking method

The ranking this directory records counts estimated visits in a web browser. A visit is one person opening a website in a browser and looking at it; the same person coming back later counts again. The measurement covers the whole world and one month, October 2026, and it was published on November 10, 2026. It counts browser visits only. It does not count people, it does not count activity inside phone apps, and it does not count time spent. A service that most people use inside an app will look smaller in this ranking than it really is, and a service people visit in a browser will look larger. 7. Science proof

The science here is the science of measurement. A ranking that counts estimated browser visits is an estimate built from samples and models, not a direct count, and it carries error. Two facts follow. First, the order at the top is more reliable than the exact numbers: a service with far more visits than the next is almost certainly ahead, while two services close together could swap places if the measurement changed. Second, what is left out shapes the result as much as what is counted: phone-app activity is excluded, so services people mostly use in an app are under-counted, and twelve ranks are omitted from this directory, so the list has gaps. A reader who understands these two facts can use the ranking well; a reader who treats it as an exact count will be misled by it.
