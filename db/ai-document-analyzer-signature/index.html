1. Description

A document analyzer reads a document and keeps its main points: word and sentence counts, key words, dates, numbers and a short summary. The local analyzer on this page works with rules in this browser, with no sign-in. Hosted AI help is labelled BLOCKED until a hosted backend is connected; nothing here pretends to be hosted.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Counting is the whole method: a document of 54 words in 5 sentences averages 54 divided by 5, which is 10.8 words a sentence. A summary keeps 3 of 5 sentences. A table of 3 rows and 2 columns holds 6 cells. A document of 500 words against a limit of 300 is over by 200 words.

3. Science proof

A document analyzer works because a text can be measured: words are counted by splitting on spaces, sentences by counting full stops, and key words by counting how often each word appears. The most common word, the longest word and the first sentences together sketch what the document is about. That is why the analyzer shows its counts instead of hiding them.
