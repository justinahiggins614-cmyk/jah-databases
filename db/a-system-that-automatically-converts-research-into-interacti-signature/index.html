This record covers A Lesson Built From One Short Research Note.

A lesson built from one short research note is the first part of the converter that can be built now: take one short note, such as how rain forms, and turn it into plain steps, questions to try, and checks that say whether the answer is right. The lesson must be built by hand at first, with a teacher reading the note and writing the lesson, because a lesson written without a teacher may teach the wrong thing. Without a hand-built first lesson, the converter must attempt automatic conversion at once, and attempting it at once is a poor way to build.
