Every line in the guide points to the full record, so the guide never replaces the record it lists.

History

Staged pranks grew out of harmless stage tricks and comedy sketches, where the surprise was planned and everyone in on it was safe; the simulator turns that tradition into a story tool with safety rules written in. The field guide itself follows the old habit of indexes and tables of contents, which let a reader find one entry in a large book without reading the whole book first; this guide does the same for a database of twenty-four records.

Proposed solution

Draw the guide as a map of the whole database, ladder order from simple to complex; Write one line per record that says what the record teaches, not only what it is called; Mark the ladder rank beside every line, so a reader can climb in order; Name the first record and the next record for two kinds of reader: newcomer and expert; Review the guide whenever a record changes, so the map never goes stale.

Dependencies

The twenty-three content records of this database, in ladder order; the record titles; and the ladder ranks simple, compounded, and complex.

Verification path

A reader opens the guide, finds any record by its title, follows the link to the full record, and checks that the ladder rank beside the line matches the rank on the record itself; all twenty-three lines must match, or the guide is out of date.
