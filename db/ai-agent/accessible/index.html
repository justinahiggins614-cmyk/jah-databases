The AI Agent Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An AI agent takes a goal, makes a plan, and works through the steps with tools. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it is rule-based, follows 5 written rules, makes 0 real AI calls, and hosted AI is BLOCKED. A real AI agent needs a hosted backend, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
