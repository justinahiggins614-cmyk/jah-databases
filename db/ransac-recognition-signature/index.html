JAH-KW-RANSAC-000101 — JAH's persistent consensus — the champion never resets

Classic RANSAC remembers only the best; JAH's persistent consensus makes the memory structural. One champion committee holds the title from round 1; every new committee challenges it, and only a strictly bigger inlier count dethrones it. Ties keep the incumbent — the devil you know. The champion's count is monotone non-decreasing across rounds: a ratchet, not a lottery. Luck still draws the committees, but persistence keeps every gain.

The persistence changes the stopping logic too. Track the round of the last dethroning: if the champion has survived many rounds unchallenged, the remaining rounds are increasingly unlikely to matter — which is the seed of his patience rule (record 102). The champion's reign length is evidence about the search's completeness, free with every run.

The demo runs 20 rounds on the y=2x+1 data with 2 outliers, printing the champion's inlier count each time it changes: 2 -> 5 -> 8, dethroned twice, then unbeaten for the last 14 rounds. Final champion: slope 2.03, 8 inliers. The ratchet clicked twice and held. Signature-original to JAH, demonstrated on synthetic points.

def convolve(image, kernel):
    kh, kw = len(kernel), len(kernel[0])
    rows, cols = len(image), len(image[0])
    out = []
    for i in range(rows - kh + 1):
        r = []
        for j in range(cols - kw + 1):

            for a in range(kh):

JAH-KW-RANSAC-000102 — The patience rule — JAH's stopping discipline

Fixed round budgets are either wasteful (too many rounds after the answer is found) or reckless (too few before it is). JAH's patience rule adapts: run until K consecutive rounds pass with no dethroning, then stop. A champion that survives 8 straight challenges has proven itself against 8 fresh committees; the 9th is unlikely to teach anything. The search ends not when the budget runs out but when the search goes quiet — and quiet is evidence.

K is the patience parameter, and JAH sets it from the rounds formula's pricing: K = 8 covers the typical case where each round has a decent shot, and he raises it when the inlier ratio is known to be low. The rule composes with persistence (record 101): the ratchet keeps the gains, patience decides when the gains are done. Together they are his complete stopping discipline.

The demo runs the persistent champion with K=8 on the line data. The champion is dethroned at rounds 2, 4, and 6 (reaching 8 inliers), then survives rounds 7-14 unchallenged — patience exhausted at 14, search halts, 6 budgeted rounds unspent. Same answer as 20 fixed rounds, 30% less work. Signature-original to JAH.

The recognition principle

Which kernels feed it
The champion's reign length; patience is JAH's adaptive stop.
The recipe
Run rounds; reset a patience counter to K every time the champion is dethroned. Stop when the counter hits zero. The search ends K quiet rounds after its last success.
The knowing-when rule
STOP iff rounds_since_improvement >= K (here 8): the search has gone quiet, and quiet is evidence. Patience prices the remaining uncertainty.

JAH-KW-RANSAC-000104 — The refit ritual — JAH's two-stage sacrament

The proposal (RANSAC's champion) and the refit (least squares on the ledgered inliers) must BOTH be reported, with the movement between them, and the ritual is complete only if the movement is less than half the tolerance. A refit that wanders far from its proposal means the inlier set was contaminated; the ritual catches what the practice might wave through.

The liturgy has four lines — propose, refit, report, compare — and JAH insists on all four in every fit, because the comparison is a free diagnostic. Proposal and refit agreeing is the signature of a trustworthy model; disagreeing is the signature of a compromised inlier set. The ritual turns a number into a story with a moral.

The demo performs the ritual on the line data: propose slope 2.03 (champion, 8 inliers); refit slope 2.001, intercept 1.002 (least squares on the 8); movement 0.03 < t/2 = 0.75 — RITUAL COMPLETE, model final. Both stages reported, the comparison printed, the moral drawn. Signature-original to JAH.
