HVAC Sizing Calculator Database (hvac-sizing-calculator)

An HVAC sizing calculator is a rule-based local demo: it calculates set toy rooms, and counts the marks. The calculator reads 3 rooms. The room north holds 42 marks. The largest holds 42 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
