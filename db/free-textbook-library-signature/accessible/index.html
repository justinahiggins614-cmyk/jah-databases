    The free textbook library is a public shelf of free books for learners: a first book of numbers, a guide to words and meanings, a book of simple science experiments, and more, each a generic kind and never a real textbook. A database of twenty-three records needs a map, or a reader will not know where to start, which records are simple and which are complex, and how the records fit together. Without an index, the simple records that teach the design are buried beside the complex records that assume it, and a visitor who wants one idea must read the whole database to find it. The index solves this by listing every record in ladder order, with its place in the ladder, so a reader can start at the beginning and climb, or jump straight to the record they need. Worked example, Signature design: a reader who wants the simplest starting point opens record 1, A First Book of Numbers for Beginners, and a reader who wants the hardest problem opens record 23, A Library That Holds Every Free Textbook in a Town, in the Signature edition.
