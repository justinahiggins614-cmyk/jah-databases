1. Description

A video transcription writes the words spoken in a video, with timestamps. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it is rule-based, types a transcript from entered words, makes 0 real transcriptions, and real speech-to-text is BLOCKED. A real transcription needs a backend, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

3. Science proof

A video transcription works because speech is words at times, and a transcript writes them in order: a word every 1 second means 10 words take 10 seconds, and a timestamp at 5 seconds marks word 6 of 10. A real transcription needs a backend that hears speech, and that is BLOCKED here. That is why the local demo types its transcript from entered words and says plainly that it makes 0 real transcriptions.
