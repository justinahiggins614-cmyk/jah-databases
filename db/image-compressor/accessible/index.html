The Image Compressor Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An image compressor makes a picture's file smaller while keeping the picture itself. Upload a picture, choose a quality and a maximum size, and download the compressed copy. The original file is never changed; everything happens on a canvas in this browser, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/image-compressor/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What Compression Means

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. Compression makes a picture's file smaller while keeping the picture itself, by storing it more cleverly. A picture of 1,200 kilobytes compresses to 300 kilobytes, a quarter of the size.

    Math proof: Scaling by a factor s multiplies width and height by s, and the pixel count by s squared. Halving each side quarters the pixels. A compression ratio is the old size divided by the new size: 1,200 kilobytes to 300 kilobytes is a ratio of 4 to 1. Saving is a difference: 1,200 minus 300 is 900 kilobytes saved, or 75 percent.

    Science proof: A picture is a grid of pixels, and neighbouring pixels in a photo are much alike, so the same picture can be stored with fewer numbers. Quality is the trade: higher quality keeps more of the fine differences and a bigger file, lower quality drops them and a smaller file. The canvas in the browser does this arithmetic for every pixel.
