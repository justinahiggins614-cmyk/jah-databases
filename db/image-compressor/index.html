1. Description

An image compressor makes a picture's file smaller while keeping the picture itself. Upload a picture, choose a quality and a maximum size, and download the compressed copy. The original file is never changed; everything happens on a canvas in this browser, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Scaling by a factor s multiplies width and height by s, and the pixel count by s squared. Halving each side quarters the pixels. A compression ratio is the old size divided by the new size: 1,200 kilobytes to 300 kilobytes is a ratio of 4 to 1. Saving is a difference: 1,200 minus 300 is 900 kilobytes saved, or 75 percent.

3. Science proof

A picture is a grid of pixels, and neighbouring pixels in a photo are much alike, so the same picture can be stored with fewer numbers. Quality is the trade: higher quality keeps more of the fine differences and a bigger file, lower quality drops them and a smaller file. The canvas in the browser does this arithmetic for every pixel.
