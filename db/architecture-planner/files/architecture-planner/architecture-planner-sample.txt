Architecture Planner Database (architecture-planner)

An architecture planner is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy plans, and counts the parts. The planner reads 3 plans. The plan house holds 15 parts. The largest holds 15 parts. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
