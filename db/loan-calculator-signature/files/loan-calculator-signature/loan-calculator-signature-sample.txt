Loan Calculator Database (loan-calculator-signature)

A loan calculator estimates loan payments from the amount, the rate and the months. This page is an ESTIMATOR for education: every rate on it is a stated example rate, shown on the page, not a real loan rate, and it is not financial advice. Type a loan, pick an example rate, and read the estimate. Everything runs in this browser.

Worked example: A loan of 10000 paid back over 12 months is 12 payments
