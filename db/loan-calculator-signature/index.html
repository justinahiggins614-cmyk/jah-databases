1. Description

A loan calculator estimates loan payments from the amount, the rate and the months. This page is an ESTIMATOR for education: every rate on it is a stated example rate, shown on the page, not a real loan rate, and it is not financial advice. Type a loan, pick an example rate, and read the estimate. Everything runs in this browser.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A loan of 10000 at 12 percent over 12 months pays 888.49 a month, and 12 payments of 888.49 total 10661.85, which is 661.85 of interest. The same loan over 24 months pays 470.73 a month, and over 6 months it pays 1725.48 a month. At 6 percent it pays 860.66 a month, and at 24 percent it pays 945.60 a month. After 1 payment of 888.49, the balance is 9211.51.

3. Science proof

Loan estimation works because the arithmetic is simple: a monthly payment is worked out from the principal, the monthly rate and the number of months, the total is the payment times the months, and the interest is the total minus the principal. A longer loan means smaller payments but more interest, and paying extra cuts the balance. The estimate is only as good as the rate, which is why every rate on this page is labelled as an example and the page never claims to be real financial advice.

The estimate is only as good as the rate, which is why every rate on this page is labelled as an example and the page never claims to be real financial advice.
