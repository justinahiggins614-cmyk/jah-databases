  1. What a Loan Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A loan is money borrowed, paid back over time with interest. A loan of 10000 paid back over 12 months is 12 payments

    Math proof: A loan of 10000 at 12 percent over 12 months pays 888.49 a month, and 12 payments of 888.49 total 10661.85, which is 661.85 of interest. The same loan over 24 months pays 470.73 a month, and over 6 months it pays 1725.48 a month. At 6 percent it pays 860.66 a month, and at 24 percent it pays 945.60 a month. After 1 payment of 888.49, the balance is 9211.51.

    Science proof: Loan estimation works because the arithmetic is simple: a monthly payment is worked out from the principal, the monthly rate and the number of months, the total is the payment times the months, and the interest is the total minus the principal. A longer loan means smaller payments but more interest, and paying extra cuts the balance. The estimate is only as good as the rate, which is why every rate on this page is labelled as an example and the page never claims to be real financial advice.
