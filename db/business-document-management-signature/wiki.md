# The Business Document Management Database

The Business Document Management Database is the business document management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full business document management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database public-documents, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
