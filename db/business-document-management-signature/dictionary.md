# Dictionary entries — The Business Document Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## document

A written paper of a business, kept as one whole, with a title, a date, and a name for what it is.

## filing

Putting a document where it belongs, under its name and its kind, so that it can be found again by anyone who needs it.

## classification

The named kind of a document, such as a contract or an invoice, written on it so that documents of one kind can be found together.

## retention

The named length of time a document must be kept, written beside it, after which it may be retired, never before.

## archive

The kept store of documents whose active life is over, held where they can still be read, in the order they were filed.

## version

One named state of a document, fixed and dated, so that the document can be read as it stood on any day.

## search

Finding a document by typing part of its title, its kind, or its words, and receiving every document that matches, in order.

## access

The named right of a person to read a document, given by the document's keepers, and written beside the document.

## disposal

Retiring a document whose retention time has passed, in writing, with the date and the name of the person who retired it.

## audit trail

The ordered record of every change made to a document, naming who changed what and when, so the document can be traced back.

## metadata

The facts written about a document, such as its title, its kind, its date, and its keeper, kept beside the document itself.

## keeper

A person trusted to keep a document's rules, who decides who may read it, names its versions, and retires it when its time is done.
