Academic Paper Search Database (academic-paper-search-signature)

An academic paper search is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy papers, and counts the matches. The search reads 3 papers. The paper paper wins 110 matches. The largest holds 110 matches. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
