A Garden Album Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A photo organizer website: a visitor picks a generic kind of album, such as a garden album, a seaside album, or a family meal album, and the site shows how pictures of that kind are sorted: by subject, by time, and by place, with a plain name for each album. The site names no real person, shows no real picture, and every example is labeled as an illustration of the method. This record covers A Garden Album.

A garden album gives a person a plain way to keep pictures of a garden together, with each picture named by its subject, its season, and its place in the garden. The organizer must sort the pictures by subject first, then by season, so a person who wants the spring roses finds them at once and not after an hour of looking. Without a plain order, the pictures sit in one long pile, and a pile with no order is a poor place to find a single rose.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a garden album runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
