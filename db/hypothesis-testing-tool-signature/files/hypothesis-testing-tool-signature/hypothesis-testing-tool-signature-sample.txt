Hypothesis Testing Tool Database (hypothesis-testing-tool-signature)

A hypothesis testing tool runs real statistical tests: type the figures, press Test, and read the p-value. 60 heads in 100 flips gives z = 2.0 and p = 0.05. A mean of 105 against 100 with standard error 3.0 gives z = 1.67 and p = 0.10. Observed 20 and 30 against 25 and 25 gives chi-square 2.0 and p = 0.16. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
