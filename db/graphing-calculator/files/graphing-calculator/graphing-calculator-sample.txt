Graphing Calculator Database (graphing-calculator)

A graphing calculator draws a function as a curve on axes, so the shape of the mathematics can be seen. Type a function of x, choose the range, and the page plots it on a canvas in this browser.

Worked example: y equals 2x plus 1 passes through (0, 1) and (1, 3).
