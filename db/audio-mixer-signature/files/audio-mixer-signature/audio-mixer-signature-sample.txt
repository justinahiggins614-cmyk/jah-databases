Audio Mixer Database (audio-mixer-signature)

An audio mixer mixes sound samples with real sample arithmetic. Set the gains, press Mix, and read the peak and the average level. Mixing tracks with peaks 0.5 and 0.25 gives a mixed peak of 0.75. The peak of the samples is 0.75. Samples of 0.5 and -0.5 have a root mean square of 0.5. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
