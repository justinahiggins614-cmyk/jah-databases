The Lesson Planner Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A lesson planner builds a structured lesson plan from entered inputs: type the minutes, press Build, and read the plan. A 60-minute lesson has 5 parts. A lesson has 5 parts: intro, teach, practice, check, close. 60 minutes in 5 parts: 12 minutes each. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
