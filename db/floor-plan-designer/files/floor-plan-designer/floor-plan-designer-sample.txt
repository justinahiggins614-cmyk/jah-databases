Floor Plan Designer Database (floor-plan-designer)

A floor plan designer is a rule-based local demo: it plans set toy rooms, and counts the rooms and the floor area. The designer plans 3 rooms. The room kitchen is 12 by 10. The largest room holds 168 square units. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
