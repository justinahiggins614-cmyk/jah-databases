History
Veterinary surgeons have kept records since the craft began, first in ledgers of horses shod and cattle treated, then in card indexes of animals and their owners, and now in systems that hold the appointment book, the vaccination record, the surgery log, and the billing ledger side by side. A veterinary practice is a small world of animals seen, treatments given, owners advised, and payments counted, and the management of it is the keeping of those records true. From the paper card filed by owner to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the practice down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every veterinary practice record true.
