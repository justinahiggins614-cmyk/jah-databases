Scope and boundaries

DESIGN TARGETS ONLY. Any NASA or DARPA label in this database is a design target. These records do not claim agency qualification, approval, or endorsement, and they describe verification practice, not a qualified system.

Every record in this database is the full record with all its data. Records render as human-readable sentences. This database marches toward a million records; it currently holds 16 records in this edition, with a boundless generator below for further generation.

Fault Tree Guide is the archive-aware AI for this database. It answers questions about these records, explains procedures, and routes questions about other databases to the relevant specialist AI. It can build an assistant like the user's personal assistant. Ask it about this database's records, procedures, and checks; it answers from this database's own data.

Boundless generator

The boundless generator for this database creates further records in the same schema, seeded and deterministic, duplicate-checked, stopping when its variation space is genuinely exhausted, with a hard cap of one quadrillion records per database and throttling per run. Generated records carry src generated and are never mixed with the assistant-built records above. Generator script: gen-fault-tree-failure-mode.js.
