## Honesty limits

- PACKAGE-ONLY — not live. This website is part of a package-only build: nothing here is deployed, pushed, or merged.
- DARPA-grade means engineering targets modeled on public standards; it is never a claim of DARPA approval, certification, funding, or endorsement.
- The pasted 100-item DARPA-level catalog reads as another AI's words; its source is NOT confirmed and it is NOT Manon's own wording.
- A generated design is never presented as a proven breakthrough, flight-ready, or deployment-ready.
- Cybersecurity here is defensive only: identify weaknesses and improve protection. No working attack code, no offensive cyber operations.
- Every major project carries the nine-rung evidence ladder with sources, assumptions, methods, test outputs, and limitations.
- Signature design-study material is a SIGNATURE DESIGN CLAIM only, never established fact.
