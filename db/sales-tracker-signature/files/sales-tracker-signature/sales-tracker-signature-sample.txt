Sales Tracker Database (sales-tracker-signature)

A sales tracker adds up sales with real arithmetic: read the sales, press Add, and watch the totals. 10 sales at 25.00 is 250.00. Sales of 100, 150 and 200 total 450. Days of 120, 300 and 180: the best day is 300. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
