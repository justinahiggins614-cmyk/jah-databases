History

The comparative method was developed in the nineteenth century by Rasmus Rask, Jacob Grimm, and Franz Bopp, who compared the languages of Europe and India and proved they were related. It remains the standard method for establishing language families and reconstructing their ancestors. The topic is still taught and researched today, and the sources cited at the end of this record give the authoritative accounts on which this explanation rests.

Description
