The Signature Medical Evidence Verification Network Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The medical evidence verification network checks a health claim against its source in plain words, one check for each claim, with the claim, the source, and the limits of the check written out. Every check is a clearly labeled design study, never a verdict on a person's health and never advice about any person's health. The network never names a condition for a person and never promises that any claim is true.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/medical-evidence-verification-network-signature/index.jsonl.gz
