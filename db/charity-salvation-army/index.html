The Salvation Army — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. The Salvation Army is an international charitable organization with a Christian mission, headquartered in the US in Alexandria, Virginia. Founded in London in 1865 by William and Catherine Booth, it operates in the US through thousands of centers offering shelter, food, disaster relief, rehabilitation programs, and the famous Red Kettle holiday campaign.
