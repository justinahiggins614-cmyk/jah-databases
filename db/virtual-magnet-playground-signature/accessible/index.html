The Signature Virtual Magnet Playground Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The virtual magnet playground is a simulation only: a screen where magnets pull and push, iron dust gathers in curves, and a visitor can move a magnet and watch the pattern follow. Every record is a clearly labeled simulation, never a claim about real magnets or a real laboratory.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
