# Dictionary entries — The Sleep Tracking Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## sleep tracking

The recorded counting of the sleep a person takes, holding the bedtime records, the waking times, the sleep diary, and the summaries together, so that the sleep of a life can be read as a line.

## bedtime record

The written account of the hour at which sleep began on a night, kept beside the date, so that the beginning of the night can be read plainly.

## wake time record

The written account of the hour at which sleep ended on a morning, kept beside the date, so that the end of the night can be read plainly.

## sleep duration

The written length of one night sleep, counted from bedtime to waking, kept beside the date, so that the hours of the night can be counted.

## sleep stages

The named parts of sleep, such as light sleep, deep sleep, and dreaming sleep, written in the record so that the shape of the night can be read.

## night waking

A waking in the middle of the night, written in the record with its hour and its length, so that the broken nights can be counted beside the whole ones.

## sleep diary

The written diary of the sleep of each night, kept in order, so that what was slept can be compared with the pattern of the week.

## sleep environment

The written account of the room in which sleep is taken, naming the light, the noise, and the temperature, so that the conditions of the night can be read.

## evening routine

The written account of what is done in the hours before bed, kept beside the night it belongs to, so that the evening can be read beside the sleep that followed.

## morning routine

The written account of what is done in the hours after waking, kept beside the night it belongs to, so that the morning can be read beside the sleep that came before.

## nap record

The written account of a sleep taken in the day, naming its start, its end, and its length, so that the daytime sleep can be counted beside the night sleep.

## sleep debt note

The written note of the sleep missed over a run of nights, kept beside the diary so that the shortfall can be read as a number.
