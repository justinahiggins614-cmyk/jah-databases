# The Sleep Tracking Database

The Sleep Tracking Database is the sleep tracking record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full sleep tracking record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database data-provenance-tracking, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
