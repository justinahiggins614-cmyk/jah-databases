History
People have watched their sleep since the first households kept tallies of hours slept and dreams remembered, first in diaries of bedtimes and wakings, then in charts of hours and naps, and now in trackers that hold the bedtime record, the sleep stages, the diary, and the weekly summary side by side. Sleep tracking is the writing down of when sleep began, when it ended, and how the night went, so that the sleep of a life can be read as a line. From the paper diary kept by the bed to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the sleep down whole. This database holds records and general guidance only, and gives no medical advice. It is built on that public practice of recording every sleep tracking record true.
