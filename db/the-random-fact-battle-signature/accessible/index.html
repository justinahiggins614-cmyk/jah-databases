The Signature The Random Fact Battle Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The random fact battle pits two surprising facts against each other, and visitors vote for the one that amazes them most: a fact about the sea against a fact about the sky, a fact about numbers against a fact about animals. Every fact is a clearly labeled design study of a plain, checkable claim, and the battle never presents a fact as proven unless its source is written out.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
