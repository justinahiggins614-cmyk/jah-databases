# Dictionary entries — The Shipping Label Creation Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## shipping label

The printed sheet fixed to a parcel, naming the sender, the destination, and the service, so that every handler can read where the parcel must go.

## sender

The person or business sending a parcel, named on the label with their address, so that a parcel that cannot be delivered can be sent back.

## recipient

The person or business a parcel is sent to, named on the label with their address, so that the parcel can be delivered to the right hands.

## destination address

The full address where a parcel must be delivered, written on the label in the order the carrier reads, so that the parcel is not delayed by a missing line.

## return address

The sender's address on the label, used when a parcel cannot be delivered, so that undelivered parcels come back to the sender instead of being lost.

## barcode

The printed pattern of bars on a label that a scanner reads as a number, so that the parcel can be tracked by machine at every hand it passes through.

## tracking number

The one number given to a parcel when its label is made, printed as a barcode and as plain figures, so that the parcel can be followed from sender to recipient.

## postage

The price paid to carry a parcel, shown on the label or its record, so that the cost of each parcel can be counted and the carrier can be paid.

## weight

How heavy a parcel is, weighed and written on the label record, so that the carrier can charge by the true weight and handle the parcel safely.

## dimensions

The length, width, and height of a parcel, measured and written on the label record, so that the parcel can be placed, stacked, and charged by its true size.

## service class

The named level of carriage, such as standard or express, written on the label, so that the parcel travels at the speed its sender chose and paid for.

## customs form

The written declaration fixed to a parcel crossing a border, naming its contents and their value, so that the parcel can pass the border lawfully.
