1. Description

Document comparison lines two texts up side by side and lists what matches and what changed: matching lines, changed lines, added lines and removed lines, with a similarity score. Paste two texts, press Compare, and read the differences. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

2 of 4 lines match, so the similarity is 50 percent. Word by word, 5 of 6 words match. 2 lines were added and 2 lines were removed. The first text has 6 words and the second has 9 words. The longest shared line is 'The bird sang.' with 3 words.

3. Science proof

Comparison works because two texts are lists of lines, and each line either matches, changes, is added or is removed: 2 lines match, 2 changed, 2 added, 2 removed. A similarity score counts the matches over the whole. That is why the comparison shows the matching lines first, then lists every change.
