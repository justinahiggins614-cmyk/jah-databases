History

Consecutive interpreting is the oldest form of interpreting, used by traders, diplomats, and explorers for centuries before any technology existed. Description

Consecutive interpreting is the interpreting done after the speaker pauses: the interpreter listens to a passage, takes notes, and then renders it in the other language. History

Whispered interpreting is as old as interpreting itself, because it needs no equipment at all.
