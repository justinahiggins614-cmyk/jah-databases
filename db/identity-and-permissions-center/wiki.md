# The Identity and Permissions Center Database

The Identity and Permissions Center Database is the identity and permissions center record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full identity and permissions center record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database identity-roles-access-permissions, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
