Eli Lilly and Company — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Eli Lilly and Company is an American pharmaceutical company headquartered in Indianapolis, Indiana, founded in 1876 by Colonel Eli Lilly. It is one of the world's largest drugmakers by market value, known for diabetes and obesity medicines Mounjaro and Zepbound, insulin products, the Alzheimer's drug donanemab, and oncology and immunology portfolios. Lilly was the first company to mass-produce insulin and the polio vaccine.
