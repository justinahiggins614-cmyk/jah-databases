    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The official nothing website is a website about nothing: a blank page, a button that does nothing, a list with no items, all kept empty on purpose. Every nothing is a clearly labeled design study, never a claim about a live service, and the site asks a visitor for nothing at all. This record covers a page with nothing on it, with the emptiness kept on purpose so a visitor can enjoy a page that asks for nothing. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.
