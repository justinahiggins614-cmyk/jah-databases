## Spec
- **Care:** Switch off before cleaning; wipe the housing and impeller blades; check duct clamps and cords; keep the intake clear of loose fabric
- **Dimensions:** 8 in (203.2 mm) duct diameter; housing about 340 mm long; rated 807 CFM; 34 dBA at full speed; 60 W
- **Materials:** Steel housing; steel mounting brackets; EC motor with smart controller; aluminium impeller; temperature and humidity sensor
- **Mechanism:** EC motor spins an impeller inside the duct, pulling air through the enclosed path; smart controller sets the airflow from temperature and humidity readings; mounted high in the tent, it exhausts warm humid air first
- **Use:** Mount the fan in the duct run with clamps, connect ducting to a carbon filter on the intake side, set the speed on the controller, check that air flows out of the tent
- **Variants:** 6 in 402 CFM; 8 in 807 CFM; 10 in 1200 CFM; smart controller; temperature and humidity sensor
