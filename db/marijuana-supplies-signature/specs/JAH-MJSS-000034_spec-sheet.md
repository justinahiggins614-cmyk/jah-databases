## Spec
- **Care:** Empty the catcher after each session; rinse with warm water and soak in isopropyl alcohol when resin builds up; handle glass carefully as it breaks if dropped
- **Dimensions:** Bowl 22 mm across and 18 mm deep; catcher chamber 34 mm across, water depth 25 mm; lower joint 18 mm male at 90 degrees
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass with a coloured trap band; built-in flower bowl fused to the catcher chamber; no metal or plastic in the smoke path
- **Mechanism:** Smoke passes from the deeper Signature bowl into the wider chamber, where it slows and ash falls out into the water; the cleaned smoke continues into the bong
- **Use:** Match the joint size and angle to the bong, add water to about 60 percent of the chamber if wet, pack the built-in bowl, light while drawing gently, empty the catcher after the session
- **Variants:** 14 mm and 18 mm joints; 45 and 90 degree angles; wet and dry forms; percless, inline-perc, and honeycomb chambers; coloured trap-band finishes
