## Spec
- **Care:** Empty water after use; rinse with warm water; clean with isopropyl alcohol and coarse salt for residue; dry fully before storage; handle glass gently
- **Dimensions:** 177.8 mm (7 in) tall; tube 28 mm diameter; beaker base 90 mm diameter, 55 mm tall; water depth 35 mm
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass body with blue tint, 5 mm wall; glass downstem with inline perc; glass bowl, 14 mm joint
- **Mechanism:** Smoke travels down the downstem and bubbles through water and the inline perc, then rises through the neck to the mouthpiece; the beaker base holds the water and steadies the piece
- **Use:** Fill with water to about 35 mm so the downstem slits are submerged, fit the bowl, light and draw slowly, clear the chamber, empty and rinse after use
- **Variants:** Beaker base with inline perc; straight tube; ice-catcher mini; 5, 6, 7, and 8 inch heights; 10 mm and 14 mm joints
