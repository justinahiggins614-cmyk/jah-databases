## Spec
- **Care:** Soak the percolator in isopropyl alcohol to clear the slits; rinse well; dry fully; handle gently
- **Dimensions:** 400 mm tall; tube 65 mm diameter; 12-arm tree percolator, each arm 45 mm long with 8 slits; water depth 55 mm
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass with reinforced arm roots; 12-arm tree percolator; glass downstem and bowl
- **Mechanism:** Grove perc: the Signature design thickens the root of each arm where it meets the central stem, so the arms resist snapping while keeping the same slit pattern
- **Use:** Fill until the arms are covered, pack the bowl, draw slowly until the chamber fills, then clear
- **Variants:** Pocket grove, standard grove, and workshop grove versions; arm count varies by variant
