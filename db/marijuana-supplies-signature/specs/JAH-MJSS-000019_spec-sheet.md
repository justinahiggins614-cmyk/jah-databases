## Spec
- **Care:** empty ash daily, wash the bowl in warm soapy water, wipe the poker clean after each session, and check the glaze for chips, since a chipped ceramic edge can scratch a table or a glass pipe
- **Dimensions:** bowl 110 mm across and 35 mm deep; poker spike 60 mm tall; overall footprint 150 mm by 110 mm; rests spaced 25 mm apart
- **Materials:** glazed stoneware ceramic, borosilicate glass, stainless steel poker, silicone non-slip base ring
- **Mechanism:** ash and spent material fall into the deep bowl, the rim rests hold a lit item clear of the table, and the poker spike or loose poker stirs, taps out, or clears a clogged bowl by direct mechanical contact
- **Use:** tap ash into the bowl, rest the item in a rim notch between draws, press the bowl of a pipe gently against the spike to empty it, and use the poker tip to clear the airway of a clogged bowl
- **Variants:** silicone debowler cup with a soft central spike for cars; heavy glass ashtray with a loose poker stored in a side slot; lidded cache-cup ashtray that traps odour between uses
