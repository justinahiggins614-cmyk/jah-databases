## Spec
- **Care:** Wash with warm soapy water; dry to keep the finish bright; polish the brass poker when it tarnishes; store the poker clipped to the rim
- **Dimensions:** Dish 120 mm across and 35 mm deep; wall 2.5 mm thick; poker 5 mm across and 140 mm long; about 500 grams total
- **Materials:** Signature stainless steel dish with a brass poker; brushed finish; no plastic in the ash path
- **Mechanism:** Ash and spent material fall into the larger Signature dish; rim rests hold a cigarette or joint by gravity; the brass poker stirs ash and clears clogged bowls
- **Use:** Set on a flat surface, rest the cigarette in a rim rest, tap ash into the dish, use the poker to stir and clear, clip the poker back after use
- **Variants:** Stainless steel, brass, and cast iron; round and square dishes; clip-on and socket pokers; two to six rim rests
