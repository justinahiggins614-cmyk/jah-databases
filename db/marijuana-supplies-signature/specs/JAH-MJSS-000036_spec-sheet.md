## Spec
- **Care:** Wash with warm soapy water or vinegar solution; avoid sudden temperature changes such as rinsing a hot ashtray with cold water; handle glass carefully as it breaks if dropped
- **Dimensions:** Dish 130 mm across and 30 mm deep; wall 5 mm thick; four rim notches; about 279 grams
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass with a coloured base band; annealed; no metal or plastic in the ash path
- **Mechanism:** Ash and spent material fall into the deeper Signature dish; rim notches hold a cigarette or joint by gravity; the heavy base keeps the dish from tipping
- **Use:** Set on a flat heat-resistant surface, rest the cigarette in a rim notch, tap ash into the dish, empty when ash reaches a third of the depth
- **Variants:** Round, square, and geometric shapes; clear, amber, and frosted glass; coloured base bands; one to six rim notches
