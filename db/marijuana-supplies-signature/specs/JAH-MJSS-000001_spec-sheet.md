## Spec
- **Care:** Brush the helical ring clean; wash in warm soapy water; dry the mesh before reassembly
- **Dimensions:** 70 mm diameter x 52 mm tall; grinding chamber 30 mm deep; 4 pieces
- **Materials:** Signature anodised aluminium body with a helical tooth ring; stainless-steel mesh; ceramic-coated grinding plate
- **Mechanism:** Helical tooth ring: teeth set on a shallow spiral so one twist advances herb steadily downward through the grinding plate instead of trapping it at the rim
- **Use:** Load the chamber, twist the lid through one full helical turn, and collect ground herb from the storage chamber below
- **Variants:** Pocket 45 mm, standard 70 mm, and workshop 110 mm helical-ring versions; tooth pitch varies by variant
