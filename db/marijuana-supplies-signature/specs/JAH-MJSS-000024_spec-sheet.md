## Spec
- **Care:** replace the odour pouches when they stop working, wipe the seals clean so grit does not break the airtight seat, keep the backup key somewhere separate from the box, and never force the lock, since forcing it can jam the hasp
- **Dimensions:** outside 235 mm by 190 mm by 127 mm (9.25 by 7.5 by 5 inches); inside 210 mm by 165 mm by 100 mm; wall thickness 4 mm; lock dials 3 digits
- **Materials:** high-impact ABS plastic shell, dual silicone seals, activated-carbon fibre lining, stainless steel lock hasp
- **Mechanism:** the dual silicone seal compresses when the lid closes, trapping air and odour inside, while the combination lock holds the lid shut and the adjustable dividers keep contents from sliding
- **Use:** set the combination from the 0-0-0 default, arrange contents in the tray and dividers, close the lid fully so both seals seat, scramble the dials, and store the box upright in a cool dry place
- **Variants:** bamboo humidor-style box with a combination lock; waterproof hard case; soft ballistic-nylon bag with a zipper combination lock; large 11 by 9.75 by 5 inch family size
