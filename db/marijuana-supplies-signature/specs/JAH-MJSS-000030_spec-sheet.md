## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; shake with isopropyl alcohol and salt; rinse well; dry fully
- **Dimensions:** 140 mm long; water chamber 50 mm diameter; water depth 18 mm; bowl 20 mm diameter and 14 mm deep
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass one-piece body; fixed glass bowl and fixed downstem; silicone grip sleeve
- **Mechanism:** Sidecar mouthpiece: the Signature design moves the mouthpiece to the side, so the piece can be held level without water running toward the mouth
- **Use:** Add water to cover the inner stem, pack the bowl, cover the carb, light while drawing slowly, then uncover the carb and inhale
- **Variants:** Pocket sidecar, standard sidecar, and workshop sidecar versions; chamber diameter varies by variant
