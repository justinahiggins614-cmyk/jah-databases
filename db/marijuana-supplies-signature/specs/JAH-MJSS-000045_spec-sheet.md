## Spec
- **Care:** Empty after use, brush off dried mix, rinse with water and dry fully before storage; check seams and handles for wear; fold flat for storage
- **Dimensions:** 7 gallon (26.5 L) nominal; 33 cm diameter x 30 cm tall; two reinforced handles
- **Materials:** 500 GSM needlepunched nonwoven polypropylene fabric; bonded polyester thread, quad-stitched; reinforced webbing handles
- **Mechanism:** Porous fabric wall lets air reach the root zone and drains excess water; root tips meeting air stop and branch, preventing circling roots
- **Use:** Set the pot on a tray, fill with growing mix, place it where it will stay, lift by both handles when moving it
- **Variants:** 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, and 20 gallon; reinforced handles; square bottom; black fabric; 500 GSM
