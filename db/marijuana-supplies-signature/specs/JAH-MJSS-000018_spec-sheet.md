# The Isopropyl Alcohol Cleaning Soak — Spec sheet

JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies-signature#JAH-MJSS-000018

## Spec
- **Care:** let the pipe dry completely before any use, since alcohol left inside is flammable; store alcohol away from flames, lighters and heat; never heat alcohol on a stove or in a microwave; never use this soak on silicone, which the alcohol degrades, or on wood, which it can crack and strip
- **Dimensions:** soak container 200 mm by 150 mm by 80 mm; 500 ml bottle of 91% isopropyl alcohol; 1 tablespoon (about 18 g) of coarse salt per soak
- **Materials:** isopropyl alcohol (propan-2-ol), coarse kosher salt (sodium chloride), low-density polyethylene bag, borosilicate glass pipe
- **Mechanism:** isopropyl alcohol is a polar solvent that dissolves the sticky resin film, while coarse salt stays undissolved in the alcohol and acts as a moving abrasive that scours resin the liquid alone cannot lift
- **Use:** let the pipe cool, tap out loose ash, place it in the bag, pour in enough alcohol to submerge it, add about a tablespoon of coarse salt, seal the bag, shake for one to two minutes, soak for 30 minutes to 12 hours, then rinse with warm water until no alcohol smell remains
- **Variants:** quick 15-minute shake clean for light buildup; overnight soak for a neglected piece; alcohol-only soak without salt for delicate thin glass; baking-soda and vinegar backup method when no alcohol is available
