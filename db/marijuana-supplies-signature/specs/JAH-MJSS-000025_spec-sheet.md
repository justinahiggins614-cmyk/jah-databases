## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; clean with isopropyl alcohol and salt; dry fully before storage
- **Dimensions:** 315 mm tall; flared base 110 mm diameter at the foot; neck 54 mm diameter; water depth 40 mm in the base
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass with a thickened 5 mm base wall; stepped ice pinches; glass downstem and bowl
- **Mechanism:** Cascade base: the Signature design steps the inside of the flared base in two shallow rings, so rising bubbles are broken twice before they reach the neck
- **Use:** Fill to the marked water line, pack the bowl, draw slowly until the neck fills, then clear
- **Variants:** Pocket cascade, standard cascade, and workshop cascade versions; ring count varies by variant
