## Spec
- **Care:** Rinse the chamber and bucket after use; check the guide ring for dents; dry fully before storage
- **Dimensions:** 1.5-litre glass chamber about 260 mm tall and 90 mm diameter; bucket larger than the chamber; lift height 200 mm
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass chamber with a machined metal cap and bowl; steel bucket with a guide ring
- **Mechanism:** Plunge guide: the Signature design adds a guide ring on the bucket rim, so the glass chamber rises and falls straight instead of tilting and catching
- **Use:** Submerge the chamber to its neck, light the bowl while lifting slowly, remove the bowl, and push the chamber down steadily while breathing in
- **Variants:** Pocket plunge, standard plunge, and workshop plunge versions; chamber volume varies by variant
