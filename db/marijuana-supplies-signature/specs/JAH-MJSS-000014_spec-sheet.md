## Spec
- **Care:** Keep packs sealed until use; do not puncture or soak; do not mix different RH targets in one container; store spare packs in an airtight bag
- **Dimensions:** Size 8 pack weighs 8 g and suits up to 1 ounce; size 60 pack weighs 60 g; size 67 pack weighs 67 g and suits up to 1 pound (450 g)
- **Materials:** Saturated solution of pure water and natural salts inside a semipermeable reverse-osmosis membrane packet; food-safe and FDA compliant
- **Mechanism:** The membrane passes only water vapour; the saturated salt solution holds a fixed equilibrium relative humidity, releasing vapour when the air is dry and absorbing it when the air is wet
- **Use:** Drop one pack into an airtight jar with the contents; use 62% for a softer feel or 58% for a drier feel; replace when the pack feels hard
- **Variants:** 58% RH and 62% RH packs; sizes 8 g, 60 g, 67 g, 320 g; packs for cigars, wood instruments and food at other RH targets
