## Spec
- **Care:** Rinse quartz screens in warm water and dry fully; discard a metal screen that is warped, torn, or clogged beyond cleaning; keep spares in a small tin
- **Dimensions:** Metal mesh screens 19.05 mm (3/4 in) diameter, also 15.9 mm (5/8 in); quartz daisy screen 14 mm diameter
- **Materials:** Titanium woven mesh, 0.3 mm wire; quartz glass daisy screen; stainless-steel storage tin
- **Mechanism:** Mesh disc sits at the bottom of the bowl; woven openings pass air and block ash and particles; quartz daisy petals hold the screen above the bowl opening and channel air around it
- **Use:** Press the mesh disc gently into the bowl so it sits flat over the opening, or set the quartz daisy in the bowl; replace a mesh screen when it clogs or warps
- **Variants:** Titanium mesh; stainless-steel mesh; quartz daisy; quartz honeycomb; brass mesh
