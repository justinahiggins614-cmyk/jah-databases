## Spec
- **Care:** keep tips dry, since damp paper collapses; use one tip per roll and discard it afterwards; do not use glossy, plastic-coated or adhesive-coated paper, which can release unwanted substances when heated
- **Dimensions:** strip 50 mm long by 15 mm wide; finished tip 6 mm in diameter and 15 mm long; booklet 55 mm by 40 mm; pack of 50 tips
- **Materials:** unbleached hemp-fibre paper, 150 gsm card-weight paper, vegetable-based gum on pre-rolled tips
- **Mechanism:** the strip is folded into an accordion of three or four folds and rolled into a cylinder, and the folded core acts as a lattice that holds the mouthpiece open under suction while blocking loose plant matter
- **Use:** fold one end of the strip back and forth three or four times into a W or M shape, roll the rest of the strip tightly around the folds, place the finished tip at the mouthpiece end of the rolling paper, and build the roll around it
- **Variants:** pre-rolled tips; extra-wide tips for large rolls; slim tips at 5 mm diameter; reusable glass tips; wooden tips
