## Spec
- **Care:** Wash or replace the pre-filter sleeve regularly; keep the canister dry; replace the carbon or the canister when odour leaks; store in a dry place
- **Dimensions:** 6 in (150 mm) flange; canister 150 mm diameter x 400 mm long; carbon bed 38 mm thick; rated about 402 CFM
- **Materials:** Stainless-steel canister and flange; virgin Australian activated carbon, 1200+ iodine value; stainless mesh cylinders; washable cloth pre-filter sleeve
- **Mechanism:** Fan pulls air through the outer mesh, across the activated carbon bed where odour molecules adsorb to the carbon pores, and out through the flange to the duct
- **Use:** Hang the canister inside the tent on straps, fit the pre-filter sleeve, connect the flange to the inline fan with ducting and clamps, run the fan so air passes through the bed
- **Variants:** 4 in x 12 in; 6 in x 16 in; 8 in x 24 in; refillable canisters; reversible flange; 38 mm and 52 mm beds
