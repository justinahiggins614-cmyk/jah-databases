## Spec
- **Care:** Replace balloons when worn; clean the chamber and screens; keep the air intake clear
- **Dimensions:** 210 mm tall x 210 mm diameter; 120 W heater; balloon holds about 3.5 litres
- **Materials:** Signature aluminium twin heat exchanger; stainless-steel chamber; food-grade balloon; mains heater and pump
- **Mechanism:** Twin heat exchanger: two aluminium blocks in series, so air is reheated between blocks and reaches the chamber at a steadier temperature than a single block
- **Use:** Fill the chamber, preheat, attach the balloon, run the fan until firm, detach and inhale through the valve
- **Variants:** Compact twin, standard twin, and lounge twin versions
