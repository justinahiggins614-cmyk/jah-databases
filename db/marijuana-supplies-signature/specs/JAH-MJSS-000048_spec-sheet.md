## Spec
- **Care:** Empty each tier after use; brush off loose material; wash the mesh gently and dry fully; collapse flat into the carry bag for storage
- **Dimensions:** 8 tiers; 600 mm (24 in) diameter; 1,700 mm hanging length; each chamber 170 mm high
- **Materials:** Non-toxic polyester mesh, heavy grade; stainless-steel wireframe; steel hanging hook with swivel; zippered chambers; carry bag
- **Mechanism:** Material is spread in a single layer on each mesh tier; air passes through the mesh floor and walls; the tower hangs from one hook so air moves around it on all sides
- **Use:** Hang the rack from a bar or hook, open the zipper of a tier, spread material in a single layer, close the zipper, repeat for the other tiers
- **Variants:** 4, 6, and 8 tiers; 600 mm (24 in) and 800 mm (32 in) diameters; removable tiers; swivel hook; with carry bag
