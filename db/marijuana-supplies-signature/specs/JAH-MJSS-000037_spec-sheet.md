## Spec
- **Care:** Wash with soap and water or in a dishwasher; do not quench a hot ashtray in cold water; handle ceramic carefully as it chips and cracks if dropped
- **Dimensions:** Dish 140 mm round and 37 mm tall; bowl 126 mm across and 30 mm deep; wall 7 mm thick; four rim notches; about 469 grams
- **Materials:** Signature glazed stoneware with a crackle glaze; fired to cone 5 or 6; food-safe glaze; unglazed base
- **Mechanism:** Ash and spent material fall into the round Signature bowl; rim notches hold a cigarette or joint by gravity; the weight keeps the dish from tipping
- **Use:** Set on a flat surface, rest the cigarette in a rim notch, tap ash into the bowl, empty when ash reaches a third of the depth
- **Variants:** Round, square, and slab shapes; earthenware, stoneware, and porcelain bodies; crackle, clear, and coloured glazes; hand-built and wheel-thrown
