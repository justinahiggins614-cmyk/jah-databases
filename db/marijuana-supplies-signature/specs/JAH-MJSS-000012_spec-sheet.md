## Spec
- **Care:** Store in a cool, dry place; keep the wax coating intact; trim charred ends before relighting; keep away from open flame when not in use
- **Dimensions:** Reference ball 20 feet (6.1 m) long, about 2 mm diameter; sold from 1 foot to 250 feet; thicknesses from 1 mm to 3 mm
- **Materials:** Hemp twine core, beeswax coating; some versions use soy wax
- **Mechanism:** Beeswax melts, is drawn up the hemp fibres by capillary action, and burns as a small steady flame; the wax slows the burn of the hemp core
- **Use:** Light the free end with a lighter or match, hold the flame to the target, pinch out to extinguish; wrap spare wick around a lighter for carrying
- **Variants:** Thin 1 mm wick, standard 2 mm wick, thick 3 mm wick; beeswax or soy wax coating; pre-wound balls and loose spools
