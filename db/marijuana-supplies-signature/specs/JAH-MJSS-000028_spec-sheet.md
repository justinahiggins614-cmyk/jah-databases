## Spec
- **Care:** Soak the loop tubes in isopropyl alcohol; rinse well; dry fully; handle gently
- **Dimensions:** 360 mm tall; main chamber 380 mL; secondary chamber 200 mL; uptake tube 12 mm diameter; drain tube 10 mm diameter; water 230 mL in total
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass with a widened uptake funnel; showerhead percolator; glass downstem and bowl
- **Mechanism:** Vortex funnel: the Signature design widens the mouth of the uptake tube into a funnel, so water enters the loop smoothly instead of choking at the tube mouth
- **Use:** Fill so the percolator is covered and the upper chamber stays dry when still, draw steadily so the loop starts, then clear
- **Variants:** Pocket vortex, standard vortex, and workshop vortex versions; uptake funnel width varies by variant
