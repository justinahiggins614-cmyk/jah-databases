## Spec
- **Care:** Brush the milling disc after use; wipe the window; recharge by USB-C; keep the motor housing dry
- **Dimensions:** 120 mm tall x 60 mm diameter; chamber holds up to 4 g per load
- **Materials:** Signature aluminium housing; stainless-steel milling disc; lithium-ion cell; USB-C port; transparent chamber window
- **Mechanism:** Pulse motor: the Signature design fires the milling disc in timed pulses with a pause between pulses, so heat does not build up and particle size stays even
- **Use:** Load the chamber, press once for a pulse cycle, and collect the ground herb when the cycle ends
- **Variants:** Pocket pulse, standard pulse, and bench pulse versions; pulse length varies by variant
