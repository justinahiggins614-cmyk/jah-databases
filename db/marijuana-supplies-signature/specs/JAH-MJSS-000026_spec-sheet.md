## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; clean with isopropyl alcohol and salt; dry fully before storage
- **Dimensions:** 340 mm tall; tube 55 mm diameter; flat foot 80 mm diameter; water depth 50 mm
- **Materials:** Signature borosilicate glass with a thickened 5 mm foot ring; glass downstem and bowl
- **Mechanism:** Lance foot: the Signature design widens the foot into a weighted ring, so the straight tube keeps its fast clear but stands more steadily than a plain flat foot
- **Use:** Fill to the marked water line, pack the bowl, draw slowly until the tube fills, then clear
- **Variants:** Pocket lance, standard lance, and workshop lance versions; foot ring width varies by variant
