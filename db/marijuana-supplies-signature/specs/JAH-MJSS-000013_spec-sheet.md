## Spec
- **Care:** Wash with warm soapy water and dry fully; check the lid seal for dents or rust; replace lids when the sealing compound is worn; do not overtighten
- **Dimensions:** Quart reference jar 946 ml, about 9.5 cm diameter x 14 cm tall; pint version 473 ml; half-gallon version 1893 ml
- **Materials:** Soda-lime or borosilicate glass body, tin-plated steel two-piece lid with plastisol sealing compound; amber glass version available
- **Mechanism:** Rigid glass walls block air and moisture transfer; the lid gasket compresses against the rim to form an airtight seal; amber glass absorbs ultraviolet light
- **Use:** Fill loosely to no more than 75% full, add a humidity pack, close the lid firmly, label with the date, store in a cool dark place
- **Variants:** Clear wide-mouth mason jar, amber glass jar, violet glass jar, borosilicate jar with silicone sleeve and rubber gasket, clamp-lid jar
