## Spec
- **Care:** Empty ash after cooling; soak in isopropyl alcohol, rinse, and dry; handle ceramic carefully as it chips and cracks if dropped; do not quench a hot bowl in cold water
- **Dimensions:** Cup 24 mm across and 18 mm deep; joint 18 mm; wall 3.5 mm thick; about 50 mm tall overall
- **Materials:** Signature glazed stoneware with a heat-band glaze; 18 mm male ground joint; ceramic-coated screen
- **Mechanism:** Ground herb packed in the deeper Signature cup; the thicker wall stores more heat for an even burn; smoke passes through the cup floor into the downstem; lifting the bowl clears the pipe
- **Use:** Check the joint size, set the ceramic-coated screen, pack ground herb loosely, slide the bowl into the joint, light while drawing gently, lift to clear, empty ash after cooling
- **Variants:** 14 mm and 18 mm joints; shallow and deep cups; heat-band glaze in three colours; wheel-thrown and hand-built finishes
