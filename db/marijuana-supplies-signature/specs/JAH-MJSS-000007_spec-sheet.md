## Spec
- **Care:** Keep dry and flat; store away from damp
- **Dimensions:** 78 mm x 44 mm; booklet of 32 leaves; gum strip 4 mm wide
- **Materials:** Signature woven hemp fibre paper at 12 gsm; gum arabic strip; unbleached
- **Mechanism:** Woven fibre: the Signature design weaves hemp fibres in a cross pattern so the paper tears cleanly along the roll direction and resists tearing across it
- **Use:** Roll as a standard paper: spread herb, roll into a cylinder, moisten the gum strip, and seal
- **Variants:** Single wide woven, 1 1/4 woven, and king slim woven versions
