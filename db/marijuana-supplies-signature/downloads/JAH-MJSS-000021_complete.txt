The Dab Tool Set

The Dab Tool Set — Signature edition — is a Signature-made design study of the same supply, drawn up as a new product rather than a report on an existing one. This design keeps the same job, dab tool set for handling concentrates, but reworks the parts as a single matched set: five interchangeable tools on one handle: a flat paddle, a pointed pick, a scoop, a spatula blade and a shovel, plus a glass bubble carb cap, a silicone mat and a zippered case. The Signature design specifies grade 2 titanium tips, stainless steel handle, borosilicate glass carb cap, food-grade silicone mat throughout, with each part sized to the others so that no tool in the case is a loose fit for its task. The design dimensions are handle 100 mm long; each tool head 30 mm long; paddle 8 mm wide; pick tip 1 mm across; carb cap 25 mm across; case 170 mm by 110 mm by 30 mm. In the Signature design, the tool tip picks up, scoops or spreads a measured piece of concentrate and carries it to the heated banger, and the carb cap then covers the banger to restrict airflow, lower the pressure inside, and direct vapour across the hot surface. Choose the tip that matches the concentrate texture, paddle for crumbly material and pick for sticky material, load the banger without touching the hot glass, cap it, and wipe the tip clean with a cotton swab while it is still warm. Maintenance of the design follows the same rules as the public version. Wipe tips after every session, soak metal tips in isopropyl alcohol when residue hardens, never use a steel tool on a hot quartz banger with force, since a hard scrape can chip it, and store tips in the case so the fine points do not bend. Planned variants of the Signature design are the single titanium dabber; dabber and carb cap combo tool; multi-tool folding set; glass sword dabber with a titanium tip. This is a design study, not a real product and not an established fact: no Signature edition unit has been manufactured, tested, or sold, and the dimensions and figures in this record are design targets, not measured results. Legal note: laws on cannabis and cannabis accessories vary by jurisdiction; check local law before buying, carrying, or using this item.

SCIENCE PROOF
The Signature design uses the same science as the public version, because the physics does not change when a product is redesigned, and the design study checks that the chosen materials and dimensions respect it. The solvent chemistry is unchanged: isopropyl alcohol dissolves resin because its polar hydroxyl group and its carbon chain can both interact with the tar-like film, and coarse salt stays undissolved in alcohol, so it keeps its edges and scours. The mechanical science is also unchanged: bristles clean by friction, a mesh separates by size, a seal contains odour by compression, and a lock resists opening by counting combinations. What the design adds is a check that the numbers fit the science. The reach must exceed the airway, the aperture must sit between the particle sizes it should pass and the ones it should hold back, the water depth must cover the percolator without reaching the mouthpiece, and the heat capacity of a tool must be small enough that it does not steal the heat of the surface it touches. Where the public record reports what guides have observed, the Signature record states what the design intends, and it labels the difference plainly. The science proof therefore proves the design is consistent with known physics, not that it has been tested, and the demo computes the numbers that show that consistency.

import hashlib, json, math, random

TOOL_MASS_G = 18
TITANIUM_CP = 0.523
DELTA_T_K = 200
PICK_TIP_MM = 1
LOAD_FORCE_N = 0.5
TOOLS_IN_SET = 5

def compute(seed):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    heat_j = round(TOOL_MASS_G * TITANIUM_CP * DELTA_T_K, 1)
    tip_radius_m = (PICK_TIP_MM / 2.0) / 1000.0
    tip_area_m2 = math.pi * tip_radius_m ** 2
    tip_pressure_pa = round(LOAD_FORCE_N / tip_area_m2, 1)
    load_g = round(TOOL_MASS_G * 0.02, 2)
    per_tool_j = round(heat_j / TOOLS_IN_SET, 1)
    cool_seconds = round(heat_j / 25.0, 1)
    wobble = round(rng.uniform(0.98, 1.02), 3)
    out = {
        "tool_mass_g": TOOL_MASS_G,
        "titanium_specific_heat_j_per_g_k": TITANIUM_CP,
        "temperature_change_k": DELTA_T_K,
        "heat_absorbed_j": heat_j,
        "heat_per_tool_j": per_tool_j,
        "pick_tip_pressure_pa": tip_pressure_pa,
        "typical_load_grams": load_g,
        "cooling_time_seconds": cool_seconds,
        "tools_in_set": TOOLS_IN_SET,
        "measurement_jitter": wobble,
    }
    return out

def main():
    first = compute(7)
    second = compute(7)
    for key, value in first.items():
        print(f"{key}: {value}")
    d1 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(first, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    d2 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(second, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    print(f"digest_run_1: {d1}")
    print(f"digest_run_2: {d2}")
    print("Deterministic: MATCH" if d1 == d2 else "Deterministic: MISMATCH")

main()
