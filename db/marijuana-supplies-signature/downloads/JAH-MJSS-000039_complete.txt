SCIENCE PROOF
A silicone ashtray works by heat tolerance, elasticity, and a contact seal. Silicone rubber tolerates continuous heat up to about 230 degrees C, the figure the cited supplier guides give, because its silicon-oxygen backbone does not break down the way a carbon backbone does, so a hot ember on the surface chars the ash but not the dish. Silicone is elastic, so the lid lip stretches over the rim and springs back to seal, and it is non-porous, so tar and smell sit on the surface instead of soaking in.
