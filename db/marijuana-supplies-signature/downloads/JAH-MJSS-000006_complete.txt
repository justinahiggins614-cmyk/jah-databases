SCIENCE PROOF
A bong is a gas-washing bottle. Smoke is a mixture of hot gases and small solid particles, and bubbling it through water does two things at once. First, the water is a heat sink: smoke leaving the bowl is hot, and contact with water at room temperature transfers heat out of the gas, so the smoke that reaches the mouth is cooler. Second, the water is a trap: heavier particles such as ash have too much inertia to follow the gas around a bubble wall and strike the water, and some water-soluble molecules dissolve. Smaller bubbles work better because surface area grows as bubble size shrinks: for the same volume of gas, many small bubbles expose far more gas-water interface than a few large ones, which is why diffused downstems and percolators with many small slits are used. The chamber above the water is a reservoir, and the water level sets the pressure the user must overcome: the draw must lift the water column by the depth of the downstem below the surface, a small hydrostatic pressure of water density times gravity times depth. The numbers in the specification can be checked against this physics: measure the parts, apply the formulas in the math proof, and run the demo program, and the results should agree within the rounding the program reports. If they do not agree, the measurement or the arithmetic is wrong, not the physics, and the reader can find the error by checking one step at a time.

MATH PROOF
The demo treats the tube as a cylinder. The cross-sectional area is pi times the tube radius squared, with a tube diameter of 55.0 mm. Multiplying that area by the water depth of 55.0 mm gives the water volume, and multiplying it by the height above the water gives the chamber volume, both reported in millilitres. A single bubble is treated as a sphere with diameter 3.0 mm: its volume is four thirds pi times radius cubed, and its surface area is four pi times radius squared. The draw pressure is water density times gravity times depth, which the program reports in pascals. HISTORY
The bong descends from the hookah and from bamboo water pipes of Asia; the word itself is adapted from the Thai word baung, meaning a wooden cylinder or pipe cut from bamboo. Glass bongs became common in the twentieth century as borosilicate glass made a heat-resistant, clean-tasting water chamber practical. A 1981 United States patent (4,253,475) describes the classic form in detail: a single vertical pipe about 1.5 inches in diameter sealed at the bottom, a bowl on a tube leading in near the bottom, water just above the junction, and a small hole above the water line to clear the chamber. Over time the same basic form was copied, adapted, and refined by many makers, so the record today covers a family of closely related objects rather than a single patented shape. Museums, trade guides, and manufacturer manuals preserve the steps of that change, and the sources cited at the end of this record give the reader places to check each stage. import hashlib, math
def compute():
    tube_d = 55.0; height = 320.0; water_h = 55.0; down_d = 12.0; bubble_d = 3.0
    area_mm2 = math.pi * (tube_d / 2.0) ** 2
    water_mm3 = area_mm2 * water_h
    chamber_h = height - water_h
    chamber_mm3 = area_mm2 * chamber_h
    bubble_r = bubble_d / 2.0
    bubble_vol_mm3 = (4.0 / 3.0) * math.pi * bubble_r ** 3
    bubble_area_mm2 = 4.0 * math.pi * bubble_r ** 2
    pressure_pa = 1000.0 * 9.81 * (water_h / 1000.0)
    return {"water_volume_mm3": round(water_mm3, 1), "water_volume_mL": round(water_mm3 / 1000.0, 1), "chamber_volume_mL": round(chamber_mm3 / 1000.0, 1), "bubble_volume_mm3": round(bubble_vol_mm3, 2), "bubble_surface_mm2": round(bubble_area_mm2, 2), "draw_pressure_Pa": round(pressure_pa, 1)}
def main():
    a = compute(); b = compute()
    sa = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(a.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    sb = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(b.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    for key in ["water_volume_mm3", "water_volume_mL", "chamber_volume_mL", "bubble_volume_mm3", "bubble_surface_mm2", "draw_pressure_Pa"]:
        print(f"{key}: {a[key]}")
    print(f"Run 1 SHA-256: {sa}")
    print(f"Run 2 SHA-256: {sb}")
    print(f"Deterministic: {'MATCH' if sa == sb else 'MISMATCH'}")
if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
