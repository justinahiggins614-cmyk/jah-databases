Filter Tips (Crutches) — Signature edition — is a Signature-made design study of the same supply, drawn up as a new product rather than a report on an existing one. This design keeps the same job, filter tips crutches for rolled joints, but reworks the parts as a single matched set: a booklet of 50 pre-cut unbleached hemp-fibre tips, each strip 50 mm long and 15 mm wide, plus 10 pre-rolled tips and a folding guide card. The Signature design specifies unbleached hemp-fibre paper, 150 gsm card-weight paper, vegetable-based gum on pre-rolled tips throughout, with each part sized to the others so that no tool in the case is a loose fit for its task. The design dimensions are strip 50 mm long by 15 mm wide; finished tip 6 mm in diameter and 15 mm long; booklet 55 mm by 40 mm; pack of 50 tips. In the Signature design, the strip is folded into an accordion of three or four folds and rolled into a cylinder, and the folded core acts as a lattice that holds the mouthpiece open under suction while blocking loose plant matter. Fold one end of the strip back and forth three or four times into a w or m shape, roll the rest of the strip tightly around the folds, place the finished tip at the mouthpiece end of the rolling paper, and build the roll around it. Maintenance of the design follows the same rules as the public version. Keep tips dry, since damp paper collapses; use one tip per roll and discard it afterwards; do not use glossy, plastic-coated or adhesive-coated paper, which can release unwanted substances when heated. Planned variants of the Signature design are the pre-rolled tips; extra-wide tips for large rolls; slim tips at 5 mm diameter; reusable glass tips; wooden tips. This is a design study, not a real product and not an established fact: no Signature edition unit has been manufactured, tested, or sold, and the dimensions and figures in this record are design targets, not measured results. STRIP_WIDTH_MM = 15
TIP_DIA_MM = 6
TIP_LENGTH_MM = 15
ACCORDION_FOLDS = 4
TIPS_PER_BOOKLET = 50

def compute(seed):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    radius = TIP_DIA_MM / 2.0
    bore_area = round(math.pi * radius ** 2, 2)
    circumference = round(math.pi * TIP_DIA_MM, 2)
    strip_area = STRIP_LENGTH_MM * STRIP_WIDTH_MM

    out = {
        "strip_length_mm": STRIP_LENGTH_MM,

        "strip_area_mm2": strip_area,
