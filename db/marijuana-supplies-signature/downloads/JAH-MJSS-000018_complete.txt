The Isopropyl Alcohol Cleaning Soak — Signature edition — is a Signature-made design study of the same supply, drawn up as a new product rather than a report on an existing one. This design keeps the same job, isopropyl alcohol cleaning soak for glass pipes, but reworks the parts as a single matched set: a bottle of 91% or 99% isopropyl alcohol, coarse kosher salt, a sealable bag or lidded container, pipe cleaners and cotton swabs. The Signature design specifies isopropyl alcohol (propan-2-ol), coarse kosher salt (sodium chloride), low-density polyethylene bag, borosilicate glass pipe throughout, with each part sized to the others so that no tool in the case is a loose fit for its task. The design dimensions are soak container 200 mm by 150 mm by 80 mm; 500 ml bottle of 91% isopropyl alcohol; 1 tablespoon (about 18 g) of coarse salt per soak. In the Signature design, isopropyl alcohol is a polar solvent that dissolves the sticky resin film, while coarse salt stays undissolved in the alcohol and acts as a moving abrasive that scours resin the liquid alone cannot lift. Let the pipe cool, tap out loose ash, place it in the bag, pour in enough alcohol to submerge it, add about a tablespoon of coarse salt, seal the bag, shake for one to two minutes, soak for 30 minutes to 12 hours, then rinse with warm water until no alcohol smell remains. Maintenance of the design follows the same rules as the public version. Let the pipe dry completely before any use, since alcohol left inside is flammable; store alcohol away from flames, lighters and heat; never heat alcohol on a stove or in a microwave; never use this soak on silicone, which the alcohol degrades, or on wood, which it can crack and strip. Planned variants of the Signature design are the quick 15-minute shake clean for light buildup; overnight soak for a neglected piece; alcohol-only soak without salt for delicate thin glass; baking-soda and vinegar backup method when no alcohol is available. This is a design study, not a real product and not an established fact: no Signature edition unit has been manufactured, tested, or sold, and the dimensions and figures in this record are design targets, not measured results. Legal note: laws on cannabis and cannabis accessories vary by jurisdiction; check local law before buying, carrying, or using this item.

import hashlib, json, math, random

ALCOHOL_PERCENT = 91
ALCOHOL_VOLUME_ML = 250
SALT_GRAMS = 18
SOAK_LIGHT_MIN = 30
SOAK_HEAVY_MIN = 720
SHAKE_SECONDS = 90

def compute(seed):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    water_fraction = 1.0 - ALCOHOL_PERCENT / 100.0
    pure_alcohol_ml = round(ALCOHOL_VOLUME_ML * ALCOHOL_PERCENT / 100.0, 1)
    water_ml = round(ALCOHOL_VOLUME_ML * water_fraction, 1)
    salt_ratio = round(SALT_GRAMS / ALCOHOL_VOLUME_ML, 3)
    k = 0.02 + 0.0006 * ALCOHOL_PERCENT
    eff_light = round(100.0 * (1.0 - math.exp(-k * SOAK_LIGHT_MIN)), 1)
    eff_heavy = round(100.0 * (1.0 - math.exp(-k * SOAK_HEAVY_MIN)), 1)
    evap_ml_per_hour = round(ALCOHOL_VOLUME_ML * 0.04 * rng.uniform(0.9, 1.1), 1)
    shakes = SHAKE_SECONDS // 30
    out = {
        "alcohol_percent": ALCOHOL_PERCENT,
        "solution_volume_ml": ALCOHOL_VOLUME_ML,
        "pure_alcohol_ml": pure_alcohol_ml,
        "water_in_solution_ml": water_ml,
        "salt_grams": SALT_GRAMS,
        "salt_grams_per_ml": salt_ratio,
        "shake_blocks_of_30s": shakes,
        "resin_dissolved_light_soak_percent": eff_light,
        "resin_dissolved_heavy_soak_percent": eff_heavy,
        "evaporation_ml_per_hour": evap_ml_per_hour,
    }
    return out

def main():
    first = compute(7)
    second = compute(7)
    for key, value in first.items():
        print(f"{key}: {value}")
    d1 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(first, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    d2 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(second, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    print(f"digest_run_1: {d1}")
    print(f"digest_run_2: {d2}")

main()
