SCIENCE PROOF
The bubbler works by shrinking the bong down to one hand. Its water chamber is small, about 24 millilitres in the common example, against about 88 millilitres for a straight tube, so the smoke passes through a short column of water and the cooling is moderate rather than strong. The bowl is small too, about 1.5 cubic centimetres, which sets a small load per bowl, and that is the trade the bubbler makes: it gives up the bong's large chamber and large bowl in exchange for fitting in a pocket. The fixed downstem is a single piece of glass with the body, so there is no joint to leak and no part to lose, but there is also no way to take the stem out for cleaning, which is why bubblers are harder to clean than bongs. The carb hole is the bubbler's clearing mechanism: covering it forces the draw through the bowl and the water, and uncovering it lets fresh air rush in and push the smoke out. Borosilicate glass is used because the piece is carried and handled often, and a glass that tolerates heat and knocks is what survives that use. HISTORY
The bubbler sits between the dry hand pipe and the full-size bong, and it grew out of the glass pipe movement of the 1970s, when glassblowers in the United States began making small pipes from borosilicate glass. A bubbler keeps the hand pipe's shape, a fixed bowl, a carb hole, and a mouthpiece, and adds a small built-in water chamber, so smoke passes through water like in a bong while the piece stays small enough to hold in one hand. Trade guides list the main forms as hammer, Sherlock, sidecar, pendant, concentrate, double, and ratchet bubblers, plus silicone versions, and they describe the bubbler as the everyday portable option against the bong's home use. The fixed downstem is the bubbler's defining compromise: it makes the piece simple and sturdy, and it makes cleaning harder, because nothing comes apart. Over time the same basic form was copied, adapted, and refined by many makers, so the record today covers a family of closely related objects rather than a single patented shape. Trade guides and manufacturer manuals preserve the steps of that change.     bowl_mm3 = math.pi * b_h / 3.0 * ((b_d / 2.0) ** 2 + (b_d / 2.0) * b_r + b_r * b_r)
    bowl_cm3 = bowl_mm3 / 1000.0
    cap = bowl_cm3 * rho
    chamber_mm3 = math.pi * (ch_d / 2.0) ** 2 * ch_len
