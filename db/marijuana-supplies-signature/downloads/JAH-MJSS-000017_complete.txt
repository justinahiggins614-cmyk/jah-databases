The Pipe Cleaning Kit — Signature edition — is a Signature-made design study of the same supply, drawn up as a new product rather than a report on an existing one. This design keeps the same job, pipe cleaning kit for smoking pipes, but reworks the parts as a single matched set: bristle pipe cleaners, soft cotton pipe cleaners, a shank brush, a 3-in-1 Czech-style pipe tool (tamper, reamer, pick), a bowl scraper and a lint-free cloth, held in a zippered case. The Signature design specifies cotton fibre, nylon bristles, twisted steel wire, stainless steel, brass, beechwood handle throughout, with each part sized to the others so that no tool in the case is a loose fit for its task. The design dimensions are cleaners 150 mm long; shank brush 120 mm long with a 12 mm brush head; 3-in-1 tool 95 mm long; case 180 mm by 120 mm by 35 mm. In the Signature design, the twisted-wire cleaner carries absorbent cotton through the stem airway, the bristle cleaner scrubs the airway wall, the shank brush sweeps the shank, and the reamer shaves the carbon cake inside the bowl back to an even layer. After the pipe has cooled, run a dry cleaner through the stem until it comes out clean, brush the shank, ream the bowl only when the cake is thicker than about 1.5 mm, and wipe the outside with the cloth. Maintenance of the design follows the same rules as the public version. Wash the shank brush in warm soapy water and let it dry, replace pipe cleaners after one use, oil the wooden handle lightly, and keep the steel tools dry so they do not rust. Planned variants of the Signature design are the travel kit with 10 cleaners; bench kit with 50 cleaners and an adjustable reamer; glass-only kit with nylon brushes and no metal reamer. This is a design study, not a real product and not an established fact: no Signature edition unit has been manufactured, tested, or sold, and the dimensions and figures in this record are design targets, not measured results. DEMO
import hashlib, json, math, random

CLEANER_LENGTH_MM = 150
GRIP_MM = 30
STEM_AIRWAY_MM = 120
BRUSH_DIA_MM = 12
BRUSH_LEN_MM = 25
CLEANERS_PER_KIT = 24

def compute(seed):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    residue = 100.0
    passes = 0
    total_cleaner_mm = 0
    while residue > 1.0 and passes < 12:
        removed = residue * rng.uniform(0.35, 0.55)
        residue -= removed
        passes += 1
        total_cleaner_mm += CLEANER_LENGTH_MM
    reach = CLEANER_LENGTH_MM - GRIP_MM
    coverage = round(100.0 * reach / STEM_AIRWAY_MM, 1)
    brush_area = round(math.pi * BRUSH_DIA_MM * BRUSH_LEN_MM, 1)
    out = {
        "cleaner_length_mm": CLEANER_LENGTH_MM,
        "usable_reach_mm": reach,
        "airway_length_mm": STEM_AIRWAY_MM,
        "airway_coverage_percent": coverage,
        "passes_to_clean": passes,
        "residue_remaining_percent": round(residue, 2),
        "cleaner_length_used_mm": total_cleaner_mm,
        "brush_contact_area_mm2": brush_area,
        "cleaners_in_kit": CLEANERS_PER_KIT,
    }
    return out

def main():
    first = compute(7)
    second = compute(7)
    for key, value in first.items():
        print(f"{key}: {value}")
    d1 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(first, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    d2 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(second, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    print(f"digest_run_1: {d1}")
    print(f"digest_run_2: {d2}")
    print("Deterministic: MATCH" if d1 == d2 else "Deterministic: MISMATCH")

main()
