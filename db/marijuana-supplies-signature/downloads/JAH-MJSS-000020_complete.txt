The Glass Dab Rig

The Glass Dab Rig — Signature edition — is a Signature-made design study of the same supply, drawn up as a new product rather than a report on an existing one. This design keeps the same job, glass dab rig water pipe for concentrates, but reworks the parts as a single matched set: a borosilicate glass body with a water chamber, a fixed or removable downstem, a percolator, a quartz banger, a carb cap, and a bent neck mouthpiece. The Signature design specifies borosilicate glass body, fused quartz banger, stainless steel or titanium carb cap option, silicone-free glass joints throughout, with each part sized to the others so that no tool in the case is a loose fit for its task. The design dimensions are overall height 200 mm; chamber diameter 70 mm; water fill 60 ml covering the percolator slits by 12 mm; banger 25 mm across with a 20 mm deep bucket; joint size 14 mm. In the Signature design, concentrate placed on the heated banger vaporises, the vapour is drawn down the stem into the water chamber, and the percolator breaks the vapour into many small bubbles so it cools against the water before rising up the neck. Fill with clean water just above the percolator slits, heat the banger, let it cool to the working range, place the concentrate in the banger, cap it, and draw slowly so the water bubbles without splashback. Maintenance of the design follows the same rules as the public version. Change the water after every session, clean the banger with a cotton swab while it is still warm, soak the glass in isopropyl alcohol and salt when residue builds, and never shake a rig hard around a percolator, since a hard shake can snap thin glass. Planned variants of the Signature design are the mini rig 150 mm tall for travel; recycler rig that loops water through a second chamber; showerhead-perc rig; honeycomb-perc rig. This is a design study, not a real product and not an established fact: no Signature edition unit has been manufactured, tested, or sold, and the dimensions and figures in this record are design targets, not measured results. Legal note: laws on cannabis and cannabis accessories vary by jurisdiction; check local law before buying, carrying, or using this item.

The solvent chemistry is unchanged: isopropyl alcohol dissolves resin because its polar hydroxyl group and its carbon chain can both interact with the tar-like film, and coarse salt stays undissolved in alcohol, so it keeps its edges and scours. The mechanical science is also unchanged: bristles clean by friction, a mesh separates by size, a seal contains odour by compression, and a lock resists opening by counting combinations. What the design adds is a check that the numbers fit the science. Where the public record reports what guides have observed, the Signature record states what the design intends, and it labels the difference plainly. The science proof therefore proves the design is consistent with known physics, not that it has been tested, and the demo computes the numbers that show that consistency.

DEMO
import hashlib, json, math, random

CHAMBER_DIA_MM = 70
WATER_DEPTH_MM = 16
PERC_SLITS = 12
BUBBLES_PER_SLIT = 8
VAPOUR_TEMP_C = 220
WATER_TEMP_C = 20

def compute(seed):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    radius = CHAMBER_DIA_MM / 2.0
    water_ml = round(math.pi * radius ** 2 * WATER_DEPTH_MM / 1000.0, 1)
    bubble_dia_mm = 3.0
    bubble_area = math.pi * bubble_dia_mm ** 2
    bubbles = PERC_SLITS * BUBBLES_PER_SLIT
    total_bubble_area = round(bubbles * bubble_area, 1)
    water_kg = water_ml / 1000.0
    vapour_drop_c = 85.0
    heat_j = round(water_kg * 4186.0 * vapour_drop_c, 1)
    out_temp = VAPOUR_TEMP_C - vapour_drop_c
    splash_margin = round(rng.uniform(4.0, 6.0), 1)
    out = {
        "chamber_diameter_mm": CHAMBER_DIA_MM,
        "water_depth_mm": WATER_DEPTH_MM,
        "water_volume_ml": water_ml,
        "percolator_slits": PERC_SLITS,
        "bubbles_per_draw": bubbles,
        "total_bubble_area_mm2": total_bubble_area,
        "vapour_in_temp_c": VAPOUR_TEMP_C,
        "vapour_out_temp_c": out_temp,
        "heat_absorbed_by_water_j": heat_j,
        "splash_margin_mm": splash_margin,
    }
    return out

def main():
    first = compute(7)
    second = compute(7)
    for key, value in first.items():
        print(f"{key}: {value}")
    d1 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(first, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    d2 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(second, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
    print(f"digest_run_1: {d1}")
    print(f"digest_run_2: {d2}")
    print("Deterministic: MATCH" if d1 == d2 else "Deterministic: MISMATCH")

main()
