The Ashtray with Poker

The Ashtray with Poker — Signature edition — is a Signature-made design study of the same supply, drawn up as a new product rather than a report on an existing one. This design keeps the same job, ashtray with built-in poker tool, but reworks the parts as a single matched set: a deep ceramic or heat-resistant glass bowl, a fixed central poker spike or a loose stainless steel poker, cigarette rests moulded into the rim, and a non-slip base. The Signature design specifies glazed stoneware ceramic, borosilicate glass, stainless steel poker, silicone non-slip base ring throughout, with each part sized to the others so that no tool in the case is a loose fit for its task. The design dimensions are bowl 110 mm across and 35 mm deep; poker spike 60 mm tall; overall footprint 150 mm by 110 mm; rests spaced 25 mm apart. In the Signature design, ash and spent material fall into the deep bowl, the rim rests hold a lit item clear of the table, and the poker spike or loose poker stirs, taps out, or clears a clogged bowl by direct mechanical contact. Tap ash into the bowl, rest the item in a rim notch between draws, press the bowl of a pipe gently against the spike to empty it, and use the poker tip to clear the airway of a clogged bowl. Maintenance of the design follows the same rules as the public version. Empty ash daily, wash the bowl in warm soapy water, wipe the poker clean after each session, and check the glaze for chips, since a chipped ceramic edge can scratch a table or a glass pipe. Planned variants of the Signature design are the silicone debowler cup with a soft central spike for cars; heavy glass ashtray with a loose poker stored in a side slot; lidded cache-cup ashtray that traps odour between uses. This is a design study, not a real product and not an established fact: no Signature edition unit has been manufactured, tested, or sold, and the dimensions and figures in this record are design targets, not measured results. Legal note: laws on cannabis and cannabis accessories vary by jurisdiction; check local law before buying, carrying, or using this item.

import hashlib, json, math, random

BOWL_DIA_MM = 110
BOWL_DEPTH_MM = 35
POKER_HEIGHT_MM = 60
POKER_FORCE_N = 2.0
ASH_ML_PER_SESSION = 4

def compute(seed):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    radius = BOWL_DIA_MM / 2.0
    gross_ml = round(math.pi * radius ** 2 * BOWL_DEPTH_MM / 1000.0, 1)
    usable_ml = round(gross_ml * 0.8, 1)
    ash_capacity_g = round(usable_ml * 0.35, 1)
    torque_nm = round(POKER_FORCE_N * POKER_HEIGHT_MM / 1000.0, 3)
    sessions = int(usable_ml // ASH_ML_PER_SESSION)
    jitter = round(rng.uniform(0.95, 1.05), 3)
    out = {
        "bowl_diameter_mm": BOWL_DIA_MM,
        "bowl_depth_mm": BOWL_DEPTH_MM,
        "bowl_gross_volume_ml": gross_ml,
        "bowl_usable_volume_ml": usable_ml,
        "ash_capacity_grams": ash_capacity_g,
        "sessions_before_emptying": sessions,
        "poker_height_mm": POKER_HEIGHT_MM,
        "poker_torque_nm": torque_nm,
        "measurement_jitter": jitter,
    }
    return out

def main():

    second = compute(7)
    for key, value in first.items():
