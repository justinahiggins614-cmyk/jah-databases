SCIENCE PROOF
Shear, not crushing, is the working principle. Opposing diamond teeth pass each other with a small clearance, and the edges cut dried flower along its natural fracture planes. Aluminium 6061 is chosen because it is light, machines cleanly, and its anodised surface resists corrosion from plant resins. The teeth stay sharp because the load is spread across 56 teeth and each tooth meets only a small piece of herb at a time. Ground particles fall through sized holes in the grinding plate by gravity once they are smaller than the hole diameter, which is why a dozen steady turns produces a fairly uniform particle size instead of powder. The 100-micron mesh in a four-piece grinder is a sieve: particles finer than 100 microns, including the small resin glands called trichomes, pass through, while larger ground herb stays above. Friction is low because the herb is dry and brittle, and the magnet lid keeps the halves aligned so the teeth never clash metal on metal. HISTORY
The herb grinder as a two-half cylindrical shredder is documented in the early 1900s, with William Wingfield and John Balding of Boort, Victoria, Australia patenting a novel grinding device in 1905, and the form was popularised in the 1960s as cannabis use spread. DEMO
"""Demo: manual grinder chamber volume — The Manual Herb Grinder."""
import hashlib, math
def compute():
    d = 70.0; h = 30.0; teeth = 56; screen_d = 62.0; turns = 8
    r = d / 2.0
    chamber_mm3 = math.pi * r * r * h
    chamber_cm3 = chamber_mm3 / 1000.0
    screen_area_mm2 = math.pi * (screen_d / 2.0) ** 2
    teeth_per_cm2 = teeth / (math.pi * r * r / 100.0)
    return {"chamber_volume_mm3": round(chamber_mm3, 2), "chamber_volume_cm3": round(chamber_cm3, 2), "screen_area_mm2": round(screen_area_mm2, 2), "teeth": teeth, "turns": turns, "teeth_per_cm2": round(teeth_per_cm2, 2)}
def main():
    a = compute(); b = compute()
    sa = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(a.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    sb = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(b.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    for key in ["chamber_volume_mm3", "chamber_volume_cm3", "screen_area_mm2", "teeth", "turns", "teeth_per_cm2"]:
        print(f"{key}: {a[key]}")
    print(f"Run 1 SHA-256: {sa}")
    print(f"Run 2 SHA-256: {sb}")
    print(f"Deterministic: {'MATCH' if sa == sb else 'MISMATCH'}")
if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
