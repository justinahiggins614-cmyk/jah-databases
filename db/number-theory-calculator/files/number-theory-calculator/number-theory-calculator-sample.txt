Number Theory Calculator Database (number-theory-calculator)

A number theory calculator works out greatest common divisors, least common multiples, remainders and totients: type two numbers, press Calculate, and read the answer. The greatest common divisor of 12 and 18 is 6. 2 to the 10 is 1024. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
