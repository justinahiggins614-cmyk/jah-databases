Bulk Record Exporter Database (bulk-record-exporter-signature)

A bulk record exporter is a rule-based local demo: it exports set toy batches, and counts the marks. The exporter reads 3 batches. The batch alpha holds 21 marks. The largest holds 21 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
