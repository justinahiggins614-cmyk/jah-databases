Collaboration Workspace Database (collaboration-workspace-signature)

A collaboration workspace holds shared channels, members, messages and tasks in one local place. Type a message, press Send, and read the channel. A workspace holds 3 channels: general, design and build. The channel holds 4 messages. 3 of 5 tasks are done. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
