Crop Identification Database (crop-identification-signature)

A crop identification is a rule-based local demo: it identifies set toy crops, and counts the marks. Sample-data identification only: no cultivation how-to, no grow schedules, no yield instructions. Sample crops only, no real fields read. The identifier reads 3 crops. The crop alpha holds 162 marks. The largest holds 162 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
