The Knowledge Time Machine Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The knowledge time machine takes a reader on a plain-word journey through time: what was known in one year, what changed in the next, and what stayed unknown, all told as a timeline a reader can walk year by year. Every journey is a design study of generic public knowledge, never a claim that the past can be visited or changed.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/knowledge-time-machine/index.jsonl.gz
