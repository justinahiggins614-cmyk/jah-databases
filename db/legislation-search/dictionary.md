# Dictionary entries — The Legislation Search Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## legislation search

The recorded searching of the laws made by a legislature, holding the query, the laws found, and the citations together, so that what was found can be read and proved.

## bill

A proposed law laid before a legislature, named by its title and its number, kept so that the proposal can be followed from its first reading to its fate.

## statute

A law as enacted by a legislature, named by its title and its year, kept so that the law as made can be read in full.

## amendment

A written change proposed or made to a bill or a statute, naming the text it changes, so that the change can be followed step by step.

## committee report

The written report of a legislative committee on a bill, naming the committee and its findings, so that the scrutiny of the bill can be read.

## debate record

The written record of what was said in a legislature about a bill, kept by date, so that the argument can be read after it ends.

## vote record

The written account of how a legislature voted on a bill, naming the members and their votes, so that the decision can be counted and checked.

## enactment date

The date on which a bill became law, written with the statute it belongs to, so that the beginning of the law can be read plainly.

## repeal record

The written account of a law that has been undone, naming the law and the act that undid it, so that the end of the law can be shown as plainly as its beginning.

## consolidated text

The text of a law as it stands after all its amendments, kept beside the original so that the law as it now reads can be found.

## legislative history

The ordered account of a law from its first bill to its final text, kept by date, so that the making of the law can be followed.

## citation record

The written reference naming a law by its title, its year, and its chapter, so that the law can be found again by anyone.
