AI Model Evaluator Database (ai-model-evaluator)

An AI model evaluator is a rule-based local demo: it scores a model on set test items, counts the right answers, and reports the share. 8 of 10 answers are right: 80 percent. 9 of 10 answers are right: 90 percent. Precision 0.75 and recall 0.75 give a balanced score of 0.75. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 8 of 10 answers are right: 80 percent
