Science Tutor Database (science-tutor)

A science tutor explains the world with worked examples: pick a topic, press Solve, and read the steps. It never pretends to be a live tutor. 100 metres in 20 seconds is 5.0 metres per second. A mass of 2 kilograms at 3 metres per second squared gives force 6 newtons. A mass of 50 grams in 25 cubic centimetres has density 2.0 grams per cubic centimetre. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
