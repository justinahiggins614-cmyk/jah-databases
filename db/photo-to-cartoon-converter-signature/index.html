1. Description and History

A photo-to-cartoon converter turns a photograph into a cartoon drawing: bold outlines, flat bright colors, and simplified shapes, while keeping the person or scene recognizable. This database designs that converter one record at a time, from a simple portrait to a whole album in one consistent style, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.

Cartooning a photograph began by hand: an artist traced a photo, simplified the shapes, and filled them with flat color. Software later learned the same steps — find the edges, flatten the colors, simplify the shapes — and the steps became fast enough to run on a phone. The design in this database follows those same steps, keeps the original photo untouched, and lets the user choose the style.
