  1. A Weekly Plan in Plain Words

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The workout planner is a plain website where a person builds a weekly activity plan from general exercises, with rest days built in and a log to mark each session done. It gives general information only: no medical claims, no promises about results, and no specific amounts of any supplement or medicine. A weekly plan that nobody can read is a plan nobody follows, because the exercises are named in jargon and the days are unclear. A plan in plain words names each session simply, says which day it falls on, and states how long it lasts. Without plain words, a person opens the plan, cannot tell what to do on Monday, and closes it again. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a plan builder, a weekly view, and a log that shows which sessions are done. Weekly plans in plain words are an old habit of coaches, who have always written a session so anyone could follow it.
