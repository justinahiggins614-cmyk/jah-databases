Marketing automation is a rule-based local demo: it sends set toy messages to set toy contacts, and counts the opens and the clicks. The automation writes to 1000 contacts. The contact is called sam. 60 of 1000 messages clicked: 6 percent. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
