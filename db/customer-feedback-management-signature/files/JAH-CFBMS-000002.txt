History
Businesses have always learned from their customers by listening: the shopkeeper heard complaints across the counter and changed the shop. As businesses grew, listening had to become a system — letters, then calls, then forms and surveys — and the system had to keep every message, sort it by what it is about, answer it or send it to the person who can act, and remember what was done. This database is built on that public practice of listening in an orderly way.
