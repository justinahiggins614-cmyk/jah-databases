The Derivative Calculator Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A derivative calculator differentiates functions by the power rule, the product rule and the chain rule, and checks the answer with a numeric slope. Type a function, and it shows the rule, the substitution and the slope. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

  1. What a Derivative Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A derivative is the slope of a curve at a point. The derivative of x squared at x = 3 is 6

    Math proof: The derivative of x to the 4 is 4x cubed: at x = 2 it is 32. The derivative of x squared + 2x is 2x + 2: at x = 3 it is 8. The derivative of (2x + 1) squared is 4(2x + 1): at x = 1 it is 12. The second derivative of x to the 4 is 12x squared: at x = 2 it is 48. The numeric slope of x squared at x = 3 is 6.0, matching the symbolic 6.

    Science proof: A derivative works because the power rule turns each term into a simpler one: x to the n becomes n times x to the n minus 1, and a constant becomes zero. Sums and differences differentiate term by term, products use the product rule, and a function of a function uses the chain rule. A numeric slope checks the symbolic answer: if the two agree, the derivative is right.
