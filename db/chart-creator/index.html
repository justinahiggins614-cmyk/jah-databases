1. Description

A chart creator turns numbers into bars, lines and pies: type numbers, pick a chart, press Draw, and read the chart. A bar chart of [10, 20, 30] has a total of 60. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

3. Science proof

A chart creator works because numbers become shapes, and each shape is plain arithmetic: a bar chart of [5, 10, 15, 20] has 4 bars and a total of 50, and doubling [10, 20] gives [20, 40]. The middle value of [10, 20, 30] is 20. That is why the local demo shows the numbers and the chart together, and runs 0 creations on a server.
