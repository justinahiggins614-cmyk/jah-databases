  1. Matching by Subject, Level, and Hours

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Study partner matching pairs learners who study the same subject at the same level, so each pair can quiz each other, share notes in the open, and keep a steady study rhythm. A study partner must match on three things at once, because a partner in the right subject at the wrong level will either race ahead or fall behind, and a partner at the right level with no shared hour will never meet. The match should weigh subject first, then level, then the hours both partners can study, and say plainly which of the three was the closest. Without all three, the pair spends its first weeks discovering the mismatch, and the study never starts. Worked example: in a website built this way, matching by subject, level, and hours runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

    History: Study partners are an old habit of schools and libraries, where two learners at the same table learned faster than one alone. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a match by subject, level, and hours, a public study room, and a shared quiz board. Matching on three things at once is a new problem, because older study pairs formed by sitting at the same table, which settled all three at once.

    Proposed solution: Name the study pair and what a visitor does in it; Write the rules in plain sentences a new visitor can follow; Give every study pair the same fair start: the same clues, the same time, the same scoring; Let a visitor try one study pair without signing in; Check the study pair works the same way on a phone and on a computer.

    Dependencies: What must exist first: a subject and level list, a matching step, a public study room, and a shared quiz board. Nothing else is needed to open the first study pair.

  13. Matching by Level as Well as by Subject

    Record 13 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: research-stage. Active public research exists on automatic matching that pairs learners by level as well as by subject, results are published, but no working system at the needed scale yet.

    Study partner matching pairs learners who study the same subject at the same level, so each pair can quiz each other, share notes in the open, and keep a steady study rhythm.
