# The Internship Discovery Database

The Internship Discovery Database is the internship discovery record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full internship discovery record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database research-discovery, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
