  12. A Streak for Checking In

    Record 12 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The personal finance dashboard is a plain website where a person types in their own example numbers and sees estimates of income, spending, and savings on one screen. It shows estimates only: it is never financial advice, it holds no real account data, and every number on it is one the visitor typed in themselves. A streak for checking in gives a person a reason to come back tomorrow: one look at the dashboard each day in a row builds a chain, and breaking the chain costs something the person has earned.
