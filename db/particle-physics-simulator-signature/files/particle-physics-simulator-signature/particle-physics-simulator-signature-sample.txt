Particle Physics Simulator Database (particle-physics-simulator-signature)

A particle physics simulator simulates particle interactions with real physics: type the particle, press Simulate, and read the energy. 1 kilogram holds 9.00e+16 joules. A mass of 2 kilograms at 3 metres per second holds 9.0 joules. At 0.8 times light speed the factor is 1.67. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
