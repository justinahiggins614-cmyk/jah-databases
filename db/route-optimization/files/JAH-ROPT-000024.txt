History
Finding the best way has always been a traveller's problem: the merchant who visited many towns asked in which order to visit them, so that the road travelled was the shortest and the day was not wasted. Mathematicians gave the problem its name — the travelling salesman problem — and its methods: count the possible orders, measure each, and keep the best. Route optimization is that counting made into a record: for each set of stops, the order chosen, the cost counted, and the reason the order was chosen, all written down. This database is built on that public practice of recording every optimized route true, with its stops and its cost.
