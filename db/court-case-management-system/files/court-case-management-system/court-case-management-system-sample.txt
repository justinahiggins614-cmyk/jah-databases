Court Case Management System Database (court-case-management-system)

A court case management system is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy cases, and counts the marks. The system reads 3 cases. The case adams holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
