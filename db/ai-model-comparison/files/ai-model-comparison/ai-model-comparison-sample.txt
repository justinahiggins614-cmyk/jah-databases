AI Model Comparison Database (ai-model-comparison)

An AI model comparison is a rule-based local demo: it sets two models on the same test, and reports the gap between their scores. Model A scores 85 and Model B scores 90: B leads by 5. Model B leads Model A by 8. Model B scores 90 over Model A at 80: a ratio of 1.12. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
