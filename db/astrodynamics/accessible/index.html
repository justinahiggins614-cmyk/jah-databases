JAH-ASTROD-000001 · the three-body problem

Description: The three-body problem is the problem of predicting the motion of three bodies pulling on each other by gravity, and it has no general closed-form solution: unlike the two-body problem, which gives neat ellipses, three bodies move chaotically and must be computed step by step.
