The The Weirdest Website Competition Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The weirdest website competition gathers the oddest websites anyone can dream up, a site that only lists left shoes, a site that counts blinks, a site that ranks puddles, and lets visitors vote for the weirdest. Every entry is a clearly labeled design study, never a claim about a live website, and every vote is fair play.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/the-weirdest-website-competition/index.jsonl.gz
