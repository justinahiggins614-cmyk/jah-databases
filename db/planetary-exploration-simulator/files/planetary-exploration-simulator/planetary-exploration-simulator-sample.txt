Planetary Exploration Simulator Database (planetary-exploration-simulator)

A planetary exploration simulator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy planets, and counts the marks. The simulator reads 3 planets. The planet mars holds 45 marks. The largest holds 45 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
