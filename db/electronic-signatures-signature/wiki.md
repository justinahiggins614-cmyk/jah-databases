# The Electronic Signatures Database

The Electronic Signatures Database is the electronic signatures record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full electronic signatures record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database software-digital-signatures, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
