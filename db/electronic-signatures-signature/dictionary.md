# Dictionary entries — The Electronic Signatures Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## signature

A mark made by a signer on a document, bound to the document's words, so that any change to the words breaks the bond and shows the change.

## signer

The person who signs, who alone holds the secret that makes their signature, and whose name the signature carries.

## document

The words being agreed to, kept exactly as signed, so that the signature can be checked against them at any time.

## key

A secret held by the signer, which makes their signature, and which is never shared, never written in the document, and never left where others can take it.

## verification

Checking a signature against its document, by the public part of the signer's key, which says whether the signer signed and whether the document is unchanged.

## certificate

A written statement by a trusted issuer that a public key belongs to a named signer, with its dates, so that a signature can be trusted without meeting the signer.

## consent

A signer's clear agreement to the document they sign, which the signature records, and which can be withdrawn only by a new document, never by erasing the old one.

## timestamp

The time a signature was made, recorded beside it, so that the order of signatures is known and a signature cannot be claimed for another time.

## revocation

Taking back a key or a certificate, so that signatures made after the taking-back are not trusted, with the taking-back written in a public list.

## envelope

The wrapper that carries a document, its signatures, and their timestamps together, so that the whole agreement travels as one thing.

## audit trail

The ordered list of who signed what and when, kept so that any signature can be traced back to its signer and its moment.

## ledger

The service's own written record of every document, every signature, and every verification, kept in order.
