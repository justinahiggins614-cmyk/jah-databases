JAH-PHOTON-APP-000001 — Light Computer Applications — Overview and definition

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Light computers excel where light is the natural carrier: massive parallelism (many wavelengths at once), matrix math at light speed, and signal processing without conversion. The killer app emerging today is AI acceleration — optical neural networks performing matrix-vector multiplications as light passes through modulator arrays, demonstrated by multiple research groups and startups. Applications cluster around linear algebra, optimization, imaging, and secure communication.

JAH-PHOTON-APP-000002 — Light Computer Applications — Optical neural network acceleration

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. A neural network layer is mostly matrix multiplication, and optics does it superbly: encode the input vector on modulators, the weights on a modulator array or photonic mesh, and the output appears at the detectors after one pass — at light speed, with energy per multiply far below electronics. Demonstrations reach tera-operations per second in single devices. Training still happens digitally, but inference — the deployed, repeated work — is the optical sweet spot.

JAH-PHOTON-APP-000003 — Light Computer Applications — Coherent Ising machines and optimization

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. A coherent Ising machine is a network of optical parametric oscillators whose phases settle into the lowest-loss configuration — which encodes the solution to a combinatorial optimization problem (scheduling, routing, portfolio). Built and demonstrated in Japan and the US, these are real machines solving real 100,000-variable problems. The light computer's optimization app is this technology, productized: state the problem, let the oscillators settle, read the answer.

JAH-PHOTON-APP-000004 — Light Computer Applications — Native optical image processing

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Images are light already, so a light computer processes them without conversion: lens-based Fourier filtering (a century-old technique called optical correlators, once used for missile guidance), holographic pattern matching, and edge detection by phase contrast.
