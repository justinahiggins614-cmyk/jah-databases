Molecular Engineering Simulator Database (molecular-engineering-simulator)

A molecular engineering simulator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy molecules, and counts the marks. The simulator reads 3 molecules. The molecule water holds 21 marks. The largest holds 21 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
