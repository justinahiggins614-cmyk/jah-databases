Learning Progress Tracker Database (learning-progress-tracker)

A learning progress tracker turns entered study records into progress figures. Type the records, press Track, and read the progress. 15 of 20 lessons done: 75.0 percent. 18 of 24 lessons done: 75.0 percent. 12 of 20 lessons mastered: 60.0 percent. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 15 of 20 lessons done: 75.0 percent

Demo output:
