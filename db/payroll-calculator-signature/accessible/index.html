The Payroll Calculator Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A payroll calculator estimates pay from hours and rates: type the figures, press Calculate, and read the pay. This is an estimator with example rates, not tax or financial advice. 40 hours at 20.00 is 800.00 gross. Gross 1000.00 minus deductions 150.00 is net 850.00. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
