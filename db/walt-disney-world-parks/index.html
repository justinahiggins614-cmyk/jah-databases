Magic Kingdom Rank 1 · simple · theme park · beginner

Magic Kingdom opened in 1971 as the first park of Walt Disney World, and it remains the resort's castle park: six lands around a castle hub, from a turn-of-the-century main street to a jungle, a frontier, a fairy-tale village, and a tomorrowland. Its ride roster spans a mountain coaster, a mine train, a pirate boat ride, and a haunted house, and its past includes a wild toad ride, a submarine lagoon, and a skyway that once crossed the park. The park rewards slow walking: shop windows, street shows, and island caves carry as much theme as the rides.

It was never announced as a built park and never constructed, and the record is careful about that status: it is a reported concept, not a confirmed announcement, and no rides or lands from it exist. It is included as a planned record so the resort's unbuilt history is preserved honestly.

Pleasure Island (past) Rank 14 · complex · nightlife district (past) · advanced

Pleasure Island opened in 1989 as a nightlife district dressed as a fictional island, with clubs, a comedy warehouse, an adventurers' club, and a nightly New Year's Eve celebration at midnight. It closed in 2008 and its venues were redeveloped into the Disney Springs district, and the record preserves it as a past district: every venue is a past venue, and the Adventurers Club is the most fondly remembered of them.

Disney Village Marketplace era (past era) Rank 15 · complex · shopping district era (past) · advanced

The Disney Village Marketplace era is recorded here as a past era, not a separate place: the resort's shopping district began in 1975 as the Lake Buena Vista Shopping Village, became the Disney Village Marketplace in 1989, and was rebuilt as Disney Springs in the 2010s. The record preserves the era's shops and names so the district's history is not flattened into its present form, and it cross-links to the Disney Springs record for the district's present.

Lands / areas

Marketplace; Lake Buena Vista Shopping Village (original name)

Rides NEW (operating)

World of Disney store (continuing element) (shopping, about 15 min wait)

Rides PAST (defunct / replaced)

Lake Buena Vista Shopping Village original shops (1975-1989 era); Disney Village Marketplace era shops (1989-2013 era); Captain Jack's Restaurant (Marketplace era) (1975-2010s era)

Theming ecosystems

A lakeside shopping village era: the resort's original shopping district, with a marketplace of shops and restaurants beside the lake, before its later rebuilds.

Hidden spots (publicly known)

The quiet lakefront paths of the original marketplace and the older shopfronts that survived into later eras are the era's best-known tucked-away corners.

Walt Disney World Speedway (past) Rank 16 · complex · motorsports track (past) · advanced

Walt Disney World Speedway opened in 1995 beside the Magic Kingdom parking area as a tri-oval track, hosting professional races and, for most of its life, a driving experience where guests could drive or ride along in race cars. It closed in 2015 and was demolished for parking expansion, and the record preserves it as a past attraction: a working speedway, not a theme-park ride, and a reminder that the resort once held a racetrack.

Theming ecosystems

A tri-oval motorsports track beside the Magic Kingdom parking area: professional races, driving experiences, and ride-alongs, framed as the resort's speedway.

Hidden spots (publicly known)
