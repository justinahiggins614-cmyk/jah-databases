History
Lending money to a government or a company, and receiving a written promise to be repaid with interest, is centuries old: the bond is the promise made paper, with the amount, the rate, and the dates written on it. Research means reading those promises — the published terms, the borrower's accounts, and the judgements of those who rate them — and writing down what they say, truly, with the sources cited. This database is research only: it gathers published facts and never advises any person to lend or to buy.
