The River Valley Civilization Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A civilization competition website: a visitor picks a season, such as a river-valley season or a mountain-pass season, and the site shows the invented civilizations in plain words, what each one builds, what each one trades, and how the points are counted. Every civilization is invented fiction, and the site never claims any of them is real. This record covers The River Valley Civilization.

An invented river-valley people who build towns along the banks, trade grain for cloth, and mark the seasons by the height of the river. The competition page for this civilization must name its towns, its trades, and its seasons in plain words, so a reader can follow the contest and find the seasons beside it. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.

Worked example: in a website built this way, the river valley civilization runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
