The Signature Personal Trivia Generator Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The personal trivia generator helps a household make a set of quiz questions about their own life, in plain words, with the subject, the difficulty, and the answers written out first. Every question is a clearly labeled illustrative sample of a generic kind, never a question about a real person, and no real person is named.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
