# The Mediation Services Database

The Mediation Services Database is the mediation services record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full mediation services record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database domestic-services-study, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
