As a Signature-generated study record, the scenario is illustrative; the core facts about Alzheimer’s disease come from published neuroscience.";

console.log("field: description · "+words.length+" words · "+sents.length+" sentences · ~"+Math.max(1,Math.round(words.length/200))+" min read");

if(sents.length>5)console.log("… +"+(sents.length-5)+" more sentences in the full record.");
