The Signature Digital Product Passports for Every Manufactured Product Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The digital product passports for every manufactured product give each made thing a plain record: who made it, what it is made of, how to mend it, and where its parts can go next. Every passport is a design study of a generic made thing, never a real product and never a claim about a real maker.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
