# Dictionary entries — The Shopping Cart System Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## cart

The kept list of goods a shopper has chosen in a shop, held together with their prices and sellers, so that the shopper can see their whole choosing before buying.

## basket

The shopper's choosing as it stands, held in the cart, so that the goods, their counts, and their prices can be read together at any moment.

## line item

One good in a cart, with its name, its count, and its price, so that the cart can be counted line by line and every line can be checked.

## quantity

How many of one good sit in a cart, written beside the good, so that the count of each good is plain and the total can be counted true.

## subtotal

The sum of the goods in a cart before any stated charges, counted by the same rule for every line, so that the shopper can see what the goods cost alone.

## total

The whole amount a cart comes to, with the stated charges beside the subtotal, so that the shopper sees the whole before the order is placed.

## checkout

The pages where a cart's chosen goods are gathered and the order is placed, kept apart from the shelves, so that browsing and buying are never confused.

## saved cart

A cart kept aside by its shopper to return to later, with its goods and prices recorded as they stood, so that the choosing is not lost when the shopper leaves.

## abandoned cart

A cart left by its shopper without an order, kept in the record with its age, so that the shop can count how many choosings end without buying.

## cart page

The page that shows a cart whole — its lines, its counts, its subtotal, and its total — so that a shopper can read their choosing and change it.

## item

One good as it sits in a cart, named with its seller and its price, so that every good in the cart can be traced to the listing it came from.

## removal

The act of taking a good out of a cart, recorded with the good and its date, so that the cart after removal can be counted true and the choosing stays whole.
