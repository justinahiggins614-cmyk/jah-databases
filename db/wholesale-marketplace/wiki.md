# The Wholesale Marketplace Database

The Wholesale Marketplace Database is the wholesale marketplace record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full wholesale marketplace record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database marketplace-seller-operations, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
