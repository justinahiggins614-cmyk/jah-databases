1. Description

A checklist creator builds lists where every item must be checked before the job counts as done. Name the checklist, add the checks in order, and reuse it every time the job is done.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A checklist score is passed checks divided by all checks. With 9 of 12 checks passed, the score is 9 over 12, which is 0.75, or 75 percent. A job is complete only when the score is 1, or 100 percent, with every required check passed.

3. Science proof

A checklist works because even skilled people skip steps when they are tired, rushed or interrupted. A written check in order removes the need to remember, and a required check that blocks the job stops a mistake from passing. That is why checklists are used wherever a skipped step is costly.
