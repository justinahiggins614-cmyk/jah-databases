The Universal Digital Rights and Permissions Infrastructure Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The universal digital rights and permissions infrastructure keeps a plain register of permissions: who may read, copy, share, or change a work, for how long, and under what terms, all written in plain words. Every entry is a design study of a generic permission, never a legal document and never a claim about any real work.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
