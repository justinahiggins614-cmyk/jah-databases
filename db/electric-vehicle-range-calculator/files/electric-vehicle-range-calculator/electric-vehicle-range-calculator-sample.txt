Electric Vehicle Range Calculator Database (electric-vehicle-range-calculator)

An electric vehicle range calculator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy trips, and counts the marks. The calculator reads 3 trips. The trip alpha holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
