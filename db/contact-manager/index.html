1. Description

A contact manager keeps every person you know in one place, with a name and a way to reach them. Add, search, edit and group contacts in this browser; everything saves locally.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A contact list is a set, and a group is a subset. With 40 contacts split into family 8, work 14 and friends 18, the parts add to the whole: 8 plus 14 plus 18 equals 40. Search is filtering: the result is the subset whose name contains the typed letters.

3. Science proof

A contact manager works because names and numbers are hard to remember in large numbers, and a written list never forgets. Sorting by family name turns finding into a short scan, and grouping turns one long list into short lists by kind. A duplicate check stops one person from becoming two entries.
