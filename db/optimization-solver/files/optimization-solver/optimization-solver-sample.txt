Optimization Solver Database (optimization-solver)

An optimization solver finds the best value: type a function, press Solve, and read the optimum. The minimum of x squared is 0 at x = 0. The maximum of -(x - 2) squared + 5 is 5 at x = 2. One gradient step from x = 4 gives x = 3.2. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
