Indo-European is the world's largest language family by speakers, with about 445 languages and roughly 3.4 billion speakers — nearly half of humanity — including English, Spanish, Hindi, Russian, Persian and most European languages. Its members descend from a single ancestor, Proto-Indo-European, spoken on the Pontic steppe roughly 6,000 years ago, and the family was established by the comparative method in the 19th century. This Signature version re-presents the topic as a designed teaching and analysis model: it keeps the established facts about the topic in view, then adds an original Signature framing, examples and checks of its own.
