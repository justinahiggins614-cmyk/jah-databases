# The Learning Management Systems Database

The Learning Management Systems Database is the learning management systems record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full learning management systems record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database college-course-catalog, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
