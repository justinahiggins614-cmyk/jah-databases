The Universal Possibility Explorer Database (Signature)

A universal possibility explorer maps the branches a choice can take, with chances and outcomes for each branch, so the best path can be seen and compared. Classification: FRONTIER — a simulation and design-study tool. It never claims to explore all possibilities or to predict the future.

Worked example: A possibility is a sentence that says what could happen, written plainly enough to test. Possibility 'It rains tomorrow' can be checked against tomorrow's weather.
