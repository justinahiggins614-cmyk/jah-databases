The Trade Imaginary Countries Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The trade imaginary countries website is a playful website where a visitor trades imaginary countries with other visitors: a small island country where everyone rides bicycles, a mountain country where the capital moves each season. Every country here is imaginary, plainly labeled so, and the trade is a game of stories and songs, with no real land, no real money, and no real transaction anywhere on the site.
