1. Description

A receipt generator makes receipts from items and prices: add items, press Generate, and read the receipt. 2 items at 3.50 is 7.00. Tax of 10 percent on 12.50 is 1.25. The total is 13.75. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

2. Math proof

2 items at 3.50 is 7.00. 3 items: 2.50, 3.00 and 4.50 add to 10.00. Tax of 10 percent on 12.50 is 1.25. The total is 13.75. Paying 20.00 for 13.75 gives 6.25 change.

3. Science proof

A receipt generator works because a receipt is items added one by one, and each line is plain arithmetic in cents: 5 items at 2.00 each is 10.00, and 4 items at 5.00 is 20.00. 10 percent off 20.00 is 2.00, so the price is 18.00. A tip of 15 percent on 20.00 is 3.00. That is why the local demo shows the items and the receipt together, and runs 0 receipts on a server.

4. Working demo

Try it: receipt generator (makes a receipt from items and prices)

Type an item name, a price and a quantity, press Add item, then press Generate receipt. Prices are kept in cents so the arithmetic is exact. Press Print to print the receipt. The demo runs 0 receipts on a server: it runs in the browser.
