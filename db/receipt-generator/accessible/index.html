  1. What a Receipt Generator Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A receipt generator makes receipts from items and prices. 2 items at 3.50 is 7.00

    Science proof: A receipt generator works because a receipt is items added one by one, and each line is plain arithmetic in cents: 5 items at 2.00 each is 10.00, and 4 items at 5.00 is 20.00. 10 percent off 20.00 is 2.00, so the price is 18.00. A tip of 15 percent on 20.00 is 3.00. That is why the local demo shows the items and the receipt together, and runs 0 receipts on a server.
