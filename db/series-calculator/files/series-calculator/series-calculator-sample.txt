Series Calculator Database (series-calculator)

A series calculator adds up the terms of a series: pick a series, press Calculate, and read the sum. 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 is 15. 1 to 100 add to 5050. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 is 15

Demo output:
