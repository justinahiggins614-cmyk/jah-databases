  1. A Map That Shows One Continent at a Time

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The geography explorer is a set of maps a reader can wander: continents one at a time, rivers traced from source to sea, mountains measured in plain numbers, and a daily place everyone explores together. A map that shows one continent at a time helps a reader learn without being overwhelmed: one land mass, its countries, its rivers, and its mountains, with nothing else to distract. The map must be described in words as well as drawn, because a reader who cannot see the map still needs to know what it shows. Without the word description, the map is closed to readers who cannot see, and the explorer is smaller for it. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design. Continent maps grew out of the old habit of atlases that gave each continent its own page, so a reader could learn it alone.
