History
Keeping track of what was spent is as old as spending itself: the traveller kept a list of coins paid at each inn, the household kept a book of what the market cost. As spending grew, the list became a system — receipts kept, costs sorted by purpose, totals counted by week and month, and each cost matched to the proof that it happened. This database is built on that public practice of writing every cost down once, in order, with its proof beside it.
