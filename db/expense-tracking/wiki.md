# The Expense Tracking Database

The Expense Tracking Database is the expense tracking record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full expense tracking record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database data-provenance-tracking, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
