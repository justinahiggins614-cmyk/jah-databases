JAH-PHOTON-TOOL-000001 — Light Computer Light Tools — Overview and definition

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Light tools are the instruments that shape, split, steer, and measure light — the screwdrivers and calipers of the photonic workshop. Lenses focus, beam splitters divide, spatial light modulators sculpt wavefronts pixel by pixel, and interferometers measure to fractions of a wavelength. Manon's order centers the light computer on these tools: the machine is as much an instrument as a computer, and its tools are first-class pieces with their own database.

const n=1.5,R1=0.1,R2=-0.1,f=1/((n-1)*(1/R1-1/R2));J({focal_m:+f.toFixed(3)});assert(f>0.09&&f<0.11,'lens');

JAH-PHOTON-TOOL-000002 — Light Computer Light Tools — Lenses, mirrors, and prisms

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. The classics: lenses focus and collimate (focal length from the lensmaker's equation), mirrors fold paths without loss (dielectric mirrors reflect over 99.9 percent), prisms and gratings split wavelengths. These are centuries-old, perfected tools — and still the fastest way to do a Fourier transform: a lens performs an exact 2D Fourier transform of the light at its focal plane, at the speed of light, with zero power. The light computer's math coprocessor is literally a lens.

JAH-PHOTON-TOOL-000003 — Light Computer Light Tools — Beam splitters and combiners

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Beam splitters divide one beam into two (50/50 cubes and plates are the standard), and run backward they combine. Polarizing splitters separate by polarization; dichroic splitters by color. Splitters are the fan-out of optical logic: one signal becomes many, or many interfere into one. Their spec is the split ratio tolerance and the loss — typically a few percent — and cascades of them build interferometric logic networks.

JAH-PHOTON-TOOL-000004 — Light Computer Light Tools — Spatial light modulators

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. A spatial light modulator (SLM) is a display for light: typically a liquid-crystal panel with millions of pixels, each setting the phase or amplitude of the light passing through. SLMs sculpt arbitrary wavefronts — holograms, lens patterns, vortex beams — under software control. They are the light tool with the most leverage: one SLM replaces rooms of fixed optics, and updating the pattern reprograms the instrument in milliseconds.

JAH-PHOTON-TOOL-000005 — Light Computer Light Tools — Holography as optical memory and processing

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Holograms store information as interference patterns in a medium (photographic plates historically, photorefractive crystals and polymers now). A single hologram stores a whole image; stacked holograms store thousands of pages in the same volume — holographic data storage is real, researched technology. For computing, holograms are fixed optical programs: shine the input through and the output is the transformed light. They are the light computer's read-only memory.

JAH-PHOTON-TOOL-000006 — Light Computer Light Tools — Interferometers: measurement to a fraction of a wavelength

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Interferometers split light, send it down two paths, and recombine it: the interference reveals path differences far below a wavelength. Michelson, Mach-Zehnder, and Fabry-Perot designs are the standards; LIGO is a Michelson interferometer measuring 10^-19 metre displacements. In the light computer, interferometers are both tools (aligning and calibrating the machine) and logic (interferometric gates compute by interference). Precision is the product.

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Focused laser beams trap and move microscopic objects — optical tweezers, Nobel-recognized in 2018. In the light computer's workshop, tweezers position components, hold samples in measurement beams, and could even arrange plasma-adjacent structures. The tool piece covers trap stiffness, positioning accuracy (nanometres), and the force calibration. Light as a hand: the most delicate tweezers ever built.

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Light tools serve both construction and operation: they build the machine (alignment, calibration), program it (SLM patterns, holograms), and verify it (interferometry, spectroscopy). The workshop piece is the lab around the computer — and per Manon's order, it is a first-class citizen, not an accessory. Beginner exercise: compute a lens's Fourier spot size for 1550-nm light and a 10-cm focal length (about 20 microns per unit spatial frequency) — and you have sized an optical processor.
