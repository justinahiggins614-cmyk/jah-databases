History
Towns and states have licensed trades for centuries, so that a baker, a builder, or a barber could show the public that the trade was allowed and the rules met. Licensing assistance grew beside the licences: clerks and advisers who read the requirements aloud, filled in the forms, and wrote down what was asked and what was answered. From the paper licence framed on a shop wall to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the help down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every act of business licensing assistance true.
