# Dictionary entries — The Business Licensing Assistance Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## business licensing assistance

The recorded help given to a business working out which licences it needs, what they cost, and how they are applied for, so that the requirements can be read and used with their terms in plain view.

## business licence

The written permission from a public office to carry on a named trade or business, kept in the record with its number and its conditions, so that the permission can be proved from the paper.

## occupational licensing

The public system under which a trade or profession may be practised only by those holding a stated licence, written in the record so that which trades need a licence can be read plainly.

## application

The written request for a business licence, naming the business, the trade, and the applicant, kept with the papers it carries, so that the request and its papers can be read together.

## renewal

The written extension of a business licence past its expiry, kept in the record with the new date, so that the longer life of the permission can be proved from the paper.

## fee

The amount charged for a licence or a renewal, written in the record with its amount and its date, so that what was charged for the permission can be counted.

## trade

A named kind of skilled work or business, such as building or food service, written in the record so that the licence can be matched to the work it allows.

## applicant

The person or body that asks for a business licence, named in the record with the application, so that who sought the permission can be read plainly.

## issuing office

The public office that grants a business licence, named in the record with the licence, so that where the permission came from can be read plainly.

## conditions

The stated terms attached to a business licence, such as hours or place of trade, written in the record so that the limits of the permission can be read in full.

## expiry date

The stated last day on which a business licence remains in force, written in the record with the licence, so that the life of the permission can be read plainly.

## assistance letter

The written note sent after business licensing assistance is given, naming the business, the licences discussed, and the answers given, so that the help can be proved from the paper.
