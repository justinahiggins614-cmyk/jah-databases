History
A shopping cart was once a basket carried through a shop: the shopper put goods in as they walked, and at the counter the basket was emptied and counted, each good named with its price. When shops moved onto the network, the basket became a cart kept by the shop itself — a list that remembers what was chosen, counts it, totals it, and holds it until the shopper is ready. A shopping cart system is that basket made digital, and this database is built on the public practice of keeping a shopper's choosing whole, counted once, and totalled true.
