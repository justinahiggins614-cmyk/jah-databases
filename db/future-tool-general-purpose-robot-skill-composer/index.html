Combines reusable movement and manipulation skills into plans for unfamiliar tasks. A proposed composer that builds robot behavior from reusable skills: given an unfamiliar task, it selects movement and manipulation primitives from a library, sequences and blends them into a plan, and verifies the plan in simulation before the robot moves.
