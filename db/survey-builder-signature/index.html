1. Description

A survey builder makes surveys: write questions, choose the answer type for each, preview the survey, collect answers, and count the results. Everything runs in this browser; answers save locally and no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A survey result is a tally: counts per choice, divided by all answers. With 12 of 20 people picking the first answer, that choice is 12 over 20, or 60 percent. An average rating is a sum divided by a count: ratings of 5, 4, 4, 5, 3, 4, 5, 4, 4, 4 add to 42, and 42 over 10 is 4.2.

3. Science proof

A survey works because a short question with a clear answer type is easy to answer, and easy questions get answered. Counting answers by choice turns many voices into a pattern that can be seen at a glance. That is why short surveys with a few answer types finish more often, and why results are counted before any decision is made from them.

    6. Use
    Use it to build a customer survey, a class survey or a short poll. Add questions, choose multiple-choice, yes-or-no, rating or free text, preview the survey, answer it, and watch the tally. Everything saves in this browser.
