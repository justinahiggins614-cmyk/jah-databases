The Neurological Exam

The neuro exam tests mental status, cranial nerves, strength, sensation, reflexes, and coordination — localizing lesions by bedside skill. A good exam often diagnoses before the MRI. It is medicine's most elegant ritual.

tPA Thrombolysis

IV alteplase dissolves clots in ischemic stroke within 4.5 hours, and every 15 minutes saved improves outcomes. Strict criteria exclude bleeding risks. It remains the cornerstone acute stroke drug.

Lumbar Puncture

Spinal taps sample cerebrospinal fluid to diagnose meningitis, MS, and subarachnoid hemorrhage. Done with the patient curled on their side, it takes minutes. Post-procedure headaches are managed with fluids and rest.
