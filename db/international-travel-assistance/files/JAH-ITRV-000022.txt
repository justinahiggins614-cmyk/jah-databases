History
Travellers abroad have always needed help from home: the consul in a foreign port, the letter of introduction, the traveller's cheque, and later the telephone call to an office that could send word or money. International travel assistance is that help made into a service — one place where a traveller abroad can ask for help, and the asking, the need, and the answer are all written down. The help is recorded as facts about the case, never as advice about what any traveller should do. This database is built on that public practice of recording every assistance case true, whole, and in order.
