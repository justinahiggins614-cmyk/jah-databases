How Patients Move Between Wings

A patient's journey often crosses many wings: the Emergency Department stabilizes, Radiology images, Surgery operates, the ICU recovers, Pharmacy doses, Laboratory monitors, Rehabilitation rebuilds, and Outpatient Clinics follow up. Handoffs between wings use structured reports so nothing is lost. The hospital works when wings work as one team.
