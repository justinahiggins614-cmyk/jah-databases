A First Book of Numbers for Beginners Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A free textbook library website: each book is a generic kind written for this library, such as a first book of numbers or a guide to growing plants at home, with chapters a reader can open, read, and keep notes on. No book names a real textbook, a real author, or a real publisher, and every book is free to read forever, with no sign-in and no payment. This record covers A First Book of Numbers for Beginners.

A first book of numbers for beginners must teach counting to ten, adding small numbers, and taking away, in short chapters a beginner can finish in one sitting. The library must offer the book as a generic kind written for this library, never naming a real textbook or a real author, with chapters that open one at a time. Without a plain first book, a beginner faces a hard book on the first visit and leaves before learning anything.
