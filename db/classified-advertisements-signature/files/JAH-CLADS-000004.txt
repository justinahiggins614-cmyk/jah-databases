History
Classified advertisements are among the oldest notices in print: short lines set in columns, sorted by kind, where anyone could say what they had to sell, what they sought, or what they could do. The columns were read daily, and the notices were kept only as long as they were paid for, then replaced by new ones. When the columns moved onto the network, the sorting stayed the same — by kind, by date, by place — and the keeping of notices became a system of its own. This database is built on that public practice of listing short notices in order, under their true headings.
