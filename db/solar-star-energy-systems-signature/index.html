Signature Solar Star Energy Highway JAH-SOSTS-000001

Signature design: this record describes an original design in the Signature edition. It is labeled Signature throughout and is never presented as an established public fact or as a working product. Its feasibility tier states plainly how far the design has been carried: buildable-now, research-stage, or speculative.

The Signature Solar Star energy highway is an original design for a long-distance energy route that carries solar power from collection regions to demand centers along a dedicated corridor, with storage stations spaced along the route so the highway delivers steadily rather than only when the sun shines. The design's corridor rule is that the highway is planned as one system, collection, transmission, storage, and delivery together, rather than as separate projects that happen to meet.

The highway's storage stations are its distinctive feature: each station holds enough energy to cover the corridor's night gap for its segment, so a failure or shortfall in one segment does not cascade down the whole route. The design gives station sizing as a formula from segment demand and night length, with worked examples, and it marks the formula's inputs as planning assumptions rather than measured values.

The demo sizes one highway segment's storage from stated demand and night length, printing the result. It is a planning calculation, not a built highway.
