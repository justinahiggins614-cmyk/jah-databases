# Dictionary entries — The Veterinary Practice Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## veterinary practice management

The recorded running of a veterinary practice, holding the animal records, the appointment book, the treatment plans, the vaccination records, and the billing ledger together, so that the whole practice can be read from its records.

## animal patient record

The written account kept for one animal in a veterinary practice, naming the animal, its owner, the treatments given, and the dates, so that the care of that animal can be followed from first visit to last.

## vaccination record

The written list of the vaccinations given to an animal, with each date and each vaccine named, kept in the patient record so that what the animal has been given can be read at a glance.

## surgery log

The written record of the operations carried out in a veterinary practice, kept by date and by animal, so that every operation can be found and followed.

## kennel register

The written list of the animals kept in a veterinary practice kennel, naming each animal, its owner, and its dates of stay, so that every animal in care can be counted.

## owner intake form

The sheet a new owner fills in, naming the owner, the animal, and the history asked for, kept at the front of the animal record so that the practice knows the animal from the first visit.

## laboratory request

The written request sending a sample from an animal to a laboratory, naming the animal, the sample, and the test asked for, kept with the result so that the request and the answer can be read together.

## parasite control record

The written account of the parasite treatments given to an animal, with each date and each treatment named, kept in the patient record so that the control can be followed.

## weight chart

The written series of weights recorded for an animal over time, kept in the patient record so that the growth or change of the animal can be read as a line.

## referral letter

The written note sending an animal to another veterinary practitioner for named care, kept with the patient record so that the referral and its reason can be read together.

## billing ledger

The written account of what a veterinary practice has charged and what it has been paid, kept by owner and by date, so that the money of the practice can be counted.

## practice policy

The written rules of a veterinary practice, covering appointments, payments, and records, kept where the staff can read them, so that the practice is run by the same rules every day.
