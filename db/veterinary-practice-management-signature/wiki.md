# The Veterinary Practice Management Database

The Veterinary Practice Management Database is the veterinary practice management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full veterinary practice management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database signature-interview-practice, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
