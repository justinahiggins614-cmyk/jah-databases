A Household Bill Kept Safe Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A document vault website: a visitor picks a generic kind of document, such as a household bill, a letter, or a certificate, and the vault shows how that kind is stored: a plain name, a date, and a place in the vault, with a copy kept safe. The vault names no real person, stores no real papers, and every example is labeled as an illustration of the method. This record covers A Household Bill Kept Safe.

A household bill kept safe gives a person a plain way to find what was paid and when, with the bill named, dated, and easy to find again. The vault must store the bill under a plain name, with the date written out, so a person who needs the bill finds it at once and not after an hour of searching. Without a plain name and date, a bill is a loose paper in a drawer, and a loose paper is lost exactly when it is needed most. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a household bill kept safe runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
