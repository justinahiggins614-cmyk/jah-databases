The Time Hierarchy Theorem

JAH-THCS-000006 · the time hierarchy theorem

Description: The time hierarchy theorem says that more time buys strictly more power: if f grows faster than g by a logarithmic factor, then the class of problems solvable in time g is strictly smaller than the class solvable in time f.
