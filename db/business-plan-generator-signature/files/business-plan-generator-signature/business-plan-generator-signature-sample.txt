Business Plan Generator Database (business-plan-generator-signature)

A business plan generator makes a structured plan from your inputs: type the figures, press Generate, and read the plan. 1000 customers at 50.00 is 50000.00. Fixed cost 10000 with margin 20 per sale breaks even at 500 sales. Revenue 12000 minus cost 8000 is profit 4000. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: A plan with 5 sections: summary, market, product, money, team
