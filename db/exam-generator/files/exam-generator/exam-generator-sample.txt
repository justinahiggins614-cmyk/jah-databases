Exam Generator Database (exam-generator)

An exam generator generates exams from entered topics and questions: type the topics, press Generate, and read the exam. Marks of 5, 5, 10, 10 and 20 total 50. A pass mark of 60 percent on 50 marks is 30. 8 right out of 10 is 80.0 percent. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: An exam of 10 questions in 60 minutes
