AI Decision Support System Database (ai-decision-support-system)

An AI decision support system scores options by weighted criteria and recommends the best: cost, quality and speed, weighted and added. Change a weight and the winner can change. The demo on this page is rule-based and local: it follows 5 written rules, makes 0 real AI calls, and hosted AI is BLOCKED. Everything runs in this browser.
