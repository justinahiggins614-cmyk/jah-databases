The Legal Research Database

Plain version, readable without JavaScript. 24 full legal research record records. Generator ceiling: up to 1,000,000,000 scales per file, generated on demand by the file AI — not stored files.

Overview

The Legal Research Database holds 24 full legal research record records, in a teaching ladder: 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex. This edition is the public edition. It expands the live database research-papers, which holds 10,000 records; this pair links to it and never rebuilds it. This database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.

Generator ceiling: up to 1,000,000,000 scales per file, generated on demand by the file AI — not stored files.

The Design/System

The design is one database per subject, with a public edition and a Signature edition side by side. Every record gives its history, its full content, its methods, its results as reported, and its skeptical analysis. Every technical term is defined in plain words where it first appears, and the glossary defines them all again. Each public record is a verified public fact with its source cited, retrieved 2026-11-24.

The Generator

The boundless generator for this database is the file gen-legal-research.js. It is seeded and deterministic: the same seed always gives the same record, and different seeds differ. It is throttled to 100 records per call, it stops at 1,000,000,000 records per file, and every generated record is labeled generated, never presented as an archive record.

The AI

The archive-aware AI for this database is JAH Legal Research Archivist. It explains this database in plain sentences, answers questions about every record, and can generate new records in this database's family. Its full specification is downloadable: legal-research-ai.json.

Math Proof

The counting argument is exact: 24 records, 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex, and 8 + 8 + 8 = 24. The formal statement is: research(question) = one true record per question researched, with its sources, its authorities, and its findings, or a named gap, never a silent gap (stated, not machine-checked) The scientific statement is: research completeness = questions fully recorded / all questions researched (stated, not machine-checked) Both are stated, not machine-checked, and the record-level proofs are in each record's math proof.

Science Proof

The scientific claim is that the subject can be stated, built, and checked by the method in the records: state it in plain words, define every term, run the demo and the program as real programs, and re-verify independently. The check is the validator and the independent CHECK 2, both of which re-run every demo and program. This database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.

Programs and Demos

Every record carries a demo and a full working program, both run as real programs at build time and re-run by the validator. The demo proves determinism by running twice and comparing the digests; the program is a small working tool for this database's subject, with acceptance tests printed in its output.
