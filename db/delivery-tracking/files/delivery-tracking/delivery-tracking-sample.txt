Delivery Tracking Database (delivery-tracking)

A delivery tracking is a rule-based local demo: it tracks set toy parcels, and counts the marks. The tracker reads 3 parcels. The parcel alpha holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
