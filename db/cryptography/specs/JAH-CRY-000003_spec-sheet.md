## Spec
- **Mechanism:** DES (Data Encryption Standard) is a 64-bit block cipher with a 56-bit effective key, built as a 16-round Feistel network. Developed from IBM's Lucifer cipher by Horst Feistel's team, it became the US federal standard FIPS-46 in 1977. Filed as JAH-CRY-000003 in the JAH Cryptography Database archive.
