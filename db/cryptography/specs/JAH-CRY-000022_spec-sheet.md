## Spec
- **Mechanism:** Serpent is a 128-bit block cipher with 32 rounds, designed by Ross Anderson, Eli Biham, and Lars Knudsen. An AES finalist in 1998, it was judged the most conservative security margin of the candidates, trading speed for safety. Its 32 rounds give it a large cushion against differential and linear cryptanalysis. Filed as JAH-CRY-000022 in the JAH Cryptography Database archive.
