DESCRIPTION
ChaCha20 is a stream cipher using a 256-bit key, a 96-bit nonce, and 20 rounds of ARX operations. Daniel J. Bernstein published it in 2008 as an improvement over Salsa20, with better diffusion per round. Paired with the Poly1305 authenticator, ChaCha20-Poly1305 is a mandatory cipher suite in TLS 1.3 and the default in WireGuard. Filed as JAH-CRY-000017 in the JAH Cryptography Database archive.
