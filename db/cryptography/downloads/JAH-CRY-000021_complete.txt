DESCRIPTION
bcrypt is an adaptive password-hashing function based on the Blowfish cipher's expensive key schedule. Designed by Niels Provos and David Mazieres in 1999 for OpenBSD, its cost factor scales the work as hardware improves. A unique salt per password defeats rainbow-table attacks, and bcrypt remains a respected default for password storage. Filed as JAH-CRY-000021 in the JAH Cryptography Database archive.
