A First Deck of Ten Number Cards Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A flashcard website: a visitor picks a topic, and the generator builds a deck of cards, each with a question on the front and the answer on the back. Cards are written for this generator from generic subjects, never copied from a real textbook or a real course, and every deck can be printed, shuffled, and studied in any order. This record covers A First Deck of Ten Number Cards.

A first deck of ten number cards must be small enough to finish in a few minutes and clear enough to understand at once: a number on the front, its name in words on the back. The generator must build the deck from a plain topic, show each card one at a time, and let the learner flip it to see the answer. Without a small first deck, a learner faces a hundred cards on the first visit and leaves before learning anything. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a first deck of ten number cards runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

History

Flashcards are an old study tool: a word on one side of a card and its meaning on the other, flipped again and again until the answer comes without thinking. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a generator that builds a deck from a topic, a shuffle, a timer, and a review that asks the hard cards first. Small first decks are as old as number cards, which have always begun with the numbers one to ten.

Proposed solution

Name the flashcard deck and what a visitor does in it; Write the rules in plain sentences a new visitor can follow; Give every flashcard deck the same fair start: the same clues, the same time, the same scoring; Let a visitor try one flashcard deck without signing in; Check the flashcard deck works the same way on a phone and on a computer.

Dependencies
