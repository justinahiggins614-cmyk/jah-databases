A Discount List for One Kind of Good Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A discount tracker website: a shopper picks a generic kind of good, such as a cotton shirt, a wooden chair, or a steel kettle, and the tracker watches its example price every day, showing the best day to buy and the largest discount so far. Discounts are sorted from largest to smallest, a watch list keeps the goods a shopper follows, and an alert arrives only when a price falls. No real store is named, no real brand is named, and no money changes hands on this site: it is a design study with example prices only. This record covers A Discount List for One Kind of Good.

A shopper who cannot see the discounts for one kind of good in one place must hunt through many pages, and the discounts found are forgotten before the shop is done. A discount list for one kind of good writes every example discount for that kind in one list, in plain numbers, so the shopper can see them all at once. Without a single list, discounts live in scattered places, the shopper compares from memory, and the largest discount is often the one forgotten.

A database of twenty-three records needs a map, or a reader will not know where to start, which records are simple and which are complex, and how the records fit together. History

Watching for a discount is an old shopping habit: a shopper who waits for the sale season, a stallholder who lowers a price at the end of the day, a neighbor who passes on the news of a bargain. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a daily watch, a best day to buy, and a watch list a shopper can return to. Field guides and indexes are as old as books themselves: a table of contents that tells a reader where everything lives. This index follows that habit, listing the twenty-three content records of this database in the order a reader should meet them.

Proposed solution

Add a tournament bracket with rounds, divisions, and a season; Add a creator studio so visitors can build a new discount watch; Add a review step so every new discount watch is checked before it is published; Add the discount watch in many languages, with the same rules in each; Add an offline pack so the discount watch works with no connection.

Dependencies

What must exist first: a daily price watch for generic kinds of goods, a best day to buy, a watch list, and a discount alert. A tournament system, a creator studio, a review step, translations, and an offline pack are also needed before the complex discount watchs open.
