Storage Box Rank 1 · simple · boxes · beginner

Source: public-harvest

A storage box is an open-top pine box 24 inches long for books, tools, or toys, with a plywood bottom set onto cleats and two handle cleats on the short sides for carrying. Butt joints and screws build it in an afternoon, and the bottom cleats, rather than nails alone, carry the weight so a loaded box does not drop its bottom. Boxes stack when their sides are kept square, and labels on the short side keep a stack readable.

Project diagram

Original dimensioned project sketch for Storage Box: a front-view diagram showing the overall 24 x 12 x 16 inch (W x H x D) form, the main cut pieces, and the overall dimensions labeled on the drawing, drawn for this record.

Specifications

Dimensions: 24 x 12 x 16 in (W x H x D)
Tolerances: Cut to within 1/16 in; check square by measuring both diagonals and matching them.
Load notes: A storage box rated for about 40 pounds of household goods.
Arithmetic: Cut list adds up: Total pieces: 7 (2 x Side, long, 2 x Side, short, 1 x Bottom, 2 x Handle cleat). Longest piece 24 in fits within the 28.8444 in face diagonal of the overall form. Cut the longest pieces first so a mistake on a short piece does not waste a long board.

Materials

  • Box boards: pine, 3/4 in
  • Bottom panel: birch plywood, 1/2 in

Cut list

PieceDimensionsQty
Side, long24 x 11 x 0.75 in2
Side, short14.5 x 11 x 0.75 in2
Bottom22.5 x 14.5 x 0.5 in1
Handle cleat8 x 2 x 0.75 in2

Fasteners

  • Wood glue and 1-1/2 in screws, 20 total

How to build

  1. Read the cut list and the arithmetic note in full, and check every piece against the plan; measure twice before cutting.
  2. Cut the pieces in order, longest first: Side, long (24 x 11 x 0.75 in), Side, short (14.5 x 11 x 0.75 in), Bottom (22.5 x 14.5 x 0.5 in).
  3. Dry-fit the frame without glue and check both diagonals match so the assembly is square.
  4. Sand the inside edges too, since hands reach inside a box more than they touch its outside.
  5. Glue and fasten as listed in the specs, keeping the assembly square while fasteners go in; wipe glue squeeze-out before it dries.
  6. Sand through the grits, ease the edges, and finish as described in the record; check the finished piece for square and for its rated load before use.

Tools required

  • Tape measure
  • Combination square
  • Sharp pencil
  • Handsaw or circular saw
  • Drill driver
  • Clamps (2)
  • Sandpaper, 80/120/180 grit
  • Workbench or sawhorses

Safety gear

  • Safety glasses
  • Hearing protection
  • Dust mask
  • Work gloves for handling rough lumber (never near rotating blades)

Wear eye protection for every cut and sanding pass, hearing protection for power tools, and a dust mask for sanding. Unplug power tools or remove batteries before changing blades or bits, keep guards in place, and keep hands clear of blades. Clamp work before cutting, never hold a small piece by hand near a blade, and clear offcuts before the next cut.

Crate Rank 2 · simple · boxes · beginner

Source: public-harvest

A crate is a slatted open box for produce, records, or firewood kindling, built from thin pine slats over four corner posts with gaps between the slats for air and handholds. The gaps keep produce ventilated and let a hand grip any side, and the corner posts carry the stacking weight so a stack of crates does not crush the lowest one. It is the cheapest storage build in the pair and the best practice for square assembly, because a crate that is not square will not stack.

Source: public-harvest

An under-bed box is a low rolling box 7 inches high that slides under a bed for blankets and out-of-season clothes. It is built from half-inch plywood to save height, rides on four small casters so it rolls out without lifting, and has a finger pull on its front face instead of a handle that would snag bedding. A hanging shelf is a single oak board suspended by rope or chain from ceiling joists or a wall bracket, for plants, books, or display pieces where a wall shelf cannot go. Joist fixing is required: a shelf hung from drywall alone will fall, so this record states the joist rule and the lower rating plainly.
