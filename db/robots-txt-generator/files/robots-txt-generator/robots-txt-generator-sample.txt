Robots Txt Generator Database (robots-txt-generator)

A robots txt generator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy rules, and counts the marks. The generator reads 3 rules. The rule allow holds 27 marks. The largest holds 27 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
