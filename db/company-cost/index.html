Costco Wholesale — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Costco Wholesale Corporation is an American multinational warehouse club headquartered in Issaquah, Washington. Founded in 1976 as Price Club (merging with Costco in 1993), it sells bulk groceries, electronics, and general merchandise to members at low margins, and is famous for its Kirkland Signature private label, food-court hot dogs, and loyal membership base.
