Signature Breath Companion

A ventilator with a soft voice that narrates each weaning trial to the patient in plain words: 'You are breathing more on your own now.' Patients wake calmer, and families understand progress without medical jargon. Concept by Manon.

Improvement: Improves on silent ventilators by making weaning a conversation the patient can follow.
