The Video Subtitle Generator Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A video subtitle generator writes timed words for a video: a transcript in, an SRT file out, with a number and a time for every line. The demo on this page generates subtitles in the browser, with no server and no connection. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/video-subtitle-generator-signature/index.jsonl.gz
