Video Subtitle Generator Database (video-subtitle-generator-signature)

A video subtitle generator writes timed words for a video: a transcript in, an SRT file out, with a number and a time for every line. The demo on this page generates subtitles in the browser, with no server and no connection. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
