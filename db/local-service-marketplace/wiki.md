# The Local Service Marketplace Database

The Local Service Marketplace Database is the local service marketplace record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full local service marketplace record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database marketplace-seller-operations, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
