# Dictionary entries — The Local Service Marketplace Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## marketplace

One place where services are listed together, each with its provider and its terms, so that a person can find the service they need without asking door to door.

## service listing

The written account of one service offered, naming the provider, the service, the area, and the terms, kept as the single true record of what is offered.

## provider

The person or business offering a service, named in the listing with their area and their terms, so that every service can be traced to who offers it.

## local service

A service carried out in a stated local area, such as mending, cleaning, tutoring, or building, listed with its area, so that only services that reach the reader are shown.

## service area

The stated places a provider works in, written in the listing, so that a service offered in one town is never shown as if it reached another.

## service request

A written ask for a service, naming the service, the place, and the time, kept with the listing it answers, so that the ask and the offer can be read together.

## quote

A stated price for a named piece of work, written down before the work begins, so that the price can be compared and the work can be judged against it.

## service agreement

The written terms under which a service is carried out, naming the work, the price, and the time, kept with the request, so that both sides can point to the same words.

## review

A written account by a person who received a service, saying what was done and how it went, kept with the listing, so that later readers can read what earlier readers found.

## category

One named kind of service, such as mending or tutoring, given to each listing, so that services of the same kind can be found together and counted.

## buyer

The person asking for a service in the marketplace, named in the service request, so that the ask can be traced to who made it and the answer can reach them.

## seller

The provider offering a service in the marketplace, named in the listing, so that every service can be traced to the party that stands behind it.
