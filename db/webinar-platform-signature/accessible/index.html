The Webinar Platform Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A webinar platform runs a simulated webinar session: read the attendees, ask a question, and vote in a poll. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it runs a simulated session with 0 real streaming and 0 real peers, and a real peer connection is BLOCKED. A webinar with 100 seats and 25 attending: 75 seats left. A poll of 20 votes: 12 yes and 8 no: 60 percent yes.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
