History
People have studied companies before buying a part of them for as long as companies have sold parts of themselves: the merchant read the ship's manifest before buying a share of the voyage. The study became research when the records became public — published accounts, prices counted by the day, and reports written by those who watch the markets. This database is research only: it gathers published facts about companies and their shares, cites them, and never advises any person to buy or sell.
