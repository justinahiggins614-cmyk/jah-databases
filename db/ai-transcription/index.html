1. Description

Transcription turns spoken words into written words, so speech can be read, searched and kept. The live demo on this page listens through the microphone only where the browser supports live speech recognition, and only after Start is pressed. Where it is not supported, the page says so plainly and transcribes nothing; no output is ever faked.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Speech is measured in words per minute: at 150 words a minute, 2 minutes hold 300 words, and 60 minutes hold 9,000 words. Accuracy is a fraction: 99 of 100 words right is 99 percent, with 1 word in 100 missed. A timestamp is a time in seconds: a word at 12 seconds is stamped 0:12.

3. Science proof

Transcription works because speech is a stream of sounds that can be matched to words, and pauses mark where sentences end. Confidence numbers say how sure the match is, so low-confidence words can be flagged and checked by a person. That is why a transcript is always checked against the recording before it is trusted.
