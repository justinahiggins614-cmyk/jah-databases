    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Online friendship discovery helps strangers with shared interests find each other and become friends, in public groups with clear rules, where a profile shows interests and never personal data. Finding friends as an adult is hard, because the places where friendships used to form, like school and the neighborhood, are behind most people, and the open web offers strangers without any shared ground. Friendship by shared interest fixes this by starting from what two people both love, so the first conversation has a subject before it has a greeting. Without a shared interest to start from, strangers must invent small talk from nothing, and most of them give up before the friendship begins.
