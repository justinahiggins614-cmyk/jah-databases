Fuel Consumption Calculator Database (fuel-consumption-calculator)

A fuel consumption calculator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy trips, and counts the litres. The calculator reads 3 trips. The trip town holds 60 litres. The largest holds 60 litres. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
