The Geological Mapping Tool Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A geological mapping tool maps entered geological data: type the readings, press Map, and read the map. 2 centimetres at 1 to 25000 is 500 metres on the ground. 3 kilometres east and 4 kilometres north is 5.0 kilometres apart. A range of 100 metres over 10 contours gives a 10 metre interval. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
