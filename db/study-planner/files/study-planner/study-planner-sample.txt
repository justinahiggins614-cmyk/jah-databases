Study Planner Database (study-planner)

A study planner shares study minutes across subjects and days: type the subjects and minutes, press Build, and read the plan. 5 subjects at 60 minutes: 300 minutes. 4 sessions of 25 minutes: 100 minutes. 300 minutes over 5 subjects: 60 each. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
