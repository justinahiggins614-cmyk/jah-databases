History
Tables of numbers are older than computers: ledgers, tally sticks, and account books all laid numbers in rows and columns so they could be added. On computers the table became a sheet where a number in one cell can be the answer of a formula that reads other cells, and where changing one number changes every answer that depends on it. The public standards for tables and for arithmetic are published openly, and this database is built on them.
