Quantum Mechanics Simulator Database (quantum-mechanics-simulator-signature)

A quantum mechanics simulator models quantum systems with textbook equations. Type the values, press Simulate, and read the chances. A qubit with amplitudes 0.6 and 0.8 gives chances 0.36 and 0.64. The first level holds 1.0 units, the second 4.0 and the third 9.0. Pinning a particle to a tenth of a billionth of a metre leaves its momentum uncertain by at least 5.27e-25 kilogram metres per second. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
