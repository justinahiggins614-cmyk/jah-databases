1. Description

A quiz generator makes quizzes from a question bank: pick how many questions, and it builds a quiz, shuffles the order, and scores the answers. The local generator on this page makes quizzes in this browser, with no connection. No sign-in, nothing uploaded.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A quiz is counted in questions and answers: a quiz of 5 questions has 5 answers, and 4 right of 5 is a score of 80 percent. Picking 5 questions from a bank of 10 gives a quiz of 5. A quiz of 5 questions at 1 minute each is 5 minutes. A passing score of 60 percent needs 3 right of 5.

3. Science proof

A quiz works because a question tests one thing: a right answer matches the answer key, and the score is right answers over all questions. Shuffling the questions and the choices keeps the quiz fresh, and a bank of questions lets every quiz be different. That is why the generator keeps every answer, so the score can be checked question by question.

6. Use
Use it to make a quiz: press Generate to pick 5 questions from the bank, answer each one, and press Score to read the result. Try the seeded quiz first, then generate a new one.
