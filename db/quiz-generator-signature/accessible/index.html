The Quiz Generator Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A quiz generator makes quizzes from a question bank: pick how many questions, and it builds a quiz, shuffles the order, and scores the answers. The local generator on this page makes quizzes in this browser, with no connection. No sign-in, nothing uploaded.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/quiz-generator-signature/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Quiz Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A quiz is a set of questions with answers, to test what is known. A quiz of 5 questions has 5 answers

    Math proof: A quiz is counted in questions and answers: a quiz of 5 questions has 5 answers, and 4 right of 5 is a score of 80 percent. Picking 5 questions from a bank of 10 gives a quiz of 5. A quiz of 5 questions at 1 minute each is 5 minutes. A passing score of 60 percent needs 3 right of 5.

    Science proof: A quiz works because a question tests one thing: a right answer matches the answer key, and the score is right answers over all questions. Shuffling the questions and the choices keeps the quiz fresh, and a bank of questions lets every quiz be different. That is why the generator keeps every answer, so the score can be checked question by question.
