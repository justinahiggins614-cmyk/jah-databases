The Signature The Digital Rock Collection Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The digital rock collection keeps a shelf of stones found on walks, a smooth grey pebble from a river, a speckled stone from a garden path, a round white stone from a beach, each described in plain words so a reader can picture it. Every rock is a clearly labeled design study of a generic stone, never a claim about a real place.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
