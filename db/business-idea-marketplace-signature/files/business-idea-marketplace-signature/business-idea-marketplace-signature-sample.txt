The Business Idea Marketplace Database (Signature) (business-idea-marketplace-signature)

A business idea marketplace catalog lists business ideas by category, each with a plain summary, its target customers, what it needs to start, and notes on how to judge it, so a reader can compare ideas side by side. Every idea here is a fictional catalog example, not a real listing, not a real price, and not a real transaction.

Worked example: A Signature stall that serves one soup of the day, invented each morning from what the market has fresh. Target customers: Hungry neighbors, workers on a lunch break, and families who want a hot Signature meal without cooking. Startup needs: A soup pot, a table, bowls and spoons, a food hygiene certificate, and a regular pitch. Judge it by repeat visitors: if the same faces return each week, the Signature soup and the day fit the street. This is a fictional catalog example, not a real listing, not a real price, and not a real transaction.
