The Signature Smart Construction Coordination Network Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The smart construction coordination network keeps the calendars, bookings, checklists, and rosters of many building sites in one plain plan, so no scaffold is booked twice and no safety step is hidden.
