Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The internet's secret rooms are hidden corners of a website, found by solving a riddle, following a clue, or noticing a small sign: a quiet reading room, a puzzle room, a room of jokes. Every room is a clearly labeled design study of a public page, and the site never hides a room behind a payment or a private message.
