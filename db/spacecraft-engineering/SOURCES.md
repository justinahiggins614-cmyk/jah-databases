## Honesty limits

- PACKAGE-ONLY — not live. This website is part of a package-only build: nothing here is deployed, pushed, or merged.
- NASA-level means engineering targets modeled on public NASA standards (NASA Systems Engineering Handbook, NPR 7123.1D) — never NASA certification, approval, affiliation, funding, or endorsement.
- No design here is presented as flight-ready. Every engineering result carries its design status, verification status, safety status, and source provenance.
- JAH live retrieval was NOT verified.
- No missile-guidance content is included. Civilian aerospace and space exploration only.
- Signature edition records are design-study, never established fact.
