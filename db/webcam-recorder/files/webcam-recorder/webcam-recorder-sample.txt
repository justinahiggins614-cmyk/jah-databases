Webcam Recorder Database (webcam-recorder)

A webcam recorder here is a local demo only: it records simulated frames, and no camera is ever opened. A simulated recording holds 6 frames. 6 frames at 2 per second last 3.0 seconds. Frames of 4 by 4 hold 16 pixels each. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
