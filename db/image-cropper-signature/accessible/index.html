The Image Cropper Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An image cropper cuts away the edges of a picture and keeps only the chosen rectangle. Upload a picture, set the rectangle, and download the cropped copy. The original file is never changed; everything happens on a canvas in this browser.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/image-cropper-signature/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What Cropping Means

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. Cropping cuts away the edges of a picture and keeps only the chosen rectangle. Cropping a 800 by 600 picture to its middle 400 by 300 keeps the centre.

    Math proof: A crop rectangle is four numbers: left, top, width and height. The kept picture is width by height pixels, and the kept pixel count is width times height. Cropping an 800 by 600 picture to 400 by 300 keeps 120,000 of 480,000 pixels, exactly one quarter.

    Science proof: A picture is a grid of pixels, and cropping keeps a sub-grid of that grid. Cutting away edges removes distractions and moves the subject in the frame, which is why a crop can improve a picture without changing any pixel that remains. The canvas copies only the kept rectangle to a new picture.
