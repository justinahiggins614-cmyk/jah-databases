# The Retail Marketplace Database

The Retail Marketplace Database is the retail marketplace record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full retail marketplace record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database marketplace-seller-operations, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
