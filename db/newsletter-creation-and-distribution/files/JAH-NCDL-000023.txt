History
Newsletters are older than the web: town criers, parish sheets, and club letters all carried news to people who asked for it. Online the letter became an email sent on a schedule, written once and read by everyone who subscribed. The public standards for email, and for asking first and stopping when asked, are published openly, and this database is built on them.
