Molecular Viewer Database (molecular-viewer)

A molecular viewer draws molecules on screen: pick a molecule, press View, and read its shape. Water has 3 atoms and 2 bonds. Water has a bond angle of 104.5 degrees. Carbon dioxide has 3 atoms and 2 bonds: it is linear at 180 degrees. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
