History
A shopfront was once a window and a door: the goods were set out where passers-by could see them, the name was painted above, and the keeper stood inside to serve. When shops moved onto the network, the window became a page and the door became a link, but the work stayed the same — choose the goods, describe them true, set the prices, and keep the shop in order. Creating a storefront is building that window and door on the network, piece by piece, and this database is built on the public craft of making a shop that can be found, read, and trusted.
