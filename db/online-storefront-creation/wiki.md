# The Online Storefront Creation Database

The Online Storefront Creation Database is the online storefront creation record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full online storefront creation record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database really-fun-creations, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
