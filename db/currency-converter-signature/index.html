Converting One Amount Between Two Currencies Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A currency converter website: the visitor enters an amount, picks two currencies, and the page multiplies the amount by a sample illustrative rate, showing the amount, the rate, and the converted figure together. Every rate is labeled a sample illustrative rate, never presented as a live market rate, and no real-money transaction or payment handling takes place anywhere on the site. This record covers Converting One Amount Between Two Currencies.

A visitor who wants converting one amount between two currencies needs one honest number: how much an amount in one currency is worth in another, with the rate shown in plain sight. Without a converter that shows its rate, the visitor cannot tell whether the figure is fair, and a hidden rate can quietly take a cut. This record states the design of a converter that shows a sample illustrative rate, labels it clearly as a sample and never as a live market rate, and converts with plain arithmetic anyone can repeat. One amount and one rate is the simplest conversion, so the converter must show the amount, the sample rate, and the result together, with the rate labeled as a sample. This is a simple record: one idea and one calculation, with nothing extra to learn.

Worked example, Signature design: the same currency conversion is rebuilt from the Signature pattern, so converting one amount between two currencies runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
