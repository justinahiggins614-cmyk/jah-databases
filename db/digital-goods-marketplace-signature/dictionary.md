# Dictionary entries — The Digital Goods Marketplace Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## digital good

A good that is a file, such as a song, a book, a picture, or a program, sold by download rather than by delivery, with its format and version named in the listing.

## download

The act of receiving a digital good as a file over the network, recorded with the good and its version, so that what was received can be shown from the record.

## licence

The written permission that says what a buyer may do with a digital good, kept with the listing, so that the buyer can read their rights before receiving the file.

## digital distribution

The sending of digital goods to buyers over the network, without carrying anything physical, recorded as downloads, so that each sale can be traced to its file.

## file

One stored set of digital content, named with its format and its size, so that the good sold is the file named, and no other.

## format

The stated form of a digital file, such as a text file or a sound file, written in the listing, so that a buyer knows whether the file will open on their device.

## creator

The person or business that made a digital good, named in the listing, so that every file in the market can be traced to the maker who stands behind it.

## seller

A party offering digital goods in the marketplace, named beside every listing, so that every file can be traced to who offers it.

## buyer

A person who seeks digital goods in the marketplace, named only in their own orders, so that the market can serve them without keeping more than the order needs.

## version

One named state of a digital good, numbered in order, so that a buyer can tell which state of the file they received and whether a newer one exists.

## catalogue

The ordered list of digital goods a marketplace holds, from every seller, kept current, so that the market shows only files its sellers truly have to sell.

## digital delivery

The handing over of a digital good by download, recorded with the file and its version, so that the delivery can be shown without any parcel ever moving.
