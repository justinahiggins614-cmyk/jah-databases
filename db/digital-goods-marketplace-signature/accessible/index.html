Overview

The Signature Digital Goods Marketplace Database holds 24 full digital goods marketplace record records, in a teaching ladder: 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex. This edition is the Signature edition. It expands the live database marketplace-seller-operations, which holds 34 records; this pair links to it and never rebuilds it. The Design/System

The design is one database per subject, with a public edition and a Signature edition side by side. Every record gives its history, its full content, its methods, its results as reported, and its skeptical analysis. Every technical term is defined in plain words where it first appears, and the glossary defines them all again. Each Signature record is a design study, labeled as a design, never presented as established fact.

The Generator

The boundless generator for this database is the file gen-digital-goods-marketplace-signature.js. It is seeded and deterministic: the same seed always gives the same record, and different seeds differ. It is throttled to 100 records per call, it stops at 1,000,000,000 records per file, and every generated record is labeled generated, never presented as an archive record.

The archive-aware AI for this database is JAH Signature Digital Goods Marketplace Designer. Its full specification is downloadable: digital-goods-marketplace-signature-ai.json.

Math Proof

The counting argument is exact: 24 records, 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex, and 8 + 8 + 8 = 24. The formal statement is: market(digital goods) = files and licences sold by download, each listing named with its seller, its format, and its version, or a named gap, never a silent gap (stated, not machine-checked) The scientific statement is: digital coverage = digital listings shown / all digital listings offered (stated, not machine-checked) Both are stated, not machine-checked, and the record-level proofs are in each record's math proof.
