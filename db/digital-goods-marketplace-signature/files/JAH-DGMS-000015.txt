History
Some goods have no weight at all: a song, a book, a picture, a program — each is a file, copied, not carried, and sold by download rather than by delivery. The market for such goods is a digital goods marketplace, where makers list their files, buyers receive them by download, and the record must say which file, which version, and which licence was sold. This database is built on the public practice of selling files in the open, with every listing named and every version recorded.
