The Video Cutter Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A video cutter keeps part of a video and drops the rest: pick a start time and an end time, and the kept part is the cut. The demo on this page cuts a seeded frame list locally: a seeded clip of 30 seconds, 900 frames and 30 megabytes. A full transcode needs a backend, and that is BLOCKED; this is a local partial demo, and it says so. You can also upload your own clip, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/video-cutter-signature/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Video Cutter Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A video cutter keeps part of a video and drops the rest. A video cutter keeps 10 seconds of a 30 second clip

    Math proof: A clip of 30 seconds has 900 frames at 30 frames a second. Cutting from 5 seconds to 15 seconds keeps 10 seconds. A cut of 10 seconds at 30 frames a second has 300 frames. A clip of 10 seconds at 8 megabits a second is 10 megabytes. Cutting frames 151 to 450 keeps 300 frames.

    Science proof: A video cutter works because a video is frames in order, and a cut keeps the frames between two times: from 5 seconds to 15 seconds keeps 10 seconds and 300 frames at 30 frames a second. A shorter cut is a smaller file: 10 seconds at 8 megabits a second is 10 megabytes. That is why the local demo cuts the seeded frame list and shows the count, and says plainly that a full transcode needs a backend and is BLOCKED.
