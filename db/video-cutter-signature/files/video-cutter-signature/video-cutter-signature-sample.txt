Video Cutter Database (video-cutter-signature)

A video cutter keeps part of a video and drops the rest: pick a start time and an end time, and the kept part is the cut. The demo on this page cuts a seeded frame list locally: a seeded clip of 30 seconds, 900 frames and 30 megabytes. A full transcode needs a backend, and that is BLOCKED; this is a local partial demo, and it says so. You can also upload your own clip, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Worked example: A video cutter keeps 10 seconds of a 30 second clip
