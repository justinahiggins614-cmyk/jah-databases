The Signature Word Combos Database

PLAIN WORDS: This is the database for word combinations up to seven words long. A word combination — a combo — is two or more words in a row, in a set order, like "joke generator" or "ai tutor". Every search anyone types is a combo of words, so this database keeps the vocabulary those combos are built from, the rules for building them, and one record for each combo that has actually been built as a website. The full space of all combos is far too big to store, so it is kept as generator option space: any combo can be made on demand, and only the highest-demand combos are stored.

Public records in this edition are public records: vocabulary counts, combo rules, and the stored combos as listed in the demand list. Signature readings inside individual records are labelled as Signature readings, never as established fact. Accessible plain version (no scripts): accessible mirror.

1. Overview

Manon ordered this program on 2026-11-23: a website for every word combo up to 7 words, so that any search always lands on an active website. Wave 1 is the first wave of that program: the database pair, the boundless generator, the Combo Router, and stored pages for the highest-demand 2-word combos. This pair holds 106 public records and 106 Signature records: 3 meta records and 103 stored-combo records per edition. The 103 stored combos are every 2-word item of List 03, the trending and viral list of 450 items, in list order, from item 14 "Joke generator" to item 441 "Community marketplace". The remaining 347 List 03 items (293 three-word, 49 four-word, 5 five-word) are not stored; they resolve live through the Combo Router at their stable combos/<slug>/ addresses, verifiable on demand, and are not listed as sites. No record is ever created for an unbuilt combo.

The vocabulary is the 102,223 headwords of the Signature Dictionary — never 1,127,335, which is the record count of the live words database, of which only 102,223 are full headword records; the composition of the remaining roughly 1,025,112 generator and invention records is unverified. The generator is word-combo-gen 1.0.1; the pair records themselves carry the stamp word-combo-gen 1.0.0. Of the 103 stored combos, 57 consist entirely of Dictionary headwords and have a computable rank, and 46 contain at least one word that is not a Dictionary headword, and their records say so.

2. The Design / System

The design has three parts: the vocabulary, the combo rules, and the hybrid delivery shape. The vocabulary is the 102,223 Dictionary headwords (file data/headwords.csv, 102,224 lines including the header, counted 2026-11-23; all 102,223 unique). Definitions exist for 99,579 headwords: 58,223 authored definitions and 41,356 template-filled definitions, with 2,644 headwords still pending a definition. Authored definitions are used as written; template-filled definitions are always labelled as such; pending headwords are labelled pending and never given an invented definition. Part of speech is recorded for only about two thirds of the headwords (noun 36,592; adjective 17,223; verb 7,804; adverb 2,723; phrase 1,497; and smaller classes), while 35,654 headwords (34.9 percent) carry no part of speech, so no combo rule in this database relies on part-of-speech tags.

The combo rules: a combo is an ordered list of 2 to 7 words; word order matters, because a search for one order is not the same search as another order. The hyphenated lowercase form of the words, joined with single hyphens, is the combo slug, and the slug is the combo's identity: the same combo always has the same slug, and no combo can be stored or generated twice under a different identity. The deterministic generator ordering is the code-point ascending sort of the 102,223 headwords, index 0-based; the rank of an ordered combo of distinct headwords is its lexicographic rank in that ordering, computed with exact integer arithmetic.

The delivery shape is the hybrid the review chose: one Combo Router site that answers any combo on demand at a stable per-combo address, combos/<slug>/, plus pre-rendered stored pages for the highest-demand combos at those same addresses. A stored page and a generated page share the same address, the same template, and the same sections; a stored page is simply a frozen, verified, indexable copy of what the generator produces. A combo with no stored page resolves to the Router's generator page that builds it on demand — never a dead link. The full space is generator option space, sampled and verified, never stored: even the smallest complete stored set, the 2-word combos of the top 1,000 words, would be 999,000 pages and 15 to 63 gigabytes. Boundary rule: combos whose words trigger a standing boundary — defeat of a military system, pathogen, toxin, explosive, ballistics or gene-drive content, and the other standing boundaries — are refused by the generator with a recorded reason and are never generated around. No brand name, logo, trademark, product name or copyrighted image appears anywhere; nothing is scraped or copied from any real website; speculative material is labelled design-study or entertainment, never fact.

3. The Generator

The combo-page generator for this pair is word-combo-gen 1.0.1. Given any combo of 2 to 7 words it emits the combo's full page (the 10 review sections: meaning, math proof, science proof, relevant data, spec, working demo, two AIs, use, sources, accessible version), its record, and the two AI spec files. It is deterministic: the same combo in gives byte-identical output, verified by two full runs with identical checksums. Boundary refusal: exit 3 with a recorded reason and no page. The hub generators shipped on this page are generator-public.js (public edition) and generator-signature.js (Signature edition). Each emits records of kind "word-combo" with its own family id prefix, verified by execution. Hard stop 1,000,000,000 per file; throttled; generated outputs labelled simulated/design, never presented as public record. Seeded generation: the same seed and range give identical output, and different seeds differ, both checked by execution in CHECK 1.

5. Math Proof

The combo space is counted with permutations, because order matters: the number of ordered combos of k distinct words from n words is P(n,k) = n!/(n-k)!. The vocabulary is n = 102,223 headwords. The exact space sizes, computed with exact integer arithmetic, are: 2-word P(102223,2) = 10,449,439,506; 3-word P(102223,3) = 1,068,152,155,742,826; 4-word P(102223,4) = 109,186,513,360,031,673,720; 5-word P(102223,5) = 11,160,936,209,149,077,655,984,680; 6-word P(102223,6) = 1,140,848,577,426,800,419,839,442,020,240; 7-word P(102223,7) = 116,614,119,038,835,258,514,728,244,982,872,080. The total ordered space of 2 to 7 words is 116,615,259,898,573,730,711,878,684,717,733,052. The rank of an ordered combo of distinct headwords is its lexicographic rank in the code-point ascending ordering of the 102,223 headwords, index 0-based: rank = the sum over positions of the count of still-unused earlier words times the permutations remaining. Worked example from the records, independently recomputed three ways: "joke generator" has rank 5,001,555,420, and "ai tutor" has rank 236,023,061. A rank is defined only when every word of the combo is a Dictionary headword and no word repeats; 57 of the 103 stored combos have a computable rank, and the other 46 have no rank, and their records state the reason instead of faking one.

The storage arithmetic is what forces the hybrid shape: a stored page is 15 to 63 kilobytes, so the 2-word combos of the top 1,000 words alone would be 999,000 pages and 15 to 63 gigabytes, and the full 2 to 7 word space is about 1.17 times 10 to the 35th power combos. No wave can store more than a rounding error of the space, and no report claims otherwise. What is stored is the highest-demand slice: 103 pages in Wave 1, covering 450 demand combos by address (103 stored + 347 generated on demand).

6. Science Proof

The science in this pair is the science of words: what the Dictionary actually holds, and what people actually search for. The Dictionary holds 102,223 headwords; 99,579 have definitions, 58,223 of them authored and 41,356 template-filled, and 2,644 are pending. A combo page can only genuinely define a word from an authored definition where one exists; template-filled definitions are labelled as such, and pending words are labelled pending, never faked. Words in the demand lists that are not Dictionary headwords — for example meme, video and podcast — are labelled not-a-Dictionary-headword and likewise never given an invented definition. A third of the vocabulary carries no part of speech, so no grammar rule here depends on part-of-speech tags.

What people search for: the demand seed is List 03, 450 authored service items, not measured search volume — no search-query source is connected and none was scraped, and that limit is stated plainly. The demand peak is 3-word combos (293 of 450), then 2-word (103 of 450). In a 100-word sample of the vocabulary, hand-classified by the reviewer and stated as judgement, about one word in five was an everyday word an ordinary adult knows and might type, and about two in three were obscure, archaic or technical words almost nobody searches for — so most headwords are structurally unlikely search terms before meaning is even considered. A 2-word search is usually a thing, a tool or a topic; a 3-word search is usually a specific kind of thing; a 6 to 7 word search is usually a question or a sentence, and its page is an answer page — what the sentence means word by word, what exists that relates to it, and what is design-study speculation versus fact. Where a combo is speculative, its page is labelled design-study or entertainment, never fact.
