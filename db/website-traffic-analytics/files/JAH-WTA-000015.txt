History
Counting visitors is as old as counting footfall: shopkeepers counted the bell over the door, and librarians counted the books taken out. Online the counting became a record of arrivals, pages, and departures, kept honestly and counted the same way every day. The public guidance for what a visit is, and for counting it without following people, is published openly, and this database is built on it.
