The Project Manager Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A project manager tracks a project: its tasks, its people, its budget and its deadline. Add tasks, mark them done, and watch the progress percent and the money left. Everything runs in this browser and saves locally; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

  1. What a Project Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A project is a piece of work with tasks, people and a deadline. A project has 3 tasks, 2 people and 1 deadline.

    Math proof: Progress is tasks done divided by all tasks: 4 of 8 tasks done is 50 percent, and 8 of 8 is 100 percent. Money left is the budget minus what is spent: a budget of 1000 with 400 spent leaves 600. Hours add across people: 3 people working 4 hours each is 12 hours. The longest chain of 3 tasks of 2 days is 6 days.

    Science proof: A project works because a big piece of work is easier as small tasks with names, dates and owners: each task can be finished, counted and checked. Progress as a percent and money left are both counts, so the state of the project can be read at a glance instead of guessed. That is why the manager lists every task and counts it.
