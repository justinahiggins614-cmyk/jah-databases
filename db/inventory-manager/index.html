1. Description

An inventory manager tracks stock levels with real arithmetic: read the stock, add or remove items, and watch the counts. 5 products, 100 items in stock. Adding 20 to 80 gives 100. Removing 30 from 100 leaves 70. 10 items at 25.00 is 250.00. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

2. Math proof

Adding 20 to 80 gives 100. Removing 30 from 100 leaves 70. 10 items at 25.00 is 250.00. Warehouse A 60 and warehouse B 40: 100 in all. 3 products: 10, 20 and 30 in stock: 60 in all.

3. Science proof

An inventory manager works because stock is counted in and counted out, and each count is plain arithmetic: counted 98, recorded 100: a difference of 2, and 2 of 100 items damaged: 98 good. 7 days of 10 sales is 70 sales. That is why the local demo shows the stock and the counts together, and runs 0 items on a server.
