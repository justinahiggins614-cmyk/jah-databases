The Inventory Manager Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An inventory manager tracks stock levels with real arithmetic: read the stock, add or remove items, and watch the counts. 5 products, 100 items in stock. Adding 20 to 80 gives 100. Removing 30 from 100 leaves 70. 10 items at 25.00 is 250.00. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
