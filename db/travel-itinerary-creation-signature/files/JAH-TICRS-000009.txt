History
Travellers have always planned their journeys on paper: the grand tour of Europe was laid out town by town before the traveller set out, and the railway age made the printed itinerary a common thing, with trains, hotels, and sights in order. Creating an itinerary means writing that plan whole — the places, the days, and the order — so that the journey can be followed as it was planned and checked as it was run. From the handwritten list to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same: write the plan down before the journey, not after. This database is built on that public practice of creating every travel itinerary true.
