# Dictionary entries — The Apprenticeship Matching Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## apprenticeship matching

The recorded pairing of a learner who seeks a trade with a master who will teach it, so that each apprentice can be followed from the asking to the finished trade.

## apprenticeship

A stated span of learning a trade beside a master, kept in the record with its trade, its master, and its term, so that the learning can be read plainly.

## apprentice

A learner who seeks to learn a trade beside a master, recorded under a name and a number so that every paper for that apprentice can be matched to the same person.

## master

The skilled worker who teaches an apprentice a trade, named in the record with the apprenticeship, so that who taught the trade can be read plainly.

## trade

A named kind of skilled work, such as carpentry or plumbing, written in the record so that the apprenticeship can be matched to the work it teaches.

## term

A stated span of an apprenticeship, written in the record with its start and its end, so that every match can be placed in time.

## indenture

The written agreement of an apprenticeship, kept in the record with the apprentice, the master, and the trade, so that the agreement can be proved from the paper.

## match

The act of pairing an apprentice with a master, kept in the record with the date and both parties, so that the pairing can be proved from the paper.

## mentor

The person who guides an apprentice, named in the record with the apprenticeship, so that who guided the learner can be read plainly.

## trade school

A school that teaches trades, named in the record with the apprenticeships it serves, so that where the teaching sits can be read plainly.

## completion

The written record that an apprenticeship ended as planned, kept with the apprentice and the date, so that the ending can be proved from the paper.

## journeyman

A worker who has finished an apprenticeship and may practise the trade, named in the record with the trade, so that the finished learning can be read plainly.
