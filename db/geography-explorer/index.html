Every place names its source, every map can be read in words as well as seen, and the explorer is free to use with no sign-in. This record covers A Map That Shows One Continent at a Time.

A map that shows one continent at a time helps a reader learn without being overwhelmed: one land mass, its countries, its rivers, and its mountains, with nothing else to distract. The map must be described in words as well as drawn, because a reader who cannot see the map still needs to know what it shows. Without the word description, the map is closed to readers who cannot see, and the explorer is smaller for it. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
