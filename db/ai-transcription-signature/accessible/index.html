The AI Transcription Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. Transcription turns spoken words into written words, so speech can be read, searched and kept. The live demo on this page listens through the microphone only where the browser supports live speech recognition, and only after Start is pressed. Where it is not supported, the page says so plainly and transcribes nothing; no output is ever faked.

  1. What Transcription Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. Transcription turns spoken words into written words, so speech can be read, searched and kept. Ten spoken words become 10 written words.

    Math proof: Speech is measured in words per minute: at 150 words a minute, 2 minutes hold 300 words, and 60 minutes hold 9,000 words. Accuracy is a fraction: 99 of 100 words right is 99 percent, with 1 word in 100 missed. A timestamp is a time in seconds: a word at 12 seconds is stamped 0:12.

    Science proof: Transcription works because speech is a stream of sounds that can be matched to words, and pauses mark where sentences end. Confidence numbers say how sure the match is, so low-confidence words can be flagged and checked by a person. That is why a transcript is always checked against the recording before it is trusted.
