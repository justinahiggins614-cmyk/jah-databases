The plasma computer's native talents are wave-speed linear algebra, massively parallel interference patterns, and physics that directly mimics other physical systems. Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Many hard problems (scheduling, routing, chip layout) are energy-minimization problems, and physical systems minimize energy naturally. A plasma annealer encodes the problem in field and density landscapes, then lets the plasma relax: the settled configuration is the answer. This mirrors quantum annealers and optical Ising machines, both real technologies. Plasma annealing adds room-temperature operation and easy reprogramming — reshape the fields, get a new problem.
