Paint Calculator Database (paint-calculator-signature)

A paint calculator is a rule-based local demo: it calculates set toy cans, and counts the marks. The calculator reads 3 cans. The can alpha holds 27 marks. The largest holds 27 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
