History
Learners have sought help with the cost of study for as long as schools have charged fees, because a bright student kept out by price is a loss to the whole community. Scholarship discovery grew beside the scholarship itself: a written search through the funds offered by colleges, charities, and public bodies, so that each learner could read which help was open to them and on what terms. From the hand-written list of bursaries pinned to a school notice board to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the finding down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every scholarship discovery true. It is a record of published awards only, and it gives no advice about any application.
