Signature Lunar Shuttle Mission Design

JAH-SPMIS-000001 · Signature lunar shuttle mission design

Math proof: The Hohmann equation governs the transit: the departure and arrival burns are computed from the orbit radii, and the round-trip total of 8,200 metres per second follows from adding the four burns of the out-and-back journey. The formal statement is: Hohmann transfer: total change in velocity = sqrt(mu / r1) * (sqrt(2 * r2 / (r1 + r2)) - 1) + sqrt(mu / r2) * (1 - sqrt(2 * r1 / (r1 + r2))) mu is the standard gravitational parameter of the central body, r1 is the radius of the starting circular orbit, r2 is the radius of the target circular orbit, and sqrt means square root. The first term is the departure burn and the second term is the arrival burn; their sum is the total change in velocity, called delta-v, measured in metres per second.
