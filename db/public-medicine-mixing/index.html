About this data base

Generator
jahdb-public-medicine-mixing-1.0 — deterministic: same seed always makes the same record. Every record validated by an independent checker before acceptance.
Drift control
Every generated record is checked against an archive sample for exact duplicates and re-validated by the independent checker.
Target
1,000,000 records. The archive marches toward the million mark with overload protection: 100-record chunks, lazy loading, and a collapsible per-genre archive so no device ever renders everything at once.
