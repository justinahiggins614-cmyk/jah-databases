The Signature Impossible Physics Laboratory Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The impossible physics laboratory is a thought-experiment notebook, not a working laboratory: it writes out ideas that cannot work under known physics, such as a machine that runs forever with no fuel or a message that arrives before it is sent, and marks each one plainly as impossible, theoretical, or physically unsupported. Nothing here is presented as working physics, and no experiment is presented as real.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
