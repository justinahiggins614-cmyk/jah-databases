The Climate Modeling Tool Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A climate modeling tool models climate scenarios with real equations: type the carbon level, press Model, and read the warming. Carbon dioxide 280 to 420 parts per million gives forcing 2.17 watts per square metre. Forcing 2.17 with response 0.8 gives warming 1.74 degrees. 2 millimetres a year for 50 years raises the sea by 100 millimetres. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
