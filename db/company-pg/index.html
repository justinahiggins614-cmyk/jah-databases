Procter & Gamble — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. The Procter & Gamble Company is an American consumer goods company headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, founded in 1837 by William Procter and James Gamble. Its brands include Tide, Pampers, Gillette, Charmin, Crest, Dawn, and Bounty — household names sold in nearly every country. P&G is a Dividend King with decades of consecutive dividend increases.
