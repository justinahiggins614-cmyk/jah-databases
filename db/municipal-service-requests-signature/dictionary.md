# Dictionary entries — The Municipal Service Requests Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## service request

A resident asking the town to do something: collect, repair, permit, or answer, kept as a numbered case.

## case number

The one number that names a request, used by resident and town alike whenever the request is discussed.

## deadline

The date by which a request must be answered or done, promised when the case is opened.

## permit

A written permission from the town to do something, such as build, hold an event, or close a street.

## collection

The town's regular picking up of bins, recycling, or bulky things, on a published round.

## escalation

Passing a request up to a senior officer when it is late, disputed, or too big for the first desk.

## service standard

The town's written promise for a service: how fast it answers, how fast it acts, and what happens if it is late.

## tracking

Following a case by its number, seeing each step it has taken and the next step it awaits.

## desk

The town hall counter, real or online, where requests are taken and where residents can ask about them.

## round

A regular route or roster, such as a bin round or an inspection round, that work is grouped into.

## resident

A person who lives in the town, whose requests the town must take and answer.

## ledger

The written record of every request and every answer, kept in order, so the town's work can be checked.
