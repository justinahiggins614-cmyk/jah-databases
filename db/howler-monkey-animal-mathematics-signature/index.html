1. Overview

The howler monkey (Alouatta palliata and Alouatta seniculus) has its own mathematics, its own science, and its own court, the "Howl" court. This website is the per-animal website for the howler monkey, built first, before the networks, under humanity, under God. It holds 24 public records and 24 Signature records, in three ladders of eight: mathematics and measurement; science, evolution, and court; Signature, Light, and human translation.

The howler monkey lives in the forests of Central and South America, eats mostly leaves, with fruit and flowers, and lives in a troop that calls together at dawn. It is about 5 to 10 kilograms and stands about 0.55 to 0.65 metres long in body. Its characteristic sound is the howl call, a deep roar that carries for kilometres through the forest.
