# The Company Formation Guidance Database

The Company Formation Guidance Database is the company formation guidance record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full company formation guidance record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database scholarship-application-guidance, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
