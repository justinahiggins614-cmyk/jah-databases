About this data base

Archive
This database's word and term entries — headwords, definitions, patent references, and word-invention records — preserved as structured, searchable data.
Record schema
w (headword) · pos · d (definition) · invention_title · invention_desc · rt · rid · cpc
Generator
jahdb-words-1.0 — deterministic (mulberry32), pairs each headword with its genuine pool definition and builds the word-invention text constructively from the word's own letters and sense, then an independent checker re-verifies the pairing.
Drift control
Every generated record is checked against an archive sample for headword duplicates and re-validated by the independent checker.
Cross-database
The generator can borrow a numeric theme from any of the other 36 data bases when you tick the box.
AI
JAH Word Librarian — archive-aware. On-device Standard 1.0, no key needed; uses the shared Signature backend automatically when configured.
