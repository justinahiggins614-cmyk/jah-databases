Quotation Generator Database (quotation-generator-signature)

A quotation generator makes price quotes from lines of work: add lines, press Generate, and read the quote. 2 hours at 50.00 is 100.00. Tax of 10 percent on 100.00 is 10.00. The total is 110.00. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 2 hours at 50.00 is 100.00

Demo output:
2 hours at 50.00 is 100.00
