1. Description

A background remover keeps the subject of a picture and drops everything behind it. Upload a picture, pick the background colour, set the tolerance, and download the cut-out. It works by colour distance and flood fill from the edges — not by machine learning, and the page says so. Everything happens on a canvas in this browser, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Colour distance is the difference between two colours, counted across red, green and blue. A tolerance of 30 removes colours within 30 steps of the background colour. Flood fill is a connected set: starting from the 4 corners, it clears every background pixel it can reach without crossing the subject. Keeping is subtraction: 480,000 pixels minus 200,000 cleared leaves 280,000 kept.

3. Science proof

A picture is a grid of pixels, and a plain background is a region of nearly the same colour. Flood fill from the edges follows that region until it meets the subject's outline, which is a change in colour. That is why a plain background clears in one pass and a busy one does not, and why fine hair, which blends into the background pixel by pixel, cannot be separated by colour alone.
