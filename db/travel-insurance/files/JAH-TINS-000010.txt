History
Insurance began with merchants sharing the risk of a sea voyage: each put in a sum, and the one whose ship was lost was paid from the common purse. Travel insurance grew from that same sharing — a policy written before a journey, naming what is covered, for whom, and for how long, so that the traveller and the insurer agree about the cover before the journey begins. The policy is a record of terms, and this database records those terms as facts about policies, never as advice to any person to take out any policy. This database is built on that public practice of recording every travel insurance policy true.
