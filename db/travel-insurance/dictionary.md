# Dictionary entries — The Travel Insurance Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## travel insurance

Insurance that covers named risks of a journey, such as cancellation or medical care abroad, recorded as a policy with its cover, its traveller, and its terms.

## insurance policy

The written contract of insurance, naming the insurer, the insured, the cover, and the term, kept as the single true record of the insurance.

## insurer

The business that provides insurance under a policy, named in the record, so that every policy can be traced to the party that stands behind it.

## insured traveller

The person whose journey a travel insurance policy covers, named in the record, so that the policy can be traced to the person it covers.

## cover

The named risks a policy pays for, written in the record, so that what is covered and what is not can be read together.

## premium

The price paid for an insurance policy, written in the record with its amount, so that what the cover cost can be counted and checked.

## claim

A written asking for payment under a policy after a covered event, recorded with the event and the amount, so that the asking can be followed to its answer.

## exclusion

A named risk or case a policy does not cover, written in the record, so that the limits of the cover can be read beside the cover itself.

## policy term

The stated dates during which a policy covers a journey, written in the record, so that when the cover begins and ends can be read plainly.

## cancellation cover

Cover for the cost of cancelling a journey under named terms, written in the record, so that what cancellation the policy pays for can be read.

## medical cover

Cover for the cost of medical care during a journey, written in the record as a stated fact about the policy, so that what medical care the policy pays for can be read; the record never gives medical advice.

## liability cover

Cover for sums a traveller may owe to another person during a journey, written in the record, so that what liability the policy covers can be read plainly.
