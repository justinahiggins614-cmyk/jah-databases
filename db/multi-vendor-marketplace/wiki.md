# The Multi-Vendor Marketplace Database

The Multi-Vendor Marketplace Database is the multi-vendor marketplace record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full multi-vendor marketplace record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database marketplace-seller-operations, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
