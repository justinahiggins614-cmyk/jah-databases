    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The fan community builder lets a group of admirers of the same books, songs, shows, or games open a shared home on the web, with a welcome page, a notice board, and a calendar, all written and kept by the fans themselves.     History: Fan clubs are an old habit: newsletters folded and posted, meetings in church halls, badges worn on coats. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a notice board, a shared calendar, and rules written down where every member can read them.     Proposed solution: Name the fan community and what a visitor does in it; Write the rules in plain sentences a new visitor can follow; Give every fan community the same fair start: the same clues, the same time, the same scoring; Let a visitor try one fan community without signing in; Check the fan community works the same way on a phone and on a computer.
