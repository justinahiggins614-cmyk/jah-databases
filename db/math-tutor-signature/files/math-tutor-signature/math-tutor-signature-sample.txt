Math Tutor Database (math-tutor-signature)

A math tutor solves problems step by step: type the numbers, press Solve, and read the worked steps. It never pretends to be a live tutor. 2x + 3 = 11 gives x = 4.0. 9 squared is 81. x squared minus 5x plus 6 is 0: x = 2 or x = 3. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: x + 5 = 12 gives x = 7
