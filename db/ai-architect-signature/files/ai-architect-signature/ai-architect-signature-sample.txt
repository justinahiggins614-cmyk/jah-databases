AI Architect Database (ai-architect-signature)

An AI architect is a rule-based local demo: it plans rooms from length and width, and checks that the plan fits the site. A room 10 by 8 covers 80 square metres. A floor 12 by 9 covers 108 square metres. A room 10 by 8 has walls of 36 in total. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
