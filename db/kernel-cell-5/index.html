JAH-KC9-000067 — Cell 5 (center) in the Prewitt kernels

In the Prewitt operator (1970) the center cell (cell 5) holds 0 in Gx and 0 in Gy. Prewitt is Sobel's plainer sibling: where Sobel doubles the middle row's smoothing weight to 2, Prewitt trusts every row equally with weight 1, so its kernels are uniform. That makes Prewitt simpler and cheaper, but a little more nervous around noise -- the center row gets no extra vote. As the center cell it is the pivot the whole operator turns on -- and every one of the three operators sets it to 0.

In the Scharr operator (2000) the center cell (cell 5) holds 0 in Gx and 0 in Gy. Scharr kept Sobel's shape but re-optimized the numbers for rotational symmetry: its gradient direction estimates stay truer as an edge rotates, which is why libraries reach for Scharr when the angle of the edge matters, not just its strength. The price is louder numbers -- 3 and 10 where Sobel uses 1 and 2 -- so Scharr's raw responses run about four times hotter. As the center cell it is the pivot the whole operator turns on -- and every one of the three operators sets it to 0.

JAH-KC9-000070 — Cell 5: sensitivity experiment -- varying the cell

What happens if cell 5 (center) is not 0? The sensitivity experiment varies just this one cell of Sobel Gx through -2, -1, 0, 1, 2 and measures two things at the edge pixel: the response on the vertical step edge, and the response on a perfectly flat field (brightness 50 everywhere, no edge at all).

Two real, measured facts come out. First, the edge response moves linearly with the cell: every +1 added to the cell shifts the edge answer by 10, the brightness of the pixel this cell weights -- the cell's influence is exactly its weight times its pixel, no more, no less. Second, touching the cell breaks the kernel's zero-sum balance: on the flat field, where the true answer is 0, the response becomes 50 times the cell's deviation from its designed value. A single changed cell makes empty sky report edges. That is why kernel design is conservative: the numbers are a balanced set, and each one earns its place by keeping flat regions quiet while letting real edges shout. JAH-KC9-000071 — Cell 5: smoothing weight vs differencing weight

Every one of these 3x3 kernels is secretly two 1-D filters multiplied together: a smoothing filter down the rows times a differencing filter across the columns. That is why cell 5 (center) always factors as smoothing[1] x differencing[1]: sobel 2x0=0; prewitt 1x0=0; scharr 10x0=0. For Gy the idea is transposed: the cell is differencing[row] x smoothing[column].

This factorization is the famous 'trust' interpretation: the smoothing number is somebody's opinion about how much you ought to trust the row of pixels you happen to be standing on. Sobel says the middle row deserves double trust (2); Prewitt says trust every row equally (1); Scharr says the middle row deserves the most trust of all (10, with 3 for the outer rows). Cell 5's own values across the three operators are just that opinion, multiplied by the differencing sign of its column. The executed demo verifies the outer-product decomposition numerically for all three Gx kernels and prints this cell's factorization in each.

# Nine-number kernel lab -- pure-Python 3x3 convolution, zero dependencies.
# Images are 2-D brightness lists; the demo convolves the interior 3x3.
SOBEL_GX = [[-1,0,1],[-2,0,2],[-1,0,1]]
SOBEL_GY = [[-1,-2,-1],[0,0,0],[1,2,1]]
PREWITT_GX = [[-1,0,1],[-1,0,1],[-1,0,1]]
PREWITT_GY = [[-1,-1,-1],[0,0,0],[1,1,1]]
SCHARR_GX = [[-3,0,3],[-10,0,10],[-3,0,3]]
SCHARR_GY = [[-3,-10,-3],[0,0,0],[3,10,3]]
V = [[10,10,10,200,200] for _ in range(5)]   # vertical step edge: dark left, bright right
H = [[10]*5,[10]*5,[10]*5,[200]*5,[200]*5]   # horizontal step edge: dark top, bright bottom
F = [[50]*5 for _ in range(5)]               # flat field: no edges at all
D = [[10,10,10,10,10],[10,10,10,10,200],[10,10,10,200,200],[10,10,200,200,200],[10,200,200,200,200]]  # diagonal edge
def conv(img, k):
    return [[sum(img[y+dy-1][x+dx-1]*k[dy][dx] for dy in range(3) for dx in range(3))
             for x in range(1,4)] for y in range(1,4)]
def show(m):
    for row in m:
        print(" ".join("%8g" % v for v in row))
r, c = 1, 1
k = SOBEL_GX
out = conv(V, k)
print("Sobel Gx convolved over the vertical step edge (interior 3x3 output):")
show(out)
touch = V[1+r][1+c]
contrib = touch * k[r][c]
print("cell value:", k[r][c], "| brightness of the pixel it weights:", touch)
print("this cell contribution at output (2,2):", contrib, "of total", out[1][1])
print("share of edge signal: %.1f %%" % (100.0*contrib/out[1][1]))
