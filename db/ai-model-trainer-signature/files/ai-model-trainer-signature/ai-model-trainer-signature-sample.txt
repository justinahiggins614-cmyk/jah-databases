AI Model Trainer Database (ai-model-trainer-signature)

An AI model trainer is a rule-based local demo: it fits a line to points by small steps, and the loss falls with each step. Starting at w of 0.0, the loss is 41.0. At w of 1.0, the loss is 13.5. After 3 epochs, w is 2.08 and the loss is 0.01. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
