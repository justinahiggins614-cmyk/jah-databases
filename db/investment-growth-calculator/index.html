This record covers A Starting Amount Growing Year by Year.

A starting amount growing year by year shows how a projection is built: each year the growth is added to the amount, and the next year grows on the larger sum. The yearly steps must be plain, because a total that jumps from year one to year ten hides the growth in between. Without the yearly steps, the learner sees only the end and never learns why the total grew as it did. All figures are educational projections, never financial advice.
