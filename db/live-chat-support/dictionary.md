# Dictionary entries — The Live Chat Support Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## live chat

A small chat window where a visitor writes a question and an agent answers in the same minute, while the visitor is still there.

## visitor

A person who opens a chat to ask a question, who may give their name or stay unnamed, and who may close the chat whenever they choose.

## agent

A person who answers chats, one at a time or several at once, whose answers are written down and whose name the visitor can see.

## queue

The ordered list of visitors waiting for an agent, worked through in order, with the longest waiting told how long the wait will be.

## first reply

The agent's first answer to a visitor, which should come quickly and say what happens next, even when the full answer takes longer.

## transcript

The written record of a chat, kept in order, which the visitor may take away and which the service keeps as proof of the help given.

## escalation

Passing a chat to a more senior agent, or to another team, when the first agent cannot answer, with the transcript carried along so the visitor need not start again.

## resolution

The end of a chat where the visitor's question is answered and the visitor confirms it, marked in the record so the chat can be counted as resolved.

## wait time

The time between a visitor opening a chat and an agent's first reply, counted and shown honestly, never hidden.

## hours

The times when agents are present, written where every visitor can see them, so that no one waits for an agent who is not there.

## canned answer

A ready-written answer for a question asked again and again, which the agent may use and adapt, never as a wall that blocks a real question.

## ledger

The service's own written record of every chat, every wait, and every resolution, kept in order.
