Faith framing: the passages in Revelation 21 are presented here as a faith interpretation and as the design brief for this database. The biblical description is the design brief, not evidence that every described material or property can be reproduced. Every record keeps a text-faithful view and an engineering view distinct, carries a feasibility label, and preserves alternatives (gemstone identifications, cubit and stadion values, wall-measurement readings) as alternatives, never silently mixed. A monumental single-pearl gate is not a practical material; the buildable interpretation is engineered nacre-inspired panels, stated as such. Energy-free self-illumination, solid touchable holograms, autonomous construction at whole-city scale, and exact physical reproduction of a supernatural city are not claimed as working anywhere in this database.

East Gate 1 Rank 1 · feasibility: not-established

Text-faithful view (faith interpretation): Revelation 21:12 says the wall has twelve gates with names of the twelve tribes of Israel inscribed, and verse 13 places three gates on each side: east, north, south, and west. Verse 21 says each gate is a single pearl. The passage does not map a particular tribe to a particular gate, so the tribe shown here is an organisational assignment in listing order, not a textual claim, and the gate-watcher name is a role-title, not a named person.

Engineering view: A monumental gate made from one single pearl is not a practical material: natural pearls do not occur at gate scale, and a single-pearl gate cannot be quarried, transported, or fixed as a structural leaf. The buildable interpretation, stated as such, is an engineered nacre-inspired panel assembly: a structural substrate faced with pearlescent coatings that reproduce nacre's lustre and iridescence. The panel assembly is achievable at door and feature-wall scale and is research-required at monumental gate scale.

Signature-original design: this record approximates the corresponding public record with Signature-original materials and detailing. Where any part of it is speculative it is labelled Signature-original, and it is never presented as an authentic public record. This gate stands on the East side of the city, in position 1 of the three gates on that side. Its inscription, for record-keeping in listing order, is the tribe of Reuben. Its gate-watcher title is Gate Watcher of the East Gate - First; this is a role-title for the gate's watch function, not a claim about a named angel or person. Its material in the vision is a single pearl; its buildable form is nacre-inspired panels on a structural frame, with a pearlescent finish, sealed joints, and a maintenance plan for recoating.

Pearl Material Summary Rank 14 · feasibility: demonstrated

Text-faithful view (faith interpretation): Revelation 21:21 describes each gate as a single pearl. The passage gives the material as pearl, not a nacre specification.

Engineering view: Natural pearl nacre is layered aragonite platelets in an organic matrix, with a refractive index around 1.53 to 1.68 depending on direction, which produces its characteristic lustre and orient. Those optical values are demonstrated for real nacre; a monumental single pearl is not an established material, so the summary separates what nacre is from what a gate can be built from.

Nacre's strength comes from its brick-and-mortar microstructure: hard aragonite tablets bonded by a small fraction of organic material, giving toughness far above that of a single aragonite crystal. Engineered nacre-inspired materials copy that layering with ceramic platelets in a polymer or mineral matrix. The demo computes refraction at a nacre face from its mid-range index.
