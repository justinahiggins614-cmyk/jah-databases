# The Product Warranty Management Database

The Product Warranty Management Database is the product warranty management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full product warranty management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database warranty-claim-assistance, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
