# The Newsletter Creation and Distribution Database

The Newsletter Creation and Distribution Database is the newsletter creation and distribution record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full newsletter creation and distribution record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database distribution, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
