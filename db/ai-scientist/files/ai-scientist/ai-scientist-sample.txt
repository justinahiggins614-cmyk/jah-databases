AI Scientist Database (ai-scientist)

An AI scientist is a rule-based local demo: it plans a test with a control and a treatment, measures both, and reports the difference. The control reads 5.0 and the treatment reads 6.5: a difference of 1.5. The treatment beats the control by 1.5. The readings average 5.5. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
