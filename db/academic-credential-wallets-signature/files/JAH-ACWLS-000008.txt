History
Holders of degrees have carried proof of their learning for centuries, in letters, diplomas, and bound registers, because a credential that cannot be shown cannot be used. Credential wallets grew beside the digital record: a single kept place where a holder gathers each award, its issuer, and its proof, so that the whole learning of a person can be shown from one store. From the leather case of diplomas kept in a drawer to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the holding down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every academic credential wallet true. It is a record of published practice only, and it issues no credential.
