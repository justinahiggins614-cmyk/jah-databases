# The Student Enrollment Management Database

The Student Enrollment Management Database is the student enrollment management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full student enrollment management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database student-loan-navigation, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
