# Dictionary entries — The Freight Transportation Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## freight

Goods carried in bulk by road, rail, sea, or air, named in the record with their kind and their weight, so that what is carried can be read plainly.

## freight movement

One carrying of freight from one place to another, recorded with its goods, its route, and its carrier, so that the movement can be followed to its end.

## carrier

The business that carries freight, named in the record, so that every movement can be traced to the party that stands behind it.

## consignment

One set of goods sent together under one record, named with its goods and its destination, so that the goods sent can be counted against the goods received.

## route

The ordered path a freight movement follows, written in the record, so that how the goods travel can be read plainly.

## loading

The act of placing freight onto a vehicle or vessel, recorded with the place and the time, so that when the goods were loaded can be read.

## unloading

The act of taking freight off a vehicle or vessel, recorded with the place and the time, so that when the goods arrived can be read.

## depot

A building where freight is gathered, sorted, and loaded, named in the record, so that every movement can be traced to the depot it left.

## intermodal transport

The carrying of freight by more than one way, such as road and rail, in one movement, written in the record, so that each way the goods travel can be read.

## freight rate

The price charged to carry freight, written in the record with its amount and what it covers, so that what the carrying cost can be counted and checked.

## manifest

The written list of all freight loaded on one vehicle or vessel for one movement, carried with the goods, so that every load on board can be checked against the list.

## delivery

The handing of freight to its recipient at the destination, recorded with the time and the name of who received it, so that the movement is proved done.
