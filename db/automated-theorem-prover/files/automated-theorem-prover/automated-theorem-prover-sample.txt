Automated Theorem Prover Database (automated-theorem-prover)

An automated theorem prover proves small theorems in a bounded fragment of propositional logic: pick a formula, press Prove, and read the verdict. The prover proves P or not P: True. The prover handles 3 variables: 8 assignments. 7 variables give 128 assignments. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: The prover proves P or not P: True
