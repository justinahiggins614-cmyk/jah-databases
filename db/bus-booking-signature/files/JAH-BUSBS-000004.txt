History
Buses have carried people between towns for more than a century: the horse-drawn omnibus gave way to the motor bus, and the motor bus gave way to networks of routes with printed timetables, fixed stops, and fares paid at the door. Booking a seat on a bus means setting down, before the journey, which bus, which seats, and what fare, so that the traveller and the operator agree about the journey before it begins. From the paper ticket sold at the depot to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the journey down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every bus booking true.
