The Invoice Generator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An invoice generator builds a bill: line items with quantity and price, a subtotal, tax, and a total due. Add lines, set the tax rate, and print or save the invoice. All money is computed in this browser; no payment is taken anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/invoice-generator/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What an Invoice Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. An invoice is a bill that says who owes what, for which work, and by when. An invoice bills 3 hours of work at 40 per hour, 120 in all.

    Math proof: Each line total is quantity times price. The subtotal is the sum of the line totals. Tax is the subtotal times the tax rate. The total due is subtotal plus tax minus discount. With lines of 80, 50 and 20, a subtotal of 150 and 10 percent tax, the total is 165.

    Science proof: An invoice works because it is a complete record: who owes, who is owed, what for, how much, and by when. A unique number stops double payment, and a written total that can be recomputed from its lines stops disputes, because any reader can check the arithmetic.
