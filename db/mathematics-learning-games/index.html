A mathematics game website: number races, times-table duels, fraction rounds, and shape sorting, with points for quick, correct answers and hints that never give the answer away. The game is fair play only, with no real-money play of any kind, and every problem set is checked before it is published. This record covers A Number Line Race.

A number line race asks the player to move along a line by solving sums: each correct answer moves the player forward, and the first to the end wins. The race needs sums that fit the player's level, or the race is won by the player who was already fastest. Without the fit, the race rewards the strongest player and teaches the weakest nothing. This record gives the sums, the line, and the points for quick answers.
