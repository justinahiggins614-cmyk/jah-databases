Main Midway Rank 1 · simple · park area · beginner

The Main Midway is Cedar Point's entrance artery, running from the marina gate into the center of the park past a carousel pavilion, midway games, and the Sky Ride gondolas overhead. Its operating rides include the racing carousel Cedar Downs, the Midway Carousel, the Sky Ride, and the big coasters Raptor and GateKeeper at its edges. Its past includes the Mill Race log flume, which closed in 1993 to make way for Raptor, and generations of midway flats that turned over across the decades.

Millennium Force Rank 6 · compounded · signature coaster · intermediate

Millennium Force, opened in 2000, is Cedar Point's giga coaster, a steel coaster over 300 feet tall with a cable lift hill and a long course along the Lake Erie shore. Disaster Transport Rank 16 · complex · past coaster · advanced

Disaster Transport, which ran as an enclosed coaster from 1990 to 2012, began life as the open-air Avalanche Run in 1985, and it is recorded here as a past coaster. Its space-themed enclosure at the main entrance made it one of Cedar Point's most distinctive indoor rides, and it closed in 2012 along with the Space Spiral observation tower. Its site is now the GateKeeper entrance plaza, so the entrance that once hid a dark coaster now flies a wing coaster overhead.
