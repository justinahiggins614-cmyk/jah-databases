# Dictionary entries — The Attorney Directory Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## attorney directory

The recorded list of attorneys with their published standing, practice areas, and offices, kept in order so that any listing can be checked against the published source.

## attorney

A person admitted to practise law, named in the record with the bar that admitted them, so that who practises can be read plainly.

## law firm

A body of attorneys practising together under one name, kept in the record with its offices and its attorneys, so that the practice can be traced to its members.

## bar association

The body that admits attorneys to practice and keeps their roll, named in the record with the attorney, so that where the standing came from can be read plainly.

## practice area

A named field of law in which an attorney practises, such as property or family law, written in the record so that the work can be matched to the field it serves.

## admission

The act by which a bar admits a person to practise law, kept in the record with its date and its bar, so that the standing can be proved from the paper.

## standing

The current state of an attorney on the roll of a bar, written in the record so that whether the attorney stands admitted can be read plainly.

## office

The published place of business of an attorney or firm, kept in the record with its address as published, so that where the practice sits can be read plainly.

## specialisation

The stated focus of an attorney on one field of law, written in the record as published, so that the focus can be read without guessing.

## roll

The ordered list kept by a bar of all attorneys it has admitted, kept so that any name can be checked against the roll itself.

## disciplinary record

The published account of any action a bar has taken against an attorney, kept in the record with its date and its outcome, so that the action can be read in full.

## directory entry

One listing in the attorney directory, naming the attorney, the firm, and the practice areas, kept so that the whole listing can be read in one place.
