# Dictionary entries — The Professional Services Marketplace Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## professional services marketplace

The recorded list of professional services offered, with their providers, terms, and prices as published, kept in order so that any listing can be checked against the published source.

## professional service

Skilled work offered by a trained provider, such as accounting or design, named in the record with its provider, so that what is offered can be read plainly.

## provider

The person or body that offers a professional service, named in the record with the service, so that who offers the work can be traced to the paper.

## listing

One entry in the marketplace, naming the service, the provider, and the terms, kept so that the whole offer can be read in one place.

## terms

The stated conditions of a service, such as scope and time, written in the record so that what is offered can be read in full.

## price

The amount charged for a service as published, written in the record with its basis, so that what the service costs can be counted.

## category

A named kind of professional service, such as legal or financial, written in the record so that the service can be matched to the kind it serves.

## engagement

The written agreement under which a service is carried out, kept in the record with its parties and its terms, so that the work can be proved from the paper.

## review

A published account of a service as received, kept in the record with its source and its date, so that the view can be traced to its writer.

## seller

A provider who offers services in the marketplace, named in the record with the listing, so that who sells can be read plainly.

## buyer

A person or body that seeks a service in the marketplace, named in the record only where published, so that who sought the work can be read plainly.

## marketplace

A kept place, physical or on a screen, where services are listed and compared, named in the record with its listings, so that where the offers stand can be read plainly.
