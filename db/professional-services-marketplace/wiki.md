# The Professional Services Marketplace Database

The Professional Services Marketplace Database is the professional services marketplace record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full professional services marketplace record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database marketplace-seller-operations, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
