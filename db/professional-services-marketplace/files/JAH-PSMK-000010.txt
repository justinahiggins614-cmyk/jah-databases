History
Towns have had marketplaces for skilled work since the first square where a scribe, a surveyor, and a smith set out their stalls. The marketplace for professional services grew beside the trades: a kept list of who offers what, on what terms, and for what price, so that any buyer could compare before engaging. From the chalked board in a town hall to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the listing down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every professional services marketplace entry true.
