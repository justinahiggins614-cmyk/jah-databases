The Universal Question Engine Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The universal question engine takes any plain question a visitor asks and answers it in plain words, with each step of the answer shown so a reader can check it. Every answer is a clearly labeled design study of a generic question, never a claim about a live service, and the engine never invents a source.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/universal-question-engine/index.jsonl.gz
