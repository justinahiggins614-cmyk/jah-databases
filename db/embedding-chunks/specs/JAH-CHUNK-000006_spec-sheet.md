## Spec
- **Mechanism:** Time zones divide Earth into regions sharing a standard time. They exist because the planet rotates: noon arrives at different moments around the globe. Most zones differ by whole hours from UTC, though a few use half or quarter hours. Daylight saving shifts clocks seasonally in many countries. Crossing zones causes jet lag as the body clock adjusts.
