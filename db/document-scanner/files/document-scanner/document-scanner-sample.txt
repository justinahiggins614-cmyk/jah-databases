Document Scanner Database (document-scanner)

A document scanner reads entered document text and counts its parts. Paste the text, press Scan, and read the counts. The scan holds 4 lines. The scan holds 5 words. A tilt of 0.5 over 10 is an angle of 2.86 degrees. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: A document of 3 pages, scanned one page at a time
