History
Wholesale is trade between traders: goods move not one by one but by the case and the pallet, from maker to shopkeeper, at a trade price that leaves room for the shop to sell on. The wholesale market is where those traders meet, with lots counted in bulk and terms written down, so that a shopkeeper can stock a shop from one place. A wholesale marketplace is that meeting made digital, and this database is built on the public practice of selling in bulk, in the open, with every lot named and every price recorded.
