# Dictionary entries — The Wholesale Marketplace Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## wholesale

Trade in goods sold in bulk, by the case or the pallet, to buyers who sell on, rather than one by one to the last buyer, with the bulk terms written down.

## wholesaler

A trader who buys goods in bulk and sells them on in bulk to shops and other traders, named in the record, so that every lot can be traced to the trader who offers it.

## bulk

Goods counted and sold in large lots, such as cases or pallets, so that the price is a price per lot and the lot size is part of the listing itself.

## trade buyer

A buyer who buys in bulk to sell on, such as a shopkeeper, named only in their own orders, so that the market can serve them without keeping more than the order needs.

## minimum order

The smallest lot a wholesaler will sell, stated in the listing, so that a buyer knows before asking whether their order is large enough to be taken.

## trade price

The price a wholesaler asks for a bulk lot, recorded with its date and its lot size, so that every trade price can be traced to when and for how much it was recorded.

## distributor

A trader who carries goods from makers to wholesalers or shops, named in the record, so that the path of goods from maker to market can be followed.

## supply chain

The ordered path of goods from maker to wholesaler to shop, written down, so that every lot in the market can be placed on the path it travelled.

## pallet

A flat platform holding many cases of goods, moved as one lot, so that bulk goods can be counted, stored, and carried in whole pallets rather than single pieces.

## case

A packed box of goods, counted as one unit of bulk trade, so that a wholesaler's lots are counted in cases and the buyer knows exactly how many goods a case holds.

## terms of trade

The written terms under which a wholesaler sells, such as payment timing and delivery, stated with the listing, so that buyer and seller agree before the order is placed.

## catalogue

The ordered list of bulk lots a wholesale market holds, from every wholesaler, kept current, so that the market shows only lots its sellers truly have to sell.
