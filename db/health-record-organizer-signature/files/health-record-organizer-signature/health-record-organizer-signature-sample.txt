Health Record Organizer Database (health-record-organizer-signature)

A health record organizer is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy folders, and counts the records. The organizer reads 3 folders. The folder visit holds 12 records. The largest holds 12 records. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
