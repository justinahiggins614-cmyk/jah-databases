Ranking Framework: Criteria and Scoring Rank 2 · simple · beginner

The Signature ranking framework is an original Signature scoring design: six criteria in the same order, each scored zero to ten, with level-fit as a hard gate rather than a soft preference. A Signature blueprint that describes a Level 4 outcome at Level 1 is relabeled or rejected, never scored highly for ambition. This is a Signature original method, not a claim about any public scoring system.
