The Attendance Tracker Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An attendance tracker is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy days, and counts the marks. The tracker reads 3 days. The day monday holds 24 marks. The largest holds 24 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/attendance-tracker/index.jsonl.gz
