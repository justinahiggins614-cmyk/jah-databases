Earthquake Data Analyzer Database (earthquake-data-analyzer-signature)

An earthquake data analyzer analyzes entered seismic data with real statistics: type the readings, press Analyze, and read the magnitude and the energy. A magnitude 5.0 quake releases 2.00e+12 joules. A magnitude 6 quake releases 31.6 times the energy of a magnitude 5 quake. A magnitude 4.0 quake releases 6.31e+10 joules. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: A magnitude 5.0 quake releases 2.00e+12 joules
