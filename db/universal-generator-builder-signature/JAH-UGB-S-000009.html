Signature search finds Signature designs by kind, by seed, and by the generator that made them, as well as by plain words. A search that finds nothing says so plainly. This record specifies what is searchable in the Signature edition, how ranking works, and how a user narrows a search without losing a true hit — all as a design for the Signature search index.
