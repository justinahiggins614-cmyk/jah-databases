The Body Measurement Tracker Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A body measurement tracker is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy weeks, and counts the measures. The tracker reads 3 weeks. The week first holds 14 measures. The largest holds 14 measures. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
