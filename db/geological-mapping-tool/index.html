1. Description

A geological mapping tool maps entered geological data: type the readings, press Map, and read the map. 2 centimetres at 1 to 25000 is 500 metres on the ground. 3 kilometres east and 4 kilometres north is 5.0 kilometres apart. A range of 100 metres over 10 contours gives a 10 metre interval. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2 centimetres at 1 to 25000 is 500 metres on the ground. 3 kilometres east and 4 kilometres north is 5.0 kilometres apart. A range of 100 metres over 10 contours gives a 10 metre interval.

3. Science proof

A geological mapping tool maps entered geological data works because each value is plain arithmetic: A 50 metre layer dipping 30 degrees is 25.0 metres thick. A fault offsets the beds by 25 metres. 12 square kilometres times 0.05 kilometres thick is 0.6 cubic kilometres. That is why the local demo shows the work and the result together, and the local demo runs 0 maps on a server: it runs in the browser.
