Cedar Point Rank 1 · simple · operating park · beginner

Cedar Point, in Sandusky, Ohio, is a Lake Erie resort park whose coaster collection is among the largest anywhere, spread from the main midway to the frontier town and the beachfront. Its operating headliners include Millennium Force, Steel Vengeance, Maverick, GateKeeper, and Valravn, alongside a water park, Cedar Point Shores, and a long beach. Its past includes Mean Streak, reborn as Steel Vengeance, the impulse coaster Wicked Twister, and the enclosed Disaster Transport, and the full park is mapped record by record in the companion cedar-point-park pair.

Location

Sandusky, Ohio, United States

Status

NEW — operating

Lands and areas

Main Midway, Frontier Town, Camp Snoopy, Gemini Midway, Cedar Point Shores, Marina Gate

Rides NEW

Millennium Force (steel coaster); Steel Vengeance (hybrid coaster); Maverick (launched coaster); GateKeeper (wing coaster); Valravn (dive coaster)

Rides PAST

Mean Streak (1991-2016, reborn as Steel Vengeance); Wicked Twister (2002-2021); Disaster Transport (1985-2012)

Theming ecosystems

Lake Erie resort theming: a main midway, a frontier town, a camp-themed kids land, and a beachfront boardwalk

Hidden spots

Publicly-known hidden spots: the quiet beach paths at the back of the park, the shaded Frontier Town trails, and the marina-gate walkways
