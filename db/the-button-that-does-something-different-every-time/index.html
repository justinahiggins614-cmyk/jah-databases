This record covers a button that opens a joke book, with the surprise written out so a visitor knows the button is play and the outcome is always harmless. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.
