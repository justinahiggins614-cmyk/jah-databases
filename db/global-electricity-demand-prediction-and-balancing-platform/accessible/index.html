The Global Electricity Demand Prediction and Balancing Platform Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The global electricity demand prediction and balancing platform writes demand plans in plain words, one plan for an hour, a place, and a kind of day, with the size of the demand written out beside it. Every plan is a clearly labeled design study of a generic kind of day, never a live reading of a real grid.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
