1. Description

3. Science proof

A banner creator works because a banner is a picture with words, and each part is plain arithmetic: 4 letters at 24 pixels each take 96 pixels, and padding of 20 pixels on 2 sides is 40 pixels in all. A banner with 5 words says 'Fresh bread baked every morning'. That is why the local demo shows the banner and the words together, and runs 0 creations on a server.
