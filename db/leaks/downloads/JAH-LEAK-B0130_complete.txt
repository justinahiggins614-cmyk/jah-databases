ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS:
  • THEORETICAL: In 1935, Albert Einstein and Nathan Rosen published a paper describing the 'Einstein-Rosen bridge' - a solution of general relativity linking distant regions of spacetime - but the original structure was non-traversable and collapses too quickly for passage.
  • Physicist John Wheeler coined the term 'wormhole' in 1957; the first traversal-suitable metrics appeared in 1973.
  • In 1988, Michael Morris and Kip Thorne showed that certain wormhole solutions of Einstein's equations could be traversable - but only with 'exotic matter' of negative energy density, which has never been observed in macroscopic quantities.
  • No wormhole has ever been detected; current searches focus on gravitational lensing anomalies and unusual gravitational-wave signals, with no confirmed result.
