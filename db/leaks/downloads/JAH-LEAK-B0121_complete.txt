ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS:
  • The term 'UFO' was officially created in 1953 by the United States Air Force as a catch-all for airborne objects that could not be identified as known aircraft or familiar objects.
  • Upon investigation, most reported UFOs are identified as known objects, natural phenomena, hoaxes, or mirages, while a small number remain unexplained.
  • Project Blue Book analyzed 12,618 UFO reports from 1952 to 1969; 701 cases remained classified as 'unidentified'.
  • The June 2021 ODNI preliminary assessment examined 144 UAP reports from U.S. government sources (2004-2021); all but one (a deflating balloon) remained unidentified.
  • NASA's UAP Independent Study Team (final report, September 14, 2023) found no evidence of extraterrestrial origin and recommended a rigorous, evidence-based approach with better data collection.

NAMED WITNESS REPORTS:
  • Kenneth Arnold (1947) — Reported nine shiny unidentified objects past Mount Rainier, estimating speeds of at least 1,200 mph; his account coined the 'flying saucer' era.
  • David Fravor (2004) — Retired U.S. Navy Commander; reported a white, oval, ~40-foot object hovering over a disturbed ocean patch off San Diego that mirrored his F/A-18's maneuvers and vanished at extraordinary speed.
  • Alex Dietrich (2004) — Retired U.S. Navy Lt. Commander; Fravor's wingman and corroborating witness to the 2004 Nimitz Tic Tac encounter.

PUBLIC INVESTIGATIONS:
  • Project Blue Book — U.S. Air Force, 1952-1969: No reported UFO was ever an indication of a threat to national security; no evidence the 'unidentified' cases were extraterrestrial vehicles or technology beyond modern science.
  • Preliminary Assessment: Unidentified Aerial Phenomena — Office of the Director of National Intelligence / UAP Task Force, 2021: 143 of 144 reports remained unexplained due to limited data; 18 incidents showed unusual flight characteristics; no evidence linked sightings to extraterrestrial life.
  • UAP Independent Study Team — NASA, 2022-2023: No evidence of extraterrestrial origin found; recommended NASA play a prominent role using its Earth-observing assets and rigorous data collection.
