ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS:
  • The 'Surgeon's photograph,' published in 1934 and attributed to Dr. Robert Kenneth Wilson, was exposed in 1994 as a hoax: Christian Spurling confessed it was a toy submarine fitted with a wooden head, staged by big-game hunter Marmaduke Wetherell.
  • Operation Deepscan (October 1987) swept Loch Ness with 24 boats running echo-sounders in formation; three deep-water contacts could not be identified or relocated, but no large animal was found.
  • The 2018 University of Otago eDNA survey led by Neil Gemmell found DNA from many fish species and abundant eels — but no DNA of any large unknown animal.
  • Modern sightings began in 1933, when John and Aldie Mackay's report of a large creature was carried by the Inverness Courier.
  • The loch is about 745 feet (227 m) deep.

NAMED WITNESS REPORTS:
  • John and Aldie Mackay (1933) — Sighting of a large 'whale-like' creature in the loch, reported via the Inverness Courier
  • Christian Spurling (1994) — Confessed to building the fake Surgeon's photograph with a toy submarine and wooden head

PUBLIC INVESTIGATIONS:
