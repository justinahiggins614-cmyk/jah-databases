ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS:
  • THEORETICAL: Anti-gravity is the concept of a force that would exactly oppose gravity; under general relativity and particle physics it is considered impossible without exotic matter such as negative mass, which has never been observed.
  • NASA funded the Breakthrough Propulsion Physics Program (BPP) from 1996 to 2002 to study speculative propulsion; anti-gravity-like concepts were investigated and categorized as 'non-viable' after the study found no evidence of anti-gravity-like forces.
  • Historical claimants such as Thomas Townsend Brown (1920s 'gravitator' devices) and others reported anti-gravity-like effects, but none of the examples in the scientific literature are accepted as reproducible.
  • Experimental measurements, including antihydrogen studies, rule out repulsion between antimatter and Earth's mass as an anti-gravity mechanism.
