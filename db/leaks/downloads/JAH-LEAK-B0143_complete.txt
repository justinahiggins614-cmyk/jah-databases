ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS:
  • The Tombstone Epitaph of April 26, 1890, ran 'A Strange Winged Monster Discovered and Killed on the Huachuca Desert': two ranchers killed a giant winged creature with bat-like featherless wings, smooth skin, and an alligator-like face, reported at a ~160-foot wingspan.
  • Claims of a surviving photograph began with a 1963 article; researcher Mark Hall documented that no copy of the photo has ever been produced — it is most likely an urban legend.
  • On July 25, 1977, in Lawndale, Illinois, 10-year-old Marlon Lowe was attacked and briefly lifted by two large birds before his mother Ruth Lowe rescued him, in front of witnesses.
  • The Native American thunderbird is a storm-bringing spirit of Pacific Northwest and Plains legend — the cryptid borrows its name.
