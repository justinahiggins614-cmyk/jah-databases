ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS:
  • Crop circles are patterns created by flattening crops such as wheat, barley, rye, and maize; documented cases substantially increased from the 1970s onward.
  • Twenty-six countries reported approximately ten thousand crop circles in the last third of the 20th century, with about 90% in southern England, often near ancient monuments like Stonehenge and Avebury.
  • In 1991, Doug Bower and Dave Chorley confessed to the press that they had made hundreds of crop circles across England since 1978 using a plank of wood, rope, and a wire-fitted cap - a demonstration accepted as proof of the hoax method.
  • Bower and Chorley were awarded the 1992 Ig Nobel Prize for their crop circle hoaxing; modern circles continue to be made by artists and advertisers.

NAMED WITNESS REPORTS:
  • Doug Bower (1991) — Southampton landscape artist; admitted in 1991 to creating hundreds of crop circles with partner Dave Chorley using simple plank-and-rope tools.
  • Dave Chorley (1991) — Bower's partner; co-admitted the 1991 crop circle hoax confession and demonstrated the technique on camera.
