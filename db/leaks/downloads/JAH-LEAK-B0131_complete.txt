ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS:
  • THEORETICAL: In 1994, theoretical physicist Miguel Alcubierre published 'The warp drive: hyper-fast travel within general relativity' in the journal Classical and Quantum Gravity (vol. 11, L73-L77).
  • The Alcubierre metric describes a 'warp bubble' of nearly flat spacetime that moves by contracting space ahead of it and expanding space behind it, allowing apparent faster-than-light travel while the ship itself stays locally below light speed.
  • The construction requires a stress-energy field with negative energy density - exotic matter or manipulated dark energy - which has not been shown to exist in usable form.
  • Alcubierre himself suggested the Casimir vacuum between parallel plates might supply the negative-energy requirement; the paper remains a proof-of-existence geometry, not an engineering proposal.
