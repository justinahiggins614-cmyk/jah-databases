import hashlib
def compute():
    title_chars = 40; desc_chars = 394; source_count = 1; field_count = 15
    total = round(title_chars + desc_chars + source_count + field_count, 2)
    mean = round(total / 4, 2)
    first_plus_second = round(title_chars + desc_chars, 2)
    first_minus_second = round(title_chars - desc_chars, 2)
    first_times_second = round(title_chars * desc_chars, 2)
    return {"total": total, "mean": mean, "first_plus_second": first_plus_second, "first_minus_second": first_minus_second, "first_times_second": first_times_second}
def main():
    a = compute(); b = compute()
    sa = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(a.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    sb = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(b.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    for key in ["total", "mean", "first_plus_second", "first_minus_second", "first_times_second"]:
        print(f"{key}: {a[key]}")
    print(f"Run 1 SHA-256: {sa}")
    print(f"Run 2 SHA-256: {sb}")
    print(f"Deterministic: {'MATCH' if sa == sb else 'MISMATCH'}")
if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()

ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS: • On June 24, 1947, private pilot Kenneth Arnold reported nine shiny objects flying past Mount Rainier, estimating their speed at a minimum of 1,200 mph (1,932 km/h) - the first post-WWII sighting to get nationwide news coverage. • Arnold described their motion as like 'a saucer if you skip it across water'; newspapers mangled this into the objects being shaped like saucers, coining the terms 'flying saucer' and 'flying disc'. • The report triggered the 1947 flying disc craze, with hundreds of sightings reported across the U.S. over the following two to three weeks. • The military said it had been conducting no test flights during the time of Arnold's sighting, though Arnold himself first suspected experimental military aircraft. NAMED WITNESS REPORTS: • Kenneth Arnold (1947) — Experienced pilot and businessman; saw nine crescent-shaped objects weaving in formation near Mount Rainier and clocked them traveling about 50 miles in 1.5 minutes. • Unnamed prospector (1947) — A prospector on Mt. Adams reportedly saw the objects at around the same time as Arnold, noted in contemporary press coverage. PUBLIC INVESTIGATIONS: • Project Sign / Project Grudge / Project Blue Book — U.S. Air Force, 1947-1969: Blue Book, the successor project, analyzed 12,618 cases (1952-1969) with 701 remaining unidentified; concluded no evidence of extraterrestrial vehicles or a national security threat.
