ESTABLISHED FACTS & MEASUREMENTS: • The term 'UFO' was officially created in 1953 by the United States Air Force as a catch-all for airborne objects that could not be identified as known aircraft or familiar objects. • Upon investigation, most reported UFOs are identified as known objects, natural phenomena, hoaxes, or mirages, while a small number remain unexplained. • Project Blue Book analyzed 12,618 UFO reports from 1952 to 1969; 701 cases remained classified as 'unidentified'. • The June 2021 ODNI preliminary assessment examined 144 UAP reports from U.S. government sources (2004-2021); all but one (a deflating balloon) remained unidentified. • NASA's UAP Independent Study Team (final report, September 14, 2023) found no evidence of extraterrestrial origin and recommended a rigorous, evidence-based approach with better data collection. NAMED WITNESS REPORTS: • Kenneth Arnold (1947) — Reported nine shiny unidentified objects past Mount Rainier, estimating speeds of at least 1,200 mph; his account coined the 'flying saucer' era. • David Fravor (2004) — Retired U.S. Navy Commander; reported a white, oval, ~40-foot object hovering over a disturbed ocean patch off San Diego that mirrored his F/A-18's maneuvers and vanished at extraordinary speed. • Alex Dietrich (2004) — Retired U.S. Navy Lt.
