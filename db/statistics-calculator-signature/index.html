A statistics calculator website: the visitor types a list of numbers, and the page works out the count, the average, the middle value, and the spread, showing the list and each result together. Every step is shown in plain numbers, so a visitor can check the summary without trusting a hidden figure. This record covers The Average of Five Numbers.

A visitor who wants the average of five numbers needs a plain summary of a list of numbers: the average, the middle value, and the spread, each worked out in the open. A list of raw numbers hides its own story, and a single average can mislead without the spread beside it. This record states the design of a calculator that summarises any list with its average, middle, and spread, showing the arithmetic for each. The average of five numbers adds them and divides by five, so the calculator must show the five numbers, their sum, and the average beside them. This is a simple record: one idea and one calculation, with nothing extra to learn.
