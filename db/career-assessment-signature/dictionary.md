# Dictionary entries — The Career Assessment Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## career assessment

The recorded look at what a person likes, what they can do, and which paths are open, so that the choice of work can be made with the facts in plain view.

## career

A named path of work followed over a life, written in the record so that the path can be read plainly.

## counsellor

The person who carries out a career assessment, named in the record with the person assessed, so that who made the assessment can be read plainly.

## interest

A named liking of a person, such as working with numbers or with people, written in the record so that the liking can be matched to the work it suits.

## skill

A named ability of a person, such as writing or measuring, written in the record so that the ability can be matched to the work it serves.

## aptitude test

A set of questions or tasks given to show what a person can do, kept in the record with the person and the date, so that the showing can be proved from the paper.

## path

A named route into a kind of work, such as a trade or a degree, written in the record so that the route can be followed step by step.

## personality test

A set of questions given to show how a person tends to act and work, kept in the record with the person, so that the showing can be proved from the paper.

## assessment

The written account of a career assessment, naming the person, the interests, the skills, and the paths, so that the whole look can be read in full.

## report

The written note sent after a career assessment, naming the person and the paths found, so that the assessment can be proved from the paper.

## goal

A stated aim of a person for their work, written in the record so that the aim can be matched to the paths that lead to it.

## review

A fresh look at a career assessment after time has passed, kept in the record with its date, so that the change can be followed.
