History
Meetings were once remembered by the notes of one person in the room, written as fast as they could write, and often wrong. The record of a meeting matters because decisions are made in meetings, and a decision without a record is a decision no one can prove. Transcription writes down every word in order, and the summary says what was decided, so that the meeting can be checked after it ends.
