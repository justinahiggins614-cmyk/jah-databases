Duplicate Content Detector Database (duplicate-content-detector-signature)

A duplicate content detector is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy texts, and counts the copies. The detector reads 3 texts. The text draft holds 16 copies. The largest holds 16 copies. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
