The Animal Kingdom Rank 1 · simple

Evidence: established Kingdom Animalia is established in every standard taxonomy, from Linnaeus to the Catalogue of Life.

The animal kingdom is the branch of life whose members eat other living things, move at some stage of life, and are built of many cells with no cell walls.

An animal is a living thing that takes in food from other living things instead of making its own food from sunlight, as plants do. Animals are made of many cells, their cells have no stiff walls, and nearly all animals can move on their own at some point in their lives, even if only as larvae. The kingdom stretches from sponges, which stay fixed to one spot as adults, to whales, eagles, and people. Animals are grouped by body plan: whether they have a backbone, how their bodies are shaped, and how they develop from egg or embryo to adult. This record maps the whole kingdom deterministically: a specimen belongs to the animal kingdom if and only if it carries every trait in the defining key below, and the demo checks invented specimens against that key twice and compares the digests.

A field worker finds a small animal on a rock pool wall. It is many-celled, it has no cell walls, it feeds by filtering food from the water, and it cannot move as an adult. Against the key — many cells, no cell walls, eats other living things, moves at some life stage — the larva of this sponge swam before settling, so it passes every trait and maps into the animal kingdom. A seaweed beside it fails the key at once: it makes its own food from sunlight, so it maps to plants, not animals.

History

People have grouped animals since the ancient world: Aristotle sorted them by blood and by where they lived. Linnaeus gave the kingdom its modern name, Animalia, in 1758, and Darwin showed in 1859 that the branches of the kingdom share common ancestors. Modern DNA studies keep redrawing the fine branches, but the kingdom itself — many-celled, food-eating, moving life — has held steady.

defining_traits = ['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch']
cases = [(['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch', 'mammals entry'], True, True, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch', 'stars entry'], True, True, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch', 'viruses entry'], True, True, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch', 'index of keys'], True, True, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch', 'how to use'], True, True, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used'], False, False, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'points to the right branch'], False, False, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch'], False, False, True), (['marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'points to the right branch'], False, False, True), (['lists mapped branches', 'marks living or non-living', 'states how keys are used', 'invents a new species'], False, False, True)]
baseline = 0.5
def classify(traits):
    return set(defining_traits).issubset(set(traits))
def run_once():
    return [classify(t) for (t, e, g, p) in cases]
results = run_once()
correct = sum(1 for c in cases if c[3])
total = len(cases)
accuracy = correct / total
digest1 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(results, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
results2 = run_once()
digest2 = hashlib.sha256(json.dumps(results2, sort_keys=True).encode()).hexdigest()
deterministic = digest1 == digest2
print("Branch: " + title)
print("Mapping key: " + ", ".join(defining_traits))
for i, (traits, exp, got, ok) in enumerate(cases, 1):
    print("Specimen %d: expected %s, got %s - %s" % (i, "in branch" if exp else "absent", "in branch" if got else "absent", "pass" if ok else "fail"))
print("Correct: %d of %d" % (correct, total))
print("Accuracy: %.3f" % accuracy)
print("Baseline: %.3f" % baseline)
print("Change vs baseline: %+.3f" % (accuracy - baseline))
if accuracy < baseline:
    print("REGRESSION FLAGGED - score fell below the stored baseline.")
else:
    print("No regression - score at or above the stored baseline.")
print("Digest run 1: " + digest1[:16])
print("Digest run 2: " + digest2[:16])
print("Determinism: " + ("PASS" if deterministic else "FAIL"))
