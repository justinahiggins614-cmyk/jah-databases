# Dictionary entries — The Expert Witness Directory Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## expert witness directory

The recorded list of expert witnesses with their published fields, qualifications, and cases, kept in order so that any listing can be checked against the published source.

## expert witness

A person called to speak in court on a matter within their special knowledge, named in the record with their field, so that who speaks can be read plainly.

## witness

A person who gives evidence in court, named in the record with the case, so that who spoke can be traced to the paper.

## testimony

The evidence given by a witness in court, kept in the record with its date and its case, so that what was said can be read again as it was said.

## field

A named area of special knowledge, such as medicine or engineering, written in the record so that the expertise can be matched to the matter it serves.

## qualification

A published degree, licence, or record of study held by an expert, kept in the record so that the basis of the expertise can be read plainly.

## case

A single matter before a court in which an expert gave evidence, recorded under its case number, so that the evidence can be matched to the matter it served.

## report

The written statement of an expert setting out their opinion, kept in the record with its date and its case, so that the opinion can be read in full.

## court

The public body that hears cases and gives judgments, named in the record with the case, so that where the evidence was given can be read plainly.

## admissibility

The published rule on whether expert evidence may be heard in court, written in the record so that the test can be read plainly.

## directory entry

One listing in the expert witness directory, naming the expert, the field, and the qualifications, kept so that the whole listing can be read in one place.

## discipline

A named branch of knowledge or practice, such as chemistry or accounting, written in the record so that the expertise can be matched to its field.
