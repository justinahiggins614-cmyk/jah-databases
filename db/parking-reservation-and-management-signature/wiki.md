# The Parking Reservation and Management Database

The Parking Reservation and Management Database is the parking reservation and management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full parking reservation and management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database yelp-eat, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
