# Dictionary entries — The Online Examination Proctoring Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## online examination proctoring

The recorded watching over of an examination taken over distance, so that the candidate, the rules, and the sitting can be read from the record rather than remembered.

## proctor

The person who watches over an examination sitting, named in the record with the examination, so that who watched the sitting can be read plainly.

## examination

A set of questions given to candidates to show what they know, kept in the record with its subject and its date, so that the test can be read plainly.

## candidate

A person who sits an examination, recorded under a name and a number so that every paper for that candidate can be matched to the same person.

## sitting

One occasion on which an examination is taken, kept in the record with its date, its place, and its candidates, so that the occasion can be followed step by step.

## rule

A written condition for an examination sitting, such as what may be brought into the room, kept in the record as published, so that the sitting can be checked against the rule.

## identity check

The recorded check that a candidate is who the record says they are, kept with the sitting and the candidate, so that the check can be proved from the paper.

## incident

A written account of something unusual in an examination sitting, kept in the record with the time and the sitting, so that the event can be followed to its close.

## result

The recorded mark given for an examination, kept with the candidate, the examination, and the date, so that the result can be proved from the paper.

## question paper

The written set of questions given in an examination, kept in the record with the examination, so that what was asked can be read in full.

## answer script

The written answers handed in by a candidate in an examination, kept in the record with the candidate, so that what was answered can be read in full.

## examination board

The body that sets and marks an examination, named in the record with the examination, so that where the test came from can be read plainly.
