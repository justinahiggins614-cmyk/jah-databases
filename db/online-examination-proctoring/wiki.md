# The Online Examination Proctoring Database

The Online Examination Proctoring Database is the online examination proctoring record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full online examination proctoring record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database godmode-adaptive-examination-factory, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
