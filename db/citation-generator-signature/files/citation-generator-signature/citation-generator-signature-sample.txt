Citation Generator Database (citation-generator-signature)

A citation generator writes real citations in real styles: APA, MLA and Chicago, from the fields you type — author, year, title, journal, volume and pages. Type the fields, press Make citation, and read all three styles side by side. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: A citation names the author, the year, the title and the place: Lovelace, A. (1843). Notes on the Analytical Engine. Scientific Memoirs, 3, 691-731.
