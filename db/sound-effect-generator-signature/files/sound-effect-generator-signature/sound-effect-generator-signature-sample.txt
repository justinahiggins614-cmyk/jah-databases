Sound Effect Generator Database (sound-effect-generator-signature)

A sound effect generator is a seeded local demo: it makes a short burst of sound samples from a fixed seed, and the same seed always makes the same burst. A burst of 6 samples starts at 41. The burst holds 6 samples. The samples average 20.0. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
