Public edition. Public science and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Public edition. Real cities follow measurable laws: Bettencourt-West scaling shows socioeconomic outputs (patents, wages) scale superlinearly with population (~1.15 power) while infrastructure scales sublinearly (~0.85) — bigger cities do more with less per person. Street networks, transit graphs, and utility flows are modeled as networks with known statistics. Urban planners use these models to site districts, size utilities, and predict growth. Any city-creation concept must respect these laws or explain why it beats them.

JAH-CITYP-CRY-000003 — Crystal Ability City and Planet Creation — Planetary formation: the accretion story

Public edition. Public science and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Public edition. Planets assemble from dust: micron grains coagulate (sticking by electrostatic and van der Waals forces), grow to pebbles and boulders, then gravity takes over — planetesimals collide and merge, and a core above ~10 Earth masses captures gas to become a giant. The solar system's architecture and thousands of exoplanets constrain the models. Timescale: millions of years. Energy: gravitational and radiogenic. This is the reality any planet-creation proposal must address.
