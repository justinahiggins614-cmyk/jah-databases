Caesars Palace — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Caesars Palace is a landmark hotel and casino on the Las Vegas Strip, opened on August 5, 1966, by Jay Sarno with a Roman Empire theme. It is now the flagship of Caesars Entertainment. The resort is known for The Colosseum showroom, home to long-running superstar residencies, the Forum Shops at Caesars Palace, and the Garden of the Gods pool oasis. It holds about 3,960 rooms across six towers.
