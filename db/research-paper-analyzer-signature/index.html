1. Description

A research paper analyzer reads paper text you paste in and counts its words, sentences and sections: paste the text, press Analyze, and read the counts. Hosted reading is BLOCKED: no hosted backend is connected, and nothing here pretends to read a paper it cannot see. The sample sentence has 9 words. The text has 9 words. 300 words at 200 words a minute take 1.5 minutes to read. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.
