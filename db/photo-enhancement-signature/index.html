1. Description and History

Photo enhancement improves an ordinary photograph: brighter shadows, sharper detail, balanced color, and less noise, while keeping the photo honest and the original untouched. This database designs that enhancement one record at a time, from a dark photo to a whole series matched in tone, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.

Enhancement began in darkrooms, where printers dodged and burned to bring out shadow and highlight detail, and later moved to software that could do the same work with sliders. Each generation added a new ability — noise reduction, highlight recovery, gentle sharpening — but the rule stayed the same: improve the photo without changing what it shows. The design in this database follows that rule, with every change explained and reversible.

Every record here is a design, never a real product. Public records are fact-checked, real, and legal-only; Signature records are the Signature original version of the same subject; speculative material is labeled design-study, never as fact. Photo tools work on the user's own photos only, never anyone else's private images.
