Animation Creator Database (animation-creator-signature)

An animation creator is a seeded local demo: it moves a shape across frames from a fixed seed, and the same seed always makes the same clip. A clip of 6 frames starts the ball at 41. Halfway from 10 to 50 is 30. 6 frames at 2 per second last 3.0 seconds. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
