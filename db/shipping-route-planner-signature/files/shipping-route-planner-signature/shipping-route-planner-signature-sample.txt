Shipping Route Planner Database (shipping-route-planner-signature)

A shipping route planner is a rule-based local demo: it plans set toy shipments, and counts the marks. The planner reads 3 shipments. The shipment alpha holds 63 marks. The largest holds 63 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
