JAH-BVLUS-000001 — What Business Valuation Is
Business valuation is the process of working out what a business is worth in money, using its earnings, its revenue, its assets, and its cash flow. An example business earns $120,000 a year and sells for 3 times earnings, so its value is $360,000.

JAH-BVLUS-000002 — Discounted Cash Flow
Discounted cash flow values a business by adding up the cash it will earn in future years, with each future year counted for less than a dollar today. An example business earns $100,000, then $110,000, then $121,000 over three years, and at a discount rate of 10 percent the three years are worth $272,727.27 today.

JAH-BVLUS-000003 — Earnings Multiples
An earnings multiple values a business by multiplying its yearly earnings by a number that reflects how much buyers pay for each dollar of earnings. An example business earns $200,000 a year and the multiple is 4, so its value is $800,000.
