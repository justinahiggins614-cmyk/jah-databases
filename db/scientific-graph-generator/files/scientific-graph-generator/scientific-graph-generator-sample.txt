Scientific Graph Generator Database (scientific-graph-generator)

A scientific graph generator draws graphs from entered numbers: type the points, press Draw, and read the graph. On the line y = 2x + 1, x = 3 gives y = 7. Bars of 5, 8 and 3: the tallest bar is 8. An axis from 0 to 100 with 10 ticks has 10 per tick. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: Points (0, 0), (1, 2) and (2, 4) lie on a line of slope 2.0
