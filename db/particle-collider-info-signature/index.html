JAH-R20-SPCOL-000004 — LEP — the Large Electron-Positron Collider — Signature layer

Signature analysis: LEP is the purest demonstration that precision is discovery — no new particle was found at LEP, yet its measurements predicted the top quark's mass and cornered the Higgs. The Signature completion works through the logic of indirect discovery: when theory relates measurable quantities to unknown masses (radiative corrections), measuring the known precisely reveals the unknown approximately. That logic now drives the entire Higgs-factory case for FCC-ee/ILC/CEPC.

JAH-R20-SPCOL-000009 — Collider records table — energies, luminosities, eras — Signature layer

This Signature-layer record mirrors the public record "Collider records table — energies, luminosities, eras". The table values and the Livingston plot belong to the public record. This layer adds original Signature analysis.

Signature analysis: the Livingston plot is really a plot of sustained institutional will — a century of roughly tenfold energy gains per decade, each step requiring a new technology when the old one walled out. The Signature completion reads the plot's slope changes as technology S-curves: electrostatic, cyclotron, synchrotron, superconducting, and now the question of what bends the curve next (plasma wakefield? muons?). The completion also notes the plot's hidden variable: cost per unit energy has stayed roughly flat only because each technology was cheaper per GeV than the last — when that stops, the plot stops.
