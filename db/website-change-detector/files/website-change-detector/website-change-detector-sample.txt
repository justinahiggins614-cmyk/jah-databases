Website Change Detector Database (website-change-detector)

A website change detector is a rule-based local demo: it detects set toy changes, and counts the marks. The detector reads 3 changes. The change alpha holds 24 marks. The largest holds 24 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
