Vehicle Diagnostic Analyzer Database (vehicle-diagnostic-analyzer)

A vehicle diagnostic analyzer is a rule-based local demo: it analyzes set toy checks, and counts the marks. The analyzer reads 3 checks. The check alpha holds 18 marks. The largest holds 18 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
