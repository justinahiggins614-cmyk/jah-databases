Rental Yield Calculator Database (rental-yield-calculator-signature)

A rental yield calculator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy rents, and counts the marks. The calculator reads 3 rents. The rent flat holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
