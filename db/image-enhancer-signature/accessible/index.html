The Image Enhancer Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An image enhancer changes a picture's pixels to make it clearer: brighter, darker, more contrast, sharper, or inverted, all by real pixel maths, drawn on a canvas. The seeded 4 by 2 picture is drawn on the page; you can also upload your own picture, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

  1. What Image Enhancement Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. Image enhancement changes the pixels to make a picture clearer: brighter, sharper, or with more contrast. Image enhancement changes the pixels to make a picture clearer: brighter, sharper, or with more contrast

    Math proof: Brightening by 20: 100 becomes 120, 120 becomes 140. Contrast 1.5 times: 100 becomes 86, 160 becomes 176. Gamma 0.5 brightens 100 to 159. Inverting 100 gives 155. Clipping: 250 plus 20 is 255, not 270. Before and after: the average brightness goes from 130 to 150.

    Science proof: Enhancement works because each pixel is a number, and changing the numbers changes the picture: adding 20 brightens, stretching away from the middle adds contrast, averaging neighbours blurs. Values are clipped to 255, so nothing overflows. That is why the enhancer shows the numbers before and after, so every change can be read.
