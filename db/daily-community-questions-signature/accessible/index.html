  1. One Question, One Day, Everyone Together

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Daily community questions give a whole community one question a day to answer together, so strangers find common ground in the open, one answer at a time. One question a day gives a whole community a single thing to think about together, because a shared question is a shared conversation, and a community that answers together learns together. The question should be short, plain, and open enough for many answers, and it should be the same for every member that day. Without one shared question, the community talks about many things at once, and nobody talks about the same thing.
