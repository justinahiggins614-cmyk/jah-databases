Noise Remover Database (noise-remover-signature)

A noise remover smooths noisy samples with a moving average, and the noisy sample settles near the clean one. Type the samples, press Remove Noise, and read the smoothed values. A noisy sample of 0.7 smooths toward 0.5. A window of 0.6, 0.4 and 0.7 averages 0.57. The average gap is 0.13. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
