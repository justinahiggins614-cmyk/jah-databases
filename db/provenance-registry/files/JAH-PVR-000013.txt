History
Provenance is an old idea with a new name: museums have always recorded where each object came from, and libraries have always recorded who printed each book. On the web the same job is done by public standards that name a record's source, its date, and its chain of hands. This database is the registry for those chains. A gap is a missing link in a chain, marked as a gap, never filled in by guessing. The mark names what is missing and why, so that a reader knows exactly where the history stops.
