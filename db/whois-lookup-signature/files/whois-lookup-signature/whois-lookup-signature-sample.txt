Whois Lookup Database (whois-lookup-signature)

A whois lookup is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy names, and counts the marks. The lookup reads 3 names. The name blue holds 12 marks. The largest holds 12 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
