Bellagio Resort & Casino — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Bellagio is a luxury hotel and casino on the Las Vegas Strip, opened on October 15, 1998, by Steve Wynn's Mirage Resorts at a then-record cost of about 1.6 billion dollars. It is now operated by MGM Resorts International. The resort is best known for the Fountains of Bellagio, the dancing water show on its 8.5-acre lake, and for the Conservatory and Botanical Gardens. It holds roughly 3,950 guest rooms and suites and a casino floor of about 116,000 square feet.
