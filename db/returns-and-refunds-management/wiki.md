# The Returns and Refunds Management Database

The Returns and Refunds Management Database is the returns and refunds management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full returns and refunds management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database return-to-work-planning, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
