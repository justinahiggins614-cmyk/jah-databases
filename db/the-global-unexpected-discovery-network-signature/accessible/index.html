The Signature The Global Unexpected Discovery Network Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The global unexpected discovery network collects surprises from around the world: a strange fact, a forgotten story, an odd place, a small wonder, shared by readers and sorted so the next visitor finds something they never expected. Every discovery is a clearly labeled design study of a plain, checkable claim, and the network never presents a discovery as proven unless its source is written out.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
