Workflow

The worked solution has 5 steps, each of which can be checked on its own. Step 1: Give each robot a gripper for its neighbour. Step 2: Dock when sensors report a gap ahead. Step 3: Grow the chain until it spans the gap. Step 4: Cross as one connected structure. Step 5: Split on the far side on the signal.
