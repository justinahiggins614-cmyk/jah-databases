Factorization Tool Database (factorization-tool)

A factorization tool splits a number into prime factors: type a number, press Factor, and read the factors. 12 = 2 times 2 times 3. 100 = 2 times 2 times 5 times 5. 97 has 2 factors: 1 and 97. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 12 = 2 times 2 times 3
