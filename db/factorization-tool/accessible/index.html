The Factorization Tool Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A factorization tool splits a number into prime factors: type a number, press Factor, and read the factors. 12 = 2 times 2 times 3. 100 = 2 times 2 times 5 times 5. 97 has 2 factors: 1 and 97. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
