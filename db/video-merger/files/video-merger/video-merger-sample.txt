Video Merger Database (video-merger)

A video merger is a local demo: it joins two frame lists end to end, and the merged clip holds both sets of frames. Joining 4 frames and 6 frames gives 10 frames. 4 frames plus 6 frames is 10 frames. Blending 80 and 210 gives 145.0. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
