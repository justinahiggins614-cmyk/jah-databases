Section 1: Description and history

The book recommendation engine suggests a kind of book a reader may like, from plain answers about subject, length, and mood, with every suggestion labeled as an illustrative sample of a generic kind, never a real book by a real author. Recommending books is an old habit: a librarian who knew your taste, a friend who lent you a novel, a bookseller who set one aside for you. The website form grows out of that habit, adding plain questions and a written reason for each suggestion. Every record below carries its own history section, so the description and the history of each design are told together, in full sentences and paragraphs, never as stubs.

Section 2: Math proof

Every record carries a worked arithmetic proof: a count multiplied by a per-item amount gives a total, and the demo program prints that same total, so a fresh run of the demo verifies the proof exactly. A Mystery Novel Set in a Small Town Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A recommendation website: a visitor answers plain questions about subject, length, and mood, and the engine suggests a generic kind of book that fits those answers, with a plain reason for each suggestion. The engine names no real book, no real author, and every suggestion is a clearly labeled illustrative sample of a generic kind. This record covers A Mystery Novel Set in a Small Town.

A mystery novel set in a small town gives a reader a patient puzzle: a missing letter, a quiet street, and a truth found slowly by reading closely. The engine must ask the subject in plain words, match it to a kind of book, and give a written reason for the match, so the reader can see why this book and not another. Without a written reason, the reader must trust the engine blind, and blind trust is a poor way to find a book. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
