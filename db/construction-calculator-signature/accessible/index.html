The Signature Construction Calculator Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The construction calculator turns measurements into a materials estimate: a length, a width, and a depth become a volume in cubic meters, with a bag count beside it, so any builder can check the estimate before ordering.
