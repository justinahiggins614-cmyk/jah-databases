Rooftop Runners Rank 1 · simple · platform · beginner

Rooftop runners race across a city of flat roofs at dawn, leaping between buildings while laundry-lines and chimney-smoke block the way. The runners collect morning newspapers scattered on the roofs, and each full route delivered earns a dawn bonus.

The rules are simple: the player crosses a course 20 steps long, typing jump or run each tick, and must jump over every obstacle at positions 6, 12, 17 to reach the end. Each missed obstacle costs a life, and the run ends at the finish or when all three lives are lost.

Laundry-lines must be jumped, not ducked, since they hang at head height across the gap. A runner who plans the route around the newspapers scores more than one who chases speed alone.

Original game note: This is an original arcade-style game created for this Signature edition, with its own title, rules, and play.

How to play

Type jump/run each tick to cross the course, quit to stop.

def auto_play():

    print("Platform tribute: auto session")
    for cmd in AUTO:
        tick += 1
        jumping = (cmd == "jump") or air > 0
        if cmd == "jump" and air == 0:
            air = 2
        pos += 1
        if air > 0:
            air -= 1
        crashed = False
        if pos in obstacles and not jumping:
            lives -= 1
            crashed = True
        print("Tick %d: %s pos %d %s lives %d" % (tick, cmd, pos, "CRASH" if crashed else "ok", lives))
        if pos >= length or lives <= 0:
            break
    score = pos * 10 + lives * 100
    print("FINAL SCORE: %d" % score)
    print("RESULT: %s" % ("WIN" if pos >= length else "OUT OF LIVES" if lives <= 0 else "ONGOING"))

Thistle Terrace Rank 16 · complex · platform · advanced

Terrace gardeners climb thistle terraces at dawn, jumping between terraces while thistle-down drifts across the steps. The gardeners collect thistle-seeds from the terraces, and each full terrace climbed plants a bonus thistle.

The rules are simple: the player crosses a course 24 steps long, typing jump or run each tick, and must jump over every obstacle at positions 5, 9, 14, 18, 21 to reach the end. Each missed obstacle costs a life, and the run ends at the finish or when all three lives are lost.

Thistle-down drifts downhill, so the gardeners jump uphill into the drift rather than with it. A thistle-seed on the highest terrace is worth the climb, since the bonus thistle blooms across all lower terraces.

Original game note: This is an original arcade-style game created for this Signature edition, with its own title, rules, and play.

How to play

Type jump/run each tick to cross the course, quit to stop.
