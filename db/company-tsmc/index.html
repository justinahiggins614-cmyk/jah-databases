Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) is the world's largest dedicated semiconductor foundry, headquartered in Hsinchu, Taiwan, and founded in 1987 by Morris Chang. TSMC manufactures chips designed by other companies — including Apple, NVIDIA, AMD, and Qualcomm — and leads the industry in advanced process nodes such as 5nm and 3nm. It is the most valuable company in Taiwan and a linchpin of the global technology supply chain.
