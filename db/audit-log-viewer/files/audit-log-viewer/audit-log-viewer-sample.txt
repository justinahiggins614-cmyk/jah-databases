Audit Log Viewer Database (audit-log-viewer)

An audit log viewer reads pasted log lines, sorts them by time, and counts each kind. Paste the log, press View, and read the counts. A log of 3 lines. 2 of 5 lines are errors. 1 of 5 lines are warnings. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
