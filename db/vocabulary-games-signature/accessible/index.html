  13. A Streak System That Rewards Returning Players

    Record 13 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Vocabulary games teach words by playing with them: matching words to meanings, building words from roots, and guessing definitions, with fair hints and points for early answers. A streak gives a returning player a reason to come back tomorrow: one word game played each day in a row builds a chain, and breaking the chain costs something the player has earned.
