1. Overview

The galago (Galago senegalensis) has its own mathematics, its own science, and its own court, the "Cry" court. This website is the per-animal website for the galago, built first, before the networks, under humanity, under God. The galago lives in the savannas and woodlands of Africa south of the Sahara, eats gum, fruit, and insects, and lives in a small family group that meets at night. It is about 200 to 300 grams and stands about 0.15 to 0.2 metres long in body. Its characteristic sound is the cry call of the bushbaby, a wailing call heard at night.
