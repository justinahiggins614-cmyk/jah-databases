The Research Paper Analyzer Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A research paper analyzer reads paper text you paste in and counts its words, sentences and sections: paste the text, press Analyze, and read the counts. Hosted reading is BLOCKED: no hosted backend is connected, and nothing here pretends to read a paper it cannot see. The sample sentence has 9 words. The text has 9 words. 300 words at 200 words a minute take 1.5 minutes to read. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
