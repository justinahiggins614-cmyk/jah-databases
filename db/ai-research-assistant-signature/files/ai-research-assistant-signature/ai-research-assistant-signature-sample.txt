AI Research Assistant Database (ai-research-assistant-signature)

A research assistant finds sources, reads them, and summarises what they say, so a question can be answered from evidence. The local assistant on this page searches only the 24 records on this page and says 'not found' where no record answers. Hosted AI help is labelled BLOCKED until a hosted backend is connected; nothing here pretends to be hosted.

Worked example: A question about rivers returns 4 sources and a summary of 3 sentences.
