1. Description

A research assistant finds sources, reads them, and summarises what they say, so a question can be answered from evidence. The local assistant on this page searches only the 24 records on this page and says 'not found' where no record answers. Hosted AI help is labelled BLOCKED until a hosted backend is connected; nothing here pretends to be hosted.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Reading is measured in words: 8 sources of 400 words each are 3,200 words read. Coverage is a fraction: 3 of 4 questions answered is 3 over 4, or 75 percent, with 1 question left as a named gap. Agreement is a count: two sources agreeing on 4 facts and disagreeing on 1 agree on 4 of 5 facts.

3. Science proof

A research assistant works because an answer is only as good as its sources: finding the right sources, checking their date and evidence, and comparing them fact by fact keeps the answer honest. Naming the gap where no source answers is part of the work, because a guessed answer looks like a known one and misleads.
