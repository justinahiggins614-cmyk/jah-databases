1. Description

A random number generator makes numbers from a seed: type a seed, press Generate, and read the numbers. The generator is seeded and deterministic: the same seed always gives the same numbers, and the seed is shown. Seed 1 gives 0.1344. Seed 3 gives 31 out of 100. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

2. Math proof

3. Science proof

A random number generator works because a seed feeds a fixed recipe, and the same seed always gives the same numbers: seed 12 gives 61, 35 and 85: mean 60.3, and seed 22 rolls 6 dice: total 16. Seed 23 gives a final value of 0.9249. That is why the local demo shows the seed and the numbers together, and runs 0 generations on a server.
