The Random Number Generator Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A random number generator makes numbers from a seed: type a seed, press Generate, and read the numbers. The generator is seeded and deterministic: the same seed always gives the same numbers, and the seed is shown. Seed 1 gives 0.1344. Seed 3 gives 31 out of 100. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
