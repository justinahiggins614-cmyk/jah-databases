Feasibility: research-stage Active public research exists on automatic linking that connects every public topic to its related topics without error, results are published, but no working system at the needed scale yet.

This record covers A System That Grows With Its Readers.

A system that grows with its readers lets every reader add a topic, a fact, or a link, with a review step that checks each addition before it is published. Growing by readers is still research-stage: small groups grow a catalog well, but a catalog that grows by thousands still loses its order, and links added in haste still point the wrong way. Without honest growth, the system either freezes with too few topics or floods with too many, and a flooded catalog is a poor way to learn.

The website form grows out of that habit, adding links a reader can follow from any fact to every related fact. Growing a catalog with its readers is an old habit, from a commonplace book kept by many hands to a library built by gifts.
