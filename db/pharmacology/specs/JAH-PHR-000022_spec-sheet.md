## Spec
- **Mechanism:** Beta-2 agonists are drugs whose mechanism of action is: stimulate beta-2 receptors in the airways, relaxing bronchial smooth muscle. Example drugs include albuterol, salmeterol, formoterol. The mechanism is what unites every member of this class. Therapeutic uses include asthma attacks, COPD, bronchospasm.
