Genome Comparison Tool Database (genome-comparison-tool)

A genome comparison tool lines two teaching sequences up: paste both, press Compare, and read the matches. Design-study only: it holds no pathogen genomes. Two sequences of 10 bases match at 9: 90.0 percent identical. 17 matches in 20 bases: 85.0 percent identical. Two sequences of 12 bases match at 11 positions. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
