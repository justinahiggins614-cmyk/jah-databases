DESCRIPTION
C1 and C2 commercial overlay districts are mapped on local commercial streets inside residential neighborhoods to host neighborhood-oriented retail and services. Commercial districts run from C1 neighborhood retail up to C8 districts that allow gas stations and car repair. This overview edition presents C-1 Commercial Overlay Districts as a core zoning concept for contemporary cities.
