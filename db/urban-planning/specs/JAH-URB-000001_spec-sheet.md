## Spec
- **Mechanism:** Transit-oriented development concentrates housing, jobs, and services within walking distance of high-capacity transit stations, typically a five to ten minute walk. The approach pairs higher-density mixed-use zoning with reduced parking requirements near stations. This overview edition presents Transit-Oriented Development as a core transit concept for contemporary cities.
