## Spec
- **Mechanism:** An urban growth boundary draws a legal line around a metro area, concentrating development inside and preserving farmland and open space outside. Oregon's 1973 statewide planning law required every city to adopt one, with Portland's boundary the best-known example. This overview edition presents Urban Growth Boundary as a core land-use concept for contemporary cities.
