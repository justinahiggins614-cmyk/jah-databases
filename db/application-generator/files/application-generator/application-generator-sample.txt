Application Generator Database (application-generator)

An application generator is a rule-based local demo: it builds set toy apps from set templates, and counts the apps, the pages and the fields. The generator builds 3 apps from 3 templates with 4 fields each. The template is blog: it makes a blog app. The generator makes 12 apps: 4, 6 and 2. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
