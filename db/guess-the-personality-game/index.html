This record covers A Personality Quiz in Ten Friendly Questions.

A personality quiz in ten friendly questions is short enough to finish in one sitting and long enough to say something real. Ten questions give the quiz its shape: each question asks about a habit, a taste, or a dream, and the answers together draw a small portrait. Without a fixed count, quizzes drift: one quiz asks three questions and says too little, another asks fifty and nobody finishes it.
