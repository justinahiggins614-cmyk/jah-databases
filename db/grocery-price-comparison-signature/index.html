A grocery comparison website for generic kinds of groceries only: each grocery is a plain kind, such as a loaf of bread, a bag of rice, a carton of milk, or a box of pasta, and each price is a plain example number, never a live price from a real store. A price list for one grocery kind writes every example price for that kind in one list, in plain numbers, so the shopper can see them all at once. Without a single list, prices live in scattered places, the shopper compares from memory, and the cheapest price is often the one forgotten.
