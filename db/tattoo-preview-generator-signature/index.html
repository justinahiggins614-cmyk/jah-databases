1. Description and History

A tattoo preview generator lets a person see a tattoo design on their own skin before committing: the design placed on the arm, leg, or shoulder, shaped to the body's curves, in black ink or color, at the size it would really be. This database designs that preview one record at a time, from a small first tattoo to a design that wraps around a limb, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.

Choosing a tattoo once meant a picture on paper and a leap of faith. Software later learned to place a design on a photo of the body: first as a flat overlay, then shaped to the skin's curves, then matched to the light and the skin's tone. The design in this database follows that path, keeps the user's photo private, and shows the design honestly, including how it would age and where placement carries risk.

Every record here is a design, never a real product. Public records are fact-checked, real, and legal-only; Signature records are the Signature original version of the same subject; speculative material is labeled design-study, never as fact. Photo tools work on the user's own photos only, never anyone else's private images.
