This record covers A Fan Circle Built Around One Story World.

A fan circle built around one story world gives every fan one place to go: the story they love, the people who love it too, and the works they are all making about it. The circle needs a name that says what the story world is, rules written in plain words, and a welcome for new members, so a stranger can join without guessing. Without a circle, the fans of one story never find each other, and each of them loves it alone. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
