Matrix Calculator Database (matrix-calculator)

A matrix calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies, inverts and transposes matrices, and shows the working. Type two 2 by 2 matrices, press a button, and read the answer. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: A 2 by 2 matrix holds 4 numbers in 2 rows and 2 columns.
