AI Tutor Database (ai-tutor)

An AI tutor explains an idea, asks a question, and checks the answer, one step at a time. The local tutor on this page teaches with templates and worked examples, in this browser, with no sign-in. Hosted AI help is labelled BLOCKED until a hosted backend is connected; nothing here pretends to be hosted.

Worked example: A tutor explains fractions, asks 3 questions, and checks the answers.
