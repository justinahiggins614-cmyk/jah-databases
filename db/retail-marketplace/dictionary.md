# Dictionary entries — The Retail Marketplace Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## retail

Trade in goods sold one by one to the people who will use them, at a price written on a tag, from a shelf they can see, with the sale recorded as it happens.

## retailer

A seller who sells goods one by one to the last buyer, named in the record, so that every listing can be traced to the retailer who offers it.

## shopper

A person who seeks goods in a retail marketplace, named only in their own orders, so that the market can serve them without keeping more than the order needs.

## shelf

One retailer's place in the marketplace, holding their listings and their name, so that a shopper can find everything one retailer sells in one place.

## product page

One page for one good, holding its description, its price, and its images, so that a shopper can judge that good alone, without the rest of the market.

## price tag

The written price of a good, shown beside it with its date, so that the price a shopper sees is the price the retailer recorded, and no other.

## basket

The kept list of goods a shopper has chosen in a market, held together until the order is placed, so that the shopper can see their whole choosing before buying.

## checkout

The pages where a shopper's chosen goods are gathered and the order is placed, kept apart from the shelves, so that browsing and buying are never confused.

## return

The act of sending a good back after a sale, recorded with the good, the reason, and the date, so that the market can show what was returned and why.

## review

A shopper's written account of a good or a retailer, kept with the good it speaks of, so that later shoppers can read what earlier shoppers found.

## seller

A retailer offering goods in the marketplace, named beside every listing, so that every price in the market can be traced to who asked it.

## storefront

The public face of one retailer's shop in the marketplace: its pages, its listings, and its name, kept together, so that a shopper can see what the shop sells and who keeps it.
