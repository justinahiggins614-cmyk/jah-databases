The Notification Center Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A notification center shows a list of notifications in one place: titles, messages and times, with read and unread counts. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: the notifications are simulated and stored locally, 0 real notifications are sent, and real push is BLOCKED. A real notification center needs a server, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/notification-center/index.jsonl.gz

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A notification center shows a list of notifications in one place. A notification center shows 1 list of notifications

    Math proof: Two unread and 1 read: 3 notifications in all. Marking 1 as read leaves 1 unread of 3. Two lists: 3 notifications and 2 notifications, 5 in all. Reading all 3 marks 3 as read: 0 unread left.

    Science proof: A notification center works because a list is notifications with states, and each action changes the count: marking 1 as read leaves 1 unread of 3, and clearing 1 notification leaves 2 of 3.
