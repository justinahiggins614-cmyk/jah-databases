1. Description

An expense tracker lists what was spent, adds it up, and shows the pattern by category. Add expenses, set a monthly budget, and watch what is left. Everything runs in this browser and saves locally; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A total is all the expenses added: 10, 20 and 30 total 60. Food taking 40 of 100 is 40 percent. A rent of 500 a month repeats 12 times a year, which is 6,000 in all. A budget of 500 with 300 spent leaves 200, and spending 550 against 500 is 50 over.

3. Science proof

An expense tracker works because spending is easier to see as a list than as a memory: each expense has a name, an amount, a category and a date, so totals by day, week and category can be counted. Comparing the total with the budget shows at a glance whether the month is over or under, which is why the tracker shows both numbers together.
