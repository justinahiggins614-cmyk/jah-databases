The Expense Tracker Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An expense tracker lists what was spent, adds it up, and shows the pattern by category. Add expenses, set a monthly budget, and watch what is left. Everything runs in this browser and saves locally; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

  1. What an Expense Tracker Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. An expense tracker lists what was spent, so the total and the pattern can be seen. 5 expenses of 100 in all are tracked in one place.

    Math proof: A total is all the expenses added: 10, 20 and 30 total 60. Food taking 40 of 100 is 40 percent. A rent of 500 a month repeats 12 times a year, which is 6,000 in all. A budget of 500 with 300 spent leaves 200, and spending 550 against 500 is 50 over.

    Science proof: An expense tracker works because spending is easier to see as a list than as a memory: each expense has a name, an amount, a category and a date, so totals by day, week and category can be counted. Comparing the total with the budget shows at a glance whether the month is over or under, which is why the tracker shows both numbers together.
