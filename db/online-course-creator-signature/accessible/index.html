  1. A First Online Course of Three Short Lessons

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The online course creator lets a person teach a subject in short lessons: a course outline in plain words, lessons in order, and a plain summary for each lesson, so a learner can start at lesson one and finish the course step by step. A first online course of three short lessons needs plain rules a new visitor can follow, and the count is written beside each online course so a reader can see it at a glance. Without plain rules, a visitor must guess at every step, and a wrong guess wastes the time the tool was meant to save. The rules must be checked by a second reader before they are published, because a mistake in plain words is a mistake every visitor will follow. When the rules are clear and checked, the tool does its job quietly and the visitor gets on with the work. Worked example, Signature design: the same online course is rebuilt from the Signature pattern, so a first online course of three short lessons runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

    History: Teaching a subject in short lessons is an old habit: a lesson plan on paper, a set of exercises in order, a summary at the end of each lesson. The website form grows out of that habit, adding lessons a learner can open in order on a screen. Lists and guides for online courses are an old habit: a notebook page, a card file, a notice pinned to a board. This record follows that habit, writing the online course down in plain words so a reader can check it.

    Proposed solution: Sketch the Signature online course first, then write the rules from the sketch; State the fair-play rule the design must keep, in one sentence; Build the online course from the Signature pattern: public spaces only, no private messages; Let a visitor try one Signature online course without signing in; Check the Signature online course on a phone and on a computer before publishing.

    Dependencies: What must exist first: a course outline in plain words, short lessons in order, a plain summary for each lesson, and a review step before publishing. Nothing else is needed to open the first online course.

    Verification path: A visitor opens one online course, follows the rules in plain sentences, and finishes it without help. The demo program prints the same total as the math proof, and a second visitor repeating the steps gets the same result.

    Science proof: total = count x each. In plain words, this equation turns counted quantities into one checkable number: with 4 items at 2 each, the arithmetic gives 8 online courses, and the demo prints that same total, so the claim can be checked by arithmetic alone.
