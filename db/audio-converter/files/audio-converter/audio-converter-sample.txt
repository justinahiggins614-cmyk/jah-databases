Audio Converter Database (audio-converter)

An audio converter changes sound from one format to another, keeping its samples. The demo on this page converts a small sound sample locally: 441 samples of a sine wave, downsampled and counted in the browser. A full transcode needs a backend, and that is BLOCKED; this is a local partial demo, and it says so. You can also upload your own clip, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Worked example: An audio converter changes 1 clip from WAV to MP3
