The Audio Converter Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An audio converter changes sound from one format to another, keeping its samples. The demo on this page converts a small sound sample locally: 441 samples of a sine wave, downsampled and counted in the browser. A full transcode needs a backend, and that is BLOCKED; this is a local partial demo, and it says so. You can also upload your own clip, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/audio-converter/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What an Audio Converter Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. An audio converter changes sound from one format to another. An audio converter changes 1 clip from WAV to MP3

    Math proof: A clip at 44100 samples a second for 10 seconds has 441000 samples. A clip of 441000 samples at 16 bits each is 882000 bytes. Halving 44100 samples a second gives 22050 samples a second, and 441000 samples become 220500 samples. Stereo to mono halves 882000 bytes to 441000 bytes. Downsampling 8 samples keeps 4 samples: 0, 87, 87 and 0.

    Science proof: An audio converter works because sound is samples in order, and each sample is a number of bits: 441000 samples at 16 bits each is 882000 bytes, and halving the sample rate halves the samples to 220500. That is why the local demo converts a small sample list and shows the count, and says plainly that a full transcode needs a backend and is BLOCKED.
