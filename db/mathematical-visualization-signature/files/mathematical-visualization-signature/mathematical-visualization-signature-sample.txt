Mathematical Visualization Database (mathematical-visualization-signature)

A mathematical visualization turns numbers into pictures: type a function, press Draw, and read the plot. A plot of y = x squared at x = 2 gives 4. A straight line y = 2x + 1 at x = 3 gives 7. A circle of radius 3 has area 28.27. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
