# Dictionary entries — The Dental Practice Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## dental practice management

The recorded running of a dental practice, holding the patient records, the appointment book, the treatment plans, the billing ledger, and the supply orders together, so that the whole practice can be read from its records.

## patient record

The written account kept for one patient in a dental practice, naming the patient, the treatments given, and the dates, so that the care of that patient can be followed from first visit to last.

## dental chart

The diagram of a patient mouth kept in the record, marking each tooth and the work done on it, so that the state of the mouth can be read at a glance.

## treatment plan

The written list of the dental work proposed for a patient, in order, with the reasons recorded, kept beside the chart so that the work planned can be compared with the work done.

## recall list

The list of patients due to return for a check, kept by due date, so that no patient due a visit is left uncalled.

## appointment book

The ordered book of a practice, naming the patient, the time, and the work for every visit, so that the day can be read before it begins.

## billing ledger

The written account of what a practice has charged and what it has been paid, kept by patient and by date, so that the money of the practice can be counted.

## consent form

The written paper on which a patient agrees to a named treatment, kept with the treatment record so that the agreement can be shown.

## sterilization log

The written record of the cleaning and sterilizing of dental instruments, kept by date and by load, so that the safety of the instruments can be proved.

## dental hygienist

A trained member of the dental team who cleans teeth and records the state of the gums, named in the practice records with the work they did, so that the work can be traced to the person who did it.

## referral letter

The written note sending a patient to another practitioner for named care, kept with the patient record so that the referral and its reason can be read together.

## practice policy

The written rules of a dental practice, covering appointments, payments, and records, kept where the staff can read them, so that the practice is run by the same rules every day.
