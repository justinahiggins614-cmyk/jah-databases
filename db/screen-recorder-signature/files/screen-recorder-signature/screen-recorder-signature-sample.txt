Screen Recorder Database (screen-recorder-signature)

A screen recorder captures what is on the screen as a video file. Press Record, choose the screen, a window or a tab, and press Stop to save the clip. It records only after permission is given, and where the browser does not allow capture, the page says so plainly and records nothing. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Worked example: A recording of 60 seconds captures 60 seconds of screen.
