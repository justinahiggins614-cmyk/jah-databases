The Screen Recorder Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A screen recorder captures what is on the screen as a video file. Press Record, choose the screen, a window or a tab, and press Stop to save the clip. It records only after permission is given, and where the browser does not allow capture, the page says so plainly and records nothing. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/screen-recorder-signature/index.jsonl.gz
