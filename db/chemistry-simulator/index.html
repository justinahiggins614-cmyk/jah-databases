3. Science proof

A chemistry simulator works because a reaction is atoms counted on both sides, and each count is plain arithmetic: CH4 + 2 O2 gives CO2 + 2 H2O: 4 oxygen atoms each side, and 2 Na + Cl2 gives 2 NaCl: 2 sodium atoms each side. A reaction halves every 10 minutes: after 20 minutes 25 percent remains. That is why the local demo shows the reaction and the counts together, and runs 0 simulations on a server.
