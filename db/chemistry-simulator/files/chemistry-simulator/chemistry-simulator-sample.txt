Chemistry Simulator Database (chemistry-simulator)

A chemistry simulator works through reactions with real numbers: pick a reaction, press Run, and read the result. Water has molar mass 18.02 grams per mole. 2 H2 + O2 gives 2 H2O: 4 hydrogen atoms in, 4 out. A hydrogen concentration of 0.01 has pH 2.0. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: Water H2O has 2 hydrogen atoms and 1 oxygen atom: 3 atoms
