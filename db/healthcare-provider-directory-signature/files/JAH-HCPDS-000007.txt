History
People have always needed to find those who care for the sick: the town doctor, the midwife, the hospital, the clinic, each known by name and by word of mouth. As care grew into many fields — physicians, dentists, nurses, therapists, hospitals, clinics — the word of mouth had to become a directory: one place where each provider is listed with its name, its field, and its place, so that a person can find the care they seek without asking door to door. A directory lists facts about providers, and it never tells any person which provider to choose, nor does it give medical advice of any kind. This database is built on that public practice of listing every healthcare provider true, in one place.
