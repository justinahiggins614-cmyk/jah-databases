  12. A Streak of Weekend Activities

    Record 12 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The weekend activity finder helps a household choose what to do on Saturday and Sunday, in plain words, with the distance, the time needed, and the cost written out before anyone sets out. Every listing is a clearly labeled illustrative sample of a generic kind of activity, never a real event at a real place. A streak gives a visitor a reason to come back tomorrow: one weekend activity each day in a row builds a chain, and breaking the chain costs something the visitor has earned.
