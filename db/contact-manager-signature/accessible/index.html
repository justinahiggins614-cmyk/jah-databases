The Contact Manager Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A contact manager keeps every person you know in one place, with a name and a way to reach them. Add, search, edit and group contacts in this browser; everything saves locally.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/contact-manager-signature/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Contact Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A contact is a person with a name and a way to reach them, kept in one place. A contact holds a name, a phone number and an email address.

    Math proof: A contact list is a set, and a group is a subset. With 40 contacts split into family 8, work 14 and friends 18, the parts add to the whole: 8 plus 14 plus 18 equals 40. Search is filtering: the result is the subset whose name contains the typed letters.

    Science proof: A contact manager works because names and numbers are hard to remember in large numbers, and a written list never forgets. Sorting by family name turns finding into a short scan, and grouping turns one long list into short lists by kind. A duplicate check stops one person from becoming two entries.
