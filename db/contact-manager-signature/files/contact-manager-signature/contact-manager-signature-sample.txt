Contact Manager Database (contact-manager-signature)

A contact manager keeps every person you know in one place, with a name and a way to reach them. Add, search, edit and group contacts in this browser; everything saves locally.

Worked example: A contact holds a name, a phone number and an email address.

Demo output:
A contact holds a name, a phone number and an email address: Ana Reyes, 555-0142, ana@example.org
