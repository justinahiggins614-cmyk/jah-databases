History
A business runs on its papers: contracts, invoices, plans, and letters, each one a promise or a record that someone will need again. Document management is the work of keeping every paper where it can be found, naming what it is, keeping its history, and retiring it when its time is done. The papers themselves never changed; only the keeping of them grew from shelves into a system that can be searched.
