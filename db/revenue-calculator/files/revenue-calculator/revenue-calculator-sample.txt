Revenue Calculator Database (revenue-calculator)

A revenue calculator works out revenue from units and price: type the figures, press Calculate, and read the revenue. 10 units at 20.00 is 200.00. 5 sales of 40.00 is 200.00. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 10 units at 20.00 is 200.00
