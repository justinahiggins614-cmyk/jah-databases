Section 1: Description and history

The consumer interest tracker is a board that shows which kinds of goods and pastimes people are curious about, in plain words, with every entry labeled as an illustrative sample, never as a claim about real shoppers or real products. Lists of goods people are curious about are an old habit: a market stall's price list, a catalog of tools, a shopkeeper's recommendations. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a board of interests with plain-word summaries and a clear label on every sample entry. Every record below carries its own history section, so the description and the history of each design are told together, in full sentences and paragraphs, never as stubs.
