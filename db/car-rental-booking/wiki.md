# The Car Rental Booking Database

The Car Rental Booking Database is the car rental booking record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full car rental booking record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database booking-app2, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
