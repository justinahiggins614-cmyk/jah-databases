History
Travellers have hired carriages and cars for as long as there have been roads to travel: the owner kept a book of who took the carriage, for how long, and at what price, so that the carriage could be counted and the renter could be found. From the stable's ledger to the car rental booking record, the craft has been the same — record the renter, the vehicle, and the rate together, so that the rental can be proved and the vehicle can be held. A car rental booking is that record: a specification of how bookings are kept, never a live booking and never a payment. This database is built on that public practice of recording every car rental booking true, in full.
