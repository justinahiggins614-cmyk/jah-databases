ETL Workflow Designer Database (etl-workflow-designer)

An ETL workflow designer is a rule-based local demo: it designs set toy workflows, and counts the marks. The designer reads 3 workflows. The workflow alpha holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
