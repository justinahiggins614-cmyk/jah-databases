History
Universities have kept lists of their graduates for centuries, because a degree that cannot be checked cannot be trusted by the next school or employer. Degree verification grew beside the degree itself: a written ask to the awarding body, a check of the name, the subject, and the year, and a written answer that the degree was truly awarded. From the hand-written letter of confirmation in a university registry to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the check down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every degree verification true. It is a record of published practice only, and it issues no credential.
