# The Degree Verification Database

The Degree Verification Database is the degree verification record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full degree verification record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database health-information-verification, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
