SSL Certificate Generator Database (ssl-certificate-generator-signature)

An SSL certificate generator is a rule-based local demo: it generates set toy certificates, and counts the marks. The generator reads 3 certificates. The certificate alpha holds 21 marks. The largest holds 21 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
