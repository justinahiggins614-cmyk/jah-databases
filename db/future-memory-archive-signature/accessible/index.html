The Signature Future Memory Archive Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The future memory archive keeps letters, notes, and records sealed for a future date, each with a plain note saying when it may be opened and who it is for, so a reader in the future can find the past in good order. Every vault is a design study of a generic sealed record, never a claim about any real letter or person.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
