Section 1: Description and history

The most shared content tracker is a board that shows which kinds of writings, pictures, and clips people pass along to each other, in plain words, with every entry labeled as an illustrative sample, never as a claim about real sharing anywhere. Lists of writings and pictures worth passing along are an old habit: a letter copied by hand for friends, a clipping pinned to a board, a song taught by ear. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a board of shared items with plain-word summaries and a clear label on every sample entry. Every record below carries its own history section, so the description and the history of each design are told together, in full sentences and paragraphs, never as stubs.
