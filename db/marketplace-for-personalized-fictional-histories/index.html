Worked example: a reader who wants the simplest starting point opens record 1, A Fictional History of a Town That Never Existed, and a reader who wants the hardest problem opens record 23, A Yearly Showcase of Fictional Histories.

History

Telling made-up histories of places that never existed is an old habit: a tale told by a fire, a story invented for a child at bedtime, a map drawn for a land that never was. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a plain telling for each fictional history and a marketplace a visitor can browse. Field guides and indexes are as old as books themselves: a table of contents that tells a reader where everything lives. This index follows that habit, listing the twenty-three content records of this database in the order a reader should meet them.
