Dependency Analyzer Database (dependency-analyzer-signature)

A dependency analyzer is a rule-based local demo: it reads a list of parts and their needs, and counts the links between them. 4 parts hold 4 links. The list holds 4 parts. 1 part is needed by nothing: app. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 4 parts hold 4 links

Demo output:
4 parts hold 4 links
