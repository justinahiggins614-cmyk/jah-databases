Purpose
Gazebo (nowgz / Ignition) is the open-source 3D robotics simulator: rigid-body physics, sensor models (lidar, cameras, IMU), and world building for testing autonomy before hardware. Public edition documents the real simulator; Signature edition documents Manon's original synthetic-world counterpart design.
