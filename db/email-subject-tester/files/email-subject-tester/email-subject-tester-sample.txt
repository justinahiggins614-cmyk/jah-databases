Email Subject Tester Database (email-subject-tester)

An email subject tester is a rule-based local demo: it tests set toy subjects, and counts the marks. The tester reads 3 subjects. The subject alpha holds 117 marks. The largest holds 117 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
