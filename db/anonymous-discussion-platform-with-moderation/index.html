Anonymous Posts With a Public Moderation Log Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An anonymous discussion website: a visitor writes a post without a name, and the post appears on a public board under a one-time number, never a personal identity. Moderation is public: written rules, a moderation queue anyone can audit, and a public log of every action. There are no private messages anywhere on the platform, no living person's private data is ever posted, and harassment is removed under the written rules. This record covers Anonymous Posts With a Public Moderation Log.

An anonymous post with a public moderation log lets anyone speak without a name while everyone can see how the speaking is governed. The log shows every action: what was removed, why it was removed, and which rule it broke, so anonymity never becomes a hiding place for harm. Without a public log, anonymous posts are moderated in secret, and the readers must take the moderators' word for everything, which is no basis for trust.

Worked example: in a website built this way, anonymous posts with a public moderation log runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

Public moderation logs grew out of the old habit of posting the rules and the punishments where everyone could read them.
