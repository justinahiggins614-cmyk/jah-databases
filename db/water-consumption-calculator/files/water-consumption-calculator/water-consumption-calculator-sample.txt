Water Consumption Calculator Database (water-consumption-calculator)

A water consumption calculator is a rule-based local demo: it calculates set toy liters, and counts the marks. The calculator reads 3 liters. The liter alpha holds 252 marks. The largest holds 252 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
