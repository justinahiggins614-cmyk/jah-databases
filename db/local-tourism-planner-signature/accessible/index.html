    The local tourism planner helps a visitor plan a day out nearby, in plain words, with generic kinds of places, the distance, the time needed, and the cost written out before the visitor sets out. A riverside day plan joins a morning walk, a market lunch, and a hilltop view before sunset into one day with the order, the distances, and the times written out.   12. A Streak of Day Plans Followed Each Month

    Record 12 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A streak gives a visitor a reason to come back next month: one day plan each month in a row builds a chain, and breaking the chain costs something the visitor has earned. The streak must be forgiving, because a visitor who misses one month through no fault of their own should not lose a year of walks.
