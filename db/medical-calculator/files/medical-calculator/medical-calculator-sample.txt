Medical Calculator Database (medical-calculator)

A medical calculator is a rule-based local demo: it calculates set toy measures, and counts the marks. Reference only: general information, no specific dosing, no cure claims, no diagnosis claims. Sample measures only, no real patient data. The calculator reads 3 measures. The measure alpha holds 36 marks. The largest holds 36 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
