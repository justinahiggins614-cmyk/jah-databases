# The Discussion and Collaboration Rooms Database

The Discussion and Collaboration Rooms Database is the discussion and collaboration rooms record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full discussion and collaboration rooms record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database escape-rooms, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
