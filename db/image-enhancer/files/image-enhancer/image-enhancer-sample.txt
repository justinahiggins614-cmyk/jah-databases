Image Enhancer Database (image-enhancer)

An image enhancer changes a picture's pixels to make it clearer: brighter, darker, more contrast, sharper, or inverted, all by real pixel maths, drawn on a canvas. The seeded 4 by 2 picture is drawn on the page; you can also upload your own picture, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.
