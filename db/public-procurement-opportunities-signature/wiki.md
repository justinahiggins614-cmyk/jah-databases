# The Public Procurement Opportunities Database

The Public Procurement Opportunities Database is the public procurement opportunities record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full public procurement opportunities record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database lifelong-learning-opportunities, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
