The Biology Simulator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A biology simulator grows populations and genes with real numbers: pick a population, press Run, and read the result. A population of 100 growing by 5 percent becomes 105. Aa times Aa gives 25 percent aa. A heart at 72 beats a minute beats 4320 times an hour. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/biology-simulator/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Biology Simulator Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A biology simulator grows populations and genes with real numbers. A population of 100 growing by 5 percent becomes 105

    Math proof: A population of 100 growing by 5 percent becomes 105. A population of 1000 growing by 3 percent for 2 years becomes 1060.9. A pond at 400 of 500 capacity grows by 40 to 440. 1 cell becomes 2, then 4, then 8. A protein of 100 amino acids is coded by 300 bases.

    Science proof: A biology simulator works because living things grow by rules, and each step is plain arithmetic: 20 rabbits and 5 foxes: 4 rabbits per fox, and 3 species: 50 plants, 20 rabbits, 5 foxes: 75 in all.
