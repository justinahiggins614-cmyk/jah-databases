History
Roads have always needed watching: the watchman at the town gate counted the carts that passed, and the railway signalman watched the trains on his stretch of line. As roads grew crowded, the watching grew into traffic monitoring — sensors, counters, and signals that record how many vehicles pass, how fast they go, and where they slow, so that the state of the roads can be read as it is, not guessed. Monitoring states facts about traffic, and it never tells any person where to drive. This database is built on that public practice of recording every monitored road true, with its flow and its time.
