AI Video Generator Database (ai-video-generator)

An AI video generator is a seeded local demo: it makes seeded frames from a fixed seed, and the same seed always makes the same frames. Type a prompt and a seed, press Generate, and read the frames. A video of 6 frames with 16 pixels each. A video holds 6 frames. 6 frames of 16 pixels hold 96 pixels. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
