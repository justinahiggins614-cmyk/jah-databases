The Virtual Liquid Playground Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The virtual liquid playground is a simulation only: a screen where coloured water pours, splashes, swirls, and settles, and a visitor can stir it, dam it, and watch it find its level. Every record is a clearly labeled simulation, never a claim about real water or a real laboratory.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
