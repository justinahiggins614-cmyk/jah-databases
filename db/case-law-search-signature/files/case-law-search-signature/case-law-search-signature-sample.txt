Case Law Search Database (case-law-search-signature)

A case law search is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy cases, and counts the matches. The search reads 3 cases. The case theft wins 16 matches. The largest holds 16 matches. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
