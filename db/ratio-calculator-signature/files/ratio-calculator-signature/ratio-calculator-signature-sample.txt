Ratio Calculator Database (ratio-calculator-signature)

A ratio calculator works out ratios: simplify them, scale them, share a total by them, and read them as decimals, percents and unit rates. Type two numbers, and it shows the simplified ratio with its steps. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: A ratio of 3 to 4 has 7 parts in all
