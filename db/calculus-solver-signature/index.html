1. Description

A calculus solver finds slopes and areas: the derivative of a function at a point, the integral between two points, and the limit a function approaches. Type a function, and it shows the rule, the substitution and the answer. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

The derivative of x squared is 2x: at x = 3 it is 6, and at x = 4 it is 8. The derivative of x cubed is 3x squared: at x = 2 it is 12. The integral of 2x from 0 to 3 is 9, and the integral of x from 0 to 4 is 8. The area under y = x squared from 0 to 2 is 8/3, about 2.67. A line through (0, 0) and (2, 6) has slope 3.

3. Science proof

Calculus works because change can be measured: the derivative is the slope of a curve at a point, found by the power rule, and the integral is the area under a curve, found by the antiderivative. The fundamental theorem links them: the integral of 2x from 0 to 3 is 9, and 3 squared is 9. That is why the solver shows the rule first, then the substitution, then the answer.

Math proof: The derivative of x squared is 2x: at x = 3 it is 6, and at x = 4 it is 8. The derivative of x cubed is 3x squared: at x = 2 it is 12. The integral of 2x from 0 to 3 is 9, and the integral of x from 0 to 4 is 8. The area under y = x squared from 0 to 2 is 8/3, about 2.67. A line through (0, 0) and (2, 6) has slope 3.
Science proof: Calculus works because change can be measured: the derivative is the slope of a curve at a point, found by the power rule, and the integral is the area under a curve, found by the antiderivative. The fundamental theorem links them: the integral of 2x from 0 to 3 is 9, and 3 squared is 9. That is why the solver shows the rule first, then the substitution, then the answer.
