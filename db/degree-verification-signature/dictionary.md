# Dictionary entries — The Degree Verification Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## degree verification

The recorded check that a named degree was truly awarded to a named holder by a named issuer, so that the award can be proved from the record rather than remembered.

## degree

A named award given by a university or college for a course of study, kept in the record with its subject and its level, so that the award can be read plainly.

## holder

The person to whom a degree was awarded, recorded under a name and a number so that every paper for that holder can be matched to the same person.

## issuer

The university or college that awarded a degree, named in the record with the degree, so that where the award came from can be read plainly.

## award

The act of giving a degree to a holder, kept in the record with the date and the subject, so that the giving can be proved from the paper.

## verification request

The written ask for a degree to be checked, naming the degree, the holder, and the issuer, kept so that the ask and its answer can be read together.

## verification answer

The written reply to a degree verification request, naming the degree and whether it was awarded, so that the answer can be proved from the paper.

## diploma

The written document given to a holder as proof of a degree, kept in the record with the holder and the issuer, so that the proof can be read plainly.

## transcript

The written record of the courses and results that led to a degree, kept in the record with the holder, so that the study behind the award can be read in full.

## accreditation

The published recognition that an issuer may award degrees, written in the record so that the standing of the issuer can be checked.

## registry

The office of an issuer that keeps the records of degrees awarded, named in the record with the issuer, so that where the records sit can be read plainly.

## graduation

The occasion on which degrees are awarded, kept in the record with the date and the issuer, so that the awarding can be placed in time.
