1. Overview

The ring-tailed lemur (Lemur catta) has its own mathematics, its own science, and its own court, the "Squeak" court. This website is the per-animal website for the ring-tailed lemur, built first, before the networks, under humanity, under God. The ring-tailed lemur lives in the dry forests and spiny thickets of southern Madagascar, eats fruit, leaves, flowers, and other plant food, and lives in a troop of about a dozen, led by females. It is about 2 to 3.5 kilograms and stands about 0.4 metres long in body, with a ringed tail about 0.6 metres long. Its characteristic sound is the squeak call, a short sharp sound used between troop members.
