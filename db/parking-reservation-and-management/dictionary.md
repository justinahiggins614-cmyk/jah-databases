# Dictionary entries — The Parking Reservation and Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## parking reservation

The written record of a parking space held for a named vehicle for named hours, with the space, the time, and the price, so that the reservation can be found and checked.

## parking space

One marked place where a vehicle may stand, named in the record with its number and its place, so that the spaces held and the spaces free can be counted.

## car park

A place with many parking spaces, named in the record, so that where the reserved space stands can be read plainly.

## parking meter

A device that takes payment for a parking space for a stated time, named in the record with its place, so that how the space was paid for can be read.

## parking lot

An open area with many parking spaces, named in the record, so that where the reserved space stands can be read plainly.

## reservation time

The stated hours a parking space is held for, written in the record, so that when the space is held and when it is free can be read.

## parking price

The price charged for a parking space for stated hours, written in the record with its amount, so that what the space cost can be counted and checked.

## valet parking

Parking in which an attendant takes the vehicle and parks it, recorded in the reservation when the service provides it, so that how the vehicle was parked can be read.

## vehicle

A car or other motor vehicle, named in the reservation by its registration, so that which vehicle the space is held for can be read plainly.

## parking garage

A building with many parking spaces on its levels, named in the record, so that where the reserved space stands can be read plainly.

## reservation reference

The one identifier given to a parking reservation when it is made, written in the record, so that the reservation can be found at any moment.

## parking operator

The business that runs a car park or garage, named in the record, so that every reservation can be traced to the party that stands behind it.
