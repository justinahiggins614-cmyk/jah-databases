1. Description

A form builder makes forms: add fields, set the rules for each answer, preview the form, and export its design. Everything runs in this browser; answers save locally and no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A form is a set of fields, and a submission is one answer per field. With 6 fields, a submission holds 6 answers. A required field is a constraint: a form with 3 required fields cannot be sent while any of the 3 is empty. Counting answers is a tally: 7 of 10 people choosing 'yes' is 7 over 10, or 70 percent.

3. Science proof

A form works because people answer better when each question is small, named, and checked at once. One question per field keeps the work in small steps, and checking each answer as it is given stops errors from reaching the end. That is why a form with clear fields and live checks gets more complete answers than a blank page.
