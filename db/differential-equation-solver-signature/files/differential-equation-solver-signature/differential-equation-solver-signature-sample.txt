Differential Equation Solver Database (differential-equation-solver-signature)

A differential equation solver steps through an equation that describes change: pick a step, press Solve, and read the solution. Euler step: y starts at 1.0, one step of 0.1 gives 1.1. The exact value of e to the 1 is 2.71828. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: dy/dx = y with y(0) = 1 grows like e to the x
