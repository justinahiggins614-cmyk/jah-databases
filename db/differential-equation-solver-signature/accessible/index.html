  1. What a Differential Equation Solver Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A differential equation solver steps through an equation that describes change. dy/dx = y with y(0) = 1 grows like e to the x

    Math proof: Euler step: y starts at 1.0, one step of 0.1 gives 1.1. Two Euler steps: y goes 1.0 to 1.1 to 1.21. Midpoint step: y goes 1.0 to 1.105. Runge-Kutta step: y goes 1.0 to 1.1052. The exact value of e to the 1 is 2.71828.

    Science proof: A differential equation solver works because change over a small step is plain arithmetic, and many small steps trace the whole curve: Euler with 100 steps of 0.01 gives 2.7048, and a cup at 100 degrees cools one step to 92.0 degrees.
