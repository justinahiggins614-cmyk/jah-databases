A Long Talk Turned Into a Short Summary Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An assistant website: a maker pastes a finished piece they made themselves, picks the shapes they want, such as a short summary, a list of key points, or a set of questions, and the assistant drafts each new piece from the original words only. Every draft is labeled a draft, every draft shows which part of the original it came from, and nothing is copied from any other person's work. This record covers A Long Talk Turned Into a Short Summary.

A long talk turned into a short summary keeps the main points and drops the rest, so a reader who has two minutes can still learn what the talk was about, and the summary shows which part of the talk each line came from. The design is stated in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits: what a visitor sees first, what they can do next, and what happens after they finish. A repurposed piece built this way stays faithful, with every draft showing its source passage and every label in plain words.
