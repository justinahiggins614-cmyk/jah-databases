# The Tax Preparation Assistance Database

The Tax Preparation Assistance Database is the tax preparation assistance record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full tax preparation assistance record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database form-filling-assistance, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
