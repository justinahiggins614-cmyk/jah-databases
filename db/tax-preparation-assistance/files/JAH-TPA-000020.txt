History
Taxes are the price of the shared things — roads, courts, schools — and preparing a tax return is the act of writing down what was earned, what was spent for the business, and what is owed, so the authority can count it. The forms grew long and the rules grew many, and assistance grew beside them: plain words for the questions, a checklist for the papers, and a check of every figure against its record before the return is sent. This database assists preparation only; it gives no advice about what any person owes.
