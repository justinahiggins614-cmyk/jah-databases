Invention Licensing Marketplace Database (invention-licensing-marketplace-signature)

An invention licensing marketplace is a catalog where fictional invention examples are listed with their licensing terms, so that a licensor who owns an idea and a licensee who can make and sell a product can find each other.

Worked example: An invention licensing marketplace is a catalog where fictional invention examples are listed with their licensing terms, so that a licensor who owns an idea and a licensee who can make and sell a product can find each other. This catalog lists twenty-four records: what the marketplace is, the roles on each side, the licence types, the money, the valuation methods, the agreement terms and the mechanics of listing, searching and negotiating. Every invention here is fictional, every figure is a worked example, and nothing here is a real invention for license or a real agreement. This is general information about licensing, not legal advice.
