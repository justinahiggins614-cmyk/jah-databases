2. Math proof

In this worked example, 200,000 dollars of sales at 5 percent gives 10,000 dollars, because 200,000 times 5 divided by 100 is 10,000.     7. How licensing works

    Licensing works in five steps: the licensor lists the invention with its terms; the licensee searches and filters the catalog; both sides check the facts and the figures; they negotiate the rate, the territory, the term and the minimums; and they sign an agreement that both sides can check. Each step is covered by its own record in this catalog, with a fictional worked example and the arithmetic shown in full.
