1. Description

A notification center shows a list of notifications in one place: titles, messages and times, with read and unread counts. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: the notifications are simulated and stored locally, 0 real notifications are sent, and real push is BLOCKED. A real notification center needs a server, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Two unread and 1 read: 3 notifications in all. Marking 1 as read leaves 1 unread of 3. Two lists: 3 notifications and 2 notifications, 5 in all. Reading all 3 marks 3 as read: 0 unread left.

3. Science proof

A notification center works because a list is notifications with states, and each action changes the count: marking 1 as read leaves 1 unread of 3, and clearing 1 notification leaves 2 of 3. A center that stores locally keeps 3 notifications and sends 0 to a server. That is why the local demo shows the list and the counts together, and says plainly that its notifications are simulated and real push is BLOCKED.
