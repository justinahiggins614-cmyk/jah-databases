Notification Center Database (notification-center-signature)

A notification center shows a list of notifications in one place: titles, messages and times, with read and unread counts. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: the notifications are simulated and stored locally, 0 real notifications are sent, and real push is BLOCKED. A real notification center needs a server, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is sent anywhere.
