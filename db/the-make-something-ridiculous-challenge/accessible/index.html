The Make Something Ridiculous Challenge Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The make something ridiculous challenge dares a visitor to make something silly on purpose: a hat from paper, a song about a sock, a tower of cups, judged kindly on effort and imagination, never on skill. Every challenge is a design study of a playful dare, and the fair-play rules are written out in plain words.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
