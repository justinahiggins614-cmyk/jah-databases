1. Description

A music notation editor turns note names into numbers and back, and shows the notes on a staff. Type the notes, press Parse, and read the numbers. A melody of 3 notes: C4, E4 and G4. C4 is note number 60. E4 minus C4 is 4 semitones. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
