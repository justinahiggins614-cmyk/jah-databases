Descriptions

How teams of robots share work, agree on plans, keep formation, avoid collisions and finish jobs together: allocation, consensus, formation control, mapping, planning and fault tolerance, with full records, sources, provenance, search and a teaching ladder from simple to most complex.

This database holds 24 records. Every record is the full file with all data: history, problem, worked solution, answer, proofs, demo, program, generator, AI and sources. Public edition: documented from public knowledge, with sources verified HTTP 200 on 2026-11-18.

Math Proof

Each record's mathematics is the arithmetic of its own checkable figures: the demo adds them, averages them and hashes the result twice. If the two digests match, the arithmetic is deterministic. The formal statement per record is total = sum of the checkable figures; it is stated, not machine-checked, except that the demo recomputes it. Open any record to see its own figures and its own proof.

Science Proof

Each record states its scientific claims in plain words and cites its sources, so any reader can check them. Where the science is unsettled, the record says so in its Limits section. Public records rest on the cited public sources.

History

This database is part of Cutting-Edge Catalog 2 (types 101–200), another AI's proposals pasted by Manon on 2026-11-18; source unconfirmed. Wave 1 built the first five genuine BUILD types as pairs. This pair covers catalog type 102.

Workflow

Browse the archive below, search by title or subject, open a record for the full file, run its demo, or generate a new record from a seed. The Ask the AI box answers from this database's records only.

Generator

The seeded boundless generator (gen-multi-robot-coordination.js) creates new records on demand: node gen-multi-robot-coordination.js [seed] prints one JSON record; node gen-multi-robot-coordination.js --test runs its 10 gate tests. Generator ceiling: up to 1,000,000,000 scales per file, generated on demand by the file AI — not stored files.

AI

The specialist AI for this database is JAH Multi-Robot Coordination Librarian. It explains the database and every record in plain words, answers questions from this database's own records, and routes harder questions to the master AI. Download it with its specification: ai-database.json (spec) and the file AI: ai-file.json (spec).
