CGA evaluates medical, functional, cognitive, nutritional, and social domains together — finding what single-disease visits miss. It reduces mortality and keeps elders home. The whole elder, not the single disease.

def morse_fall_scale(history_fall, secondary_dx, ambulatory_aid, iv, gait, mental):
    """Morse Fall Scale: 0-24 low, 25-50 moderate, >50 high risk."""
    total = history_fall + secondary_dx + ambulatory_aid + iv + gait + mental
    band = "low" if total <= 24 else "moderate" if total <= 50 else "high"
    return f"Morse {total} -> {band} fall risk"
