GPS Coordinate Converter Database (gps-coordinate-converter-signature)

A GPS coordinate converter is a rule-based local demo: it turns set toy degrees, minutes and seconds into a decimal, and counts the parts. The converter turns 3 points. The coordinate holds 40 degrees. The largest holds 74.0 values. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
