# Dictionary entries — The Court Records Discovery Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## court records discovery

The recorded search of published court papers for a named case, carried out so that the filings, the orders, and the docket can be read from the record rather than remembered.

## court

The public body that hears cases and gives judgments, named in the record with the case, so that where the papers came from can be read plainly.

## docket

The ordered list of filings and events in a case, kept in date order, so that the life of the case can be followed step by step.

## filing

A written paper placed with the court in a case, kept in the record with its date and its filer, so that what was placed before the court can be read in full.

## order

The written direction or decision of a court in a case, kept in the record with its date and its terms, so that what the court directed can be proved from the paper.

## case law

The body of past court decisions that later courts may read, kept in the record so that the published reasoning behind a decision can be followed.

## transcript

The written record of what was said in a court hearing, kept in the record with its date, so that the words spoken can be read again as they were spoken.

## judgment

The final decision of a court in a case, kept in the record with its date and its terms, so that the outcome can be proved from the paper.

## party

A person or body named as a side in a case, written in the record so that who stood on each side can be read plainly.

## clerk

The officer of the court who keeps its records, named in the record with the papers filed, so that who kept the papers can be traced to the office.

## public record

A court paper open to the public by law, kept in the record with its source, so that what the public may read can be shown from the paper.

## case number

The number given to a case by the court, written in the record with the court, so that the case can be found by its place in the books.
