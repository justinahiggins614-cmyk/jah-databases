# Dictionary entries — The Application Marketplace Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## marketplace

A square, real or online, where many sellers list their goods in one place and buyers walk the rows.

## listing

One good offered for sale or for free, with its name, its description, its seller, and its price.

## seller

The person or team offering a good, named beside every listing, with their history of listings beside their name.

## buyer

The person choosing a good, who reads the listing, the reviews, and the seller's history before deciding.

## review

A buyer's written account of a good, with a rating, kept beside the listing so the next buyer can read it.

## rating

A number from one to five that sums up a review, shown with the count of reviews behind it.

## category

One shelf of the marketplace, such as tools or games, that listings are grouped into so buyers can browse.

## search

Finding listings by words, by category, or by seller, with the results ordered by match and by rating.

## update

A new version of an application, listed beside the old one, with the changes named.

## free

Costing nothing: every listing in this marketplace is free, and no payment of any kind is taken or handled.

## trust

The confidence that a listing is what it says it is, built from verified sellers, honest reviews, and quick answers.

## ledger

The marketplace's own written record of every listing and every change, kept in order.
