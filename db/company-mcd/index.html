McDonald's — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. McDonald's Corporation is an American fast-food company headquartered in Chicago, Illinois, founded in 1955 by Ray Kroc (from the McDonald brothers' restaurant). It is the world's largest restaurant chain by locations, famous for the Big Mac, Quarter Pounder, french fries, and the golden arches. Most restaurants are run by independent franchisees.
