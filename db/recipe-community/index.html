A Tomato Soup With Plain Measures Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A community website: a cook writes a recipe with a list of ingredients in plain measures, numbered steps, a cooking time, and a serving count, and other cooks try it and write back what worked. Every recipe is an original, generic dish, written by its author, and the site makes no medical or nutrition claim, gives no dietary advice, and names no real chef or cookbook. This record covers A Tomato Soup With Plain Measures.

A tomato soup with plain measures lists tomatoes, onions, water, and herbs in cups and spoons, so a cook can gather everything before the pot is lit and never stop halfway to measure. The design is stated in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits: what a visitor sees first, what they can do next, and what happens after they finish. A shared recipe built this way stays honest, with plain measures, numbered steps, and no medical or nutrition claim.
