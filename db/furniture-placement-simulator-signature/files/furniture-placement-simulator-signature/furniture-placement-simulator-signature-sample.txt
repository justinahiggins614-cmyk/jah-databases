Furniture Placement Simulator Database (furniture-placement-simulator-signature)

A furniture placement simulator is a rule-based local demo: it places set toy rooms, and counts the marks. The simulator reads 3 rooms. The room north holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
