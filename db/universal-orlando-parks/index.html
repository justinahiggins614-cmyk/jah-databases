Universal Studios Florida: Hollywood Rank 1 · simple · park land · beginner

Hollywood is the entrance land of Universal Studios Florida, a boulevard dressed as golden-age Hollywood, with a drive-in restaurant, theater facades, and the park's steel coaster rising behind it. It sets the park's frame story, that the guest is walking onto a studio lot, and its past includes tribute exhibits and a prop boneyard that once let guests walk among movie props. Today it is a compact entrance: a coaster, a stunt show, shops, and the boulevard itself.

Rides PAST (defunct / replaced)

The Boneyard (1990-2008); Lucy: A Tribute (1990-2015); Stage 54 (1990s-2000s)

Theming ecosystems

A golden-age Hollywood boulevard: palm-lined street, theater facades, a drive-in restaurant, and a stunt show, framed as the park's front door and its movie-premiere fantasy.

Hidden spots (publicly known)

The quiet shop windows and the upper facades of Hollywood Boulevard are the land's best-known tucked-away details; the boulevard rewards guests who look up.

What remains is a harbor and a city street, a smaller land than it once was, and a reminder that parks rewrite themselves land by land.

Islands of Adventure: Skull Island Rank 14 · complex · park land · advanced

Skull Island opened in 2016 as Islands of Adventure's smallest land, built around a single ride: a trackless dark ride through a jungle temple to a giant-ape encounter. The land is a skull-shaped gate, a temple queue, and a ride, and its compactness is its character. It replaced part of the park's earlier landscape and gave the park a monster-movie anchor without a full island's worth of rides.

A monster-movie island: a skull-shaped gate, a jungle temple, and a trackless ride through a giant-ape encounter, where the theme is a lost expedition's monster story.

Hidden spots (publicly known)

The ride's queue temple and the jungle paths around the skull gate are the land's best-known tucked-away corners; the queue itself is a walk-through temple.

Universal Volcano Bay (whole water park) Rank 15 · complex · water park · advanced

Volcano Bay is included in the Orlando pair as a whole-park record because it is the resort's water park, and its four villages sort water by mood: a wave pool, a lazy river, a fearless action river, and a set of trap-door plunges through the volcano. It opened in 2017 on the site era that followed Wet 'n Wild, and its tap-to-queue system changed how water parks handle lines. The record covers the whole park, not a single land, because the park's villages are its lands.

No retired slides of note; the park's lineup is recorded here as opened in 2017 (2017-present)

Theming ecosystems

A Polynesian island water park: four villages arranged by water type around a tall volcano, with a tap-to-queue system replacing slide lines.

Hidden spots (publicly known)

The quiet beach coves of Wave Village and the shaded paths behind the volcano's base are the park's best-known tucked-away corners; the volcano's inner caverns are part of the ride queues.

    print("%s: %d" % (kind, counts[kind]))

Universal Epic Universe (whole park, five lands as areas) Rank 16 · complex · theme park · advanced

Epic Universe is included in the Orlando pair as a whole-park record whose five lands are listed as its areas, because the pair's sixteen slots cover the resort completely only if Epic's five worlds are carried together. The park opened in 2025 with a celestial garden hub and portal entrances into a wizard Paris, a video-game kingdom, a dragon isle, and a monster village, and its headline rides span dueling coasters, a dragon glider, and a monster manor dark ride. The record is an opening-day snapshot, with no past rides yet.

Theming ecosystems

A hub-and-spoke cosmos: a celestial garden hub with portal entrances into five worlds, a wizard Paris, a video-game kingdom, a dragon isle, and a monster village, each with its own rides, dining, and shows.

Hidden spots (publicly known)

The portal passages themselves are the hidden layer: each world's entrance tunnel is a themed transition, and Celestial Park's quiet garden paths are the park's best-known tucked-away corners.
