# Dictionary entries — The Customer Feedback Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## feedback

A message from a customer about what was good, what was bad, or what should change, kept as a record and read by the business, not merely heard and forgotten.

## feedback channel

One way customers can reach the business with a message, such as a form, a letter, a call, or a comment card, each with its own inbox and its own count.

## survey

A set of questions put to customers in the same words, so their answers can be counted and compared, with the questions written before the answers are known.

## satisfaction

How pleased a customer says they are with what they received, measured on a stated scale, and counted across many customers to see the pattern.

## complaint

A message that says something went wrong and asks for it to be put right, recorded with the date and the customer, and tracked until the answer is given.

## suggestion

A message that proposes a change, recorded with the customer's words kept whole, so the idea is judged as the customer meant it, not as the reader paraphrased it.

## sentiment

Whether a message is pleased, displeased, or neither, judged from the customer's own words and recorded beside the message, so the mood of many messages can be counted.

## feedback loop

The circle of receiving a message, acting on it, and telling the customer what was done, so the customer knows they were heard and the business proves it listened.

## response time

The time between a message arriving and the business answering it, measured for every message and counted, because a late answer can undo a good one.

## feedback report

A written summary of the messages received in a period, what they were about, what was done, and what remains open, kept so the pattern can be seen over time.

## open item

A message that has been read but not yet answered or acted on, kept on the open list with its age counted, so nothing waits unnoticed.

## closed item

A message that has been answered and acted on, with the answer and the action written beside it, so the record shows the work is done.
