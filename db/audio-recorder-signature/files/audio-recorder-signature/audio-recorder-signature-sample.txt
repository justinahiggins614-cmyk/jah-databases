Audio Recorder Database (audio-recorder-signature)

An audio recorder captures sound and keeps it as a clip, in the browser: press Start, allow the microphone, and press Stop to save. The demo on this page records for real through the browser media API, and it is local-demo-only: a real microphone is needed, and no recording is ever faked. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
