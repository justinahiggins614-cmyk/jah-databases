The The Internet Courtroom Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The internet courtroom is a play courtroom where small everyday cases are heard as a game: the case of the missing biscuit, the case of the borrowed umbrella, each tried in plain words with a playful verdict. It is plainly labeled play: its verdicts are not legally recognized and it does not override any real court.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
