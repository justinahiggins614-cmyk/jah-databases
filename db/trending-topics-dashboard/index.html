A Morning Board of Three Sample Topics Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A dashboard website: one board shows the leading topics as cards, each card carrying a plain-word summary and a count of how the topic is grouped, not a live number from anywhere. Every entry on this site is a clearly labeled illustrative sample, written to show how a dashboard works, never a claim about what is actually trending in the real world, and the site makes no real-time claim of any kind. This record covers A Morning Board of Three Sample Topics.

A morning board of three sample topics gives a reader the morning's talk in three cards, no more and no less, so the board can be read in one sitting. Three cards give the board its shape, because each card gets a plain-word summary and the reader is never faced with a wall of names. Without a fixed count, boards are uneven: one morning brings one card and the next brings twenty, and a reader cannot tell whether the morning was quiet or the board was lazy.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a morning board of three sample topics runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

History

Boards that show what people are talking about are an old habit: a noticeboard of the day's talk, a newspaper's front page, a club's chalkboard. The website form grows out of that habit, adding cards, a plain-word summary for each, and a clear label on every sample entry. Three cards became the natural shape once readers compared boards and asked why one morning was longer than another.

Proposed solution

Name the topic board and what a visitor does in it; Write the rules in plain sentences a new visitor can follow; Give every topic board the same fair start: the same clues, the same time, the same scoring; Let a visitor try one topic board without signing in; Check the topic board works the same way on a phone and on a computer.

Dependencies

What must exist first: a board of topic cards, a plain-word summary for each card, a clear illustrative-sample label on every entry, and an archive of past boards. Nothing else is needed to open the first topic board.

Feasibility
