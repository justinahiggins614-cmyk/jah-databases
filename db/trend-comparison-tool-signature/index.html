Section 1: Description and history

The trend comparison tool puts two sample trends side by side on one screen, in plain words, so a reader can see how they differ, with every entry labeled as an illustrative sample, never as a claim about the real world. Comparing two things side by side is an old habit: two cloth samples on a counter, two apples cut open, two routes drawn on one map. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a side-by-side screen with plain-word summaries and a clear label on every sample entry.
