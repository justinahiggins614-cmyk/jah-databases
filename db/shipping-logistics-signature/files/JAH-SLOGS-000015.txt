History
Shipping is the carrying of goods by sea and by land, and logistics is the ordering of that carrying — which goods, which path, which handlers, and in what order. The merchant who sent goods by sea kept a ledger of every bale and every port; the railway age added the wagon and the timetable; the container age added the standard box and the port that could move a thousand boxes in a day. Through every age, the honest practice has been the same: write the shipment down whole, so that the goods can be followed from the sender to the receiver. This database is built on that public practice of recording every shipment true.
