Workflow

The worked solution has 5 steps, each of which can be checked on its own. Step 1: Give each robot a gripper that can hold a neighbour. Step 2: Let robots meet and dock when their sensors report a gap ahead. Step 3: Grow the structure until it spans the gap. Step 4: Cross the gap as one connected structure. Step 5: Split apart on the far side and resume separate work.
