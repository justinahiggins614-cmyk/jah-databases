# The Medical Equipment Inventory Database

The Medical Equipment Inventory Database is the medical equipment inventory record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full medical equipment inventory record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database inventory-warehouse-state, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
