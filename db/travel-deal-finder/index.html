Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A travel website: a visitor names a place and a date, and the finder shows travel offers in one plain list, each with the full price including fees, the length of the trip, and the number of stops, sorted cheapest first. Prices are estimates for comparison only, the finder never books a trip and never handles a payment, and every offer states its fees in plain words. This record covers A Travel Deal With the Full Price Shown.

A travel deal without the full price shown is a deal nobody can trust: the headline price looks small, and the fees appear only at the end. A full price shows every fee beside the headline, so the traveler compares true costs, not headlines. Without the full price, the cheapest deal on the list is often the most expensive at the end, and the traveler learns the truth only when paying. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
