1. Description

A budget planner plans money: income in, spending out, and what is left. Enter income and expenses by kind, and the page adds them up, shows what is left, and flags a shortfall.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Total income is the sum of all income lines. Total spending is the sum of all expense lines. Money left is income minus spending. The 50 30 20 rule splits income into 50 percent needs, 30 percent wants and 20 percent saving: income of 1,000 splits into 500, 300 and 200.

3. Science proof

A budget works because money spent without a plan is spent by habit, and habit spends more than the plan allows. Writing every expense by kind makes the spending visible, and a visible total can be compared with income. Saving first works because money moved before spending starts is never spent by accident.
