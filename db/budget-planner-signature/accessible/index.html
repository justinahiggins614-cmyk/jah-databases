The Budget Planner Database (Signature)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A budget planner plans money: income in, spending out, and what is left. Enter income and expenses by kind, and the page adds them up, shows what is left, and flags a shortfall.

  1. What a Budget Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A budget is a plan for money: what comes in, what goes out, and what is left. Income of 1,000 and spending of 800 leaves 200.

    Math proof: Total income is the sum of all income lines. Total spending is the sum of all expense lines. Money left is income minus spending. The 50 30 20 rule splits income into 50 percent needs, 30 percent wants and 20 percent saving: income of 1,000 splits into 500, 300 and 200.

    Science proof: A budget works because money spent without a plan is spent by habit, and habit spends more than the plan allows. Writing every expense by kind makes the spending visible, and a visible total can be compared with income. Saving first works because money moved before spending starts is never spent by accident.
