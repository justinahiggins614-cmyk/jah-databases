  1. A Neighborhood Soup Stall

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. Category: food. A small stall that serves one hot soup each day, made in the morning and sold until the pot is empty. Target customers: Hungry neighbors, workers on a lunch break, and families who want a hot meal without cooking. Startup needs: A soup pot, a table, bowls and spoons, a food hygiene certificate, and a regular pitch. Judge it by repeat visitors: if the same faces return each week, the soup and the price fit the street. This is a fictional catalog example, not a real listing, not a real price, and not a real transaction.

    Science proof: A soup stall works because a hot meal is a daily need, and a single dish keeps shopping, cooking and waste simple. That is why a catalog entry that names the customers, the needs and the test gives a reader a fair way to judge the idea.
