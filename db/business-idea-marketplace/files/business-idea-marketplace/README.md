# The Business Idea Marketplace Database — files (business-idea-marketplace)

- `demo-business-idea-marketplace.py` — runnable demo program (run with `python3 demo-business-idea-marketplace.py`).
- `business-idea-marketplace-sample.txt` — a plain-text sample of the database.
- The full database is `data/business-idea-marketplace/index.jsonl.gz` (24 records).
- The generator is `gen-business-idea-marketplace.js`; the generator website is `generator/`.
- The two AI specs are `ai/website-ai.json` and `ai/thing-ai.json`.
- Every idea is a fictional catalog example: no real listings, prices, sellers, buyers, or transactions.
