The Business Idea Marketplace Database (business-idea-marketplace)

A business idea marketplace catalog lists business ideas by category, each with a plain summary, its target customers, what it needs to start, and notes on how to judge it, so a reader can compare ideas side by side. Every idea here is a fictional catalog example, not a real listing, not a real price, and not a real transaction.

Worked example: A small stall that serves one hot soup each day, made in the morning and sold until the pot is empty. Target customers: Hungry neighbors, workers on a lunch break, and families who want a hot meal without cooking. Startup needs: A soup pot, a table, bowls and spoons, a food hygiene certificate, and a regular pitch. Judge it by repeat visitors: if the same faces return each week, the soup and the price fit the street. This is a fictional catalog example, not a real listing, not a real price, and not a real transaction.
