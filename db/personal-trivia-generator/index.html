Questions About a Favorite Season Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A personal trivia generator website: a visitor picks a generic kind of trivia, such as questions about a favorite season or questions about a household habit, and the site shows illustrative sample questions of that kind, each with a plain question, four plain answers, and the right answer marked. The site names no real person, copies no real quiz, and makes no claim that any sample question is about a real person. This record covers Questions About a Favorite Season.

Questions about a favorite season test what a household knows about the year: which season brings the first snowdrops, which month the leaves turn, and when the first frost comes. The generator must give a plain question, four plain answers, and the right answer marked, so a household can play together without arguing over the answer. Without a marked answer, every round ends in a dispute, and a disputed round is a poor game. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
