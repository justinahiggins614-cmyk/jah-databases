House Floor Plan Generator Database (house-floor-plan-generator)

A house floor plan generator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy plans, and counts the marks. The generator reads 3 plans. The plan alpha holds 27 marks. The largest holds 27 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
