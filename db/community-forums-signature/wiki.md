# The Community Forums Database

The Community Forums Database is the community forums record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full community forums record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database software-forum-software, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
