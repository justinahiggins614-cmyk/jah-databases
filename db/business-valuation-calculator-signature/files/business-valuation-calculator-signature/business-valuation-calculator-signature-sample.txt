Business Valuation Calculator Database (business-valuation-calculator-signature)

A business valuation calculator is a rule-based local demo: it values a set toy business from set toy numbers, and counts the value. The business earns 500000. The revenue is 500000. The profit is 200000. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
