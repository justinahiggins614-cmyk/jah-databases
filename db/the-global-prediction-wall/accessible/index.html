The The Global Prediction Wall Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The global prediction wall is a wall of playful guesses about tomorrow: visitors pin a prediction, such as what the weather will feel like or which team will win a friendly match, and the wall keeps score when the day arrives. Every prediction is play, never presented as a real forecast, and no prediction is ever presented as a claim about the future.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/the-global-prediction-wall/index.jsonl.gz
