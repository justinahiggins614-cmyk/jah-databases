Section 1: Description and history

The monster creator helps a storyteller invent a monster of their own, in plain words, with the shape, the home, and the habits written out first. Matching by plain words needs a system that understands what the plain words mean for a monster, and turns those plain words into a fitting answer, such as a moss-backed gentle giant.
