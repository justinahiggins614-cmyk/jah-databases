Flashcard Study System Database (flashcard-study-system)

A flashcard study system runs spaced-repetition-style study sessions locally: type the cards, press Study, and read the plan. 16 right out of 20 is 80.0 percent. Intervals double: 1, 2 and 4 days. 12 of 20 cards mastered: 60.0 percent. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
