Chemical Equation Balancer Database (chemical-equation-balancer)

A chemical equation balancer balances equations with real stoichiometry: type an equation, press Balance, and read the balanced form. 2 H2 + O2 gives 2 H2O: balanced, 4 hydrogen and 2 oxygen each side. N2 + 3 H2 gives 2 NH3: 2 nitrogen and 6 hydrogen each side. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: H2 + O2 gives H2O: unbalanced, 2 oxygen on the left and 1 on the right
