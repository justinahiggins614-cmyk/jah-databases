The Chemical Equation Balancer Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A chemical equation balancer balances equations with real stoichiometry: type an equation, press Balance, and read the balanced form. 2 H2 + O2 gives 2 H2O: balanced, 4 hydrogen and 2 oxygen each side. N2 + 3 H2 gives 2 NH3: 2 nitrogen and 6 hydrogen each side. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

  1. What a Chemical Equation Balancer Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A chemical equation balancer counts atoms on both sides of an equation. H2 + O2 gives H2O: unbalanced, 2 oxygen on the left and 1 on the right

    Math proof: 2 H2 + O2 gives 2 H2O: balanced, 4 hydrogen and 2 oxygen each side. 4 Fe + 3 O2 gives 2 Fe2O3: 4 iron each side. CH4 + 2 O2 gives CO2 + 2 H2O: balanced, 1 carbon each side. C6H12O6 has 6 carbon, 12 hydrogen and 6 oxygen: 24 atoms.

    Science proof: A chemical equation balancer works because atoms are never made or lost, and each side is counted: 2 KClO3 gives 2 KCl + 3 O2: 6 oxygen each side, and H2SO4 + 2 NaOH gives Na2SO4 + 2 H2O: 2 sodium each side. 2 moles of H2 need 1 mole of O2: ratio 2 to 1. That is why the local demo shows the counts and the balanced form together, and runs 0 equations on a server.
