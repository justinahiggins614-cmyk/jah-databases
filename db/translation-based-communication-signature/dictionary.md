# Dictionary entries — The Translation Based Communication Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## translation

Carrying a message from one language into another, keeping its meaning and, where possible, its tone.

## source language

The language a message was first written or spoken in, named beside every message so the reader knows where it began.

## target language

The language a message is carried into, named beside the translation so the reader knows what they are reading.

## interpreter

A person who carries spoken words from one language into another as they are spoken, in the moment, without a written text.

## translator

A person who carries written words from one language into another, with time to read, check, and revise.

## faithfulness

How closely a translation keeps the meaning of the original, judged by reading both side by side.

## gap

A place where a meaning does not cross between languages, named plainly in the translation instead of being filled with a guess.

## tone

The feeling carried by a message, such as formal or friendly, which the translation must keep where the language allows.

## glossary

A shared list of words and their agreed translations, kept so that the same word is always translated the same way.

## review

Reading a translation beside its original, by a second person, to find where the meaning slipped or the tone broke.

## conversation

A back-and-forth exchange between people, where each message is translated in turn, so the talk can flow both ways.

## translation log

The written record of every message translated, naming the languages, the translator, and the review, kept in order.
