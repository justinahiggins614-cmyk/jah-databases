2. Math proof

Counting the votes gives 10 votes in all: white 5, brown 3, rye 2. White has 5 of 10 votes: 50 percent. The winner is white, with 5 votes of 10. Checking a poll: 5 + 3 + 2 is 10, matching the total 10. 10 voters cast 10 votes: 1 vote each.

3. Science proof

A polling system works because a poll is a question with choices, and each vote adds one to a choice: 5 + 3 + 2 is 10 votes in all, and 5 of 10 is 50 percent. The winner is the choice with the most votes. That is why the local demo shows the votes and the percent together, and says plainly that its votes are simulated and real voting is BLOCKED.
