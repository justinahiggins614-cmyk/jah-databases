History
People have flown for barely a century, and from the first scheduled flights the seat had to be written down: who was flying, on which flight, and at what fare. From the paper ticket kept in a drawer to the booking record kept in a database, the craft has been the same — record the passenger, the flight, and the fare together, so that the seat can be proved and the journey can be followed. A flight booking is that record: a specification of how bookings are kept, never a live booking and never a payment. This database is built on that public practice of recording every flight booking true, in full.
