1. Description and History

Room redesign from photos lets a person see their own room changed: a different wall color, furniture moved, a new sofa, better light — all previewed on a photo of the real room. This database designs that redesign one record at a time, from one wall to a whole home, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.

Redesigning a room once meant sketches, samples, and moving heavy furniture to see if it worked. Cameras and software later made it possible to preview changes on a photo of the room: recolor a wall, move a sofa, try new flooring, all before lifting anything. The design in this database follows that path, keeps the room's real dimensions honest, and estimates the cost of each change.

Every record here is a design, never a real product. Public records are fact-checked, real, and legal-only; Signature records are the Signature original version of the same subject; speculative material is labeled design-study, never as fact. Photo tools work on the user's own photos only, never anyone else's private images.
