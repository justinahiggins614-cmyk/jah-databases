Section 1: Description and history

The popular question tracker is a board that shows which questions many people are asking, in plain words, with every entry labeled as an illustrative sample, never as a claim about real questions asked anywhere. Lists of questions people are asking are an old habit: a teacher's list of questions from the class, a newspaper's letters page, a noticeboard of the week's queries. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a board of questions with plain-word answer sketches and a clear label on every sample entry. Every record below carries its own history section, so the description and the history of each design are told together, in full sentences and paragraphs, never as stubs.

Section 2: Math proof

Every record carries a worked arithmetic proof: a count multiplied by a per-item amount gives a total, and the demo program prints that same total, so a fresh run of the demo verifies the proof exactly.
