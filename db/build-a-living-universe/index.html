The index solves this by listing every record in ladder order, with its place in the ladder, so a reader can start at the beginning and climb, or jump straight to the record they need.

Worked example: a reader who wants the simplest starting point opens record 1, A Quiet Star and Its First World, and a reader who wants the hardest problem opens record 23, A Yearly Showcase of Living Universes.

History

Imagining whole worlds is an old habit: a map of a made-up land, a tale of a distant star, a story of creatures that exist only in words. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a plain way to shape an imaginary universe and tell its story. Field guides and indexes are as old as books themselves: a table of contents that tells a reader where everything lives. This index follows that habit, listing the twenty-three content records of this database in the order a reader should meet them.
