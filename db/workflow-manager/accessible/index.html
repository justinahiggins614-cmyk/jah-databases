The Workflow Manager Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A workflow manager moves tasks through steps, from start to done. Add tasks, move them forward a step at a time, count them by step, and watch the report. Everything runs in this browser and saves locally; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/workflow-manager/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Workflow Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A workflow is a list of steps that a task moves through, from start to done. A workflow of 4 steps moves a task from start to done.

    Math proof: A workflow is a list of steps, and a task sits at one step at a time. With 5 tasks split 2, 2 and 1 across three steps, the counts add to 5. A report is a count: 5 tasks, 3 done, 2 still moving. Adding a step makes a workflow of 4 steps into 5, and removing one makes 5 into 4.

    Science proof: A workflow works because work is easier in small named stages: each step has one job, and a task moves only when its step is finished. Counting tasks by step shows where work piles up, which is why a manager watches the counts rather than guessing. That is why the same steps, used for every task, keep the work steady.
