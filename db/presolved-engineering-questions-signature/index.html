A simply supported beam of span 8 m carries a central point load of 12 kN. Find the maximum bending moment in the beam.

A cantilever beam 1.5 m long carries a tip load of 2,000 N. The beam's flexural rigidity is EI with E = 200 GPa and I = 1.2 × 10⁻⁵ m⁴. Find the tip deflection.

The Worked Solution

The solution proceeds in the following steps. Step 1: The tip deflection of a cantilever under a tip load is δ = PL³/3EI. Step 2: Numerator: P × L³ = 2,000 × 1.5³ = 2,000 × 3.375 = 6,750 N·m³. That completes the worked solution; the answer is 0.00094 m (0.94 mm).
