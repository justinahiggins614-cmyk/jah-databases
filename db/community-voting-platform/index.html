A proposal written in plain words is a proposal every member can judge, because a proposal full of long words hides what it really asks for. The proposal should name the question, the choices, and what will happen after the vote, all in sentences a new member can follow. Without plain words, the vote is decided by whoever can read the jargon, and the members who cannot are left out of their own community.
