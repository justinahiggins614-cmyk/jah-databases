Heart Rate Tracker Database (heart-rate-tracker-signature)

A heart rate tracker is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy readings, and counts the beats. The tracker reads 3 readings. The reading morning shows 72 beats. The largest holds 78 beats. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
