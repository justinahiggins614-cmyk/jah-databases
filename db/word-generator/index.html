Capabilities

Capability 1
Word mixing: blends two or more words by shared sounds, syllables, or meaning.
Capability 2
Pattern extraction: learns prefix/suffix/syllable patterns from word lists.
Capability 3
Pattern application: applies a pattern to new roots to coin consistent new words.
Capability 4
Compound building: joins words into compounds with meaning carried through.
Capability 5
Portmanteau crafting: fuses words at natural sound boundaries.
Capability 6
Description-to-program: turns a word description ("a counter") into a runnable program.
Capability 7
Pronunciation notes: renders phonetic-style pronunciation for coined words.
Capability 8
Name safety check: flags coins that collide with real product or trademark names.

Boundless generator

Generator script: gen-word-generator.js — run node gen-word-generator.js --n 5. Self-regulating: throttled, duplicate-checked, stops at 1015 or when complete. Generated output is labeled "generated" and never mixed with assistant-built records.
