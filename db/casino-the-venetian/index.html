The Venetian Resort Las Vegas — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. The Venetian Resort Las Vegas is a luxury all-suite hotel and casino on the Las Vegas Strip, opened on May 3, 1999, by Sheldon Adelson's Las Vegas Sands. Together with its sister tower The Palazzo, the complex holds about 7,090 suites, among the most on the Strip. Signature features include indoor and outdoor gondola rides, the Grand Canal Shoppes, and St. Mark's Square recreations. The property is now operated by Apollo Global Management affiliates with real estate owned by VICI Properties.
