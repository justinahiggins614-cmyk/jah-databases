# The Patent Filing Assistance Database

The Patent Filing Assistance Database is the patent filing assistance record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full patent filing assistance record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database form-filling-assistance, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
