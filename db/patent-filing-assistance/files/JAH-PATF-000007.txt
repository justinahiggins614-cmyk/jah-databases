History
Inventors have sought patents since the first letters patent were sealed, so that a new machine or process could be named, described, and protected for a time. Filing assistance grew beside the patent offices: clerks and advisers who read the published requirements aloud, named the right papers, and wrote down what was filed and when. From the paper specification tied with ribbon to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the help down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every patent filing assistance true. It is guidance about published requirements only, and it files nothing on any person behalf.
