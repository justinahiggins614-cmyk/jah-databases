Overview

R is a functional, procedural programming language with dynamic typing, created in 1993 by Ross Ihaka and Robert Gentleman. It is used for statistics and data analysis, and is known as the lingua franca of statisticians. Its file extension is .r and its hello world is print("Hello, world!").
