1. One Photograph, One Guess on the Map

JAH-GGG-000001 — simple

The geography guessing game shows one photograph of a place and asks the player to guess where it is on the map: clues a player can read in the landscape, scoring that rewards a close guess honestly, and a daily challenge everyone plays together. A photograph of a place, shown without a name, is a puzzle most players cannot solve by memory alone: the player must read the landscape, the light, the plants, the signs, and the style of the buildings, and guess where on earth the photograph was taken. A game that shows one photograph, lets the player place one guess on the map, and scores the guess honestly by distance, turns looking into playing: the player studies the photograph, places a guess, and learns how close they were. The photograph is the clue, the map is the answer, and the distance is the score. Worked example: a photograph of a city crossed by a wide river, with a tall iron tower, is guessed as Paris, and the score is perfect, with the distance zero.
