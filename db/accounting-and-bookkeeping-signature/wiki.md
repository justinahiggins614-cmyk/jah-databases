# The Accounting and Bookkeeping Database

The Accounting and Bookkeeping Database is the accounting and bookkeeping record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full accounting and bookkeeping record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database bookkeeping, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
