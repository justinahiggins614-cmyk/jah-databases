# Dictionary entries — The Universal Service Status Page Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## status page

One public board that says whether each service is working, in green, amber, or red, with its history beside it.

## green

The color that means a service is working as it should, with nothing wrong to report.

## amber

The color that means a service is degraded: working, but slower or weaker than it should be.

## red

The color that means a service is down: not working at all, with the reason named beside it.

## uptime

The share of time a service was working, counted over days or months, shown as a number beside the service.

## incident

An event that harms a service, named on the status page with its start time and, when known, its cause.

## maintenance

Planned work that will pause or weaken a service, announced on the status page before it starts.

## subscriber

A person who asks to be told when a service changes, by message, and who can stop the messages at any time.

## history

The past record of a service's status, shown day by day, so that anyone can see how often it failed.

## degraded

Working, but slower or weaker than it should be, which is worse than down in one way: it is easy to miss.

## outage

A time when a service is down, counted from the first report to the return, and written into the history.

## ledger

The status page's own written record of every change, every incident, and every return, kept in order.
