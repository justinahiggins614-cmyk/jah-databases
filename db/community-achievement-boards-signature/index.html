Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An achievement board website: a public board lists members by a chosen name or pen name, never by private data, and shows the badges each member has earned for finished tasks, challenges, and streaks. A weekly challenge gives everyone the same task, a streak rewards a member for coming back, and a season board with rounds and divisions gives the year its shape. The board is fair play only, with a review step before any badge is published, and every claim on the board can be checked. This record covers A Board That Shows Member Progress in Plain Sight.

A community where nobody can see what anyone has done feels quiet and still, because good work happens in corners and nobody knows to praise it. A board that shows member progress in plain sight changes that: every finished task, every badge, every streak is written where any member can read it, under the name or pen name the member chose. Without a public board, members work alone, new members cannot see what is possible, and the community has no shared story of its own progress to tell.
