DNA Sequence Analyzer Database (dna-sequence-analyzer)

A DNA sequence analyzer reads teaching sequences you paste in: paste a sequence, press Analyze, and read its bases, GC content and translation. Design-study only: it holds no pathogen sequences. The sequence ATGCGTACGTAGCTAGCTAA has 20 bases. GC content is 45.0 percent. ATG codes for M and GCT codes for A: ATGGCT translates to MA. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
