The Ratio Calculator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A ratio calculator works out ratios: simplify them, scale them, share a total by them, and read them as decimals, percents and unit rates. Type two numbers, and it shows the simplified ratio with its steps. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

  1. What a Ratio Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A ratio compares two numbers, part to part. A ratio of 3 to 4 has 7 parts in all

    Math proof: A ratio of 3 to 4 has 7 parts. 6:8 simplifies to 3:4. 3/4 as a decimal is 0.75, and 3 out of 4 is 75 percent. Sharing 100 in the ratio 3:2 gives 60 and 40. 120 miles in 2 hours is 60 miles an hour. A picture 1920 by 1080 has aspect ratio 16:9.

    Science proof: A ratio works because two numbers in proportion stay in proportion when both are scaled: scaling 1:2 by 3 gives 3:6, and scaling 10:15 down by 5 gives 2:3. Sharing 100 in the ratio 2:3:5 gives 20, 30 and 50, and the shares add to the whole. That is why the calculator shows the simplified ratio first, then the answer.
