1. Description

A ratio calculator works out ratios: simplify them, scale them, share a total by them, and read them as decimals, percents and unit rates. Type two numbers, and it shows the simplified ratio with its steps. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A ratio of 3 to 4 has 7 parts. 6:8 simplifies to 3:4. 3/4 as a decimal is 0.75, and 3 out of 4 is 75 percent. Sharing 100 in the ratio 3:2 gives 60 and 40. 120 miles in 2 hours is 60 miles an hour. A picture 1920 by 1080 has aspect ratio 16:9.

3. Science proof

A ratio works because two numbers in proportion stay in proportion when both are scaled: scaling 1:2 by 3 gives 3:6, and scaling 10:15 down by 5 gives 2:3. Sharing 100 in the ratio 2:3:5 gives 20, 30 and 50, and the shares add to the whole. That is why the calculator shows the simplified ratio first, then the answer.
