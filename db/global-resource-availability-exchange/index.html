An availability exchange website: a visitor picks a kind of resource, such as timber or wool, and the exchange shows the listing in plain words, how much is spare, where it rests, and how a neighbor asks for it. Spare timber, cut and stacked, listed in plain words with how much is spare and who may ask for it. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything.
