Designing a Character With One Clear Trait Rank 1 · simple

Feasibility: research-stage Public research and working examples exist in this area, but no complete system at the needed scale exists yet; the gap is named on the record.

The virtual personality creator is a tool for designing fictional characters with a clear voice and manner, who stay in character, admit they are fictional, and admit what they do not know.

This record covers designing a character with one clear trait in the virtual personality creator. The starting point is the voice: getting that part right is what makes the whole thing work, because a virtual personality creator that is sloppy about voice is sloppy everywhere else too. Without a careful approach, the result is easy to get wrong: the work looks finished while the fair part, the honest part, or the kind part is missing, and nobody notices until someone is treated unfairly or a newcomer cannot follow what happened. That matters because the virtual personality creator is meant to be used by everyone, not only by experts, and a tool or contest that only experts can trust is a tool that has failed its own purpose. A good version of designing a character with one clear trait is plain to describe: it states its rules in public, it shows its working, it credits the people who made it, and it can be checked by any reader who follows the steps on this record.

History

Fictional characters grew out of novels, plays, and puppet shows, where a made-up person could teach, amuse, and ask good questions; the creator turns that craft into a tool for writing characters who can hold a conversation. The part of the story this record covers, designing a character with one clear trait, grew out of that habit: people tried it informally first, learned what went wrong when it was done carelessly, and then wrote the careful version down so the next person did not have to learn it the hard way.

Proposed solution

Dependencies
