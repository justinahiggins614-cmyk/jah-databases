Metaverse Environment Creator Database (metaverse-environment-creator)

A metaverse environment creator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy worlds, and counts the marks. The creator reads 3 worlds. The world alpha holds 24 marks. The largest holds 24 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
