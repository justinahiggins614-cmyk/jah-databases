Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Doctors Without Borders (Medecins Sans Frontieres, MSF) is an international medical humanitarian organization; its US office is headquartered in New York. Founded in 1971 in France, MSF provides emergency medical care in conflict zones, epidemics, and disasters, operating with independence and neutrality. It was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 1999.
