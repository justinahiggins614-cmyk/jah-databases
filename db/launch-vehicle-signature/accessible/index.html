Signature Two-Stage Orbital Rocket

JAH-LVEHS-000001 · Signature two-stage orbital rocket

Math proof: The Tsiolkovsky equation governs both stages: the speed gain depends on the logarithm of the mass ratio, and the two stages add their changes together to reach the 9,400 metres per second of low Earth orbit. The formal statement is: Tsiolkovsky rocket equation: delta-v = Isp * g0 * ln(m0 / mf) delta-v is the change in velocity in metres per second, Isp is the specific impulse in seconds, g0 is standard gravity, 9.80665 metres per second squared, ln is the natural logarithm, m0 is the starting mass including propellant, and mf is the final mass after the propellant is spent.

Formal equation: Tsiolkovsky rocket equation: delta-v = Isp * g0 * ln(m0 / mf)
