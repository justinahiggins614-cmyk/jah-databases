1. Description

A color picker picks a colour and shows its numbers: pick a colour, press Pick, and read the hex code, the three numbers, the complement and the contrast ratios. The colour #FF0000 is [255, 0, 0]: 3 numbers. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

2. Math proof

The colour #FF0000 is [255, 0, 0]: 3 numbers. The complement of red #FF0000 is #00FFFF. Black on white has a contrast ratio of 21 to 1. Red on white has a contrast ratio of 4.0 to 1. Black on yellow has a contrast ratio of 19.6 to 1.

3. Science proof

A color picker works because a colour is three numbers and a place on the wheel: red sits at 0 degrees, green sits at 120 degrees and blue sits at 240 degrees. Mixing red and yellow halfway gives [255, 128, 0]. That is why the local demo shows the colour and its numbers together, and runs 0 picks on a server.

def lum(r, g, b):
    def ch(c):
        c = c / 255.0
        return c / 12.92 if c <= 0.04045 else ((c + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4
