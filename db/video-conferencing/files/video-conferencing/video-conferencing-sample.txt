Video Conferencing Database (video-conferencing)

Video conferencing lets people in different places talk face to face on screen. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it previews your camera where the browser allows it and shows simulated participants, with no server and no real call. A real multi-party call needs a server, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is recorded and nothing is uploaded.

Worked example: A call with 3 people shows 3 pictures, 1 per person.
