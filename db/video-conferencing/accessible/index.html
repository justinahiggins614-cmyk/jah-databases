The Video Conferencing Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. Video conferencing lets people in different places talk face to face on screen. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it previews your camera where the browser allows it and shows simulated participants, with no server and no real call. A real multi-party call needs a server, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is recorded and nothing is uploaded.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/video-conferencing/index.jsonl.gz

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. Video conferencing lets people in different places talk face to face on screen, each showing one picture. A call with 3 people shows 3 pictures, 1 per person.
