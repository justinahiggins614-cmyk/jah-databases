Interactive Textbook Database (interactive-textbook)

An interactive textbook is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy chapters, and counts the pages and the questions. The textbook reads 3 chapters. The chapter intro holds 40 pages. The largest holds 45 pages. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
