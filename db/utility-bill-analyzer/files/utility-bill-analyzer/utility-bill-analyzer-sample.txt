Utility Bill Analyzer Database (utility-bill-analyzer)

A utility bill analyzer is a rule-based local demo: it analyzes set toy bills, and counts the marks. The analyzer reads 3 bills. The bill alpha holds 144 marks. The largest holds 144 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
