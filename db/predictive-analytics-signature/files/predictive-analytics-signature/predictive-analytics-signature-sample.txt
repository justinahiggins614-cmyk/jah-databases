Predictive Analytics Database (predictive-analytics-signature)

A predictive analytics is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy forecasts, and counts the marks. The analytics reads 3 forecasts. The forecast sunny holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
