  1. What a Writing Assistant Does

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A writing assistant helps draft, fix and polish text, while the writer keeps every decision. A draft of 200 words is polished to 180 clearer words.

    Math proof: Word count is a sum: the number of words in a text. Reading ease grows as sentences shorten: average sentence length is words divided by sentences. Cutting a 120 word paragraph to 88 words removes 32 words, which is 32 over 120, about 27 percent.

    Science proof: A writing assistant works because drafting and editing use different thinking: drafting is fast and messy, editing is slow and careful. Splitting the two lets the writer produce words first and judge them second. A summary written first keeps the draft on topic, because every later sentence can be checked against it.
