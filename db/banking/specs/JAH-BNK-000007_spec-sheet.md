## Spec
- **Mechanism:** Fedwire is the Federal Reserve's real-time gross settlement system, in which each wire settles individually and immediately. Domestic wires usually complete within hours, are effectively final, and cost roughly 25 to 50 dollars. This overview edition presents Fedwire as a core payments concept in modern banking.
