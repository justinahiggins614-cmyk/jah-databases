## Spec
- **Mechanism:** FDIC insurance covers 250000 dollars per depositor, per insured bank, for each account ownership category, backed by the full faith and credit of the United States government. Ownership categories include single, joint, certain retirement, trust, employee benefit plan, corporate, and government accounts. This overview edition presents FDIC Deposit Insurance as a core regulation concept in modern banking.
