Employee Onboarding Database (employee-onboarding-signature)

An employee onboarding is a rule-based local demo: it starts set toy steps, and counts the marks. The onboarding reads 3 steps. The step ada holds 24 marks. The largest holds 24 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
