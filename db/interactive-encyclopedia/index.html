An interactive encyclopedia website: each entry answers in plain words, links to its neighbors, shows its sources, and carries a timeline and a map beside it. An entry that answers in plain words must give the reader the answer in the first paragraph, without jargon and without a long introduction. The problem is that many entries begin with the history of the topic and only reach the answer at the end, so a reader in a hurry leaves before finding it. This record puts the answer first, the detail after, and the sources at the end, so every reader gets what they came for in the first few lines.
