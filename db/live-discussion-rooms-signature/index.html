A Room That Opens at a Stated Time Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A live discussion website: a room opens at a stated time with a written topic and a named host, members join and talk in the open, and the room keeps a public log so anyone who missed it can read what was said. There are no private messages anywhere on the site, every room has written rules, and every moderation action is written in a public log. This record covers A Room That Opens at a Stated Time.

A room that opens at a stated time is a room people can plan for, because a member who knows the hour can arrive on time, and a member who does not know it will miss the talk. The room should name its day, its hour, and its topic in advance, and open on time. Without a stated time, the room opens when someone remembers, and the members who came on time find an empty room. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
