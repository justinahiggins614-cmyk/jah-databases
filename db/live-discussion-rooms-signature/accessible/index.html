  1. A Room That Opens at a Stated Time

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Live discussion rooms are public rooms where people talk together at the same time, with a host, a written topic, and a public log, so a good conversation can be joined or read later. A room that opens at a stated time is a room people can plan for, because a member who knows the hour can arrive on time, and a member who does not know it will miss the talk. The room should name its day, its hour, and its topic in advance, and open on time. Without a stated time, the room opens when someone remembers, and the members who came on time find an empty room. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
