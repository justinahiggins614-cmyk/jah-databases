# The Recruitment and Hiring Database

The Recruitment and Hiring Database is the recruitment and hiring record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full recruitment and hiring record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database e2-sig-biz-hiring-first-employee, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
