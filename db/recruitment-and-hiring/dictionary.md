# Dictionary entries — The Recruitment and Hiring Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## role

A job to be filled, written down with its duties, its skills, and its pay, so that every applicant is judged against the same words.

## applicant

A person who has asked for a role, named in the record with the date they applied and the papers they sent.

## application

The papers an applicant sends, such as a resume and a letter, kept beside the role they applied for, in the order they arrived.

## criteria

The named measures every applicant is judged by, written before any application is read, so the judgement is the same for all.

## interview

A meeting where an applicant is asked about their work, with the same questions asked of every applicant for the role.

## shortlist

The named set of applicants chosen for interviews, written down with the reasons each one was chosen.

## offer

A written invitation to take a role, naming the duties, the pay, and the start date, given to the chosen applicant.

## rejection

A written note to an applicant who was not chosen, naming that the role was filled, kept with the record of the hire.

## onboarding

The work of welcoming a new hire, showing them their duties, their colleagues, and their tools, in their first days.

## reference

A person named by an applicant who can speak about their past work, asked only with the applicant's say.

## hiring record

The kept record of a whole hire, from the written role to the offer, naming every applicant, every interview, and every decision.

## hiring panel

The named people who judge the applicants for a role together, each writing their judgement beside the same criteria.
