History
Finding the right person for a job has always been a matter of saying plainly what the job needs, asking fairly, and choosing on the merits. Recruitment is the work of writing the role, inviting applicants, reading them against the same criteria, and keeping a record of every step, so that the choice can be explained to anyone who asks.
