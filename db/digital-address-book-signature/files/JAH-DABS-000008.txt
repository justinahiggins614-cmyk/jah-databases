History
The address book began as a small paper book beside the telephone, holding the names and numbers of the people its owner needed to reach. As the book went digital, it learned to hold more: many addresses per person, notes, birthdays, and groups, and to be searched in an instant. The book's job never changed: one name, one entry, kept true, so that the right person can always be reached.
