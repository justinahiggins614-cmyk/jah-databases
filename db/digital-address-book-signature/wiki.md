# The Digital Address Book Database

The Digital Address Book Database is the digital address book record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full digital address book record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database books, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
