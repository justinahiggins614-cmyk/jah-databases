UNOS Waiting List

The national organ waiting list allocates by medical urgency, blood type, and waiting time — not wealth or fame. Over 100,000 Americans wait. The list is organ transplantation's conscience.

def meld_score(bilirubin, inr, creatinine, dialysis=False):
    """MELD liver-transplant priority estimator (simplified)."""
    import math
    cr = 4.0 if dialysis else max(creatinine, 1.0)
    m = 3.78*math.log(max(bilirubin,1)) + 11.2*math.log(max(inr,1)) + 9.57*math.log(cr) + 6.43
    return f"MELD ~{round(m)} (higher = higher liver transplant priority)"
print(meld_score(3.2, 1.8, 1.4))

Immunosuppression

Anti-rejection drugs — tacrolimus, mycophenolate, steroids — suppress immunity enough to keep the organ, inviting infection and cancer risk. Levels are monitored obsessively. The balance is lifelong.
