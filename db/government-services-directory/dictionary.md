# Dictionary entries — The Government Services Directory Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## government services directory

The recorded list of the services a government provides, naming each service, the office that gives it, and the terms, so that any service can be found and read.

## service entry

One named service in the directory, with its office, its hours, and its terms, kept so that the service can be read as a whole.

## department listing

The written list of the departments of a government, naming each one and the services it gives, so that the shape of the government can be read.

## office address

The written place where a government service is given, naming the building, the street, and the town, so that the service can be found.

## opening hours

The written hours during which a government office is open, kept with the service so that the times for visiting can be read plainly.

## contact record

The written account of how to reach a government office, naming the telephone, the address, and the counter, so that the office can be reached.

## service category

One of the named kinds of government service, such as health, housing, or tax, written in the directory so that the services can be found by kind.

## eligibility note

The written note naming who a government service is for, kept with the service so that the conditions can be read beside the service.

## application form

The printed or on-screen sheet on which a government service is asked for, with boxes for the name and the details, kept as a copy so that what was asked can be read again.

## fee schedule

The written list of the fees charged for government services, naming each service and its fee, so that the cost can be read plainly.

## accessibility note

The written note naming how a government office can be reached by a person with limited mobility, kept with the service so that the access can be read beside the service.

## service update

The written notice of a change to a government service, naming the service, the change, and the date, so that the directory can be kept true.
