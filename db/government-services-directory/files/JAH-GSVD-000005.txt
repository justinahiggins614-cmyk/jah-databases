History
Governments have published lists of their services since the first towns posted notices of the market, the court, and the tax office on the church door, first in printed directories of offices and hours, then in booklets of forms and fees, and now in directories that hold the department, the office, the contact, and the terms side by side. A government services directory is the map of what a government does for its people and where each service can be found. From the printed directory kept in the library to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the services down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every government services directory record true.
