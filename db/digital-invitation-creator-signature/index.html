A Garden Party Invitation Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An invitation creator website: a visitor picks a generic kind of gathering, such as a garden party, a birthday tea, or a village fair visit, and the creator shows how the invitation is written: the day, the time, the place, and a plain way to answer. The creator names no real person, copies no real invitation, and every example is labeled as an illustration of the method. This record covers A Garden Party Invitation.

A garden party invitation gives a person a plain way to invite friends to a garden, with the day, the time, and the place written out, and a plain way to answer. The creator must show the invitation in plain words, so every friend who reads it understands the day, the time, and what to bring. Without those plain facts, a friend cannot tell when the party is or where to go, and an invitation without a day is a poor way to gather friends.

Worked example, Signature design: the same invitation is rebuilt from the Signature pattern, so a garden party invitation runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
