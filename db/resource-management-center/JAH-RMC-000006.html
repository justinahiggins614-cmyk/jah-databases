Description

This record covers Orderly lines for waiting work in the Resource Management Center Database. A work queue is an orderly line of jobs waiting their turn, each with its place, its priority, and its stated needs. Job scheduling takes jobs from the queue in a stated order, starts them when resources are free, and records the start and finish of each. The center keeps the queue visible, so a job that waits too long is seen, not forgotten.
