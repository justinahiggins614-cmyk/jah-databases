The Digital Fireworks Laboratory Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The digital fireworks laboratory is a simulation only: a night sky on a screen where bursts of light bloom, fade, and bloom again, and a visitor can plan a show, pick its colours, and watch it unfold. Every record is a clearly labeled simulation, never a claim about real fireworks or real explosives, and it contains no instructions for making anything that burns.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
