# Dictionary entries — The Visa Requirement Lookup Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## visa

A permission written in a passport or travel document by the country to be visited, stating that the traveller may enter under stated terms, recorded as a fact about the requirement, never as advice.

## visa requirement

The published rule of a country about whether a traveller of a named nationality needs a visa, stated with its source and its date, so that the rule can be checked against the source.

## visa policy

The full set of a country's published rules about visas, kept beside the lookup, so that one requirement can be read against the whole policy it comes from.

## passport

A travel document issued by a country to its own nationals, proving identity and nationality, named in the lookup by the issuing country, so that whose passport is meant can be read.

## nationality

The country whose national a traveller is, written in the lookup, so that the requirement stated is the requirement for that nationality and no other.

## destination country

The country a traveller means to visit, written in the lookup, so that the requirement stated is the requirement of that country and no other.

## lookup

One written question about a visa requirement, with the traveller's nationality, the destination country, and the stated answer, kept as a single record.

## entry

The act of passing into a country, named in the lookup with the terms the published rule states, so that what entry the rule allows can be read plainly.

## stay length

The number of days a published visa rule allows a traveller to remain, written in the lookup, so that the length stated can be checked against the source.

## consulate

The office of one country in another country that issues visas and states requirements, named in the lookup when it is the source, so that where the rule came from can be read.

## immigration

The movement of people into a country and the rules that govern it, named in the lookup as the subject the requirement belongs to, so that the rule can be placed in its field.

## lookup date

The date on which a visa lookup was made, written in the record, so that how old the stated requirement is can be read, because published rules change.
