A Creation Queue Open to Every Visitor is one record in this database, and it asks for a design a visitor can try in one sitting, with the steps written out, the cost named plainly, and the rules stated so everyone gets the same fair start. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.
