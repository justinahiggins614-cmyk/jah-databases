The Template Generator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A template generator fills templates: text with placeholders such as {name}, filled with values to make a finished letter, invoice or resume. Type a template, fill in the values, and press Fill. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/template-generator/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Template Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A template is text with placeholders, waiting for its values. 'Dear {name},' with name Ana becomes 'Dear Ana,'

    Math proof: One placeholder used 3 times gives 'Hello Ana, Ana, Ana!'. An invoice for 3 chairs at 20 each totals 60. A long template of 24 words fills to 27 words with 4 placeholders. One template reused for 5 letters. Checking a template: 2 of 3 placeholders filled, 1 missing.

    Science proof: A template works because a placeholder is a named hole in a text, and filling it replaces every copy with the same value: one placeholder used 3 times gives the same name 3 times. A missing placeholder stays visible, so it can be fixed. That is why the generator shows the filled text beside the template, so every replacement can be checked.
