1. Description

A template generator fills templates: text with placeholders such as {name}, filled with values to make a finished letter, invoice or resume. Type a template, fill in the values, and press Fill. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

One placeholder used 3 times gives 'Hello Ana, Ana, Ana!'. An invoice for 3 chairs at 20 each totals 60. A long template of 24 words fills to 27 words with 4 placeholders. One template reused for 5 letters. Checking a template: 2 of 3 placeholders filled, 1 missing.

3. Science proof

A template works because a placeholder is a named hole in a text, and filling it replaces every copy with the same value: one placeholder used 3 times gives the same name 3 times. A missing placeholder stays visible, so it can be fixed. That is why the generator shows the filled text beside the template, so every replacement can be checked.
