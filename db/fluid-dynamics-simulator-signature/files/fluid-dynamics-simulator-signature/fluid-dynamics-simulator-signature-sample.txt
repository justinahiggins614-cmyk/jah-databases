Fluid Dynamics Simulator Database (fluid-dynamics-simulator-signature)

A fluid dynamics simulator simulates fluid flow with real equations: type the flow, press Simulate, and read the speed and the pressure. A pipe of 2 square metres at 3 metres per second carries 6 cubic metres per second. Water at 2 metres per second in a 0.1 metre pipe has Reynolds number 200000: turbulent. Water leaving a tank 5 metres deep flows at 9.9 metres per second. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
