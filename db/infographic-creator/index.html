1. Description

An infographic creator turns numbers into bars, pies and big facts: type numbers, press Draw, and download the infographic. Bars [10, 20, 30] total 60. The slice of 30 is 50 percent of 60. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

2. Math proof

Bars [10, 20, 30] total 60. The biggest bar is 30. The slice of 30 is 50 percent of 60. 10 of 60 is 16.7 percent. The mean of [10, 20, 30] is 20. The total of [5, 10, 15, 20] is 50.

3. Science proof

An infographic creator works because numbers become shapes, and each shape is plain arithmetic: doubling [10, 20] gives [20, 40]. 50 percent of 60 is 30. A canvas 600 by 400 has 240000 pixels. A canvas 1200 by 800 has 960000 pixels. That is why the local demo shows the numbers and the infographic together, and runs 0 creations on a server.
