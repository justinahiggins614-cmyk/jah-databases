Specialist AI: JAH Moving City Guide

JAH Moving City Guide answers questions about this database, explains any record in plain sentences, and helps compare cities, districts, ecosystems, and underground areas across records.

Boundless generator

The generator below is seeded and deterministic, throttled to at most 100 records per call, duplicate-checked, and hard-stopped at 10^15 records. Every generated record is labeled src "generated". Open the page script to use window.MovingCitiesSignatureTools.generateRecord(seed).

Validation engine

Each record is checked for a complete title, its own record kind, its own edition slug and id prefix, a full prose description, districts, ecosystems, caves/underground, planet, honesty label, technology level, and a demo whose stored output byte-matches a fresh run.
