# Dictionary entries — The School Administration Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## school administration

The recorded keeping of the running of a school, so that every decision, every member of staff, and every sum spent can be followed from the paper that made it to the record that keeps it.

## principal

The head of a school who leads its staff and answers for its running, named in the record with the school, so that who leads the school can be read plainly.

## school district

The public body that oversees a group of schools in one area, named in the record with the schools it serves, so that where the oversight sits can be read plainly.

## staff

The teachers and other workers employed by a school, kept in the record with their posts and their dates, so that who works at the school can be counted.

## budget

The written plan of money for a school in one year, kept with its sums and its purposes, so that what the school may spend can be read in full.

## governor

A member of the body that oversees a school and approves its rules, named in the record with the meeting, so that who approved a decision can be traced to the paper.

## meeting minutes

The written record of what was said and decided at a meeting, kept with the date and the decisions, so that the decisions can be proved from the paper.

## policy

A written rule adopted by a school, kept in the record as the school published it, so that the rule can be checked against what the school does.

## timetable

The written plan of lessons for a school across the week, kept in the record with the classes and the terms, so that when each lesson falls can be read plainly.

## school year

The full span of terms in one year of teaching, written in the record with its start and its end, so that every act of administration can be placed in its year.

## inspection

The visit by an outside body to a school to check its running against the published rules, kept in the record with its date and its findings, so that the check can be followed.

## school fund

The money held by a school for its running, kept in the record with its sums and its uses, so that what was held and spent can be counted.
