# The School Administration Database

The School Administration Database is the school administration record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full school administration record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database inheritance-administration, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
