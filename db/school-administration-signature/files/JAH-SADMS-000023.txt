History
Schools have been governed by written rules for as long as they have kept pupils, because a school without a keeper of its papers cannot pay its teachers or keep its doors open in order. Administration grew beside the classroom: a head who leads, a staff who teach, a budget that pays for books and roofs, and minutes that record what was decided. From the hand-written minute book in a school office to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the running of the school down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every act of school administration true.
