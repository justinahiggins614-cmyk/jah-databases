Image Format Converter Database (image-format-converter)

An image format converter changes a picture from one format to another, and changes its pixels for real: grey, resize, flip, rotate and crop, all on the pixel list, drawn on a canvas. The seeded 4 by 2 picture is drawn on the page; you can also upload your own picture, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Worked example: An image format names how the picture is stored: PNG and JPEG are 2 formats
