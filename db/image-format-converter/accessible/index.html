The Image Format Converter Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An image format converter changes a picture from one format to another, and changes its pixels for real: grey, resize, flip, rotate and crop, all on the pixel list, drawn on a canvas. The seeded 4 by 2 picture is drawn on the page; you can also upload your own picture, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/image-format-converter/index.jsonl.gz
