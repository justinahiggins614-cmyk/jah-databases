History
Hospitals and clinics have counted their equipment since the first wards were stocked, first in ledgers of beds and instruments, then in card indexes of machines and their makers, and now in systems that hold the register, the maintenance schedule, the calibration record, and the loan book side by side. Medical equipment is a world of machines that must be found, kept in working order, and retired when worn, and the inventory of it is the keeping of those records true. From the paper list pinned to the store room door to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the equipment down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every medical equipment inventory record true.
