# Dictionary entries — The Medical Equipment Inventory Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## medical equipment inventory

The recorded count and state of the medical equipment held by a service, naming each item, where it is, and whether it works, so that the equipment can be found and counted.

## equipment register

The written list of all the medical equipment a service holds, naming each item with its serial number, so that the whole stock can be read in one place.

## serial number

The unique number a maker stamps on a piece of equipment, written in the register so that one item can be told from another of the same kind.

## calibration record

The written account of the checking of a measuring instrument against a known standard, kept by date, so that the readings of the instrument can be trusted.

## maintenance schedule

The written plan of the servicing due for each piece of equipment, kept by date, so that no machine misses the care that keeps it working.

## inspection log

The written record of the inspections carried out on equipment, kept by date and by item, so that every inspection can be found and followed.

## loan record

The written account of a piece of equipment lent to another ward or service, naming the item, the borrower, and the dates, so that the loan can be followed until the item returns.

## spare parts list

The written list of the spare parts held for a piece of equipment, naming each part and its quantity, so that a repair can be made without waiting for a part.

## warranty record

The written account of the guarantee a maker gives for a piece of equipment, naming the item, the period, and the terms, so that the guarantee can be read when a repair is needed.

## disposal record

The written account of a piece of equipment taken out of service, naming the item, the date, and the reason, so that the end of the item can be shown as plainly as its beginning.

## audit trail

The ordered series of records showing every change made to an inventory, kept by date, so that the history of any item can be followed step by step.

## reorder point

The stock level at which a new order for a named item is placed, written in the inventory with the item it belongs to, so that the order is placed before the stock runs out.
