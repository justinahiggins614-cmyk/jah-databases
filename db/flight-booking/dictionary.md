# Dictionary entries — The Flight Booking Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## flight booking

The written record of a seat held on a flight, naming the passenger, the flight, and the fare, kept as the single true record of the booking. It is a record only: no live booking and no payment happens here.

## booking record

The full written account of one booking, with its reference, its passenger, its flight, and its fare, so that the booking can be found and proved from the record alone.

## passenger

The person named on a booking as the one who will fly, recorded with the name written exactly as it stands on their travel document.

## itinerary

The written plan of a journey, listing each flight in order with its times and places, so that the whole journey can be read from one record.

## fare

The price stated for a flight booking, recorded with its amount and what it covers, so that the cost of each booking can be counted and checked. No payment is taken here; the fare is a recorded figure only.

## seat

The place on an aircraft held by a booking, named by its row and letter, so that the passenger's place can be found and no seat is booked twice.

## flight number

The number an airline gives to one scheduled flight, written on the booking, so that the flight can be found among all the flights of the day.

## departure

The leaving of a flight from its airport, recorded with the place and the time, so that the start of the journey can be read from the booking.

## arrival

The coming of a flight to its airport, recorded with the place and the time, so that the end of the journey can be read from the booking.

## booking reference

The one code given to a booking when it is made, written on every message about it, so that the booking can be found in the record at any moment.

## ticket

The written proof of a booking, naming the passenger, the flight, and the fare, kept with the booking record, so that the seat can be shown when asked.

## check-in

The act of confirming a booking before departure and receiving a boarding pass, recorded with its time, so that the booking moves from held to confirmed.
