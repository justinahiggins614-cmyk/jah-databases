1. Description

A flashcard creator makes decks of flashcards: a question on the front, the answer on the back. Show a card, read the front, flip it to see the back, and mark it right or wrong. The local creator on this page makes decks in this browser, with no connection. No sign-in, nothing uploaded.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A deck of 10 cards. Answering 4 of 5 cards right is 80 percent. A hard card is answered right by 2 of 10 people, and an easy card by 9 of 10. A deck of 10 cards has 20 sides: 10 fronts and 10 backs. A streak of 4 right answers in a row.

3. Science proof

A flashcard works because asking and answering is a test of memory: the front asks, the back answers, and the score counts the right answers over all cards. Shuffling mixes the order so the order cannot be memorised instead of the answers. That is why the creator shows the front first, then the back, then the score.
