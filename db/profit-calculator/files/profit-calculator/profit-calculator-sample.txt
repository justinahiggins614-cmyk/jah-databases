Profit Calculator Database (profit-calculator)

A profit calculator works out profit from revenue and cost: type the figures, press Calculate, and read the profit. Revenue 100 minus cost 60 is profit 40. Profit 40 on revenue 100 is a margin of 40 percent. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: Revenue 100 minus cost 60 is profit 40
