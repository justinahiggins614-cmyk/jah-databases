This record covers A Text Adventure That Remembers Your Choices.

A text adventure that forgets your choices is not an adventure at all: you save a village in one scene and the next scene asks you to save it again, as if nothing happened. Remembering choices is what makes the story yours, because every decision you made stays true for the rest of the adventure. Without memory, the story is a set of disconnected scenes, and the player learns that nothing they do matters, which is the fastest way to end a game.
