The Digital Asset Exchange Database (digital-asset-exchange)

A digital asset exchange is a market where fictional digital assets are listed, priced in fictional credits, and swapped through an order book. This is a fictional-only design study: every asset, price, account, and trade here is invented for teaching. No real assets, real prices, real accounts, or real transactions appear anywhere, and nothing here is financial advice.

Worked example: A worked fee example, step by step: buying 10 Aurora Tokens at 12 credits is 120 credits, the taker fee of 0.2 percent is 0.24 credits, and the buyer pays 120.24 credits in total.

Demo output:
Buy 5 at 13 meets sell 5 at 13: trade 5 Aurora Tokens for 65 credits
10 AUR at 12 credits = 120 credits; fee 0.24 credits; total 120.24 credits
