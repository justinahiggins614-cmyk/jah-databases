# The Digital Asset Exchange Database — files (digital-asset-exchange)

- `demo-digital-asset-exchange.py` — runnable demo program (run with `python3 demo-digital-asset-exchange.py`).
- `digital-asset-exchange-sample.txt` — a plain-text sample of the database.
- The full database is `data/digital-asset-exchange/index.jsonl.gz` (24 records).
- The generator is `gen-digital-asset-exchange.js`; the generator website is `generator/`.
- The two AI specs are `ai/website-ai.json` and `ai/thing-ai.json` (Signature specs: `ai/website-ai-signature.json` and `ai/thing-ai-signature.json`).

Fictional only: no real assets, prices, accounts, or transactions. Nothing here is financial advice.
