Records: What a Vector Calculator Is; Adding Vectors; Subtracting Vectors; Scaling a Vector; The Dot Product; At Right Angles; The Cross Product; A Unit Vector; A Vector in Three Parts; The Zero Vector; The Angle Between; Scaling Again; The Local Demo; Two Vectors; The Parts of a Vector; Three Parts; Dotting with Itself; Length Squared; A Vector Along One Axis; A Vector That Grows; All Checks Pass; A Diagonal Vector; Adding Zero.
Math proof: The vector [3, 4] has length 5.0. The dot product of [1, 2] and [3, 4] is 11. The cross product of [1, 0, 0] and [0, 1, 0] is [0, 0, 1]. The unit vector of [3, 4] is [0.6, 0.8]. The vector [1, 2, 2] has length 3.0.
Science proof: A vector calculator works because a vector is parts in order, and each rule works part by part: [1, 2] + [3, 4] is [4, 6], and 2 times [3, 4] is [6, 8]. The dot product of [1, 0] and [0, 1] is 0, so they are at right angles. That is why the local demo shows the vectors and the answer together, and runs 0 calculations on a server.
