# The Financial Audit Management Database

The Financial Audit Management Database is the financial audit management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full financial audit management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database audit, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
