Mohegan Sun — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Mohegan Sun is a large casino and entertainment resort in Uncasville, Connecticut, owned by the Mohegan Tribe. Opened on October 12, 1996, it features about 1,563 rooms across the Sky Tower and Earth Tower, the 10,000-seat Mohegan Sun Arena, and the Casino of the Sky, Earth, and Wind. It is one of the most visited entertainment complexes in New England.
