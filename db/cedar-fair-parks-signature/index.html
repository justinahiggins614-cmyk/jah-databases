This is a Signature-original design inspired by the idea of a river water park, never a record of the real park.

Location

New Braunfels, Texas, United States

Status

NEW — operating water park; Cedar Fair era from 2019

Lands and areas

Water park areas: the original river section, the Blastenburg section, the Surfenburg section, and tube chutes along the Comal River

Rides NEW

Master Blaster (uphill water coaster); Wolf Pack (raft water slide); Dragon Blaster (water coaster); Black Knight (tube slide); Comal River tube chute (river tube chute)

Rides PAST

Original 1979-era attractions (retired or replaced over time); Early tube chute formats (replaced); Former slide towers (retired)

Theming ecosystems

River water-park theming: a natural-river water park on the Comal River, opened in 1979, with uphill water coasters as its signature

Hidden spots

Publicly-known hidden spots: the shaded river-bank paths, the quiet corners of the original section, and the back walkways along the tube chutes

Working demo program

RIDES = [
    ('Master Blaster', 'uphill water coaster', 8),
    ('Wolf Pack', 'raft water slide', 8),
    ('Dragon Blaster', 'water coaster', 8),
    ('Black Knight', 'tube slide', 7),
    ('Comal River tube chute', 'river tube chute', 5),
]
print('Ride-type classification: Schlitterbahn New Braunfels')
counts = {}
for name, kind, thrill in RIDES:
    counts[kind] = counts.get(kind, 0) + 1
    print('%s -> %s (thrill %d/10)' % (name, kind, thrill))
for kind in sorted(counts):
    print('%s rides: %d' % (kind, counts[kind]))
print('Total rides classified: %d' % len(RIDES))

Schlitterbahn Galveston Rank 16 · complex · water park property · advanced

The Signature Galveston-inspired design is an original Signature concept called Gulf Island Water Park, with original indoor sections and an original water coaster named the Island MASSIV Dive, drawn without copying any real attraction. This is a Signature-original design inspired by the idea of an island water park, never a record of the real park.

Location

Galveston, Texas, United States

Status

NEW — operating water park; Cedar Fair era from 2019

Lands and areas

Water park areas: indoor and outdoor sections, slide tower complexes, a lazy river, and a kids water play area

Rides NEW

MASSIV (water coaster); Rohr! (water slide); Towering (slide tower complex); Wasserfest (water play area); Lazy River (lazy river)

Rides PAST

Original 2006-era slide formats (replaced over time); Early tower attractions (retired); Former kids water features (replaced)

Theming ecosystems

Island water-park theming: a Gulf Coast island water park with indoor sections for year-round use, opened in 2006

Hidden spots

Publicly-known hidden spots: the shaded indoor-section paths, the quiet corners of the lazy river route, and the back walkways between slide towers
