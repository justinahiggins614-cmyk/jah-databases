Knowledge Assessment Database (knowledge-assessment)

A knowledge assessment asks fixed questions and scores the answers. Answer the questions, press Score, and read the result. 8 right out of 10 is 80.0 percent. A score of 8 right out of 10 is 80.0 percent. A pass mark of 60 percent on 10 questions needs 6 right. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
