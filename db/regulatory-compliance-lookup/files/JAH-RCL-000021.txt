History
Rules have been written down since the first guilds set standards for their trades, first in charters and ordinances kept by towns, then in printed codes of practice, and now in registers that hold the rule, the requirement, the check, the inspection, and the audit side by side. Regulatory compliance lookup is the finding of what a rule asks and whether it has been met, so that any reader can trace a requirement from its text to its proof. From the printed code kept in the office to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the lookup down whole.
