# The Regulatory Compliance Lookup Database

The Regulatory Compliance Lookup Database is the regulatory compliance lookup record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full regulatory compliance lookup record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database tool-encyclopedia-lookup, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
