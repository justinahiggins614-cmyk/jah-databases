Hash Generator Database (hash-generator)

A hash generator computes real hashes of entered text locally: type the text, press Hash, and read the digest. It is defensive and educational only. SHA-256 of hello is 2cf24dba5fb0a30e26e83b2ac5b9e29e1b161e5c1fa7425e73043362938b9824. A SHA-256 digest has 64 hex characters. SHA-256 holds 256 bits: 32 bytes. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: SHA-256 of hello is 2cf24dba5fb0a30e26e83b2ac5b9e29e1b161e5c1fa7425e73043362938b9824
