1. Description and History

A virtual clothing try-on lets a person see a garment on their own photo: the shirt, jacket, or dress placed on the body in the photo, in the right size, the right shape, and the right light. This database designs that try-on one record at a time, from a single shirt to a whole outfit, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.

Trying on clothes meant going to a shop and changing in a fitting room. Cameras and software later made it possible to see a garment on a photo of the wearer instead: first as a flat overlay, then shaped to the body, then matched to the light. The design in this database follows that path, keeps the user's photo private, and shows the fit honestly, including where a garment would be tight or loose.

Every record here is a design, never a real product. Public records are fact-checked, real, and legal-only; Signature records are the Signature original version of the same subject; speculative material is labeled design-study, never as fact. Photo tools work on the user's own photos only, never anyone else's private images.
