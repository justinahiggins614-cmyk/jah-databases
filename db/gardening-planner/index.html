This record covers Drawing Your Garden Beds.

A garden with no drawn beds is a garden that is planted by guess, because nobody knows where one bed ends and the next begins. Drawing your garden beds fixes the map: each bed named, each bed measured, each bed ready for planting. Without the drawing, seeds are sown in the wrong bed, plants crowd each other, and the harvest is smaller than the garden promised.
