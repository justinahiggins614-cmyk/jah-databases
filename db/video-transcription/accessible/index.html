The Video Transcription Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A video transcription writes the words spoken in a video, with timestamps. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it is rule-based, types a transcript from entered words, makes 0 real transcriptions, and real speech-to-text is BLOCKED. A real transcription needs a backend, and this page honestly says so. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
