1. Description

A logo creator draws logos from shapes and words: pick shapes, pick colours, add a word, and download the picture. The local creator on this page draws on a canvas in this browser, with no connection. No sign-in, nothing uploaded.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A logo is counted in shapes and colours: a logo of 3 shapes names 1 business, and a palette of 3 colours lists blue, gold and white. A circle of radius 50 has an area of about 7854, a square of side 40 has an area of 1600, and a rectangle 60 by 40 has an area of 2400. A logo 100 pixels wide scales to 200 pixels wide, 2 times bigger.

3. Science proof

A logo works because a small picture can name a business: a few simple shapes, two or three colours, and one word are easier to remember than a long name. Symmetry and a grid keep the parts lined up, and a vector drawing stays sharp at any size. That is why the creator lists its shapes and colours beside the drawing: the design is the logo.
