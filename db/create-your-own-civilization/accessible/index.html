  12. A Streak of Civilizations

    Record 12 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Create your own civilization helps a storyteller invent a people of their own, in plain words, with the land, the food, the crafts, and the customs written out first. A streak gives a visitor a reason to come back tomorrow: one civilization each day in a row builds a chain, and breaking the chain costs something the visitor has earned. The streak must be forgiving, because a visitor who misses one day through no fault of their own should not lose a month of civilizations.
