Section 1: Description and history

Create your own civilization helps a storyteller invent a people of their own, in plain words, with the land, the food, the crafts, and the customs written out first. Every civilization is a clearly labeled invented creation, built from generic parts, and no real people or real place is named. Inventing peoples is an old habit of storytellers: a people given a home, a food, a craft, and a custom, all written down so the tale holds together. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a builder that joins the parts into one people and a plain description for every part. Every record below carries its own history section, so the description and the history of each design are told together, in full sentences and paragraphs, never as stubs.

A River Valley People Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A civilization builder website: a visitor picks the parts of a civilization, such as a river valley home, a grain they grow, and a craft they practice, and the site joins them into one invented people, with a plain description of the land, the food, the crafts, and the customs. Every civilization is an invented creation written for this database, and no part of it names real people or a real place. This record covers A River Valley People.

A river valley people live by a slow river and green fields, growing grain and catching river fish, with milling and weaving as their crafts. The builder must show the land, the food, the crafts, and the customs in plain words, so a storyteller can see the whole people at once and change any part. Without those plain words, a storyteller cannot tell how the people live, and a people that cannot be seen cannot be told. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a river valley people runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

History

Inventing peoples is an old habit of storytellers: a people given a home, a food, a craft, and a custom, all written down so the tale holds together. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a builder that joins the parts into one people and a plain description for every part. River valley peoples are an old storyteller habit, from a tale of a slow river to a people built around grain and fish.

Proposed solution

Name the civilization and what a visitor does in it; Write the rules in plain sentences a new visitor can follow; Give every civilization the same fair start: the same clues, the same time, the same scoring; Let a visitor try one civilization without signing in; Check the civilization works the same way on a phone and on a computer.
