# The Universal Product Search Database

The Universal Product Search Database is the universal product search record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full universal product search record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database semantic-search-public-ai, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
