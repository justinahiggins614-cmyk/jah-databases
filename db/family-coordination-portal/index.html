A Week of School Runs Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A family portal website: a household picks a generic kind of plan, such as a week of school runs, a weekend visit, or a shared shopping list, and the portal joins it into one plain plan with the order, the times, and who does what written out. The portal names no real family, holds no private messages, and every sample plan is labeled as an illustration of the method. This record covers A Week of School Runs.

A week of school runs gives a household a plain plan for getting children to school and back, with who drives each day, the times, and the route written out for all to see. The portal must show the order of the runs, so no child is left at the stop and no driver is asked to be in two places at once. Without those plain facts, a family must guess at the plan each morning, and guessing at the plan on a school morning is a poor way to start the day.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a week of school runs runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
