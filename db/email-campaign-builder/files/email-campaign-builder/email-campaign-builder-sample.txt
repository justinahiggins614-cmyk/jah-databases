Email Campaign Builder Database (email-campaign-builder)

An email campaign builder is a rule-based local demo: it writes set toy messages in set sections, and counts the words and the readers. The builder writes 3 sections. The section is called hello. The sections average 40 words. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
