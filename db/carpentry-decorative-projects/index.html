Carved Spoon Rank 1 · simple · carving · beginner

A carved spoon is shaped from a single blank of hardwood, with a bowl hollowed by gouge and a handle shaped to the hand. This guide carves a 10 inch spoon from maple, starting with a 10 by 2 by 1 inch blank, roughing the outline with a saw, hollowing the bowl, and finishing with food-safe oil. The bowl holds about 2 tablespoons, the handle is oval in section for grip, and the spoon is sanded to 220 grit before oiling.

Dimensioned sketch of Carved Spoon: overall 10 x 2 x 1 in in, showing the plan/elevation outline with dimension labels and key features of the design.

Specifications

Dimensions: 10 x 2 x 1 in in. Materials: blank (hard maple, 1 in). Fasteners: food-safe oil finish. Tolerances: Bowl depth 1/2 in plus or minus 1/16 in; handle oval within 1/16 in..

Cut list

How to make it

  1. Draw the spoon outline on the blank.
  2. Saw the outline and shape the handle oval.
  3. Hollow the bowl with a gouge, working with the grain.
  4. Sand through the grits to 220.
  5. Oil with food-safe oil, let it cure, and re-oil.

Tools required

tape measure, pencil, square, cordless drill/driver, sander, carving knife or chisel set, clamps

Safety gear

Safety notes: Wear eye and hearing protection whenever power tools run; keep blades guarded and hands behind the cutting edge; carve away from the body with the non-dominant hand behind the blade; never present an unsafe cut as acceptable.
