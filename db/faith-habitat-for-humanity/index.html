Habitat for Humanity International — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Habitat for Humanity International is a nonprofit housing organization headquartered in Americus, Georgia, founded in 1976 by Millard and Linda Fuller. With Christian roots and an open-door policy, it builds and repairs homes with volunteer labor and partner families who contribute sweat equity, operating in all 50 US states and more than 70 countries.
