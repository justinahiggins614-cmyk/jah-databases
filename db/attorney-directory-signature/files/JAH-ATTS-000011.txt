History
Lawyers have been listed by town and by trade since the first bar roll was written, so that any person seeking counsel could find who practised and where. Directories grew beside the profession: printed lists of names, offices, and practice areas, kept current by the bar associations that admit attorneys to practice. From the printed roll in a law library to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the listing down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every attorney directory entry true. It is a directory of published listings only, and it gives no advice about choosing any attorney.
