The index solves this by listing every record in ladder order, with its place in the ladder, so a reader can start at the beginning and climb, or jump straight to the record they need. Worked example, Signature design: a reader who wants the simplest starting point opens record 1, A Long Talk Turned Into a Short Summary, and a reader who wants the hardest problem opens record 23, A Reshaper That Never Changes What the Original Meant, in the Signature edition.
