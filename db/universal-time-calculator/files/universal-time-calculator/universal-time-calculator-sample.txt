Universal Time Calculator Database (universal-time-calculator)

A universal time calculator turns any time question into arithmetic: it converts between seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks and years, adds and subtracts durations, finds the time between two clock readings, converts between time zones, handles daylight saving, counts down to a date, counts business days, and links speed, distance and time.

Worked example: One minute holds 60 seconds and one hour holds 3,600 seconds, so any count of seconds divides into hours, minutes and seconds. 7,265 seconds is 2 hours, 1 minute and 5 seconds, because 7,265 divided by 3,600 gives 2 with 65 left over, and 65 seconds is 1 minute and 5 seconds.

Demo output:
7265 seconds = 2h 1m 5s
1500 minutes = 1d 1h (25h total)
09:15 to 14:40 = 5h 25m
240 km at 80 km/h takes 3 hours
Countdown: 35 days, 18 hours and 26 minutes
2026-11-23 to 2026-11-30: 6 business days of 8 calendar days
