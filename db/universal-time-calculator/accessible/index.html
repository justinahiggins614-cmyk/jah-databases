A universal time calculator turns any time question into arithmetic: it converts between seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks and years, adds and subtracts durations, finds the time between two clock readings, converts between time zones, handles daylight saving, counts down to a date, counts business days, and links speed, distance and time.

  1. Seconds to Minutes and Hours

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. One minute holds 60 seconds and one hour holds 3,600 seconds, so any count of seconds divides into hours, minutes and seconds. 7,265 seconds is 2 hours, 1 minute and 5 seconds, because 7,265 divided by 3,600 gives 2 with 65 left over, and 65 seconds is 1 minute and 5 seconds.

    Math proof: 7,265 divided by 3,600 is 2 with a remainder of 65, so there are 2 whole hours. 65 divided by 60 is 1 with a remainder of 5, so the remainder is 1 minute and 5 seconds. Together: 7,265 seconds is 2 hours, 1 minute and 5 seconds. As a check, 2 times 3,600 plus 1 times 60 plus 5 is 7,200 plus 60 plus 5, which is 7,265.

    Science proof: Time is measured in fixed units: 60 seconds make a minute and 60 minutes make an hour, because the counting system divides each larger unit into 60 smaller ones. Dividing by 60 steps down one unit at a time, and the remainder at each step is the count in that smaller unit, which is why the same division works for any number of seconds.

  2. Minutes to Hours and Days

    Record 2 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. One hour holds 60 minutes and one day holds 1,440 minutes, so minutes divide into hours and days. 1,500 minutes is 25 hours, which is 1 day and 1 hour, because 1,500 divided by 60 is 25, and 25 divided by 24 is 1 with 1 left over.

    Math proof: 1,500 divided by 60 is 25 hours. 25 divided by 24 is 1 with a remainder of 1, so 25 hours is 1 day and 1 hour. As a check, 1 times 1,440 plus 1 times 60 is 1,440 plus 60, which is 1,500 minutes.

    Science proof: A day is defined as 24 hours, and each hour as 60 minutes, so a day holds 24 times 60, which is 1,440 minutes. Dividing a minute count by 60 gives hours, and dividing the hours by 24 gives days, with the remainder as extra hours.

  3. Hours to Days and Weeks

    Record 3 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. One day holds 24 hours and one week holds 168 hours, so hours divide into days and weeks. 336 hours is 14 days, which is exactly 2 weeks, because 336 divided by 24 is 14, and 14 divided by 7 is 2.

    Math proof: 336 divided by 24 is 14 days. 14 divided by 7 is 2 weeks, with no remainder. As a check, 2 times 7 times 24 is 336 hours.

    Science proof: A week is seven days by convention, and a day is 24 hours, so a week holds 7 times 24, which is 168 hours. Dividing hours by 24 gives days, and dividing days by 7 gives weeks.

  4. Days to Weeks, Months and Years

    Record 4 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. One week holds 7 days and one ordinary year holds 365 days, so a day count divides into weeks and years. 730 days is 104 weeks and 2 days, and it is also exactly 2 ordinary years, because 730 divided by 7 is 104 with 2 left over, and 730 divided by 365 is 2.

    Math proof: 730 divided by 7 is 104 with a remainder of 2, so 730 days is 104 weeks and 2 days. 730 divided by 365 is 2, so 730 days is 2 ordinary years. As a check, 104 times 7 plus 2 is 728 plus 2, which is 730.

    Science proof: A year is the time the Earth takes to travel once around the Sun, about 365 days, and a week is a conventional seven-day cycle. Because 365 is not a multiple of 7, a year always leaves a remainder of days when counted in weeks.

  5. Adding Times Together

    Record 5 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. Adding two durations means adding their minutes and carrying every 60 minutes into an hour. 2 hours 45 minutes plus 1 hour 30 minutes is 4 hours 15 minutes, because 165 minutes plus 90 minutes is 255 minutes, and 255 minutes is 4 hours and 15 minutes.

    Math proof: 2 hours 45 minutes is 2 times 60 plus 45, which is 165 minutes. 1 hour 30 minutes is 90 minutes. 165 plus 90 is 255 minutes. 255 divided by 60 is 4 with a remainder of 15, so the sum is 4 hours 15 minutes.

    Science proof: Durations add like ordinary numbers once they share a unit, and minutes are the natural shared unit because both hours and minutes convert to minutes exactly. Carrying 60 minutes into one hour is the same regrouping used when adding numbers in base 60.

  6. Subtracting Times

    Record 6 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. Subtracting one duration from another means subtracting their minutes, borrowing an hour when the minutes run short. 5 hours 20 minutes minus 2 hours 45 minutes is 2 hours 35 minutes, because 320 minutes minus 165 minutes is 155 minutes, and 155 minutes is 2 hours and 35 minutes.

    Math proof: 5 hours 20 minutes is 320 minutes. 2 hours 45 minutes is 165 minutes. 320 minus 165 is 155 minutes. 155 divided by 60 is 2 with a remainder of 35, so the difference is 2 hours 35 minutes. As a check, 155 plus 165 is 320.

    Science proof: Subtraction works in the shared minute unit, just as addition does. Borrowing one hour gives 60 extra minutes, which is why a smaller minute count can still be subtracted from a larger one after the borrow.

  7. Elapsed Time Between Two Clock Times

    Record 7 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. The time between two clock readings on the same day is their difference in minutes. From 09:15 to 14:40 is 5 hours 25 minutes, because 09:15 is 555 minutes after midnight, 14:40 is 880 minutes after midnight, and 880 minus 555 is 325 minutes.

    Math proof: 09:15 is 9 times 60 plus 15, which is 555 minutes after midnight. 14:40 is 14 times 60 plus 40, which is 880 minutes after midnight. 880 minus 555 is 325 minutes. 325 divided by 60 is 5 with a remainder of 25, so the elapsed time is 5 hours 25 minutes.

    Science proof: A clock reading is a count of minutes since midnight, so the elapsed time between two readings is an ordinary subtraction. This works whenever both readings fall on the same day and the later one comes after the earlier one.

  8. Duration Across Midnight

    Record 8 of 24. simple. Feasibility: proven. When a duration crosses midnight, add 24 hours to the later clock reading before subtracting. From 22:30 to 01:15 the next day is 2 hours 45 minutes, because 01:15 the next day is 1,515 minutes after the first midnight, and 1,515 minus 1,350 is 165 minutes.

    Math proof: 22:30 is 22 times 60 plus 30, which is 1,350 minutes after midnight. 01:15 is 75 minutes, plus 1,440 minutes for the new day, giving 1,515. 1,515 minus 1,350 is 165 minutes. 165 divided by 60 is 2 with a remainder of 45, so the duration is 2 hours 45 minutes.

    Science proof: Midnight resets the clock to zero, so a reading after midnight belongs to the next day and must carry the full 1,440 minutes of the day before it. Adding one whole day to the later reading restores the true order of the two moments.

  9. Converting Between 12-Hour and 24-Hour Clock

    Record 9 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. In the 24-hour clock, morning hours keep their number, afternoon hours add 12, midnight is 00 and noon is 12. 3:45 PM is 15:45, and 12:30 AM is 00:30, because 3 plus 12 is 15, and 12 AM is the midnight hour, numbered 0.

    Math proof: 3:45 PM: 3 plus 12 is 15, so the time is 15:45. 12:30 AM: 12 AM is hour 0, so the time is 00:30. As a check, 15:45 minus 12 is 3:45, and 00:30 plus 12 hours is 12:30 PM.

    Science proof: The 12-hour clock repeats the numbers 1 to 12 twice a day, using AM for the first cycle and PM for the second. The 24-hour clock counts 0 to 23 without repeating, so each moment of the day has one number, which is why timetables and computers prefer it.

  10. Time Zones: Converting Between Zones

    Record 10 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. A time zone is an offset from Coordinated Universal Time, so converting between zones means adding the difference of the two offsets. 14:00 at UTC is 19:30 in a zone at UTC+5:30 and 09:00 in a zone at UTC-5, because 14:00 plus 5 hours 30 minutes is 19:30, and 14:00 minus 5 hours is 09:00.

    Math proof: UTC+5:30: 14:00 plus 5:30 is 19:30 the same day. UTC-5: 14:00 minus 5:00 is 09:00 the same day. The two zones are 10 hours 30 minutes apart: 5:30 minus negative 5:00 is 10:30.

    Science proof: The Earth turns 360 degrees in 24 hours, so each hour of time is 15 degrees of longitude, and zones are set by whole or half-hour offsets from UTC. Adding the offset to the UTC reading gives the local clock reading in that zone.

  11. Daylight Saving Time: Spring Forward

    Record 11 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. When clocks spring forward, the hour from 02:00 to 03:00 is skipped, so a real duration lands one clock hour later than it would otherwise. Starting at 01:30 and waiting 2 real hours ends at 04:30 on the clock, because the first 30 minutes reach 02:00, the clock jumps to 03:00, and the remaining 90 minutes reach 04:30.

    Math proof: 01:30 to 02:00 is 30 real minutes. The jump from 02:00 to 03:00 takes no real time. The remaining 120 minus 30, which is 90 real minutes, run from 03:00 to 04:30. So 2 real hours from 01:30 end at 04:30 on the clock.

    Science proof: Daylight saving time shifts the clock forward by one hour in spring so that evening daylight lasts longer. The skipped hour never happens as a clock reading, which is why real elapsed time and clock difference disagree on that day.

  12. Daylight Saving Time: Fall Back

    Record 12 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. When clocks fall back, the hour from 01:00 to 02:00 happens twice, so a real duration lands one clock hour earlier than it would otherwise. Starting at 00:30 and waiting 2 real hours ends at 01:30 on the clock, because 90 real minutes reach 02:00, the clock falls back to 01:00, and the remaining 30 minutes reach 01:30.

    Math proof: 00:30 to 02:00 is 90 real minutes. The fall back from 02:00 to 01:00 takes no real time. The remaining 120 minus 90, which is 30 real minutes, run from 01:00 to 01:30. So 2 real hours from 00:30 end at 01:30 on the clock.

    Science proof: Daylight saving time ends by shifting the clock back one hour in autumn, so the hour before 02:00 is lived twice. The repeated hour means a clock difference on that day understates the real elapsed time by one hour.

  13. Countdown to a Future Date and Time

    Record 13 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. A countdown is the difference between two date-times, counted in days, hours and minutes. From 2026-11-26 at 05:34 to 2027-01-01 at 00:00 is 35 days, 18 hours and 26 minutes, because the full difference is 35 days plus 18 hours 26 minutes, and 35 times 24 plus 18 is 858 hours with 26 minutes left over.

    Math proof: From 2026-11-26 05:34 to 2026-12-26 05:34 is 30 days, and from there to 2027-01-01 00:00 is 5 days 18 hours 26 minutes. The total is 35 days 18 hours 26 minutes. In minutes: 35 times 1,440 plus 18 times 60 plus 26 is 50,400 plus 1,080 plus 26, which is 51,506 minutes.

    Science proof: A countdown subtracts two moments on the same calendar, borrowing a day when the later time of day is earlier than the starting time of day. Days are counted on the calendar, so month lengths and leap days are handled by the calendar itself.

  14. Calendar Days Between Two Dates

    Record 14 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. The days between two dates are counted on the calendar, not by multiplying months. From 2026-03-01 to 2026-03-15 is 14 days, because March 1 plus 14 days is March 15, and the difference of the two dates is 14.

    Math proof: 2026-03-15 minus 2026-03-01 is 14 days. As a check, 2026-01-01 to 2026-12-31 is 364 days, because 2026 is not a leap year and holds 365 days, and the last day is 364 days after the first.

    Science proof: Calendar dates are labels on a fixed sequence of days, so the gap between two dates is a plain subtraction of their positions in that sequence. Month lengths vary, which is why counting must use the calendar rather than a fixed number of days per month.

  15. Business Days Between Two Dates

    Record 15 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. Business days are Monday to Friday, so counting them means counting weekdays and skipping Saturdays and Sundays. From Monday 2026-11-23 to Monday 2026-11-30 inclusive there are 6 business days, because the eight calendar days hold the five weekdays of the first week plus the Monday of the second, and the weekend in between adds none.

    Math proof: The eight dates are 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29 and 30 November 2026. The weekdays among them are Monday 23, Tuesday 24, Wednesday 25, Thursday 26, Friday 27 and Monday 30: six days. Saturday 28 and Sunday 29 are skipped. As a check, 6 business days plus 2 weekend days is 8 calendar days.

    Science proof: The working week is a social convention, Monday to Friday in most countries, laid over the seven-day week. Counting business days is counting the days of a date range whose weekday number is below 5, with Monday as 0 and Sunday as 6.

  16. Weeks and Weekdays: Finding a Weekday

    Record 16 of 24. working. Feasibility: proven. Adding days to a date moves the weekday forward by the remainder after dividing by seven. 2026-11-26 is a Thursday, so 10 days later is a Sunday, because 10 divided by 7 leaves 3, and Thursday plus 3 days is Sunday.

    Math proof: 10 divided by 7 is 1 with a remainder of 3, so 10 days is one full week plus 3 days. A full week returns to the same weekday, so only the 3 extra days matter. Thursday plus 1 is Friday, plus 2 is Saturday, plus 3 is Sunday. 2026-11-26 plus 10 days is 2026-12-06, a Sunday.

    Science proof: Weekdays repeat every seven days, so weekday arithmetic is arithmetic modulo 7. Adding a multiple of seven days never changes the weekday, and any other addition changes it by the remainder.

  17. Speed, Distance and Time

    Record 17 of 24. advanced. Feasibility: proven. Time, speed and distance are linked by time equals distance divided by speed. Travelling 240 kilometres at 80 kilometres per hour takes 3 hours, because 240 divided by 80 is 3, and the same rule gives speed as distance divided by time.

    Math proof: Time is 240 divided by 80, which is 3 hours. As a check, distance is speed times time, which is 80 times 3, giving 240 kilometres. Speed is 240 divided by 3, which is 80 kilometres per hour, returning the starting speed.

    Science proof: Speed is distance per unit of time, so the three quantities form one relation with three forms: time is distance over speed, speed is distance over time, and distance is speed times time. The relation holds whenever the speed stays steady over the whole trip.

  18. Pace and Time per Distance

    Record 18 of 24. advanced. Feasibility: proven. Pace is time per unit of distance, the reverse of speed. Running 10 kilometres in 50 minutes is a pace of 5 minutes per kilometre, and at that pace 20 kilometres takes 100 minutes, which is 1 hour 40 minutes, because 20 times 5 is 100.

    Math proof: Pace is 50 divided by 10, which is 5 minutes per kilometre. 20 kilometres times 5 minutes per kilometre is 100 minutes. 100 divided by 60 is 1 with a remainder of 40, so 100 minutes is 1 hour 40 minutes.

    Science proof: Pace and speed are reciprocals: a pace of 5 minutes per kilometre is a speed of 12 kilometres per hour, because 60 minutes divided by 5 minutes per kilometre is 12 kilometres. Runners use pace because it answers how long a distance takes directly.

  19. Adding Durations in Hours, Minutes and Seconds

    Record 19 of 24. advanced. Feasibility: proven. Adding durations with seconds means adding each unit and carrying 60 seconds into a minute and 60 minutes into an hour. 1 hour 20 minutes 45 seconds plus 45 minutes 30 seconds is 2 hours 6 minutes 15 seconds, because the total is 7,575 seconds, and 7,575 seconds is 2 hours, 6 minutes and 15 seconds.

    Math proof: 1 hour 20 minutes 45 seconds is 3,600 plus 1,200 plus 45, which is 4,845 seconds. 45 minutes 30 seconds is 2,700 plus 30, which is 2,730 seconds. 4,845 plus 2,730 is 7,575 seconds. 7,575 divided by 3,600 is 2 with a remainder of 375, and 375 divided by 60 is 6 with a remainder of 15, so the sum is 2 hours 6 minutes 15 seconds.

    Science proof: Seconds, minutes and hours are nested units of 60, so any duration has one exact count in seconds. Adding in seconds and converting back once at the end carries every overflow correctly, which is why the method never loses a second.

  20. Time Remaining in a Day

    Record 20 of 24. advanced. Feasibility: proven. The time left in a day is 24 hours minus the time already passed. At 14:40 there are 9 hours 20 minutes left in the day, because 14:40 is 880 minutes after midnight, 24 hours is 1,440 minutes, and 1,440 minus 880 is 560 minutes.

    Math proof: 14:40 is 14 times 60 plus 40, which is 880 minutes after midnight. 24 hours is 1,440 minutes. 1,440 minus 880 is 560 minutes. 560 divided by 60 is 9 with a remainder of 20, so 9 hours 20 minutes remain. As a check, 880 plus 560 is 1,440.

    Science proof: A day is a fixed 1,440 minutes, so the time passed and the time remaining always add to 1,440. Subtracting the minutes since midnight from 1,440 gives the minutes until the next midnight.

  21. Leap Years and February Days

    Record 21 of 24. advanced. Feasibility: proven. A year is a leap year when it divides by 4, except centuries, which must divide by 400. 2028 divides by 4, so it is a leap year and February has 29 days, while 2026 does not divide by 4, so February has 28 days, and 2100 divides by 100 but not by 400, so it is not a leap year.

    Math proof: 2028 divided by 4 is 507 exactly, so 2028 is a leap year. 2026 divided by 4 is 506 with a remainder of 2, so 2026 is not. 2100 divided by 100 is 21 exactly but 2100 divided by 400 is 5 with a remainder of 100, so 2100 is not a leap year. 2000 divided by 400 is 5 exactly, so 2000 was a leap year.

    Science proof: The Earth takes about 365.2422 days to orbit the Sun, so the calendar adds one day every four years and skips it in most century years to stay aligned with the seasons. Without the correction, the calendar would drift by about one day every four years.

  22. Months: Days in Each Month and Adding Months

    Record 22 of 24. advanced. Feasibility: proven. Months hold 28, 29, 30 or 31 days, and adding months moves the month number forward, carrying 12 months into a year. January and February 2026 together hold 59 days, because January has 31 and February 2026 has 28, and adding 3 months to 2026-11-26 gives 2027-02-26.

    Math proof: 31 plus 28 is 59 days for January and February 2026. Adding 3 months to November 2026: 11 plus 3 is 14, and 14 minus 12 is 2, so the month is February and the year rises by 1, giving 2027-02-26.

    Science proof: Months follow the Moon's cycle of about 29.5 days, rounded into the 12-month calendar, which is why their lengths vary from 28 to 31 days. Adding months is arithmetic modulo 12 on the month number, with the year advancing each time the count passes December.

  23. Average Time and Total Time

    Record 23 of 24. advanced. Feasibility: proven. The total of several times is their sum, and the average is the total divided by how many there are. Three times of 30, 45 and 60 minutes total 135 minutes, which is 2 hours 15 minutes, and their average is 45 minutes, because 135 divided by 3 is 45.

    Math proof: Total is 30 plus 45 plus 60, which is 135 minutes. 135 divided by 60 is 2 with a remainder of 15, so the total is 2 hours 15 minutes. Average is 135 divided by 3, which is 45 minutes. As a check, 45 times 3 is 135.

    Science proof: An average spreads a total evenly across its parts, so the average times the count always returns the total. Averaging times works whenever the times measure the same kind of event, such as laps of one run.
