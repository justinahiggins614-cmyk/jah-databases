Specifications. The Signature Beacon Tower Clock is an original Signature design for harbour and coastal towers, built as a flatbed movement 90 centimetres wide with a 1.5 metre pendulum and a 4 metre illuminated dial. Its going weight is 120 kilograms and its strike weight 100 kilograms, each hanging in a dedicated shaft. This is a Signature-original design, clearly labelled as such, and never presented as an authentic historic public record.

Mechanism. A double three-legged gravity escapement gives the pendulum a constant impulse regardless of wind load on the great dial, and a separate strike train sounds the hour on a single harbour bell. The dial is edge-lit at night so ships and harbour workers can read it from the water, and the hands are counterweighted in stainless steel to resist salt air.

Maintenance. Rinse salt deposits from the dial frame and hand fixings quarterly, oil the train annually, and inspect the weight lines for corrosion twice a year. The harbour environment is harder on a tower clock than an inland one, so the inspection interval is shorter by design.

Build and restoration guidance. Build the movement on a stainless bed frame, fit the dial glazing with marine seals, and prove the strike tone from the harbour entrance before commissioning. Keep a spare set of hand counterweights on site.

Tools. Marine sealant, stainless spanners, salt-rinse kit, oil cup.

The Signature Solar Tower Clock is an original Signature design for a sunny site, with solar panels on the tower roof charging a battery that powers the dial drive and LED illumination. Its dial is 2.5 metres across, its pendulum is a 1.2 metre compensated pendulum, and its drive is a stepping motor under radio correction. This is a Signature-original design.

Mechanism. Solar power charges the battery by day, and the battery drives the stepping motor and the LED dial lighting at night. The radio correction keeps the dial exact, and the compensated pendulum is retained as a visible working regulator in the chamber, even though the dial drive is electric, so visitors can see a real pendulum at work.

Maintenance. Clean the solar panels quarterly, test the battery capacity annually, and check the radio correction log monthly. Keep the pendulum oiled and beating, since it is the visible heart of the exhibit even when the dial drive is electric.

Build and restoration guidance. Size the battery for at least five sunless days, fit the panels where they can be cleaned safely, and prove the system through a simulated power cut before commissioning.

Tools. Solar panel brush, battery capacity tester, radio log, pendulum oil kit.

Signature Grand Terminus Tower Clock: The Four-Face Master Design JAH-TWCS-000016

Specifications. The Signature Grand Terminus Tower Clock is an original Signature design for a great station or civic tower, with four 4 metre dials, one on each face, a 2 metre pendulum, a gravity escapement, and a full Westminster chime on five bells. Its movement is a large flatbed 120 centimetres wide, and its weight shafts run the full height of the tower. This is a Signature-original design, the most complex in this Signature set.

Mechanism. One going train drives all four dials through bevel leading-off work, so the four faces can never disagree. The chime barrel sounds the quarters on four bells, the hour strike follows on the great bell, and an auto-wind system keeps the great weights raised. The gravity escapement isolates the pendulum from the wind load of four great dials, which is the central engineering problem of a four-face tower.

Maintenance. Inspect all four dials and their leading-off work quarterly, oil the great movement annually, test the auto-wind monthly, and listen to the full chime and strike sequence from all four sides of the tower at least once a year.

Build and restoration guidance. Build the four dials from one drawing, balance all eight hands before fitting, align the bevel work face by face, and prove four-face agreement and the full chime sequence from the ground on all sides before handover.

Tools. Four-face alignment gauge, bevel spanners, chime chart, auto-wind tester.
