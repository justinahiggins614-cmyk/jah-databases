1. Description

A biology simulator grows populations and genes with real numbers: pick a population, press Run, and read the result. A population of 100 growing by 5 percent becomes 105. Aa times Aa gives 25 percent aa. A heart at 72 beats a minute beats 4320 times an hour. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A population of 100 growing by 5 percent becomes 105. A population of 1000 growing by 3 percent for 2 years becomes 1060.9. A pond at 400 of 500 capacity grows by 40 to 440. 1 cell becomes 2, then 4, then 8. A protein of 100 amino acids is coded by 300 bases.

3. Science proof

A biology simulator works because living things grow by rules, and each step is plain arithmetic: 20 rabbits and 5 foxes: 4 rabbits per fox, and 3 species: 50 plants, 20 rabbits, 5 foxes: 75 in all. A generation of 25 years: 4 generations in 100 years. That is why the local demo shows the population and the result together, and runs 0 simulations on a server.
