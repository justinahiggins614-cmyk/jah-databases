Digital Signature Tool Database (digital-signature-tool-signature)

A digital signature tool signs entered text with a toy key and checks the signature, so a tampered message is caught. Type the text, press Sign, and read the signature and the check. Signing hello gives signature 1444. Two primes 61 and 53 multiply to 3233. Verifying the signature of hello gives True. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
