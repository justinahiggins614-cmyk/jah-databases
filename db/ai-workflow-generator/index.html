1. Description

An AI workflow generator is a rule-based local demo: it builds a workflow from steps in order, and lists the steps with their minutes. The workflow holds 3 steps: gather, sort and share. The workflow holds 5 steps. Steps of 5, 10 and 5 minutes take 20 minutes in total. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
