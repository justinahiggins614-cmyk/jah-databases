History
Writing tools are older than paper: the stylus, the quill, the typewriter, each letting a writer change words without starting again. On computers the editor became a program where words can be moved, undone, and saved, and where many writers can work on one document at once. The public standards for documents, and for keeping them readable, are published openly, and this database is built on them.
