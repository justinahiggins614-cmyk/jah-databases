# Dictionary entries — The Insurance Policy Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## policy

The written contract of insurance, naming the holder, the cover, the dates, and the price, kept as the single true record of what was agreed.

## policyholder

The person or business named on a policy as the one whose loss is covered, recorded with the name written exactly as it stands on the policy.

## insurer

The party that agrees to carry the covered loss under a policy, named in the record so that every policy can be traced to the party that stands behind it.

## cover

What a policy promises to pay for, stated in plain words with its limits, so that a holder can see what is covered and what is not before any loss happens.

## premium

The price paid for a policy over a stated period, recorded with its amount and its due dates, so that the cost of each policy can be counted and checked.

## underwriting

The work of judging a risk before a policy is written, weighing what may happen against what it would cost, and recording the judgement that set the terms.

## claim

A holder's written request for payment after a covered loss, recorded with the date, the loss, and the policy it rests on, and tracked until it is answered.

## deductible

The part of a covered loss the holder pays first, written as an amount in the policy, so that small losses and large ones are handled by the same stated rule.

## exclusion

Something a policy states it does not cover, written in the policy itself, so that a holder is never surprised by a gap the policy had already named.

## renewal

The act of continuing a policy for a further period, on terms written down again, so that cover never lapses by silence and every period has its own record.

## lapse

The ending of cover because a premium was not paid or a term was not met, recorded with its date, so that the record shows plainly when cover stopped.

## endorsement

A written change to a policy, such as added cover or a new holder, filed with the policy it changes, so that the policy as it stands can always be read whole.
