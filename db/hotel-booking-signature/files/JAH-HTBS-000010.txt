History
Travellers have slept under others' roofs for as long as there have been roads: the innkeeper kept a book of who slept where, and for how long, so that the rooms could be counted and the guests could be found. From the inn's ledger to the hotel's booking record, the craft has been the same — record the guest, the room, and the rate together, so that the stay can be proved and the room can be held. A hotel booking is that record: a specification of how bookings are kept, never a live booking and never a payment. This database is built on that public practice of recording every hotel booking true, in full.
