History
Travellers have rented whole houses for their holidays for as long as there have been seaside towns: the owner kept a book of who stayed, for how long, and at what price, so that the house could be counted and the guests could be found. From the owner's ledger to the rental booking record, the craft has been the same — record the guest, the property, and the rate together, so that the stay can be proved and the house can be held. A vacation rental booking is that record: a specification of how bookings are kept, never a live booking and never a payment. This database is built on that public practice of recording every rental booking true, in full.
