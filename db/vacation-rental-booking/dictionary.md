# Dictionary entries — The Vacation Rental Booking Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## vacation rental

A whole house, flat, or cabin let to a guest for a holiday stay, named in the booking with its place and its kind, so that the stay can be read against the property it rests on.

## host

The person or business letting a property for a holiday stay, named in the booking with their place, so that every stay can be traced to who lets the property.

## guest

The person named on a booking as the one who will stay, recorded with the name written exactly as it stands on their travel document.

## stay

The nights a guest will spend in a rental property, written with the arrival and departure dates, so that the length of the booking can be read from the record.

## rental agreement

The written terms under which a property is let, naming the property, the stay, and the rate, kept with the booking, so that both sides can point to the same words.

## nightly rate

The price stated for one night in a rental property, recorded with its amount and what it covers, so that the cost of a stay can be counted. No payment is taken here; the rate is a recorded figure only.

## cleaning fee

The stated price for cleaning a property after a stay, written beside the rate, so that the whole cost of the stay can be counted from the record.

## occupancy

The number of guests a property holds, written on the booking, so that the property held is the property that fits the party that will stay in it.

## house rules

The written rules of a property, such as quiet hours and no smoking, kept with the booking, so that the guest can read what is asked of them before they arrive.

## check-in

The act of arriving and taking the property, recorded with its time, so that the booking moves from held to occupied.

## booking reference

The one code given to a booking when it is made, written on every message about it, so that the booking can be found in the record at any moment.

## property

The house, flat, or cabin let for a stay, named in the booking with its address and its kind, so that the stay can be traced to the place it was spent in.
