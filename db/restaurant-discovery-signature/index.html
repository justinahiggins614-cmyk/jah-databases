design_title = 'Restaurant Discovery Field Guide Index'
design = 'A discovery website: a visitor types a place or a kind of food, and the site lists eating places by kind, such as a noodle house, a bakery cafe, a grill room, or a soup counter, each with a plain description, a price range in round figures, and opening hours. Every listing on this site is a clearly labeled illustrative sample, written to show how a listing works, never a claim about a real restaurant. This record is the field guide index for the whole database: it lists all twenty-three content records in ladder order, simple to compounded to complex, and tells a reader where to start.'
steps = ['Add a Signature tournament bracket with rounds, divisions, and a season', 'Add a Signature creator studio so visitors can build a new restaurant listing', 'Add a Signature review step so every new restaurant listing is checked before it is published', 'Add the Signature restaurant listing in many languages, with the same rules in each', 'Add a Signature offline pack so the restaurant listing works with no connection']
