Six Flags Magic Mountain Rank 1 · simple · operating park · beginner

Six Flags Magic Mountain, in Valencia, California, is a hillside thrill park known for a very large coaster collection spread across steep terrain. Its operating headliners include the hybrid Twisted Colossus, the flying coaster Tatsu, the rotating-seat X2, the launched Full Throttle, and the steel giant Goliath, alongside family areas and shows. The park's history is written in replacements: the original wooden Colossus was reborn as Twisted Colossus, while Deja Vu and Flashback are retired names that older visitors still recall.

Location

Valencia, California, United States

Status

NEW — operating

Lands and areas

Bugs Bunny World, DC Universe, Thrill Capital areas, Samurai Summit, The Underground

Rides NEW

Twisted Colossus (hybrid coaster); Tatsu (flying coaster); X2 (4D coaster); Full Throttle (launched coaster); Goliath (steel coaster)

Rides PAST

Colossus (wooden, 1978-2014); Deja Vu (2001-2011); Flashback (1992-2003)

Theming ecosystems

Thrill-capital theming: superhero districts, a mountain summit village, and a cartoon family land sharing one hillside park

Hidden spots

Publicly-known hidden spots: the shaded paths of Samurai Summit, the quiet overlooks above the lower park, and the back walkways between the mountain coasters

Six Flags Great Adventure Rank 2 · simple · operating park · beginner

Six Flags Great Adventure, in Jackson, New Jersey, is a large park paired with a drive-through safari, and its coaster lineup mixes a celebrated wooden coaster, El Toro, with tall steel rides like Nitro and the single-rail Jersey Devil Coaster. Its past is just as famous: Kingda Ka, once the world's tallest coaster, and Green Lantern both closed after the 2024 season, and the old wooden Racing coaster Rolling Thunder closed in 2013. The park keeps reinventing its skyline while its frontier, movie, and boardwalk districts carry the day-to-day family visit.

Rides PAST

Kingda Ka (2005-2024); Green Lantern (2011-2024); Rolling Thunder (1979-2013)

Theming ecosystems

Safari-adjacent adventure theming: a frontier town, a movie-stunt district, a golden temple area, and a boardwalk midway

Hidden spots
