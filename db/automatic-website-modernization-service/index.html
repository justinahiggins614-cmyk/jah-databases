An Old Photo-Gallery Page Made New Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A modernization service website: a visitor names an old page, and the service writes a plain report of what is wrong with it, then shows a modernized version with clearer text, a layout that fits a phone, and pictures that load in good time. Every report names what changed and what stayed the same, and no page is published until a reader has reviewed it. This record covers An Old Photo-Gallery Page Made New.

A gallery page whose pictures still load but whose layout no longer fits a phone screen. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.
