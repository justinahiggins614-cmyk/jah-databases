Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Wave channels can be intercepted — a probe near a density channel reads the traffic — so plasma networking needs cryptography like any network: encrypted payloads, authenticated endpoints, and key exchange over the same I/O ports. The medium offers one exotic defense: spread-spectrum wave encoding that hides signals below the thermal noise floor, readable only with the spreading code. Security here is conventional crypto plus physics-flavored stealth.
