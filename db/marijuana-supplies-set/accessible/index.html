3. Math proof (standard math + metamath)

The mix ladder for the set’s flagship mix, the Complete Grinder System: A1 Basic (pocket) 40 mm, 17,592.92 mm³; A2 Improved (standard) 70 mm, 115,453.53 mm³; A3 Best (workshop) 100 mm, 314,159.27 mm³. Total cutting passes across the ladder: 1,228. The mixed A2 chamber gains +32.28% over the public record. Each record page carries its own full math proof.

4. Science proof

Shear, not crushing (grinders): opposing diamond teeth pass each other with a small clearance, and the edges cut dried flower along its natural fracture planes.

Water filtration and cooling (bongs): water absorbs heat well, and a beaker’s wide base holds more water than any other single-chamber shape, so smoke has more water to bubble through and more room to cool.

Ground-glass joints (bowls and bongs): the taper itself is the seal — two ground surfaces pressed together by friction, with no gasket and no thread.

Skeptical limit, stated in every record: the science proof explains the physics of the mechanism, not a verdict on the practice; it makes no claim about health, safety, or benefit, and gives no advice about how much of anything to use.
