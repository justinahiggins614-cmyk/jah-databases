Live Streaming Studio Database (live-streaming-studio-signature)

A live streaming studio is a local demo only: it works with set toy frames and viewer counts, and no live stream is connected. The studio shows 30 frames in 1 second. 60 frames in 2 seconds is 30 frames per second. 6000 bits over 2 seconds is 3000 bits per second. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
