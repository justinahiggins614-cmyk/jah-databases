1. Description

A research assistant organizes and summarizes entered sources locally: paste the sources, press Organize, and read the summary. It never pretends to be a live researcher. The word data appears 3 times. 300 words at 200 words a minute take 1.5 minutes to read. The most common word is the: 4 times. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
