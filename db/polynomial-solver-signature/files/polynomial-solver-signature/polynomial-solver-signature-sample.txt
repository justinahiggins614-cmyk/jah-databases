Polynomial Solver Database (polynomial-solver-signature)

A polynomial solver finds the roots of a polynomial: type the coefficients, press Solve, and read the roots. The discriminant of x squared - 5x + 6 is 1, and its roots are 2 and 3. The solver also works out values, slopes and vertices. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: The polynomial x squared - 5x + 6 at x = 0 is 6
