# Dictionary entries — The Payroll Processing Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## payroll

The list of people to be paid in one period, with each person's agreed wage, each withholding, and the amount that remains, all written down.

## pay period

The named span of time one payroll covers, such as a week or a month, written beside every payroll so the figures can be dated.

## wage

The agreed amount a person is paid for their work in one period, written in the payroll before any withholding is taken.

## withholding

An amount held back from a wage, named with its reason, such as a tax or a contribution the person agreed to.

## deduction

A withholding agreed by the person, such as a contribution to a savings plan, named beside the wage it comes from.

## pay slip

The written note given to one person for one period, naming their wage, every withholding, and the amount that remains.

## timesheet

The written record of the hours a person worked in a period, kept beside the payroll, so the wage can be checked against the hours.

## overtime

Hours worked beyond the agreed hours, named in the timesheet and paid at the agreed rate, with the rate written down.

## pay run

One complete pass through a payroll, from the timesheets to the pay slips, checked before any payment is made.

## audit trail

The ordered record of every change made to a payroll, naming who changed what and when, so the figures can be traced back.

## employee

A person named in a payroll, with their agreed wage, their withholdings, and their pay slips, kept in one place.

## payroll register

The kept record of every pay run, holding every pay slip and every figure, in order, so the whole history can be read.
