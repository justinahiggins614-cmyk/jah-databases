The Integral Calculator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An integral calculator integrates functions by the power rule for integrals, and gives the area under a curve between two points. Type a function, and it shows the antiderivative, the substitution and the area. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

  1. What an Integral Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. An integral adds up tiny pieces to find an area. The integral of 1 from 0 to 5 is 5

    Math proof: The integral of x squared is x cubed over 3: from 0 to 3 it is 9. The integral of x + 1 from 0 to 2 is 4, and the integral of 2x from 1 to 3 is 8. The area under y = x from 0 to 4 is 8, and under y = x squared from 0 to 2 it is 2.67. The integral of x from -1 to 1 is 0, because equal areas above and below cancel.

    Science proof: An integral works because area can be added up: the antiderivative reverses differentiation, and a definite integral is the antiderivative at the top minus the bottom. Rectangles approximate the same area from below: 4 rectangles under y = x from 0 to 4 give 6, close to the true 8. That is why the calculator shows the antiderivative first, then the substitution.
