Integral Calculator Database (integral-calculator)

An integral calculator integrates functions by the power rule for integrals, and gives the area under a curve between two points. Type a function, and it shows the antiderivative, the substitution and the area. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: The integral of 1 from 0 to 5 is 5
