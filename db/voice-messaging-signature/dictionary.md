# Dictionary entries — The Voice Messaging Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## voice message

A spoken note, recorded and sent as sound, which the listener plays when they choose, and which keeps the speaker's voice with its words.

## recording

The act of capturing spoken words as sound, with a start and a stop, and a length counted in seconds, so the listener knows how long it is.

## playback

Playing a voice message back, from its start to its end, at the listener's speed, which the listener may pause, repeat, or stop.

## transcript

The written words of a voice message, made from its sound, so that the message can be read as well as heard, with mistakes marked as guesses.

## duration

The length of a voice message, counted in seconds, shown beside it before it is played, so the listener knows what they are taking on.

## delivery

The carrying of a voice message to a listener, marked as sent, delivered, or played, so the sender knows how far it reached.

## played

The mark that a voice message was played to its end, shown beside the message, which a listener may keep private if they wish.

## speed

The rate at which a voice message is played, which the listener may raise or lower, without changing the words.

## storage

The keeping of voice messages after they are sent, for as long as the sender and listener choose, after which they are deleted, not kept.

## quality

How clearly a voice message can be heard, which depends on the recording and the road it travelled, and which is checked, not guessed.

## reply

A voice message sent in answer to another, which is kept beside the one it answers, so the talk can be followed in order.

## ledger

The service's own written record of every voice message, every listener, and every play, kept in order.
