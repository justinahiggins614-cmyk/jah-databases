Vector Calculator Database (vector-calculator)

A vector calculator adds, subtracts and measures arrows: type two vectors, press Calculate, and read the sum, the dot product and the lengths. The vector [3, 4] has length 5.0, and the dot product of [1, 2] and [3, 4] is 11. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
