A Home Feed Built Around Your Interests Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A social website built around interests: a visitor picks an interest, joins a public group for it, and reads and writes public posts about that interest. Profiles show interests, never personal data. There are no private messages anywhere on the network: every conversation is public, every group has written rules, and every moderation action is written in a public log. This record covers A Home Feed Built Around Your Interests.

A home feed built around interests shows a visitor the posts about the things they care about, not the posts of the people they happen to know. The feed needs to be honest about why each post appears, so a visitor can tell whether a post is there because of their interest or because someone paid for it. Without an interest feed, the network shows everyone the same noise, and the visitor who came for one interest must wade through a hundred others to find it.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a home feed built around your interests runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

History

Feeds built around interests grew out of the old habit of subscribing to a magazine about one subject, rather than reading a whole newspaper.

Finding People Who Share One Interest Record 5 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A social website built around interests: a visitor picks an interest, joins a public group for it, and reads and writes public posts about that interest. Profiles show interests, never personal data. There are no private messages anywhere on the network: every conversation is public, every group has written rules, and every moderation action is written in a public log. This record covers Finding People Who Share One Interest.

Finding people who share one interest is the heart of the network: a visitor names an interest and the network shows the public groups and public posts about it, with no personal data shown to anyone.
