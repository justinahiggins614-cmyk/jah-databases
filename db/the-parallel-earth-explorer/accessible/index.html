The The Parallel Earth Explorer Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The parallel earth explorer maps invented earths that differ from our own by one small change, such as an earth where the seas are sweet or where the moon is larger, written as a playful atlas. Every earth is invented, every map is a story, and the explorer never claims a real parallel earth exists or can be visited.
