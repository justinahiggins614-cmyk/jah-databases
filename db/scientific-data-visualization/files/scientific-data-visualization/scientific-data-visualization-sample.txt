Scientific Data Visualization Database (scientific-data-visualization)

Scientific data visualization turns entered numbers into pictures: type the values, press Visualize, and read the statistics. Values 10, 20 and 30 have mean 20.0. The mean of [4, 8, 12, 16] is 10.0. [10, 12, 14, 16, 18] has mean 14.0 and standard deviation 2.83. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
