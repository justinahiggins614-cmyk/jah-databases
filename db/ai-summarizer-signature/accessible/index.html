Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A summarizer keeps the main points of a text and drops the detail. The demo on this page summarizes in this browser by scoring sentences on the words they share with the whole text, with no sign-in and no hosted call.

  1. What Summarizing Means

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. Summarizing keeps the main points and drops the detail, so a long text fits in a few sentences. A text of 500 words becomes a summary of 90 words.

    Math proof: A sentence scores by word frequency: each word in the sentence earns its count in the whole text, and the sentence score is the sum. The top sentences are the highest scorers, kept in their original order. A quarter summary keeps one sentence in four: 20 sentences become 5.

    Science proof: Extractive summarizing works because the words that matter in a text repeat, while passing detail does not. A sentence holding many repeated words is likely a main sentence. Picking top sentences in original order keeps the summary readable, because the text's own order is also its argument's order.
