AI Summarizer Database (ai-summarizer-signature)

A summarizer keeps the main points of a text and drops the detail. The demo on this page summarizes in this browser by scoring sentences on the words they share with the whole text, with no sign-in and no hosted call.

Worked example: A text of 500 words becomes a summary of 90 words.

Demo output:
A text of 500 words becomes a summary of 90 words
