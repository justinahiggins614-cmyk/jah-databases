Digital Certificate Checker Database (digital-certificate-checker)

A digital certificate checker reads certificate data a visitor pastes in and checks it locally: paste the details, press Check, and read the verdict. A live fetch of a site’s certificate honestly needs a network backend, so live checks are BLOCKED and nothing here is faked. A certificate from 2026-01-01 to 2027-01-01 is valid for 365 days. A certificate that ended 2025-06-01, checked 2026-06-01, expired 365 days ago. An RSA key of 4096 bits is strong: True. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
