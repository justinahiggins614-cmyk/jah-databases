Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A gathering website: a host announces a gathering with a written agenda and a set time, guests join from wherever they are, and the gathering keeps a public record of what was shared. There are no private messages anywhere on the site, every gathering has written rules, and every moderation action is written in a public log. This record covers A Gathering With a Written Agenda.

A gathering with a written agenda lets every guest know what will happen and when, because a guest who can read the agenda can choose the parts that matter to them. The agenda should name each part, its time, and its speaker, all in plain words. Without a written agenda, guests sit through the parts they do not need and miss the parts they came for. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
