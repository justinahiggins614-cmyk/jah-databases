Six Flags Magic Mountain Rank 1 · simple · operating park · beginner

The Signature Magic Mountain-inspired design is an original Signature concept called Summit Thrill Park, a hillside park whose headliner is an original hybrid coaster named the Twin Timber Tempest, drawn as two wooden-style tracks braided into one steel spine. Its family land is an original cartoon-free meadow village, and its summit walk is an original quiet-trail design. This is a Signature-original design inspired by the idea of a hillside thrill park, never a copy of the real park.

Location

Valencia, California, United States

Status

NEW — operating

Lands and areas

Bugs Bunny World, DC Universe, Thrill Capital areas, Samurai Summit, The Underground

Rides NEW

Twisted Colossus (hybrid coaster); Tatsu (flying coaster); X2 (4D coaster); Full Throttle (launched coaster); Goliath (steel coaster)

Rides PAST

Colossus (wooden, 1978-2014); Deja Vu (2001-2011); Flashback (1992-2003)

Theming ecosystems

Thrill-capital theming: superhero districts, a mountain summit village, and a cartoon family land sharing one hillside park

Hidden spots

Publicly-known hidden spots: the shaded paths of Samurai Summit, the quiet overlooks above the lower park, and the back walkways between the mountain coasters

Working demo program

RIDES = [
    ('Twisted Colossus', 45),
    ('Tatsu', 40),
    ('X2', 50),
    ('Full Throttle', 35),
    ('Goliath', 30),
]

    total += wait
    print('%s: %d min' % (name, wait))
print('Rides listed: %d' % len(RIDES))
print('Total queue time if ridden once each: %d min' % total)
