# Dictionary entries — The Discussion and Collaboration Rooms Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## room

A shared place where a named group talks and works together, with its own topic, its own members, and its own kept record.

## member

A person who belongs to a room, named in its list, with the right to read and, where the rules allow, to write.

## topic

The subject a room exists to discuss, written at its door, so that talk that belongs elsewhere can be moved, not lost.

## thread

One line of talk inside a room, kept in order from the first message to the last, so it can be read from the start.

## message

One thing written by one member in a room, kept with its author and its time, and never changed without a mark.

## moderator

A member trusted to keep a room's rules, who can move talk, quiet a member, and close a thread, with every act written down.

## shared record

The room's own kept copy of its talk and its work, held where every member can read it, in the order it happened.

## presence

The simple fact of who is in the room now, shown so that members know who can answer and who is away.

## invitation

A named offer to join a room, given by a member and accepted by the newcomer, written in the room's list.

## room rules

The written rules of a room, agreed by its members, naming what may be said, what may be shared, and what is out of bounds.

## archive

The room's old threads, kept and readable after the talk has ended, so that nothing the group learned is thrown away.

## shared work

Work done together inside a room, such as a document or a plan, kept beside the talk that shaped it, with every change named.
