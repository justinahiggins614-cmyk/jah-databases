# The Patent Portfolio Management Database

The Patent Portfolio Management Database is the patent portfolio management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full patent portfolio management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database intellectual-property-portfolio-database, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
