# Dictionary entries — The Patent Portfolio Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## patent portfolio management

The recorded keeping of a set of patents held by one owner, so that every grant, term, and licence can be followed from the paper that granted it to the day it ends.

## patent portfolio

A set of patents held together by one owner, kept in the record with each patent named, so that the whole holding can be counted.

## patent

The written grant from a public office that gives an inventor exclusive rights in an invention for a stated term, kept in the record so that what was granted can be read plainly.

## term

The stated span of years for which a patent lasts, written in the record so that how long the rights run can be read plainly.

## owner

The person or body that holds a patent, named in the record with the patent, so that who holds the grant can be traced to the paper.

## licence

The written permission from a patent owner for another party to use the invention, kept in the record with its terms, so that the permission can be read in full.

## maintenance fee

The amount charged to keep a patent in force, written in the record with its amount and its due date, so that what was charged to keep the grant can be counted.

## assignment

The written transfer of patent rights from one holder to another, kept in the record so that the change of holding can be followed.

## family

The set of patent filings for one invention in different offices, kept in the record so that the whole family can be counted together.

## expiry date

The stated last day on which a patent remains in force, written in the record with the patent, so that the life of the grant can be read plainly.

## valuation

The published estimate of what a patent is worth, kept in the record with its method and its date, so that the estimate can be read beside its basis.

## portfolio review

The written count and check of a patent portfolio at a stated date, kept so that the holding can be read at a glance.
