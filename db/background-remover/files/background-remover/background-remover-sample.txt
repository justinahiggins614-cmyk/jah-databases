Background Remover Database (background-remover)

A background remover keeps the subject of a picture and drops everything behind it. Upload a picture, pick the background colour, set the tolerance, and download the cut-out. It works by colour distance and flood fill from the edges — not by machine learning, and the page says so. Everything happens on a canvas in this browser, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Worked example: A picture of 800 by 600 keeps a subject of 400 by 300 and drops the rest.
