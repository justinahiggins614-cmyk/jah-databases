The Background Remover Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A background remover keeps the subject of a picture and drops everything behind it. Upload a picture, pick the background colour, set the tolerance, and download the cut-out. It works by colour distance and flood fill from the edges — not by machine learning, and the page says so. Everything happens on a canvas in this browser, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/background-remover/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What Removing a Background Means

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. Removing a background keeps the subject of a picture and drops everything behind it, leaving the subject alone. A picture of 800 by 600 keeps a subject of 400 by 300 and drops the rest.

    Math proof: Colour distance is the difference between two colours, counted across red, green and blue. A tolerance of 30 removes colours within 30 steps of the background colour. Flood fill is a connected set: starting from the 4 corners, it clears every background pixel it can reach without crossing the subject. Keeping is subtraction: 480,000 pixels minus 200,000 cleared leaves 280,000 kept.

    Science proof: A picture is a grid of pixels, and a plain background is a region of nearly the same colour. Flood fill from the edges follows that region until it meets the subject's outline, which is a change in colour. That is why a plain background clears in one pass and a busy one does not, and why fine hair, which blends into the background pixel by pixel, cannot be separated by colour alone.
