The Metal Bowl

The metal bowl is one of the basic supplies of the marijuana supplies archive: a metal bowl that works by the bowl holds the material in its metal cup while air drawn through the hole in the bottom of the cup, usually fitted with a metal screen, carries smoke down the joint and into the water pipe. It is made from brass bowl body, stainless-steel screen, ground joint to fit 14 mm female downstem seats, and a common example measures 14 mm male joint, cup 20 mm diameter at the rim and 16 mm deep, screen 15 mm diameter, wall 2 mm thick. The metal bowl is the oldest bowl form of all: brass pipe bowls appear in museum collections as inverted cones with decorative rims, made long before glass bowls existed. Metal bowls are favoured for resilience and for keeping their shape under repeated heating, and the common metals are brass, aluminium, and stainless steel, each with its own trade-offs. Brass gives a warm golden look and good heat conduction but needs polishing and can develop a taste over time; aluminium is very light and cheap but heats quickly; stainless steel resists rust and heat, cleans easily, and does not alter flavour. Modern designs combine metals, such as an anodised aluminium bowl with a stainless-steel sleeve insert that regulates heat and keeps the material in contact with steel only. Metal bowls fit the same 14 mm and 18 mm joints as glass bowls, and they survive drops that would shatter glass. SCIENCE PROOF
The metal bowl works by heat conduction and by strength. Brass conducts heat well, so the cup warms evenly and the material at the edges burns at nearly the same rate as the material in the middle, and the stainless-steel sleeve in modern designs regulates that heat further, keeping the load in contact with steel only. The cup is a frustum, wide at the rim and narrow at the bottom, so the material sits in a cone that funnels smoke toward the hole, and the screen over the hole stops fine particles without blocking the airflow. Metal survives what glass does not: a dropped metal bowl dents where a glass bowl shatters, and a metal bowl can be scrubbed with a brush that would scratch glass. import hashlib, math
def compute():
    R = 10.0; r = 4.0; h = 16.0; oR = 12.0; o_r = 6.0; o_h = 18.0; rho_metal = 8.5; scr = 15.0; joint = 14.0; rho_pack = 0.15
    cup_mm3 = math.pi * h / 3.0 * (R * R + R * r + r * r)
    cup_cm3 = cup_mm3 / 1000.0
    cap = cup_cm3 * rho_pack
    outer_mm3 = math.pi * o_h / 3.0 * (oR * oR + oR * o_r + o_r * o_r)
    metal_cm3 = (outer_mm3 - cup_mm3) / 1000.0
    mass = metal_cm3 * rho_metal
    screen_area = math.pi * (scr / 2.0) ** 2
    return {"cup_volume_cm3": round(cup_cm3, 2), "capacity_g": round(cap, 2), "metal_volume_cm3": round(metal_cm3, 2), "mass_g": round(mass, 2), "screen_area_mm2": round(screen_area, 2), "joint_mm": joint}
def main():
    a = compute(); b = compute()
    sa = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(a.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    sb = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(b.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
