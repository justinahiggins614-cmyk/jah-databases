The Gravity Bong
Record JAH-MJS-000029 — public

The gravity bong is one of the basic supplies of the marijuana supplies archive: a gravity bong that works by moving water creates suction that pulls air through the bowl into a chamber, and pushing the chamber back down into the water forces the collected smoke out through the opening in one steady delivery. It is made from plastic 2-litre bottle with its bottom cut off, plastic bucket or larger container, metal or glass bowl fitted to the bottle cap, aerator screen, water as the displacement medium, and a common example measures 2-litre bottle chamber about 280 mm tall and 105 mm diameter, bucket larger than the bottle, lift height 180 mm. The gravity bong comes from do-it-yourself culture rather than from a glass workshop. The bucket bong is made from two containers, a smaller bottle with its bottom cut off and a bowl fitted to its cap, set inside a larger container of water, and the waterfall bong from a single bottle with a small hole near its base. Both use water displacement instead of the lungs to move air, and both are documented in the same public account that notes neither actually fits the strict definition of a bong, because the smoke is not bubbled through water to cool and filter it. The device is also called a GB, a grav, a geeb, or a bucket bong, and the common pairing in guides is a 2-litre bottle as the chamber with a 16 to 20 ounce bottle, or a bucket, as the water container. Glass gravity bongs are also sold, but the homemade bottle-and-bucket version remains the form most people picture. SCIENCE PROOF
The gravity bong works by displacement, not by suction from the lungs. When the bottle is lifted out of the bucket, water leaves the inside of the bottle, and the space the water leaves must be filled by something: the only opening is through the bowl, so air is pulled across the burning material and into the chamber. The volume drawn in is the cross-section of the bottle times the lift height, which is why a wide bottle lifted a short way draws the same volume as a narrow bottle lifted further. Pushing the bottle back down reverses the process: the water level inside rises, the pressure in the chamber rises above the outside air, and the smoke is forced out through the opening. The waterfall version works the same way in reverse: water drains out through a hole near the base, and the speed of the draining water follows Torricelli's law, the square root of twice gravity times the height of the water column. The water never filters the smoke in either version, because nothing bubbles through it: it only moves air, so the draw is not cooled or diffused the way a water pipe cools it. """Demo: gravity bong displaced volume and waterfall drain rate — The Gravity Bong."""
import hashlib, math
def compute():
    vol = 2000.0; d = 105.0; lift = 180.0; hole = 6.0; col = 200.0
    displaced = math.pi * (d / 2.0) ** 2 * lift / 1000.0
    v = math.sqrt(2.0 * 9.81 * (col / 1000.0))
    hole_area = math.pi * (hole / 2.0) ** 2
    flow = hole_area * v * 1000.0 / 1000.0
    drain_time = vol / flow
    return {"chamber_volume_mL": vol, "displaced_volume_mL": round(displaced, 2), "lift_mm": lift, "drain_velocity_m_s": round(v, 2), "drain_flow_mL_per_s": round(flow, 2), "drain_time_s": round(drain_time, 2)}
def main():
    a = compute(); b = compute()
    sa = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(a.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    sb = hashlib.sha256(repr(sorted(b.items())).encode()).hexdigest()
    for key in ["chamber_volume_mL", "displaced_volume_mL", "lift_mm", "drain_velocity_m_s", "drain_flow_mL_per_s", "drain_time_s"]:
        print(f"{key}: {a[key]}")
    print(f"Run 1 SHA-256: {sa}")
    print(f"Run 2 SHA-256: {sb}")
    print(f"Deterministic: {'MATCH' if sa == sb else 'MISMATCH'}")
if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
