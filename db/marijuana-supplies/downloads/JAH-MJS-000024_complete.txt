The Lockable Stash Box
Record JAH-MJS-000024 — public
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000024

The Lockable Stash Box is a cleaning and maintenance supply for smoking pipes, and this record describes the standard kit as it is actually sold and used. The kit contains a hard-shell box with a 3-digit resettable combination lock and key backup, a dual silicone seal, a removable silicone organiser tray, adjustable dividers, and two odour-absorbing pouches. Its parts are made from high-impact ABS plastic shell, dual silicone seals, activated-carbon fibre lining, stainless steel lock hasp, chosen because each material suits its own job: cotton absorbs, nylon scrubs, steel scrapes without bending, and brass works on stubborn buildup without gouging. The kit measures outside 235 mm by 190 mm by 127 mm (9.25 by 7.5 by 5 inches); inside 210 mm by 165 mm by 100 mm; wall thickness 4 mm; lock dials 3 digits. In use, the dual silicone seal compresses when the lid closes, trapping air and odour inside, while the combination lock holds the lid shut and the adjustable dividers keep contents from sliding. That combination is what makes the kit more than a loose handful of tools: every step of a full clean, from the first dry pass through the stem to the last wipe of the outside, has a tool made for it. Routine use is simple. Set the combination from the 0-0-0 default, arrange contents in the tray and dividers, close the lid fully so both seals seat, scramble the dials, and store the box upright in a cool dry place. Care of the kit matters as much as care of the pipe. Replace the odour pouches when they stop working, wipe the seals clean so grit does not break the airtight seat, keep the backup key somewhere separate from the box, and never force the lock, since forcing it can jam the hasp. The common variants are the bamboo humidor-style box with a combination lock; waterproof hard case; soft ballistic-nylon bag with a zipper combination lock; large 11 by 9.75 by 5 inch family size. Legal note: laws on cannabis and cannabis accessories vary by jurisdiction; check local law before buying, carrying, or using this item.

SCIENCE PROOF
The science of this supply is the science of dissolving, scrubbing, and separating, and each part of the kit uses a different piece of it. Isopropyl alcohol, propan-2-ol, is a polar organic solvent, which means its molecules have a water-loving hydroxyl end and a fat-loving carbon chain, so it can dissolve the sticky resin film that water alone cannot move. Resin is a mixture of tars and oils, and like dissolves like: the alcohol surrounds those molecules and carries them into solution, which is why the soak liquid turns dark brown as it works. Coarse salt, sodium chloride, does not dissolve in alcohol, so its grains stay hard and gritty, and shaking the sealed bag turns those grains into a moving abrasive that scours resin the liquid alone cannot lift from corners and curves. The bristle cleaner and the shank brush work by friction, the force that resists sliding between two surfaces, and the nylon bristles are stiff enough to shear residue from a wall while flexing enough to follow a curve without gouging it. Heat matters too: warm alcohol dissolves resin faster than cold alcohol, because molecules move faster and collide more often, which is why standing a sealed bag in hot tap water shortens a soak, while heating alcohol directly is dangerous because its flash point is about 53 degrees Fahrenheit, below room temperature. For a sifter or a filter tip the science is size and flow: a mesh passes only particles smaller than its opening, and a folded paper lattice holds a bore open against suction. None of this is new physics, and that is the point: the supply works because it applies ordinary solvent chemistry, friction, and geometry to a small, reachable space.

HISTORY
The stash box is the descendant of the tobacco humidor and the cash box: a container that keeps contents fresh, organised, and out of other people's hands. As cannabis moved into legal retail in several jurisdictions, makers added the two features buyers asked for most, odour control and a lock. Odour control came from gaskets and carbon linings borrowed from food storage and filtration, and the lock came from travel cases, a resettable three-digit combination with a key backup for a forgotten code. Reviewers in 2026 list boxes from about 8.9 by 7.1 by 3.9 inches up to 11 by 9.75 by 5 inches, in ABS plastic, bamboo, or ballistic nylon, and the honest makers say the same thing in their manuals: the lock stops casual hands and children, it does not stop a determined person with tools, and the seal works only when both zipper pulls or both lid seals are fully seated.
