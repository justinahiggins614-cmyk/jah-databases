SCIENCE PROOF
The fabric grow pot works by air pruning, and the mechanism is simple enough to state in one sentence: a root tip that reaches air stops growing and branches instead. In a rigid plastic pot the root hits a solid wall, turns, and circles the container, and the circling mass chokes water and nutrient uptake. In a fabric pot the wall is porous nonwoven polypropylene, so air passes through the fabric and dries the root tip on contact, which triggers lateral branching and a dense fibrous root mass with far more root ends — the sources report up to 200 percent more root ends than rigid containers. The same porosity gives superior drainage: excess water passes through the fabric instead of pooling at the bottom, and the sources quote water passing at 50 to 150 litres per square metre per second through the nonwoven wall. Fabric thickness is measured in grams per square metre: budget bags sit near 200 to 250 GSM, 300 GSM is the long-term recommendation, and premium pots reach 500 GSM. The trade-off is that the breathable wall lets the contents dry faster than a sealed pot, so the container needs checking more often. HISTORY
The fabric grow pot is a soft-sided container made of breathable nonwoven fabric, and its history starts with the Smart Pot, which has been made since the 1980s and proved that a porous wall changes how roots behave. Before fabric pots, containers were rigid plastic or clay, and roots reaching the wall would circle it, strangling the plant and starving it of water and nutrients. The fabric pot breaks that pattern: when a root tip reaches the porous wall it meets dry air and stops, and the plant answers by sending out branching roots behind the tip instead. The form spread through garden centres and grow shops in every size from a quarter gallon to a thousand gallons, with 5 gallons the common home size and 7, 10, 15, and 20 gallons for larger plants. Modern pots use needlepunched nonwoven polypropylene in 200 to 500 grams per square metre, double-stitched seams, and sewn handles, and some have square bottoms for stability. import hashlib, math
def compute():

    vol_cm3 = math.pi * (d_cm / 2.0) ** 2 * h_cm
    vol_L = vol_cm3 / 1000.0
    vol_gal = vol_L / 3.78541
    side_area = math.pi * d_cm * h_cm
    bottom_area = math.pi * (d_cm / 2.0) ** 2
    total_area = side_area + bottom_area
    mass_kg = vol_cm3 * density / 1000.0
