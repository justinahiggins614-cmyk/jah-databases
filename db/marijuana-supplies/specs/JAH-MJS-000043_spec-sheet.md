## Spec
- **Care:** Empty water after use; rinse with warm water; clean with isopropyl alcohol and coarse salt for residue; dry fully before storage; handle glass gently
- **Dimensions:** 152.4 mm (6 in) tall; tube 25 mm diameter; beaker base 82 mm diameter, 50 mm tall; water depth 31.75 mm (1.25 in)
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass body, 5 mm wall; glass downstem; glass bowl, 10 mm joint
- **Mechanism:** Smoke travels down the downstem and bubbles through water, then rises through the neck to the mouthpiece; the beaker base holds the water and steadies the piece
- **Use:** Fill with water to about 31.75 mm so the downstem slits are submerged, fit the bowl, light and draw slowly, clear the chamber, empty and rinse after use
- **Variants:** Beaker base; straight tube; percolator mini; ice-catcher mini; silicone mini; 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 inch heights; 10 mm and 14 mm joints
