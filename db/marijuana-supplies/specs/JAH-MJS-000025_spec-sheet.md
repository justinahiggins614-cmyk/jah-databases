# The Beaker Bong — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000025 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000025

## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; rinse the base; clean glass with isopropyl alcohol and salt; dry fully; replace a cracked downstem
- **Dimensions:** 300 mm tall; flared base 100 mm diameter at the foot; neck 50 mm diameter; water depth 40 mm in the base
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass tube, base, downstem, and bowl; plain water as the filtration medium; optional ice pinches in the neck
- **Mechanism:** Smoke from the bowl travels down the downstem below the water line in the flared base, bubbles up through the water, collects in the neck, and is drawn to the mouthpiece when the bowl is lifted or the carb is opened
- **Use:** Add water so the downstem sits below the surface, pack the bowl, cover the carb, light while drawing slowly until the neck fills, then open the carb and inhale
- **Variants:** Standard beaker; beaker with ice pinches; beaker with percolator; thick-glass (5 mm and 9 mm wall) versions; mini beakers under 200 mm
