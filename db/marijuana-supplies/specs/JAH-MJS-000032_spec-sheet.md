# The Metal Bowl — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000032 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000032

## Spec
- **Care:** Empty the cup after use; scrub with a brush; soak in isopropyl alcohol; polish brass when it dulls; dry fully to prevent tarnish
- **Dimensions:** 14 mm male joint; cup 20 mm diameter at the rim and 16 mm deep; screen 15 mm diameter; wall 2 mm thick
- **Materials:** Brass bowl body; stainless-steel screen; ground joint to fit 14 mm female downstem seats
- **Mechanism:** The bowl holds the material in its metal cup; air drawn through the screen and the hole in the bottom of the cup carries smoke down the joint and into the water pipe; lifting the bowl clears the pipe
- **Use:** Fit the screen, pack the cup loosely, seat the bowl in the downstem, light while drawing, then lift the bowl to clear
- **Variants:** Brass bowl; stainless-steel bowl; anodised aluminium bowl with steel sleeve; titanium bowl; 14 mm and 18 mm, male and female
