## Spec
- **Care:** Wash with warm soapy water or in a dishwasher; do not leave a lit ember pressed against one spot; check the lid lip for wear and replace the lid if it no longer seals
- **Dimensions:** Dish 100 mm across and 28 mm deep; wall 4 mm thick; lid 104 mm across and 5 mm thick; about 124 grams total
- **Materials:** Food-grade silicone rubber; heat tolerance about 230 degrees C; no metal or plastic in the ash path
- **Mechanism:** Ash and spent material fall into the soft rubber dish; rim rests hold a cigarette or joint by gravity; the lid lip seals by contact to keep ash in and smell down
- **Use:** Set on a flat surface, rest the cigarette in a rim rest, tap ash into the dish, close the lid when the session ends or wind picks up, empty when ash reaches a third of the depth
- **Variants:** Round and square dishes; hinged and loose lids; folding travel forms; plain and glow colours; pocket and table sizes
