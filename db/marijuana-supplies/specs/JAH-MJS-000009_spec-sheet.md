## Spec
- **Care:** Brush out loose material after use; wipe metal with a damp cloth and dry at once; oil wooden trays occasionally; do not soak wood
- **Dimensions:** Medium reference size 27 cm long x 16 cm wide x 2.5 cm deep; small 18 x 14 x 1.5 cm; large 34 x 27.5 cm
- **Materials:** Powder-coated tin-plated steel tray, rolled rim; optional bamboo or walnut wood versions
- **Mechanism:** Flat rigid work surface with a raised retaining lip; gravity and the lip keep loose material on the tray
- **Use:** Place papers, loose material and tools on the tray; roll on the flat base; gather spilled material from the corners
- **Variants:** Small pocket tray, medium standard tray, large table tray; metal, wood, bamboo, plastic and glass versions; trays with built-in storage compartments
