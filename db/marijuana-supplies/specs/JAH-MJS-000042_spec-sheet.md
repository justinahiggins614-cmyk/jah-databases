## Spec
- **Care:** Rinse glass screens in warm water and dry fully; discard a metal screen that is warped, torn, or clogged beyond cleaning; keep spares in a small tin
- **Dimensions:** Metal mesh screens 15.9 mm (5/8 in) diameter, also 12.7 mm (1/2 in) and 19.05 mm (3/4 in); glass daisy screen 12 mm diameter
- **Materials:** Stainless-steel woven mesh, 0.25 mm wire; brass mesh variant; borosilicate glass daisy screen variant
- **Mechanism:** Mesh disc sits at the bottom of the bowl; woven openings pass air and block ash and particles; glass daisy petals hold the screen above the bowl opening and channel air around it
- **Use:** Press the mesh disc gently into the bowl so it sits flat over the opening, or set the glass daisy in the bowl; replace a mesh screen when it clogs or warps
- **Variants:** Brass mesh; stainless-steel mesh; titanium mesh; borosilicate glass daisy; glass honeycomb; quartz screens
