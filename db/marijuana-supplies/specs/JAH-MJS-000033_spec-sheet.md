# The Ceramic Bowl — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000033 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000033

## Spec
- **Care:** Empty ash after cooling; soak in isopropyl alcohol, rinse, and dry; handle ceramic carefully as it chips and cracks if dropped; do not quench a hot bowl in cold water
- **Dimensions:** Cup 22 mm across and 16 mm deep; joint 14 mm; wall 3 mm thick; about 45 mm tall overall
- **Materials:** Glazed stoneware ceramic; 14 mm male ground joint; metal screen optional
- **Mechanism:** Ground herb packed in the ceramic cup; flame lights the surface; smoke passes through the hole in the cup floor into the downstem; lifting the bowl clears the pipe
- **Use:** Check the joint size, set a screen if used, pack ground herb loosely, slide the bowl into the joint, light while drawing gently, lift to clear, empty ash after cooling
- **Variants:** 14 mm and 18 mm joints; shallow and deep cups; clear, coloured, and matte glazes; hand-painted and wheel-thrown finishes
