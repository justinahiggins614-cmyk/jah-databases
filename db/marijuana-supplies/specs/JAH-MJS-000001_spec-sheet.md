# The Manual Herb Grinder — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000001 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000001

## Spec
- **Care:** Brush out residue after use; wash aluminium parts in warm soapy water, dry fully; clean the mesh screen with a soft brush; keep the magnet faces free of metal dust
- **Dimensions:** 63 mm diameter x 48 mm tall; grinding chamber 28 mm deep; 4 pieces
- **Materials:** Anodised aluminium (6061) body; stainless-steel teeth; stainless-steel 100-micron mesh screen; neodymium magnet in lid
- **Mechanism:** Two halves with opposing diamond teeth; hand-twisted in opposite directions; ground herb falls through holes into a storage chamber; fine particles pass the mesh into a kief chamber
- **Use:** Break dried herb into pea-sized pieces, place between the teeth, replace the lid, twist 10 to 15 times until the halves turn freely, then collect ground herb from the storage chamber
- **Variants:** 2-piece (grind only); 3-piece (grind plus storage); 4-piece (grind, storage, and kief screen); wood, plastic, zinc, aluminium, and stainless-steel versions
