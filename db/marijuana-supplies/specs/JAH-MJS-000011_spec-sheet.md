## Spec
- **Care:** Refill only with butane in a ventilated place away from flame; purge air from the tank before refilling; keep away from heat above 50 C; do not puncture
- **Dimensions:** Pocket size about 80 mm tall x 25 mm wide x 15 mm thick; fuel capacity about 8 g of butane; flame length about 25 mm
- **Materials:** Metal or high-temperature plastic body, brass jet nozzle, piezoelectric crystal igniter, butane fuel tank with refill valve
- **Mechanism:** Pressurised liquid butane vaporises on release, mixes with air in a venturi, and is ignited by a piezoelectric spark; the jet nozzle shapes a narrow premixed flame
- **Use:** Point the nozzle at the target, press and hold the ignition button, release to stop; adjust the gas valve for flame size
- **Variants:** Single-jet pocket torch, dual-jet torch, triple-jet torch, table-top torch, windproof soft-flame lighter
