# The Ash-Catcher Bowl — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000034 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000034

## Spec
- **Care:** Empty the catcher after each session; rinse with warm water and soak in isopropyl alcohol when resin builds up; handle glass carefully as it breaks if dropped
- **Dimensions:** Bowl 20 mm across and 16 mm deep; catcher chamber 30 mm across, water depth 20 mm; lower joint 14 mm male at 90 degrees
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass throughout; built-in flower bowl fused to the catcher chamber; no metal or plastic in the smoke path
- **Mechanism:** Smoke passes from the bowl into the wider chamber, where it slows and ash falls out into the water; the cleaned smoke continues into the bong
- **Use:** Match the joint size and angle to the bong, add water to about 60 percent of the chamber if wet, pack the built-in bowl, light while drawing gently, empty the catcher after the session
- **Variants:** 14 mm and 18 mm joints; 45 and 90 degree angles; wet and dry forms; percless, inline-perc, and honeycomb chambers; built-in bowl or separate bowl
