# Rolling Papers — Spec sheet

JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000007

## Spec
- **Care:** Keep booklets dry and flat; store away from damp; use papers before the gum strip dries out or picks up dust
- **Dimensions:** Single wide 70 mm x 36 mm; 1 1/4 size 78 mm x 44 mm; 1 1/2 size 78 mm x 60 mm; King size 110 mm x 44 mm; King slim 110 mm x 53 mm
- **Materials:** Hemp, rice, flax, or wood-pulp paper at about 10 to 14 grams per square metre; gum arabic adhesive strip along one long edge
- **Mechanism:** Ground herb is spread along the paper, the paper is rolled into a cylinder, the gum strip is moistened and pressed to seal the seam, and the finished roll is lit at the tip and drawn from the filter end
- **Use:** Place a filter tip at one end, spread ground herb evenly, roll the paper into a cylinder, lick the gum strip, seal the seam, and twist the open tip closed
- **Variants:** Hemp, rice, flax, wood-pulp, and clear cellulose papers; bleached white and unbleached brown; flavoured papers; rolls of 3 to 5 metres for custom lengths
