## Spec
- **Care:** Brush out loose material after each use; wash the belt with warm soapy water and dry fully; keep the rollers free of sticky residue
- **Dimensions:** 70 mm long (king size 110 mm version also made); rollers 12 mm diameter; finished roll about 70 mm long x 8 mm diameter
- **Materials:** ABS plastic frame, steel roller cores, cotton-polyester looped belt, stainless hinge pins
- **Mechanism:** Two parallel rollers with a looped belt; moving the rollers together deepens the belt groove and compresses material into a cylinder; rotating the rollers draws the paper around it
- **Use:** Open the rollers, spread material evenly in the belt groove, close and roll forward to shape, feed the paper adhesive-side up, roll to wrap, seal the adhesive edge
- **Variants:** Pocket belt roller, metal roller, king-size 110 mm roller, adjustable-thickness roller, injector tube filler (separate family)
