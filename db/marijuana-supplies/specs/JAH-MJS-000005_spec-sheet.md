# The Glass Spoon Pipe — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000005 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000005

## Spec
- **Care:** Empty ash after cooling; soak in isopropyl alcohol and coarse salt, shake gently, rinse, and dry; handle glass carefully as it breaks if dropped
- **Dimensions:** About 100 mm long; bowl about 20 mm across and 18 mm deep; stem bore about 6 mm; mouthpiece about 10 mm across
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass, formed in one piece; no metal or plastic in the smoke path
- **Mechanism:** Bowl holds ground herb; a small hole in the bowl floor leads to the stem; the carb hole on the side of the bowl controls airflow; covering the carb fills the chamber with smoke, uncovering it lets fresh air push the smoke through
- **Use:** Pack ground herb loosely in the bowl, hold the pipe, cover the carb with a thumb, light the surface of the herb while drawing gently, then release the carb to clear the smoke
- **Variants:** Classic spoon; Sherlock with a curved stem; hammer with a flat base; sidecar with an offset mouthpiece; colour and fuming variations in the glass
