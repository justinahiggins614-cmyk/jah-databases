## Spec
- **Care:** Tap out ash after cooling; soak in isopropyl alcohol, rinse, and dry; handle glass carefully as it breaks if dropped; keep the stone fitted or replace it if it loosens
- **Dimensions:** Tube 110 mm long; bowl end 18 mm across; mouthpiece 8 mm across; outer diameter 22 mm at the bowl end; stone 10 mm across
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass tube; chillum stone of fitted pebble or ceramic; safi cloth optional
- **Mechanism:** Ground herb packed in the wide bowl end; flame lights the surface; smoke travels straight through the tapered bore to the mouthpiece; the chillum stone stops loose material
- **Use:** Set the stone inside, pack the bowl end by pressing and turning in ground herb, hold at a slight angle, light while drawing slowly, tap out the ash when spent
- **Variants:** Clay, glass, wood, stone, and metal; straight and slightly flared bores; with or without a safi cloth; pocket and ceremonial lengths
