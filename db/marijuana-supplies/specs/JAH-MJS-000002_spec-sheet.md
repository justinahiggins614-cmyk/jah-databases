## Spec
- **Care:** Empty the chamber after use; brush blades clean; wipe the housing; recharge before storage; do not immerse the motor housing in water
- **Dimensions:** 110 mm tall x 55 mm diameter; grinding chamber holds up to 3 g per load
- **Materials:** ABS or aluminium housing; stainless-steel blades or milling plate; lithium-ion rechargeable battery; USB-C port
- **Mechanism:** Battery-powered motor spins blades or a milling plate; some models sense load and reverse or change speed; grind time controls fineness, about 2 seconds coarse, 4 seconds medium, 6 seconds finer
- **Use:** Charge fully, load dried herb without overfilling, close the chamber, press and hold the button for the chosen time, then open and collect the ground herb
- **Variants:** Blade grinders; burr and milling-plate grinders; mill-and-fill models that drop ground herb into a cone; replaceable-battery and USB-C rechargeable models
