## Spec
- **Care:** Brush the bat bore with a pipe cleaner after use; empty and brush the stash chamber; wipe the case with a dry cloth; keep the spring chamber free of grit
- **Dimensions:** Case 102 mm long x 28 mm wide x 18 mm thick; stash chamber 16 mm diameter x 70 mm deep; bat 76 mm long, 8 mm diameter, bowl 6 mm diameter x 12 mm deep
- **Materials:** Hardwood (maple) case; brass bat with copper tip; steel spring in bat chamber; swivel hardwood lid
- **Mechanism:** Push the bat into the stash chamber and twist to pack a single hit; light the bowl end and draw through the straight tube; blow through the bat to clear ash
- **Use:** Grind material finely, fill the stash chamber, push the bat in and twist to pack, light and inhale one hit, clear the bat and return it to its chamber
- **Variants:** Maple, walnut, and rosewood cases; aluminium and acrylic cases; spring-loaded bat chamber; swivel lid; kits with poker and grinder compartment
