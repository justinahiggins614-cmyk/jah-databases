# The Digital Pocket Scale — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000016 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000016

## Spec
- **Care:** Calibrate with a 50 g weight when new, after battery changes or drops; keep the platform clean and dry; do not overload past 50 g; store with the cover on
- **Dimensions:** Pocket size about 100 mm x 65 mm x 18 mm; platform about 60 mm x 55 mm; capacity 50 g; readability 0.01 g
- **Materials:** ABS plastic body, stainless steel weighing platform, strain-gauge load cell, LCD display, two CR2032 lithium batteries
- **Mechanism:** Strain-gauge load cell: the load bends a metal beam, the gauge resistance changes, the processor converts the voltage to a weight and displays it
- **Use:** Place on a flat stable surface, turn on, wait for zero, tare the container, add the material gently to the centre, read the display
- **Variants:** 50 g x 0.01 g pocket scale, 100 g x 0.01 g scale, 500 g x 0.01 g scale, 1000 g x 0.1 g scale; with or without a protective cover
