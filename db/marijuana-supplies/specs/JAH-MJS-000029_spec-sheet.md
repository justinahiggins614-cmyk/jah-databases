# The Gravity Bong — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000029 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000029

## Spec
- **Care:** Rinse the bottle and bucket after use; replace a bottle that is cracked or cloudy; keep the bowl and screen clean; dry fully before storage
- **Dimensions:** 2-litre bottle chamber about 280 mm tall and 105 mm diameter; bucket larger than the bottle; lift height 180 mm
- **Materials:** Plastic 2-litre bottle with its bottom cut off; plastic bucket or larger container; metal or glass bowl fitted to the bottle cap; aerator screen; plain water as the displacement medium
- **Mechanism:** Lifting the bottle out of the water creates a vacuum that pulls air in through the bowl; pushing the bottle back down displaces the water and forces the collected smoke out through the opening in one steady delivery
- **Use:** Submerge the bottle to its neck, light the bowl while lifting slowly until the chamber fills, remove the bowl, place the mouth over the opening, and push the bottle down steadily while breathing in
- **Variants:** Bucket gravity bong; waterfall gravity bong with a drain hole near the base; glass gravity bong with a machined chamber; lung variant using a plastic bag instead of water
