## Spec
- **Care:** Wash with warm soapy water; dry to keep the finish bright; handle the poker by its handle as the tip gets hot; store the poker clipped to the rim
- **Dimensions:** Dish 100 mm across and 30 mm deep; wall 2 mm thick; poker 4 mm across and 120 mm long; about 282 grams total
- **Materials:** Stainless steel dish and poker; brushed finish; no plastic in the ash path
- **Mechanism:** Ash and spent material fall into the steel dish; rim rests hold a cigarette or joint by gravity; the poker stirs ash and clears clogged bowls
- **Use:** Set on a flat surface, rest the cigarette in a rim rest, tap ash into the dish, use the poker to stir and clear, clip the poker back after use
- **Variants:** Stainless steel, brass, and cast iron; round and square dishes; clip-on and socket pokers; two to six rim rests
