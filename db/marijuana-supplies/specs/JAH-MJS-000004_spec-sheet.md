## Spec
- **Care:** Empty the chamber after use; replace balloons when worn; clean the filling chamber and screens; wipe the housing; keep the air intake clear
- **Dimensions:** About 200 mm tall x 200 mm diameter base; 100 W convection heater; Easy Valve balloon holds about 3 litres when filled
- **Materials:** Aluminium heat exchanger; stainless-steel filling chamber and screens; food-grade balloon material; mains-powered heater and air pump
- **Mechanism:** Pure convection in the Classic: heated air passes through the herb and carries vapour into the balloon; the Hybrid adds conduction and a whip; temperature range 130 C to 230 C (266 F to 446 F) on nine dial levels
- **Use:** Grind medium-fine, fill the chamber loosely, preheat until the temperature settles, attach the balloon, run the fan until the balloon is firm, detach it, and inhale through the valve mouthpiece
- **Variants:** Volcano Classic (analog dial, balloon only); Volcano Hybrid (digital display, balloon plus whip, app control); other desktop whip and balloon models
