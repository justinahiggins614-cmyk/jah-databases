# The Pipe Cleaning Kit — Spec sheet

## Spec
- **Care:** wash the shank brush in warm soapy water and let it dry, replace pipe cleaners after one use, oil the wooden handle lightly, and keep the steel tools dry so they do not rust
- **Dimensions:** cleaners 150 mm long; shank brush 120 mm long with a 12 mm brush head; 3-in-1 tool 95 mm long; case 180 mm by 120 mm by 35 mm
- **Materials:** cotton fibre, nylon bristles, twisted steel wire, stainless steel, brass, beechwood handle
- **Mechanism:** the twisted-wire cleaner carries absorbent cotton through the stem airway, the bristle cleaner scrubs the airway wall, the shank brush sweeps the shank, and the reamer shaves the carbon cake inside the bowl back to an even layer
- **Use:** after the pipe has cooled, run a dry cleaner through the stem until it comes out clean, brush the shank, ream the bowl only when the cake is thicker than about 1.5 mm, and wipe the outside with the cloth
- **Variants:** travel kit with 10 cleaners; bench kit with 50 cleaners and an adjustable reamer; glass-only kit with nylon brushes and no metal reamer
