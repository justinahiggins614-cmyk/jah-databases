## Spec
- **Care:** wipe tips after every session, soak metal tips in isopropyl alcohol when residue hardens, never use a steel tool on a hot quartz banger with force, since a hard scrape can chip it, and store tips in the case so the fine points do not bend
- **Dimensions:** handle 100 mm long; each tool head 30 mm long; paddle 8 mm wide; pick tip 1 mm across; carb cap 25 mm across; case 170 mm by 110 mm by 30 mm
- **Materials:** grade 2 titanium tips, stainless steel handle, borosilicate glass carb cap, food-grade silicone mat
- **Mechanism:** the tool tip picks up, scoops or spreads a measured piece of concentrate and carries it to the heated banger, and the carb cap then covers the banger to restrict airflow, lower the pressure inside, and direct vapour across the hot surface
- **Use:** choose the tip that matches the concentrate texture, paddle for crumbly material and pick for sticky material, load the banger without touching the hot glass, cap it, and wipe the tip clean with a cotton swab while it is still warm
- **Variants:** single titanium dabber; dabber and carb cap combo tool; multi-tool folding set; glass sword dabber with a titanium tip
