# The Glass Bowl (14mm/18mm male/female) — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000031 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000031

## Spec
- **Care:** Empty the cup after use; soak in isopropyl alcohol to clear residue; rinse well; dry fully; replace a bowl with a chipped joint
- **Dimensions:** 14 mm male joint; cup 22 mm diameter at the rim and 18 mm deep; hole 3 mm at the bottom of the cup; stem 35 mm long with an 8 mm bore
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass bowl; ground-glass joint; stainless-steel screen (optional)
- **Mechanism:** The bowl holds the material in its cup; air drawn through the hole in the bottom of the cup carries smoke down the ground-glass joint and into the water pipe; lifting the bowl clears the pipe
- **Use:** Fit a screen if desired, pack the cup loosely, seat the bowl in the downstem, light while drawing, then lift the bowl to clear
- **Variants:** 14 mm male; 14 mm female; 18 mm male; 18 mm female; 10 mm for mini pieces; push bowl; funnel bowl; ice-pinch bowl; coloured glass bowls
