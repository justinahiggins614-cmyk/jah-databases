# The Water Pipe (Bong) — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000006 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000006

## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; rinse the base; clean glass with isopropyl alcohol and salt; dry fully; replace a cracked downstem
- **Dimensions:** About 300 mm tall; tube 50 mm in diameter; base holds water about 50 mm deep; downstem reaches about 25 mm below the water surface
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass tube, base, downstem, and bowl; plain water as the filtration medium; optional ice catcher and percolator
- **Mechanism:** Negative pressure from the draw pulls smoke down the downstem and out below the water line; smoke bubbles up through the water, which cools it and traps heavier particles and some water-soluble molecules; the chamber above the water stores smoke until the carb is opened
- **Use:** Add water so the downstem sits below the surface, pack the bowl, cover the carb, light while drawing slowly until the chamber fills, then open the carb and inhale
- **Variants:** Straight tube; beaker base; percolator models with extra diffusion slits; ice-catcher models; multi-chamber models
