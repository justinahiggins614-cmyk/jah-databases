## Spec
- **Care:** Empty the oven after it cools; brush out residue; clean the mouthpiece and oven with isopropyl alcohol on a cotton swab when cool; recharge the battery
- **Dimensions:** 100 mm tall x 30 mm wide x 22 mm deep; oven holds about 0.25 g of ground herb
- **Materials:** Anodised aluminium or stainless-steel body; ceramic or stainless-steel oven; lithium-ion battery; glass or plastic mouthpiece
- **Mechanism:** Conduction, convection, or hybrid heating; temperature set by presets or a digital control, typically 160 C to 220 C (320 F to 428 F); heat-up in 10 to 90 seconds depending on method
- **Use:** Grind herb medium-fine, fill the oven loosely, set the temperature, wait for the ready signal, then draw slowly through the mouthpiece
- **Variants:** Conduction-only pocket vapes; convection portables; hybrid models such as the Mighty+ and Crafty+; one-button preset models and full digital-control models
