# The Glass Dab Rig — Spec sheet

## Spec
- **Care:** change the water after every session, clean the banger with a cotton swab while it is still warm, soak the glass in isopropyl alcohol and salt when residue builds, and never shake a rig hard around a percolator, since a hard shake can snap thin glass
- **Dimensions:** overall height 200 mm; chamber diameter 70 mm; water fill 60 ml covering the percolator slits by 12 mm; banger 25 mm across with a 20 mm deep bucket; joint size 14 mm
- **Materials:** borosilicate glass body, fused quartz banger, stainless steel or titanium carb cap option, silicone-free glass joints
- **Mechanism:** concentrate placed on the heated banger vaporises, the vapour is drawn down the stem into the water chamber, and the percolator breaks the vapour into many small bubbles so it cools against the water before rising up the neck
- **Use:** fill with clean water just above the percolator slits, heat the banger, let it cool to the working range, place the concentrate in the banger, cap it, and draw slowly so the water bubbles without splashback
- **Variants:** mini rig 150 mm tall for travel; recycler rig that loops water through a second chamber; showerhead-perc rig; honeycomb-perc rig
