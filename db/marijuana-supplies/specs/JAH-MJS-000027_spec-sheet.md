# The Percolator Bong (Tree/Arm) — Spec sheet

## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; soak the percolator in isopropyl alcohol to clear the slits; rinse well; dry fully; handle gently because the arms are fragile
- **Dimensions:** 380 mm tall; tube 60 mm diameter; 8-arm tree percolator, each arm 40 mm long with 6 slits; water depth 50 mm
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass tube and base; glass tree percolator with 8 hollow arms; glass downstem and bowl; plain water as the filtration medium
- **Mechanism:** Smoke is forced down the central stem of the tree percolator and out through the slits in each hollow arm, breaking into many small bubble streams in the water before rising to the mouthpiece
- **Use:** Add water so the percolator arms are covered, pack the bowl, light while drawing slowly until the chamber fills, then lift the bowl and inhale
- **Variants:** 4-arm, 6-arm, 8-arm, and 12-arm tree percs; honeycomb disc percs; showerhead percs; matrix percs; double and triple perc stacks
