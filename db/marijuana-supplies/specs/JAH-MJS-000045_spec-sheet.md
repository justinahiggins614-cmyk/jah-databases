# The Fabric Grow Pot — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000045 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000045

## Spec
- **Care:** Empty after use, brush off dried mix, rinse with water and dry fully before storage; check seams and handles for wear; fold flat for storage
- **Dimensions:** 5 gallon (18.93 L) nominal; 30.5 cm diameter x 25.4 cm tall; two handles
- **Materials:** 300 GSM needlepunched nonwoven polypropylene fabric; bonded polyester thread; sewn webbing handles
- **Mechanism:** Porous fabric wall lets air reach the root zone and drains excess water; root tips meeting air stop and branch, preventing circling roots
- **Use:** Set the pot on a tray, fill with growing mix, place it where it will stay, lift by both handles when moving it
- **Variants:** 1, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25, and 30 gallon; with and without handles; square bottom; tan and black fabric; 200, 300, and 500 GSM
