## Spec
- **Care:** Wipe the interior clean; keep dry; reactivate the carbon lining in direct sunlight where the maker allows it; replace the bag when odour starts to escape
- **Dimensions:** Reference pouch 20 cm x 14 cm; wallets from 4 x 6 inches; duffle 20 x 11 x 11 inches; Revelry Confidant 27.94 cm x 15.24 cm, 0.5 litres
- **Materials:** Activated carbon lining (bamboo carbon) with protective mesh, water-resistant 600D polyester or mylar outer shell, sealed zip, optional combination lock
- **Mechanism:** Adsorption: odour molecules bind to the microscopic pores of activated carbon as air passes through the lining; the sealed zip limits bulk air flow
- **Use:** Place contents inside, close the zip fully, set the combination lock if fitted; open only when needed so the carbon is not exhausted by outside air
- **Variants:** Carbon-lined wallet, pouch, fanny pack, large case, backpack, duffle bag; resealable mylar pouches; bags with one or two combination locks
