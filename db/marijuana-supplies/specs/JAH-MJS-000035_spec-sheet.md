## Spec
- **Care:** Let the cap cool on its holder; soak in isopropyl alcohol, rinse, and dry; handle glass carefully as it breaks if dropped; do not quench a hot cap in cold water
- **Dimensions:** Cap 30 mm across; wall 2 mm thick; inlet hole 2 mm; fits a 25 mm flat-top banger
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass or quartz dome; flat polished rim; no metal or plastic in the vapour path
- **Mechanism:** Cap covers the banger, traps heat, restricts airflow, lowers pressure inside the chamber, and sets up a swirling current that moves concentrate across the hot floor
- **Use:** Heat the banger, drop the concentrate, cap at once, draw slowly while turning the cap to aim the airflow, lift the cap when the vapour stops
- **Variants:** Bubble, directional, spinner, dome, disc and UFO caps; glass, quartz, and titanium; 20, 25, and 30 mm banger sizes
