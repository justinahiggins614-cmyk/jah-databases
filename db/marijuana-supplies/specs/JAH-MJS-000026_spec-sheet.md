# The Straight-Tube Bong — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000026 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000026

## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; rinse the tube; clean glass with isopropyl alcohol and salt; dry fully; replace a cracked downstem
- **Dimensions:** 320 mm tall; tube 50 mm diameter; flat foot 70 mm diameter; water depth 45 mm
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass tube and flat foot; glass downstem and bowl; plain water as the filtration medium
- **Mechanism:** Smoke from the bowl travels down the downstem below the water line, bubbles up through the water in a single straight cylinder, and is drawn straight to the mouthpiece with very little drag
- **Use:** Add water so the downstem sits below the surface, pack the bowl, light while drawing slowly until the tube fills, then lift the bowl and inhale to clear
- **Variants:** Plain straight tube; straight tube with ice pinches; straight tube with a flared or weighted foot; thick-glass (5 mm and 9 mm wall) versions
