## Spec
- **Care:** brush the mesh clean after every use, wash the tray with warm soapy water, let the screen dry fully before storage, and replace a torn or stretched screen, since a sagging mesh passes plant matter it should hold back
- **Dimensions:** box 200 mm by 150 mm by 90 mm; screen area 180 mm by 130 mm; standard screen 120 microns; collection tray 20 mm deep
- **Materials:** beechwood box, stainless steel mesh, food-grade nylon mesh option, borosilicate glass plate
- **Mechanism:** dried plant material is agitated above a woven mesh screen, and particles smaller than the mesh opening fall through into the tray below while larger plant matter stays on top
- **Use:** place a small amount of dry material on the screen, close the lid, shake gently for two to three minutes, rest, tap the frame, then brush the collected kief from the tray onto the glass plate
- **Variants:** single-screen shaker box at 120 microns; three-screen stack at 160, 120 and 75 microns; tumbler drum with interchangeable 100, 150 and 200 micron screens
