# The Recycler Bong — Spec sheet

Record JAH-MJS-000028 — public edition, marijuana-supplies.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000028

## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; soak the loop tubes in isopropyl alcohol; rinse well; dry fully; handle gently because the loop tubes are thin
- **Dimensions:** 340 mm tall; main chamber 300 mL; secondary chamber 150 mL; uptake tube 10 mm diameter; drain tube 8 mm diameter; water 180 mL in total
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass main chamber, secondary chamber, uptake tube, and drain tube; showerhead percolator; glass downstem and bowl; plain water as the filtration and circulation medium
- **Mechanism:** The percolator diffuses smoke in the main chamber; the draw pulls water and smoke up the uptake tube into the secondary chamber, where water and smoke separate; gravity drains the water back through the drain tube to the main chamber, and the loop repeats while the draw continues
- **Use:** Add water so the percolator is covered but the upper chamber stays dry when still, pack the bowl, draw steadily so the loop starts, then clear
- **Variants:** External recycler with visible parallel tubes; internal recycler with hidden tubes; Klein recycler with a sealed loop; double showerhead recycler
