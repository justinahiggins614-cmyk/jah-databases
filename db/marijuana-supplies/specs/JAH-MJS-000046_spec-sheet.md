## Spec
- **Care:** Switch off before cleaning; wipe the housing and impeller blades; check duct clamps and cords; keep the intake clear of loose fabric
- **Dimensions:** 6 in (152.4 mm) duct diameter; housing about 300 mm long; rated 402 CFM; 32 dBA at full speed; 40 W
- **Materials:** ABS plastic housing; steel mounting brackets; EC motor; aluminium impeller; variable speed controller
- **Mechanism:** EC motor spins an impeller inside the duct, pulling air through the enclosed path; speed controller sets the airflow; mounted high in the tent, it exhausts warm humid air first
- **Use:** Mount the fan in the duct run with clamps, connect ducting to a carbon filter on the intake side, set the speed on the controller, check that air flows out of the tent
- **Variants:** 4 in 205 CFM; 6 in 402 CFM; 8 in 807 CFM; 10 in 1200 CFM; with and without smart controller; with temperature and humidity sensor
