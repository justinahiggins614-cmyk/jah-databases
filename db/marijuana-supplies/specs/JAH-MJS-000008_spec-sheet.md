# Pre-Rolled Cones — Spec sheet

## Spec
- **Care:** Keep cones dry and upright; store in their pack so they are not crushed; check the filter tip is firmly attached before filling
- **Dimensions:** Mini 70 mm; Small / 1 1/4 size 84 mm; Classic 98 mm; King Size 109 mm; filter tip 24 mm; a 1 1/4 cone holds about 0.75 g
- **Materials:** Hemp, rice, or wood-pulp paper; gum arabic seam; rolled paper or cardboard filter tip, usually 24 mm long
- **Mechanism:** Trapezoid-cut paper is rolled into a tapered tube around a form; the filter tip at the narrow end supports the cone, keeps material out of the mouth, and sets the airflow; the wide end is filled, packed, and twisted closed
- **Use:** Hold the cone upright with the filter end down, add ground herb in small amounts, tap to settle, leave about 5 mm at the top, pack gently, and twist the tip closed
- **Variants:** Mini 70 mm; 1 1/4 size 84 mm; Classic 98 mm; King Size 109 mm; mega 305 mm; hemp, rice, and wood-pulp papers; glass-tip and charcoal-tip versions
