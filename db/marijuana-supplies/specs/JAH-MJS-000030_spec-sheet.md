# The Bubbler — Spec sheet

JCC v1 code: JAHDB/marijuana-supplies#JAH-MJS-000030

## Spec
- **Care:** Change the water after each session; shake with isopropyl alcohol and salt to clean the fixed chamber; rinse well; dry fully
- **Dimensions:** 130 mm long; water chamber 45 mm diameter; water depth 15 mm; bowl 18 mm diameter and 12 mm deep
- **Materials:** Borosilicate glass one-piece body; fixed glass bowl and fixed downstem; plain water as the filtration medium
- **Mechanism:** Smoke from the fixed bowl travels down the fixed stem below the water line, bubbles up through the small water chamber, and is drawn to the mouthpiece; the carb hole is uncovered to clear the chamber
- **Use:** Add a little water, just enough to cover the bottom of the inner stem, pack the bowl, cover the carb, light while drawing slowly, then uncover the carb and inhale
- **Variants:** Hammer bubbler; Sherlock bubbler; sidecar bubbler; pendant bubbler; concentrate bubbler; double bubbler; ratchet bubbler; silicone bubbler
