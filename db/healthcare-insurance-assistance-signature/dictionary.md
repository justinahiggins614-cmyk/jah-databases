# Dictionary entries — The Healthcare Insurance Assistance Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## healthcare insurance assistance

The recorded help given to a person who is working out what a health insurance policy covers, what it costs, and how a claim is made, so that the policy can be read and used with its terms in plain view.

## insurance policy

The written contract between an insurer and a policyholder that states what health care is covered, what is excluded, and what the policyholder pays, kept with the assistance record so that the help given can be checked against the contract.

## premium

The amount paid, usually each month, to keep a health insurance policy in force, written in the assistance record with its amount and its due date, so that what was charged for the cover can be counted.

## deductible

The amount a policyholder pays toward covered care before the insurer begins to pay, written in the record with its amount and its period, so that the share of the cost can be read plainly.

## copayment

A fixed amount a policyholder pays for a covered service at the time of care, written in the record with the service it belongs to, so that the payment for each visit can be counted.

## claim

The written request sent to an insurer asking for payment for covered care, kept in the record with its date and its outcome, so that what was claimed and what was paid can both be shown.

## explanation of benefits

The written statement an insurer sends after a claim, showing what was charged, what was covered, and what remains, kept beside the claim so that the outcome can be read in full.

## provider network

The list of doctors, hospitals, and clinics that have agreed to care for a policyholder under the terms of a policy, written in the record so that which providers belong to the policy can be checked.

## pre-authorization

The written approval an insurer gives before certain care is provided, kept in the record with its date and its scope, so that the approval can be shown when the care is claimed.

## enrollment period

The named span of days during which a person may join or change a health insurance policy, written in the record with its start and its end, so that the window for joining can be read plainly.

## appeal

The written request asking an insurer to look again at a claim that was denied or paid in part, kept in the record with its date and its outcome, so that the second look can be followed.

## assistance letter

The written note sent after healthcare insurance assistance is given, naming the policy, the questions asked, and the answers given, so that the help can be proved from the paper.
