History
Towns have licensed building for centuries, because a wall raised wrong can fall on the street below. Permits grew beside the building trade: an application describing the work, plans drawn to scale, a fee paid, and an inspector who walks the site and signs that the work matches the plan. From the paper permit nailed to a site fence to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the permission down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every building permit true.
