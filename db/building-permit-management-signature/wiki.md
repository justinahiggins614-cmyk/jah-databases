# The Building Permit Management Database

The Building Permit Management Database is the building permit management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full building permit management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database permit-and-inspection-navigation, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
