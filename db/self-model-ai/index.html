This record defines the update rule: the system receives an observation O_t, performs an action A_t, observes the result R_t, and updates its internal model, S_{t+1} = F(S_t, O_t, A_t, R_t). Evidence: established The Brier score and calibration are documented standards; the pairs are a model, labeled as such.
