Vehicle Identification Lookup Database (vehicle-identification-lookup)

A vehicle identification lookup is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy vehicles, and counts the marks. The lookup reads 3 vehicles. The vehicle sedan holds 27 marks. The largest holds 27 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
