AI Mathematician Database (ai-mathematician-signature)

An AI mathematician is a rule-based local demo: it proves and checks number work with plain steps, and shows each step so a reader can check it. Adding the numbers from 1 to 10 gives 55. The numbers 3, 7, 11 and 19 add to 40. 9 squared is 81. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
