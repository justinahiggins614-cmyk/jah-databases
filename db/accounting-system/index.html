1. Description

An accounting system records entries with real double-entry arithmetic: type a debit and a credit, press Record, and read the balance. A debit of 100 and a credit of 100 balance: 0 difference. Debits 700 equal credits 700: balanced. Assets 6000 equals liabilities 2500 plus equity 3500. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

3. Science proof

An accounting system works because every debit has a matching credit, and each total is plain arithmetic: cash 1000 plus equipment 5000 is 6000 in assets, and a loan of 2000 and bills of 500 is 2500 in liabilities. Revenue 10000 minus expenses 7000 is profit 3000. That is why the local demo shows the entries and the balance together, and runs 0 entries on a server.
