Accounting System Database (accounting-system)

An accounting system records entries with real double-entry arithmetic: type a debit and a credit, press Record, and read the balance. A debit of 100 and a credit of 100 balance: 0 difference. Debits 700 equal credits 700: balanced. Assets 6000 equals liabilities 2500 plus equity 3500. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: A debit of 100 and a credit of 100 balance: 0 difference
