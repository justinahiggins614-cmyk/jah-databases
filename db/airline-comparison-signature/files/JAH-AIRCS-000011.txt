History
Travellers have always compared the ways to fly: which airline flies a route, at what time, at what fare, and with what service. When flying moved onto the network, the comparing had to become a table — the same flight, from each airline, at its recorded fare and time, laid side by side, so that the differences could be seen at a glance. Airline comparison is that table: a record of facts about airlines and their flights, never advice about which airline any person should choose. This database is built on that public practice of comparing the same journey, in the same words, from every airline.
