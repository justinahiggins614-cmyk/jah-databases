# The Tax Record Management Database

The Tax Record Management Database is the tax record management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full tax record management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database public-records, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
