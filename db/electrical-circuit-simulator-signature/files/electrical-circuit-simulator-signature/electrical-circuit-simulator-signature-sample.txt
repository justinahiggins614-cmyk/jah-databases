Electrical Circuit Simulator Database (electrical-circuit-simulator-signature)

An electrical circuit simulator works out currents and voltages with real laws: type the figures, press Run, and read the result. A current of 2 amps through 3 ohms gives 6 volts. 12 volts across 4 ohms gives 3.0 amps. Resistors of 10, 20 and 30 ohms in series give 60 ohms. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
