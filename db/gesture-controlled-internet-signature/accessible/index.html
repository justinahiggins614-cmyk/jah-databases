The Signature Gesture-Controlled Internet Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The gesture-controlled internet lets a visitor steer a page with hand motions: a wave to open, a point to choose, a swipe to turn the page. Every gesture is a clearly labeled design study of a generic motion, and the guide never claims the motions work on a live website.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
