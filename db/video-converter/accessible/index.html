The Video Converter Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A video converter changes a video from one format to another, keeping its length and its frames. The demo on this page converts a seeded clip locally: a clip of 30 seconds, 900 frames and 30 megabytes. A full transcode needs a backend, and that is BLOCKED; this is a local partial demo, and it says so. You can also upload your own clip, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
