## Spec
- **Mechanism:** Key points.
Sedimentation. Floc settles by gravity in quiet basins.

In depth.
Treated water rests in sedimentation basins while floc sinks to the bottom as sludge. Clear water flows off the top to filtration. Patience does the work — no energy required.
