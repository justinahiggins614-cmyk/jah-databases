About this data base

Archive
This database's mix-and-match archive (JAH-MIX records): blends of two AIs with fused abilities — preserved as structured, searchable data.
Record schema
id · name · sources[] · abilities[] · blurb
Generator
jahdb-mixmatch-1.0 — deterministic (mulberry32), builds each blend from two real archive AIs, then an independent re-builder re-derives the name, sources, abilities and blurb from the private parameters before acceptance.
Drift control
Every generated record is checked against an archive sample for exact duplicates and re-validated by the independent verifier.
Cross-database
The generator can borrow a theme from any of the other 36 databases when you tick the box.
AI
