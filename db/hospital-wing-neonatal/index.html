Levels of Neonatal Care

NICUs are leveled I through IV — from basic newborn care to surgical and ECMO capability for the tiniest preemies. Regionalization sends the sickest babies to Level IV centers. Level matches need.

Respiratory Distress Syndrome

Preemie lungs lack surfactant, collapsing with each breath; treatment is CPAP, ventilation, and surfactant down the breathing tube. Most RDS resolves as lungs mature. Breathing support is the NICU's core craft.

Prolonged ventilation scars developing lungs into BPD — chronic lung disease of prematurity. Gentle ventilation and steroids (judiciously) reduce it. Some babies go home on oxygen.

Retinopathy of Prematurity

Oxygen and prematurity can derange retinal vessels; timed eye exams catch ROP, laser or injections save sight. Screening is mandatory for tiny babies. Sight, vigilantly guarded.
