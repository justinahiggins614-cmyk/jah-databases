Chemical Formula Calculator Database (chemical-formula-calculator)

A chemical formula calculator parses formulas and weighs molecules: type a formula, press Calculate, and read the molar mass. Water H2O has molar mass 18.02 grams per mole. Carbon dioxide CO2 has molar mass 44.01 grams per mole. Glucose C6H12O6 has molar mass 180.16 grams per mole. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: Water H2O has 2 hydrogen atoms and 1 oxygen atom: 3 atoms
