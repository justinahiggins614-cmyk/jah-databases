History
Tax records are the papers that prove what was earned, what was spent, and what was filed: the statements, the receipts, the returns themselves. Keeping them was once a matter of a box on a shelf; it became a system when the law began to ask for old papers years after they were written. Management means naming each paper, filing it where it can be found, keeping it for the stated time, and destroying it only when the time has passed and the destruction is recorded.
