# Dictionary entries — The Tax Record Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## tax record

A paper that proves a figure on a tax return, such as a statement of pay, a receipt for a cost, or the return itself, kept so the figure can be checked years later.

## retention period

The stated time a tax paper must be kept, counted from the date it was filed, so that no paper is destroyed while it may still be asked for.

## filing system

The ordered way papers are named and stored, so that any paper can be found by its year, its kind, and its owner, without searching the whole store.

## index

The list of what a record store holds, with each paper named and its place given, kept current, so that a paper can be found from the index alone.

## original

The first paper, as it was made, kept whole, so that no later copy can be mistaken for it and no change to it can hide.

## copy

A second paper made from an original, marked as a copy, kept for working use, so that the original need not be handled and risked.

## archive

The kept set of old tax papers, filed in order, so that any past year can be re-read and any figure re-checked against the paper that first proved it.

## destruction log

The written record of papers destroyed, naming each paper, the date, and the reason, so that a paper's absence can be explained and never mistaken for a loss.

## audit

A check of records by an outside reader, who asks for papers and reads them against the returns, so that the truth of the records can be tested by someone who did not make them.

## access control

The rule of who may read, copy, or move a tax paper, written down and kept, so that papers are seen only by those who should see them.

## backup

A second kept copy of the record store, held apart from the first, so that the records survive if the first store is lost or damaged.

## record owner

The person answerable for a tax paper, named in the index, so that every paper has one person who can say where it is and why it is kept.
