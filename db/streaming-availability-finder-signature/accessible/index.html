The Signature Streaming Availability Finder Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The streaming availability finder answers one plain question: where can a kind of film or show be watched, by rental or as part of a viewing plan? It describes the method only, in generic kinds of services, and names no real service. Every sample result is a clearly labeled illustration, never a real-time availability claim.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

  12. A Streak of Checks Run Each Week

    Record 12 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The streaming availability finder answers one plain question: where can a kind of film or show be watched, by rental or as part of a viewing plan?
