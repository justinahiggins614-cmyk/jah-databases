Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An availability finder website: a visitor names a generic kind of film or show, and the finder explains, in generic kinds of viewing services, how availability is checked: by kind of service, by rental or included viewing, and by region rules a viewer can read. Checking a mystery film by kind gives a viewer a plain answer about where that kind of film can be watched, by rental or as part of a viewing plan. The check must name the kind of service in generic terms, say whether the viewing is by rental or included, and never claim a live result, because availability changes and this site makes no real-time claim. Without a plain method, a viewer must search every service in turn, and searching every service in turn is a poor way to spend an evening.

An availability finder website: a visitor names a generic kind of film or show, and the finder explains, in generic kinds of viewing services, how availability is checked: by kind of service, by rental or included viewing, and by region rules a viewer can read. The finder names no real service, makes no real-time claim, and every sample result is labeled as an illustration of the method. This record covers A Check Matched to a Plain-Word Wish.

A check matched to a plain-word wish would let a person who cannot name a kind of film still find it, by saying something plain like a quiet film for tonight. Matching by plain words needs a system that understands what quiet means for a viewing check, and turns those plain words into a fitting kind. That understanding is still research-stage: small tests match simple wishes well, but longer wishes and unusual tastes still confuse the matcher.

Worked example, Signature design: the same availability check is rebuilt from the Signature pattern, so a check matched to a plain-word wish runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
