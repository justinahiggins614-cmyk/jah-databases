# Dictionary entries — The Financial Audit Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## audit

An independent check of books, records, or statements against their proof, carried out by someone who did not write them, and reported in writing.

## auditor

The person who carries out an audit, independent of the books being checked, whose findings are written down with the evidence for each.

## audit plan

The written plan of an audit, naming what will be checked, in what order, and by which tests, so that the work can be followed and nothing is skipped.

## evidence

The proof an auditor gathers, such as a receipt, a statement, or a counted stock, kept with the finding it supports, so that every finding can be shown.

## finding

One thing an audit discovered, written down with what was found, what it should have been, and the evidence that shows it, so that it can be answered.

## materiality

The size at which an error matters to a reader of the statements, stated before the audit begins, so that small errors and large ones are judged by the same rule.

## sampling

Checking a chosen part of the records instead of every entry, by a stated method, so that the part checked can stand for the whole it was drawn from.

## internal control

The checks a business builds into its own work, such as one person writing and another approving, so that errors are caught by the system before an audit finds them.

## audit report

The written report of an audit, saying what was checked, what was found, and what the auditor concludes, kept as the public record of the audit.

## opinion

The auditor's stated conclusion about whether the statements tell the truth fairly, written in the report in fixed words, so that readers know exactly what was concluded.

## follow-up

The work of checking, after an audit, that each finding was answered and each correction made, with the answers written beside the findings they close.

## working papers

The auditor's kept notes and copies, filed in order, showing every test done and every piece of evidence gathered, so that the audit can be re-traced.
