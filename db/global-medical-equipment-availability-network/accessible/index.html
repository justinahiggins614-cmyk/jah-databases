The Global Medical Equipment Availability Network Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The global medical equipment availability network lists medical equipment that can be shared or lent, a wheelchair, a hospital bed, a monitor, each described in plain words with its kind, its place, and its state. Every listing is a clearly labeled design study of a generic kind of equipment, never a live offer and never advice about any person's health. The network never names a condition for a person, never names an amount of any medicine, and never promises that any condition will improve.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
