A medical equipment network website: a visitor picks a kind of equipment, such as a wheelchair or a monitor, and the network shows listings in plain words, with the kind, the place, and the state of each item, and a plain note on how to ask about it. Every listing is a generic study, and the network never claims a listing is live, never gives health advice, and never names a condition for a person. This record is the field guide index for the whole database: it lists all twenty-three content records in ladder order, simple to compounded to complex, and tells a reader where to start.

A database of twenty-three records needs a map, or a reader will not know where to start, which records are simple and which are complex, and how the records fit together. Without an index, the simple records that teach the design are buried beside the complex records that assume it, and a visitor who wants one idea must read the whole database to find it. The index solves this by listing every record in ladder order, with its place in the ladder, so a reader can start at the beginning and climb, or jump straight to the record they need.

Worked example: a reader who wants the simplest starting point opens record 1, A Shared Oxygen Concentrator Listing, and a reader who wants the hardest problem opens record 23, A Yearly Showcase of Equipment Listings.

History

Lending a useful tool to a neighbor is an old habit: a borrowed ladder, a shared kettle, a spare chair for a guest. The website form grows out of that habit, adding one plain listing for each kind of equipment and a list anyone can search. Field guides and indexes are as old as books themselves: a table of contents that tells a reader where everything lives. This index follows that habit, listing the twenty-three content records of this database in the order a reader should meet them.

Proposed solution

What must exist first: a list of generic kinds of equipment, a plain listing for each kind, a search box, and a review step before publishing. A tournament system, a creator studio, a review step, translations, and an offline pack are also needed before the complex equipment listings open.
