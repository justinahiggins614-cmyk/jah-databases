The Signature The Worldwide Banana Ranking Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The worldwide banana ranking lines up bananas from everywhere and ranks them in plain words: the longest, the sweetest, the curviest, the best for baking, each rank written out so a reader can see how it was judged. Every ranking is a clearly labeled design study of a generic fruit, never a claim about a real seller.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
