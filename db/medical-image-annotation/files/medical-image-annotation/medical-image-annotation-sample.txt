Medical Image Annotation Database (medical-image-annotation)

A medical image annotation is a rule-based local demo: it annotates set toy annotations, and counts the marks. Reference only: general information, no specific dosing, no cure claims, no diagnosis claims. Sample images only, no real patient images or data. The annotator reads 3 annotations. The annotation alpha holds 12 marks. The largest holds 12 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
