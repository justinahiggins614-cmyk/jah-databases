Gradient Generator Database (gradient-generator-signature)

A gradient generator is a seeded local demo: it blends one colour into another in set steps, and counts the stops. The gradient blends two colours in 5 stops. The colour is red 51, green 102 and blue 204. Halfway from 51 to 255 is 153. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
