# The Banking Information Portal Database

The Banking Information Portal Database is the banking information portal record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full banking information portal record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database website-information, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
