Laboratory Data Analyzer Database (laboratory-data-analyzer-signature)

A laboratory data analyzer turns raw readings into means, spreads and verdicts: type the readings, press Analyze, and read the result. The mean of [10, 12, 14] is 12.0. [10, 12, 14, 16, 18] has mean 14.0 and standard deviation 2.83. [10, 11, 12, 13, 50] has mean 19.2 and median 12: 50 is the outlier. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: The mean of [10, 12, 14] is 12.0
