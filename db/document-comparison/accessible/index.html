The Document Comparison Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. Document comparison lines two texts up side by side and lists what matches and what changed: matching lines, changed lines, added lines and removed lines, with a similarity score. Paste two texts, press Compare, and read the differences. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/document-comparison/index.jsonl.gz
