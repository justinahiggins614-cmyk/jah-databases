3. Science proof

A flowchart creator works because a flowchart is nodes joined by arrows, and each path is plain counting: a decision has 2 exits: yes and no, and a decision diamond has 4 sides. A loop visits the step 3 times. A straight run visits 5 nodes. That is why the local demo shows the flow and the chart together, and runs 0 creations on a server.
