History
Trust on the web was once a matter of knowing the person behind the page, and then of knowing the organization, and then of reading signals a stranger can check: who runs the site, how long it has stood, whether its words match its deeds, and what others report. Verification is the work of gathering those signals into one written judgement, with every signal named, so a reader can see why a site is trusted or not. False trust is a good verdict a site does not deserve, won by faked signals, bought reports, or a borrowed name.
