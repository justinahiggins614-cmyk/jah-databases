# Dictionary entries — The Website Reputation Verification Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## reputation

What is said about a site by those who have used it, gathered over time, so that a new reader can learn from the experience of others.

## verification

Checking a site's claims against evidence that can be read and repeated, and writing down what was checked and what was found.

## signal

One small piece of evidence about a site, such as its age, its owner, or a report from a reader, weighed with the other signals.

## trust score

A single number that sums the signals about a site, shown beside the reasons that produced it, never alone.

## identity check

Confirming who runs a site, by reading the name, address, and contact details the site itself publishes.

## age check

Reading how long a site has stood, because a site that appeared yesterday has no history to vouch for it.

## content check

Reading a site's own words for signs of care or carelessness, such as copied text, broken promises, or missing sources.

## reader report

A report from a person who used the site, naming what happened in plain words, kept with its date and its source.

## phishing

A trick in which a site pretends to be another site, asking for secrets or money under a borrowed name.

## verdict

The written judgement about a site, naming the signals that produced it, the checks that ran, and the date it was made.

## recheck

Running the checks again after time has passed, because a site that was trusted last year may not deserve trust today.

## appeal

A site owner's written answer to a verdict, giving new evidence and asking for the checks to be run again.
