Foxwoods Resort Casino — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Foxwoods Resort Casino is a large resort casino in Mashantucket, Connecticut, owned and operated by the Mashantucket Pequot Tribal Nation. It began as a bingo hall in 1986 and expanded into a full casino in 1992, growing into one of the largest casino resorts in North America with about 2,266 rooms across six hotel properties, including the Grand Pequot Tower.
