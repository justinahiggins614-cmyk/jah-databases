The mechanism has three parts: a partition rule that assigns every record to exactly one shard, a directory that remembers the assignment, and a router that sends each query to the right shard. The circular economy treats waste as feedstock: one process's output becomes another's input, and materials loop instead of flowing to landfill. Aluminum recycling is the textbook case — recycling aluminum uses about 95% less energy than refining new metal, which is why the loop is decades old and still growing. The mechanism is loop-closing at every stage: design products for disassembly, collect at end of life, reprocess into feedstock.
