History

Tags for sorting writing are an old habit: a word written in the margin of a notebook, a label on a card in a file, a heading in an index. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a count for each tag and a grouping by topic. Field guides and indexes are as old as books themselves: a table of contents that tells a reader where everything lives. This index follows that habit, listing the twenty-three content records of this database in the order a reader should meet them.
