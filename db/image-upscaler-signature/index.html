1. Description and History

An image upscaler enlarges a small image without making it look like blocks: edges stay sharp, textures stay believable, and fine detail is rebuilt rather than invented. This database designs that upscaler one record at a time, from doubling a small photo to preparing a poster, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own images only.

Upscaling began as simple interpolation — guessing the pixels between the pixels — which made bigger but blurrier images. Later methods learned from real images what edges and textures should look like, and could rebuild a small image at a larger size with far less blur. The design in this database follows that path: rebuild what the image supports, never invent detail, and say plainly where the limit is.

Every record here is a design, never a real product. Public records are fact-checked, real, and legal-only; Signature records are the Signature original version of the same subject; speculative material is labeled design-study, never as fact. Photo tools work on the user's own photos only, never anyone else's private images.

Later methods learned from real images what edges and textures should look like, and could rebuild a small image at a larger size with far less blur. The design in this database follows that path: rebuild what the image supports, never invent detail, and say plainly where the limit is. This record grows out of that history directly: Scanned documents are text and lines on paper, and they need to stay readable when enlarged. The record designs document upscaling that sharpens the text, cleans the paper background, and keeps the page readable.

Proposed solution

Begin with the Signature design: the user's own photo stays on the device and the original is never altered; Signature approach: sharpen text and clean the paper background; Apply the Signature change step by step, checking the result after each one; Offer the user a strength control and a side-by-side comparison; Export the result with its settings, so the same look can be repeated.

Dependencies

The user's own image, kept on the device; edge-aware enlargement that runs locally; artifact removal for compressed images; a before-and-after comparison at full size; the original image never overwritten. This record also needs sharpen text and clean the paper background done well.

Feasibility

Verification path

Compare the result with the original side by side: the change is visible, the subject is still recognizable, and the result can be undone. For this record, the check is sharpen text and clean the paper background, tested on a few of the user's own photos first.
