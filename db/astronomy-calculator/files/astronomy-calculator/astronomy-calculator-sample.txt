Astronomy Calculator Database (astronomy-calculator)

An astronomy calculator works out distances, times and brightnesses: type the figures, press Calculate, and read the result. Light travels 299792 kilometres in 1 second. 1 light year is 9.46 trillion kilometres. Mars at 1.52 AU takes 1.87 years to orbit the Sun. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
