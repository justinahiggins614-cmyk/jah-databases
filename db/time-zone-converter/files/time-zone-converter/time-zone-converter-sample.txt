Time Zone Converter Database (time-zone-converter)

A time zone converter is a rule-based local demo: it moves a set toy hour from one zone to another, and counts the hours. The hour is 14. The hour is 14 o'clock. 22 plus 5 wraps to 3. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
