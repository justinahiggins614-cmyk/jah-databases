ARIA Resort & Casino — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. ARIA Resort & Casino is a modern luxury resort at the heart of CityCenter on the Las Vegas Strip, opened on December 16, 2009. Operated by MGM Resorts International, it is known for its glass-tower architecture, in-room technology, and about 4,000 guest rooms. The resort anchors the CityCenter complex alongside the Shops at Crystals.
