# Dictionary entries — The Scholarship Discovery Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## scholarship discovery

The recorded search of published scholarships, so that each award, its funder, and its terms can be read from the record rather than remembered.

## scholarship

A sum of money given to a learner to help pay for study, kept in the record with its funder, its terms, and its deadline, so that the award can be read plainly.

## funder

The body that gives a scholarship, named in the record with the award, so that where the money comes from can be read plainly.

## applicant

The person who asks for a scholarship, recorded under a name and a number so that every paper for that applicant can be matched to the same person.

## terms

The stated conditions attached to a scholarship, such as the study it supports, written in the record so that the limits of the award can be read in full.

## deadline

The stated last day on which a scholarship may be asked for, written in the record with the award, so that when the asking closes can be read plainly.

## award

The act of giving a scholarship to an applicant, kept in the record with the date and the sum, so that the giving can be proved from the paper.

## bursary

A sum of money given to a learner for study, kept in the record with its funder and its terms, so that the gift can be read plainly.

## grant

A sum of money given for a stated purpose, kept in the record with its giver and its terms, so that the gift can be read plainly.

## eligibility

The stated conditions that an applicant must meet to ask for a scholarship, written in the record so that who may ask can be read plainly.

## application

The written request for a scholarship, naming the applicant and the award sought, kept with the papers it carries, so that the request and its papers can be read together.

## notice board

The place where scholarships are published for learners to read, named in the record with the awards listed, so that where the finding was made can be read plainly.
