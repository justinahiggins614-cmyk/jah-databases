Reaction Predictor Database (reaction-predictor-signature)

A reaction predictor predicts the products of a chemical reaction from its reactants: type the reactants, press Predict, and read the products. CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O: C 1, H 4, O 4 on both sides. CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O is balanced: True. 2 moles of H2 and 1 mole of O2 make 2 moles of H2O. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: CH4 + 2O2 -> CO2 + 2H2O: C 1, H 4, O 4 on both sides
