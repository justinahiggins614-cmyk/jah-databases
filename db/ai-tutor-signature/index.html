1. Description

Hosted AI help is labelled BLOCKED until a hosted backend is connected; nothing here pretends to be hosted.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A quiz score is right answers divided by all questions. With 4 of 5 right, the score is 4 over 5, which is 0.8, or 80 percent. Mastery is a threshold: 9 of 10 right, twice in a row, is 90 percent twice. A level rises after two right answers in a row, and falls after a miss.

3. Science proof

A tutor works because learning is built in small steps with quick checks: an idea is explained, a question tests it, and a mistake is explained at once, while the working is still fresh. Repeating a missed question on later days moves it into lasting memory, which is why spaced repetition beats cramming.
