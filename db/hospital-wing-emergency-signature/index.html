Signature Triage Lantern

A wall of soft lantern lights above each triage chair that shifts color with the patient's live acuity score, so the whole team reads the waiting room's urgency at a glance without a screen. The lantern pulses gently when a patient's vitals drift, calling a nurse before an alarm ever sounds. Concept by Manon: ambient awareness instead of alarm fatigue.

Improvement: Improves on overhead paging and monitor alarms by making acuity visible to everyone in the room at once.

# Signature Triage Lantern — Signature design prototype
def esi_triage(hr, sbp, spo2, gcs, pain, resources_needed):

    return 5

Signature One-Touch Stroke Clock

A single oversized wall clock in every ED bay that starts counting the moment stroke symptoms are charted, with rings marking the tPA and thrombectomy windows. The ring turns from green to amber to red as each window closes, keeping the whole team time-aware without anyone doing math. Concept by Manon.

Improvement: Improves on manual time tracking by making the treatment window a shared physical object in the room.

Signature Calm Corridor

Improvement: Improves on standard seclusion rooms by treating agitation with environment instead of force.

Signature Sepsis Hourglass

An hourglass-shaped timer that starts at triage for any patient flagged for possible sepsis, visible on the room door and the charge nurse's dashboard. Sand runs for 60 minutes: cultures, antibiotics, fluids, lactate — each completed step lights a band. Concept by Manon.

Improvement: Improves on bundle checklists by turning the one-hour sepsis target into an unavoidable visual.

Signature Warm Handoff Rail

A continuous handrail from the ambulance bay to the trauma bay with a built-in audio strip that plays the paramedic's handoff on loop as the stretcher rolls. The team hears age, mechanism, vitals, and treatment while moving, so nobody waits for a verbal report. Concept by Manon.

Improvement: Improves on single spoken handoffs by repeating the report exactly, hands-free, during transport.
