SEO Content Analyzer Database (seo-content-analyzer)

An seo content analyzer is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy pages, and counts the marks. The analyzer reads 3 pages. The page home holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
