About this database

Archive
This database’s Signature study archive (JAH-PHYSICS-STUDY-S- records), preserved as structured, searchable data.
Record schema
id · signature_title · field · version · system_lens_review · refined_findings · experiment_solver{method, findings} · scholar_notes · year · source
Generator
jahdb-physics-study-1.0 — deterministic, every record validated by an independent checker before acceptance.
Drift control
Every generated record is checked against an archive sample for exact duplicates and re-validated by the independent checker.
AI

Generates new Physics study entries from descriptions and pulls up existing records on request; generated entries are never presented as stored archive records.
