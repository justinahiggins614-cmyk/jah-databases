Signature Spartan Ladder Chassis JAH-SCCLS-000001

Signature design: this record describes an original design in the Signature edition. It is labeled Signature throughout and is never presented as an established public fact or as a working product. The Signature Spartan ladder chassis is an original design for a simple stamped-steel ladder frame: two rails, five crossmembers, and bolted body mounts that let a small workshop build, inspect, and repair the frame with ordinary tools. The design's rule is repairability first: every joint is visible, every member is replaceable, and no structural member hides inside another one. The design is presented as a design, not as a tested vehicle.

Stamping the rails from steel sheet keeps the tooling simple, and the design specifies channel sections with reinforcement only where the engine mounts and suspension pickups land. That restraint is deliberate: adding material everywhere would make the frame heavier without making it more repairable, and the Spartan line's identity is a light, honest frame that a builder can understand at a glance.

The demo checks a Spartan frame specification: two rails, at least four crossmembers, and a rail length within the design's range, printing the verdict. It is a specification check in Python, not a structural test.
