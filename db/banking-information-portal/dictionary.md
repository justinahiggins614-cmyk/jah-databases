# Dictionary entries — The Banking Information Portal Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## bank

An institution that keeps money for others, lends money under written terms, and keeps the records of both, under the rules of the law that governs it.

## account

A named record kept by a bank for one person or business, showing money held, money paid in, and money paid out, with the balance counted at every step.

## deposit

Money paid into an account, recorded with its date and its amount, so that the account shows what was put in and the balance that followed.

## withdrawal

Money taken out of an account, recorded with its date and its amount, so that the account shows what was taken and the balance that followed.

## interest

Money paid for the use of money, counted by a stated rate over a stated time, and shown separately from the money itself, so the cost or the gain can be seen plainly.

## loan

Money lent under written terms, naming the amount, the rate, and the dates by which it must be returned, so that both lender and borrower can see the same agreement.

## central bank

The bank that stands behind the other banks of a country, setting the rules they follow and keeping the money system steady, named in the public record.

## statement

A written list of an account's movements over a stated period, with each movement dated and the balance shown, so the account holder can check every entry.

## fee

A stated charge for a banking service, named with its amount and the service it pays for, so that the account holder can see what each service costs.

## overdraft

A balance below zero, reached when more was taken out than was held, recorded with its size and its date, and charged as the account's terms allow.

## portal page

One page of the portal, answering one banking question in plain words, with its facts cited and its date shown, so the reader knows what it says and where it came from.

## public source

A published record open to any reader, named with its address and the date it was read, so that any fact in the portal can be checked by going to the source.
