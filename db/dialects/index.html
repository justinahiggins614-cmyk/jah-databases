Results reported

Australian English varies less than British or American English, but it does vary: vowels differ by state, vocabulary differs by region, and social varieties run from Broad to Cultivated. The differences are real and measurable, though smaller than in older English-speaking countries.

Skeptical analysis

Regional differences are small and shrinking, the Broad-General-Cultivated labels are idealisations, and Australian English is often described as uniform precisely because its variation is subtle.
