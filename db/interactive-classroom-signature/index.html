Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An interactive classroom website: a teacher opens a room, learners join with no sign-in and no payment, and the room offers a live poll, a hand-raising control, a shared board for notes, small group tables, and a class library. Every room is public inside the classroom, with written rules and a public moderation log, and no private messages exist anywhere in the design. This record covers A First Lesson With a Live Poll.

A first lesson with a live poll must ask one plain question and let every learner answer at once, so the teacher sees in seconds what the whole room thinks. The classroom must offer a poll with four plain choices, show the result as soon as the votes are in, and keep the lesson moving after the poll. Without a live poll, the teacher asks a question aloud and hears only the loudest learner, while the rest of the room stays silent.
