# Dictionary entries — The Regulatory Compliance Lookup Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## regulatory compliance lookup

The recorded looking up of what a rule asks and whether it has been met, holding the rule, the check, and the result together, so that the requirement can be traced from its text to its proof.

## regulation record

The written account of one rule made by a regulator, naming the rule, its maker, and its date, so that the rule can be read as it was made.

## standard record

The written account of a named standard, naming the standard and the body that set it, so that the measure can be read beside the rule it serves.

## requirement list

The written list of the things a rule asks for, kept in order, so that what must be done can be counted and checked.

## compliance check

The recorded comparison of what was done against what a rule asks, written with the rule and the result, so that the finding can be followed step by step.

## inspection record

The written account of an inspection made under a rule, naming the inspector, the place, and the findings, so that the visit can be read after it ends.

## audit record

The written account of a formal examination of records against a rule, naming the auditor and the findings, so that the examination can be followed.

## violation record

The written account of a rule found broken, naming the rule and the facts found, so that the breach can be read as it was recorded.

## corrective action

The written account of the steps taken to put right a breach of a rule, naming the breach and the steps, so that the remedy can be followed to its end.

## guidance document

The written document in which a regulator explains how a rule is to be read, kept beside the rule so that the reading can be found with the text.

## agency record

The written account of a body that makes or enforces rules, naming the body and its powers, so that the authority behind a rule can be found.

## lookup log

The ordered list of the lookups made in a compliance register, kept by date, so that every lookup can be found and followed.
