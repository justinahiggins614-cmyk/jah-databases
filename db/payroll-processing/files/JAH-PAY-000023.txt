History
Paying people for their work is as old as work itself, and the record of that payment has always mattered as much as the payment. A payroll is the list of who is paid, how much, and what was held back and why, kept so that every person can see their own figures and every figure can be checked. This database describes the payroll, its figures, and its record. It handles no money and moves no payment; it is the specification and the record only.
