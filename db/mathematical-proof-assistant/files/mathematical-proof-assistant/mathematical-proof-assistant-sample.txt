Mathematical Proof Assistant Database (mathematical-proof-assistant)

A mathematical proof assistant checks proof steps: type a step, press Check, and read the verdict. 2 + 2 = 4: True. 2 + 2 = 5: False. The sum of the first 10 numbers is 55. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: Modus ponens: from P and P implies Q, Q follows: True
