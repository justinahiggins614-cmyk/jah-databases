This record covers A First Contest Round in General Knowledge.

A first contest round in general knowledge must be small enough to finish in a few minutes and clear enough to understand at once: ten plain questions about the world around us, with four choices each. The competition must give the questions one at a time, a timer the player can see, and the answers explained in plain words after the round. Without a small first round, a player faces a hard round on the first visit and leaves before learning anything.
