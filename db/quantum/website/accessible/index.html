4. Relevant data

Browse the first 24 records

gates (24)

Gate: Pauli-X (NOT)

Quantum NOT: flips |0> to |1> and back. Hermitian, unitary, its own inverse.

Full record

Gate: Pauli-Y

Bit+phase flip: maps |0> to i|1>. Hermitian, unitary, traceless like all Paulis.

Full record

Gate: Pauli-Z (phase flip)

Leaves |0> alone, flips the phase of |1>. Diagonal in the computational basis.

Full record

Gate: Hadamard

Creates superposition: H|0> = (|0>+|1>)/√2. Hermitian and its own inverse; the workhorse of quantum parallelism.

Full record

Gate: Phase (S)

Square root of Z: adds a π/2 phase to |1>. Together with H generates the Clifford group.

Full record

Gate: T gate

Adds a π/4 phase: T = diag(1, e^{iπ/4}). With Clifford gates it gives universal quantum computation.

Full record

Gate: Controlled-NOT

Flips the target iff the control is |1>. Creates entanglement; with single-qubit gates it is universal.

Full record

Gate: Controlled-Z

Applies Z to the target iff the control is |1>. Symmetric in control and target.

Full record

Gate: SWAP

Exchanges two qubits. Decomposes into three CNOTs.

Full record

Gate: Toffoli (CCNOT)

Doubly-controlled NOT: flips the target iff both controls are |1>. Universal for classical reversible computing.

Full record

Gate: Square root of NOT

V gate: applied twice it equals X. Native on several superconducting platforms.

Full record
