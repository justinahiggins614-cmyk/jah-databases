AI Data Analyst Database (ai-data-analyst-signature)

A data analyst reads a table of numbers and finds what it means: totals, averages, the biggest and smallest values, the middle value and the pattern. The local analyst on this page works with rules in this browser, with no sign-in. Hosted AI help is labelled BLOCKED until a hosted backend is connected; nothing here pretends to be hosted.

Worked example: A table of 10 rows is read and its average is found.
