  1. What a Data Analyst Does

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A data analyst reads a table of numbers and finds what it means: averages, biggest values, and patterns. A table of 10 rows is read and its average is found.

    Math proof: An average is the total divided by the count: the average of [4, 8, 6, 10, 2] is 30 divided by 5, which is 6.0. A range is the biggest minus the smallest: 12 minus 3 is 9. A percentage change is the difference over the starting value: from 50 to 60 is a rise of 20 percent. The middle value of [1, 3, 5, 7, 9] is 5.

    Science proof: A data analyst works because numbers in a table have a shape: the total says how much, the average says what is typical, the range says how spread out, and the most common value says what repeats. Sorting the numbers makes the shape visible, which is why the analyst shows the sorted list beside the summary.
