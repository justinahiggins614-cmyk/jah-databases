# The Travel Insurance Database

The Travel Insurance Database is the travel insurance record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full travel insurance record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database insurance, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
