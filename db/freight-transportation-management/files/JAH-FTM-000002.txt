History
Freight is goods carried in bulk, and moving it has always needed managing: the canal boat, the railway wagon, the lorry, and the container ship each carry goods that must be matched to a route, a carrier, and a time, and the match must be written down. Managing freight transportation means keeping that match true for every movement, from the loading to the delivery, so that every load can be followed and every carrier can be held to account. From the merchant's ledger to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the movement down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every freight movement true.
