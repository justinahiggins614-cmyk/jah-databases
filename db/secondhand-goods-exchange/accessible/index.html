The Secondhand Goods Exchange Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The secondhand goods exchange helps a person pass a used thing to someone who needs it, in plain words, with what it is, its condition, and its price written out before anyone sets out. Every listing is a clearly labeled illustrative sample of a generic kind of used thing, never a real listing from a real person.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The secondhand goods exchange helps a person pass a used thing to someone who needs it, in plain words, with what it is, its condition, and its price written out before anyone sets out. Every listing is a clearly labeled illustrative sample of a generic kind of used thing, never a real listing from a real person. A used bicycle passed to someone who needs it gives a person a plain way to pass on a bicycle that is still sound, with what it is, its condition, and its price written out before anyone sets out. The exchange must show the bicycle in plain words, so a person who reads the listing knows whether the wheels are sound and what it costs. Without those plain facts, a person cannot tell a sound bicycle from a broken one, and a listing without a condition is a poor way to pass on a bicycle.   12. A Streak of Exchanges Seen Each Week

    Record 12 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A streak gives a person a reason to come back next week: one exchange seen each week in a row builds a chain, and breaking the chain costs something the person has earned. The streak must be forgiving, because a person who misses one week through no fault of their own should not lose a month of exchanges. Without a streak, every exchange is a first exchange, and the site gives a loyal person nothing that a stranger does not also get.
