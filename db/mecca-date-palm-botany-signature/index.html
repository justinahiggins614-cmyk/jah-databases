The date palm feeds oases, shades gardens, and supplies fruit, fibre, and timber from a single tree. This database follows the palm from root to fruit to product, keeping published facts, ranges, and tradition strictly labeled, and giving every record a working calculator a reader can run. Every record is the full, fully-formed file: full prose description in sentences and paragraphs, an explicit faith label (fact, tradition, or interpretation) with the distinction stated in the text, a named source with a source-quality label, and a working demo program. Everything is free forever, with no payments and no upsells.
