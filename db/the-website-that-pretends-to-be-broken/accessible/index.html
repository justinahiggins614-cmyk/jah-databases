The The Website That Pretends to Be Broken Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The website that pretends to be broken is a playful trick: its buttons wobble, its pictures load upside down, and its text pretends to fall apart, while nothing is really wrong and every visitor can find the real page underneath. It is a design study of a joke, and it never hides a real fault or a real page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
