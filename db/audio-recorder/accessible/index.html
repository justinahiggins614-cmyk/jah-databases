The Audio Recorder Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An audio recorder captures sound and keeps it as a clip, in the browser: press Start, allow the microphone, and press Stop to save. The demo on this page records for real through the browser media API, and it is local-demo-only: a real microphone is needed, and no recording is ever faked. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/audio-recorder/index.jsonl.gz
