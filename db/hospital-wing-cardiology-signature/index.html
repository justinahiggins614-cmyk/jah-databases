Signature Heartbeat Room

A recovery room where the patient's own steady heartbeat plays softly through the walls, synced live from their monitor. Hearing a strong, regular rhythm calms post-heart-attack fear like nothing else. Concept by Manon.

Improvement: Improves on silent recovery rooms with the reassuring sound of one's own healing heart.
