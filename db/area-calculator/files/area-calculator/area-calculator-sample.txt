Area Calculator Database (area-calculator)

An area calculator is a rule-based local demo: it counts the square units inside set toy shapes, and counts the shapes. The calculator counts 3 shapes. The square is 6 by 6: area 36. The shapes hold 180 square units in total. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
