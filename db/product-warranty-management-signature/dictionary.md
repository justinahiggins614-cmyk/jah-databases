# Dictionary entries — The Product Warranty Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## warranty

The written promise that a product will work as stated for a stated period, naming the product, the period, and the cover, kept as the single true record of the promise.

## guarantee

A promise about a product, often in plain words, that it will work or be put right, recorded with its terms, so that the promise can be proved when the product fails.

## warranty period

The stated length of time a warranty covers, written with its start and end dates, so that a claim inside the period and one outside it are handled by the same rule.

## warranty claim

A holder's written request under a warranty after a product fails, recorded with the product, the fault, and the warranty it rests on, and tracked until it is answered.

## covered defect

A fault a warranty promises to put right, named in the warranty itself, so that a holder can see what is covered and what is not before any failure happens.

## exclusion

Something a warranty states it does not cover, written in the warranty itself, so that a holder is never surprised by a gap the warranty had already named.

## registration

The act of recording a product and its buyer with the maker, so that the warranty can be found and proved when a claim is made, kept with the warranty record.

## proof of purchase

The written proof that a product was bought, such as a receipt, kept with the warranty, so that the purchase date and the seller can be shown when a claim is made.

## repair

The mending of a faulty product under warranty, recorded with what was mended and when, so that the repair can be shown and the warranty can be read after it.

## replacement

A new product given in place of a faulty one under warranty, recorded with what was given and when, so that the exchange is complete on the record.

## service centre

The place where warranty repairs are carried out, named in the warranty record, so that a holder knows where to take a faulty product and the repair can be traced.

## extended warranty

A warranty that continues cover beyond the first period, on terms written down again, so that the longer cover has its own record and its own dates.
