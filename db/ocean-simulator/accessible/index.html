The Ocean Simulator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. An ocean simulator simulates ocean currents and waves with real physics: type the depth, press Simulate, and read the wave speed. A wave in 10 metres of water travels 9.9 metres per second. At 100 metres the pressure is 1004.5 kilopascals. A tsunami in 4000 metres of water travels 197.99 metres per second. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
