The Signature Family History Database

Exhaustive

march to a million: in progress

Archive records: 16 / 1,000,000 target · 16 records (populated from the live archive index)

Sixteen full records for family history record, ordered simple to compounded to complex, each with a full prose description in sentences and paragraphs, an evidence label kept distinct from fact, study, aggregate, method, likely inference, and Signature original, a named source, and a working demo program whose stored output byte-matches a fresh run. Everything is free forever, with no payments and no upsells. This database holds public records and published research only: no living person's personal data, no private test results, no individual modern genomes, and no medical claims of any kind. Every inferred relationship in this suite is labeled LIKELY with its evidence basis and confidence, never as proven fact.

Specialist AI: JAH Family History Guide

JAH Family History Guide answers questions about this database, explains any record in plain sentences, compares records side by side on the same fields, and helps a reader follow the ladder from simple to compounded to complex. It reports evidence under its own label and never presents a generated design as a public record.

Boundless generator

The generator below is seeded and deterministic, throttled to at most 100 records per call, duplicate-checked, and hard-stopped at 10^15 records. Every generated record is labeled src "generated". Open the page script to use window.FamilyHistorySignatureTools.generateRecord(seed).

Validation engine

Each record is checked for a complete title, its own record kind, its own edition slug and id prefix, its evidence label, a full prose description, a named source, and a demo whose stored output byte-matches a fresh run.

What Family History Is Rank 1 · simple

Signature companion: an original family-history method framework that treats each claim as a node with a citation edge, a confidence weight, and a visible gap marker. The framework is the Signature edition's own design, and it claims no historical fact; it is a tool for organizing public records, not a substitute for them. The Signature edition presents this framework as an original companion to the public record of the same title; it does not replace the public record, and it must never be quoted as an authentic public record. Each Signature framework in this pair is labeled as an original design wherever it appears, so that no reader mistakes a model, a worksheet, or a scoring rule for a public record or for a proven finding about any person.
