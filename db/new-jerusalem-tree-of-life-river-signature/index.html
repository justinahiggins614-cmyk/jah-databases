River Hydrology Model

ID: JAH-NJTRS-000009 · Edition: signature · Ladder: compounded · Status: established · Kind: tree of life river record

Text-faithful view: The faith view describes a river flowing from the throne through the city (Revelation 22:1); hydrology models how fast a river flows, given its channel shape, slope, and roughness, using the Manning equation, an established empirical formula.

Engineering view: The Manning equation states that velocity equals (1/n) times hydraulic radius to the two-thirds power times slope to the one-half power, where n is a roughness coefficient (about 0.03 for a clean natural channel, higher for weedy or rocky channels). Discharge equals velocity times cross-sectional area.

History and description: The model is empirical, not exact: it predicts well for steady, uniform flow and poorly for floods, bends, and obstructions, which is why real river design pairs it with physical measurement and safety margins.

Science proof: Science proof: the demo computes velocity and discharge for a model channel 10 metres wide, 1 metre deep, slope 0.001, roughness 0.03, standard Manning arithmetic.

Mathematics and computation: Computation: hydraulic radius equals area divided by wetted perimeter; velocity follows Manning; discharge equals velocity times area.
