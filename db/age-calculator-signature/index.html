An Age in Years From a Birth Date Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An age calculator website: the visitor enters a birth date, and the page counts the days from that date to a stated reference date, showing the birth date, the reference date, the day count, and the age in years beside it. Leap days are handled by the calendar rules, and every step is shown so a visitor can check the age by hand. This record covers An Age in Years From a Birth Date.

A visitor who wants an age in years from a birth date needs an exact age, not a rounded guess: years, months, and days counted from a birth date to a chosen date, with leap days handled correctly. Guessing an age from the year alone is often wrong by a whole year, and a wrong age can misstate eligibility for school, work, or retirement. This record states the design of a calculator that counts from a birth date with calendar arithmetic and shows every step. An age in years is the simplest age calculation, so the calculator must count full years from the birth date to the reference date and show the birth date it used. This is a simple record: one idea and one calculation, with nothing extra to learn.

Worked example, Signature design: the same age calculation is rebuilt from the Signature pattern, so an age in years from a birth date runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
