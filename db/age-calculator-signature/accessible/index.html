  1. An Age in Years From a Birth Date

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The age calculator turns a birth date into an exact age in years and days, counted against a stated reference date with calendar arithmetic, so a visitor can see the age and the dates it came from. A visitor who wants an age in years from a birth date needs an exact age, not a rounded guess: years, months, and days counted from a birth date to a chosen date, with leap days handled correctly. Guessing an age from the year alone is often wrong by a whole year, and a wrong age can misstate eligibility for school, work, or retirement. This record states the design of a calculator that counts from a birth date with calendar arithmetic and shows every step. An age in years is the simplest age calculation, so the calculator must count full years from the birth date to the reference date and show the birth date it used. This is a simple record: one idea and one calculation, with nothing extra to learn.
