The Guess What Happens Next Arena Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The guess what happens next arena stops a story halfway and asks every visitor to guess the ending: a door about to open, a gift about to be unwrapped, a race about to begin. Every round is a design study of a guessing game, and the fair-play rules are written out in plain words.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
