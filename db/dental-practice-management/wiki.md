# The Dental Practice Management Database

The Dental Practice Management Database is the dental practice management record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full dental practice management record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database signature-interview-practice, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
