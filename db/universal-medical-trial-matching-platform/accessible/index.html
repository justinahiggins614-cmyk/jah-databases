The Universal Medical Trial Matching Platform Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The universal medical trial matching platform lists medical trials in plain words, one listing for each study, with the study, the place, and a plain-word summary written out. Every listing is informational only, a clearly labeled design study, never a claim of enrollment and never advice about any person's health. The platform never names a condition for a person, never names an amount of any medicine, and never promises a place on any trial.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/universal-medical-trial-matching-platform/index.jsonl.gz

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The universal medical trial matching platform lists medical trials in plain words, one listing for each study, with the study, the place, and a plain-word summary written out. Every listing is informational only, a clearly labeled design study, never a claim of enrollment and never advice about any person's health. The platform never names a condition for a person, never names an amount of any medicine, and never promises a place on any trial. This record is about a trial listing written in plain words, one listing in a shared list of medical trials that any reader can search in plain words, with the study, the place, and the plain-word summary written out, and no promise of a place on any trial. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.
