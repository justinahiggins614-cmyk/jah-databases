3. Math proof (standard math + metamath)

Formal statement: solve 3x^2 − 75 = 0; the solution is x = 5 or x = −5.

Worked steps — the proof

1
3x^2 − 75 = 0
2
Discriminant D = b² − 4ac = 0² − 4(3)(-75) = 900
3
√D = 30
4
x = (−b ± √D) / 2a = (0 ± 30) / 6
5
x = 5 or x = −5
