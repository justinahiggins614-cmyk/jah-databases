The Equations Database is the official archive of solved equations: 132,300 records, each with its equation, its worked steps, its check by substitution, and its interactive demo program. Every record carries the archive’s stamp: “Official JAH Equation Archive — solved once, published officially, retrieved thereafter.”

This website opens the first 24 records — linear, quadratic, system, trig, power, log, exponential, evaluate, percent, derivative, integral, and geometry — one full website per record. The full archive of 132,300 records lives on the live hub page for the equations database.

3. Math proof (standard math + metamath)

Example — the first record’s proof: solve 3x − 19 = −13.

1
3x − 19 = −13
2
3x = −13 − (−19) = 6
3
x = 6 / 3 = 2

Each record page carries its own full proof — the worked steps, verified by substitution — and its own demo program.

4. Science proof

Verification by substitution: every record’s check field substitutes the solution back into the equation and confirms it holds. That is how this archive is verified — no record is published without its check.
