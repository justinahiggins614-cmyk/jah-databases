## Equation

d/dx[3x^2 − x − 9] = ?

## Solution

f'(x) = 6x − 1

## Steps
1. f(x) = 3x^2 − x − 9
2. Apply the power rule d/dx[x^n] = n·x^(n−1):
3. d/dx[3x^2] = 6x
4. d/dx[−x] = -1
5. d/dx[-9] = 0 (constant)
6. f'(x) = 6x − 1
