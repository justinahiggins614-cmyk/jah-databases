## Equation

5x + 4y = 71 and 4x + 5y = 73

## Solution

x = 7, y = 9

## Steps
1. (1) 5x + 4y = 71
2. (2) 4x + 5y = 73
3. Determinant = 5(5) − 4(4) = 9
4. x = (5(71) − 4(73)) / 9 = 7
5. y = (5(73) − 4(71)) / 9 = 9

## Check

5(7) + 4(9) = 71 ✓
