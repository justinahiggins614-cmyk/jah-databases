# The Delivery Dispatch Database

The Delivery Dispatch Database is the delivery dispatch record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full delivery dispatch record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database tech-887-ai-assisted-emergency-medical-dispatch, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
