# Dictionary entries — The Delivery Dispatch Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## dispatch

The work of sending deliveries out, deciding who carries what, in which order, and by which road, and writing the decision down, so that every delivery can be followed.

## dispatcher

The person who plans and sends out deliveries, matching each delivery to a driver and a route, and keeping the record of what was sent and when.

## delivery

One parcel or set of goods carried to one recipient at one address, recorded with its contents, its destination, and its time, so that the delivery can be followed to its door.

## route

The ordered list of stops a driver follows, written down before the vehicle leaves, so that the deliveries are made in a sensible order and none is missed.

## driver

The person who carries deliveries along a route, named in the dispatch record, so that every delivery can be traced to the driver who carried it.

## vehicle

The van, truck, or other carriage used for deliveries, named in the dispatch record with its load, so that the load and the carriage agree.

## manifest

The written list of all deliveries loaded on one vehicle for one route, carried by the driver, so that every parcel on board can be checked against the list.

## drop-off

The act of handing a delivery to its recipient at the destination, recorded with the time and the name of who received it, so that the delivery is proved done.

## pick-up

The act of collecting goods from a sender to be delivered, recorded with the time and the place, so that the goods are known to be on their way.

## time window

The stated hours during which a delivery is promised, written in the dispatch record, so that the recipient knows when to expect the delivery and the driver knows when it is due.

## proof of delivery

The written or signed proof that a delivery reached its recipient, kept with the dispatch record, so that a delivery can be shown done, not merely claimed.

## depot

The building where parcels are gathered, sorted, and loaded for dispatch, named in the record, so that every delivery can be traced to the depot it left.
