History
When goods must reach many doors in one day, someone must decide who carries what, in which order, and by which road: that deciding is dispatch. From the messenger who ran between towns to the depot that sends out a fleet each morning, the craft has been the same — match each delivery to a driver, lay the stops in a sensible order, and keep the record so that every parcel can be followed. Dispatch is the ordering of deliveries, and its record is the proof that each delivery was sent. This database is built on that public practice of dispatching every delivery true, in order, with its driver named.
