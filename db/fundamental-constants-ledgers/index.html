Quantum Chromodynamics Vacuum Condensate Database Rank 4 · simple

Evidence: established Established: the condensate scale is the standard value from sum rules and lattice QCD; the record states the extraction method and its scale dependence.

The QCD vacuum is not empty: it carries condensates, non-zero expectation values of quark and gluon fields. The quark condensate <q-bar q> is about -(0.24 GeV)^3, or -0.0138 GeV^3 in magnitude, and the gluon condensate <(alpha_s / pi) G^2> is about 0.012 GeV^4.
