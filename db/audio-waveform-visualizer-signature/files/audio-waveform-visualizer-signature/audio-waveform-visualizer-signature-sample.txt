Audio Waveform Visualizer Database (audio-waveform-visualizer-signature)

An audio waveform visualizer is a seeded local demo: it draws set toy samples as bars, and counts the loudest, the quietest and the average. The visualizer draws 6 samples. The loudest sample is 60. A sample of 40 draws a bar 4 high. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
