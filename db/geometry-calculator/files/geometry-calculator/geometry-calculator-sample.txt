Geometry Calculator Database (geometry-calculator)

A geometry calculator finds areas, perimeters and volumes: pick a shape, type its sizes, press Calculate, and read the answer. A rectangle 4 by 6 has area 24, and a circle of radius 5 has area 78.54. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
