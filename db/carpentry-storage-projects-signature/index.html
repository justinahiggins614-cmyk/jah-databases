Storage Box Rank 1 · simple · boxes · beginner

Source: signature-original

Signature framing: the Signature storage box adds a fitted lid with a finger pull, turning the open box into a dust-proof case. The Signature edition of this build uses its own dimensions, 26 x 12 x 16 inches (W x H x D), and its own cut list below. This is a Signature original design, labeled signature-original, and it is not presented as an authentic public record.

Project diagram

Original dimensioned project sketch for Storage Box: a front-view diagram showing the overall 26 x 12 x 16 inch (W x H x D) form, the main cut pieces, and the overall dimensions labeled on the drawing, drawn for this record.

Specifications

Dimensions: 26 x 12 x 16 in (W x H x D)
Tolerances: Cut to within 1/16 in; check square by measuring both diagonals and matching them.
Load notes: A storage box rated for about 40 pounds of household goods.
Arithmetic: Cut list adds up: Total pieces: 7 (2 x Side, long, 2 x Side, short, 1 x Bottom, 2 x Handle cleat). Longest piece 26 in fits within the 30.5287 in face diagonal of the overall form. Cut the longest pieces first so a mistake on a short piece does not waste a long board.

Materials

  • Box boards: pine, 3/4 in
  • Bottom panel: birch plywood, 1/2 in

Cut list

PieceDimensionsQty
Side, long26 x 11 x 0.75 in2
Side, short14.5 x 11 x 0.75 in2
Bottom22.5 x 14.5 x 0.5 in1
Handle cleat8 x 2 x 0.75 in2

Fasteners

  • Wood glue and 1-1/2 in screws, 20 total

How to build

  1. Read the cut list and the arithmetic note in full, and check every piece against the plan; measure twice before cutting.
  2. Cut the pieces in order, longest first: Side, long (26 x 11 x 0.75 in), Side, short (14.5 x 11 x 0.75 in), Bottom (22.5 x 14.5 x 0.5 in).
  3. Dry-fit the frame without glue and check both diagonals match so the assembly is square.
  4. Sand the inside edges too, since hands reach inside a box more than they touch its outside.
  5. Glue and fasten as listed in the specs, keeping the assembly square while fasteners go in; wipe glue squeeze-out before it dries.
  6. Sand through the grits, ease the edges, and finish as described in the record; check the finished piece for square and for its rated load before use.

Tools required

  • Tape measure
  • Combination square
  • Sharp pencil
  • Handsaw or circular saw
  • Drill driver
  • Clamps (2)
  • Sandpaper, 80/120/180 grit
  • Workbench or sawhorses

Safety gear

  • Safety glasses
  • Hearing protection
  • Dust mask
  • Work gloves for handling rough lumber (never near rotating blades)

Wear eye protection for every cut and sanding pass, hearing protection for power tools, and a dust mask for sanding. Unplug power tools or remove batteries before changing blades or bits, keep guards in place, and keep hands clear of blades. Clamp work before cutting, never hold a small piece by hand near a blade, and clear offcuts before the next cut.

    print("%s: %.2f board feet" % (name, bf))

print("Total pieces: %d" % 7)

Specifications

Dimensions: 50 x 72 x 24 in (W x H x D)
Tolerances: Cut to within 1/16 in; check square by measuring both diagonals and matching them.
Load notes: A wardrobe rated for about 100 pounds of hanging clothes plus shelf goods; it must be strapped to the wall.
Arithmetic: Cut list adds up: Total pieces: 10 (2 x Side, 1 x Top, 1 x Bottom, 2 x Shelf, 2 x Door, 1 x Hanging rail, 1 x Back panel). Longest piece 72 in fits within the 87.6584 in face diagonal of the overall form. Cut the longest pieces first so a mistake on a short piece does not waste a long board.

How to build

  1. Read the cut list and the arithmetic note in full, and check every piece against the plan; measure twice before cutting.
  2. Cut the pieces in order, longest first: Side (24 x 71 x 0.75 in), Top (46.5 x 23 x 0.75 in), Bottom (46.5 x 23 x 0.75 in).
  3. Dry-fit the frame without glue and check both diagonals match so the assembly is square.
  4. Hang the doors last, after the case is level and strapped, so they hang true.
  5. Glue and fasten as listed in the specs, keeping the assembly square while fasteners go in; wipe glue squeeze-out before it dries.
  6. Sand through the grits, ease the edges, and finish as described in the record; check the finished piece for square and for its rated load before use.
