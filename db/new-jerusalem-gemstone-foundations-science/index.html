Engineering view: Jasper in modern gemology is an opaque variety of chalcedony, a microcrystalline quartz coloured by iron oxides and other inclusions. A foundation course described as jasper is therefore, in engineering terms, a dense, opaque, polishable silica stone, not a transparent gem.

History and description: Jasper has been cut, polished, and set since antiquity, used for seals, beads, vases, and wall facing.
