History
Owners of many patents have long kept them in lists and ledgers, so that no grant was lost, no term lapsed unseen, and no licence was given twice. Portfolio management grew beside the patent offices: a kept record of every patent held, its dates, its owners, and its licences, read as one whole. From the leather-bound ledger of grants to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the holding down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every patent portfolio true. It is a catalog of published patent records only, and it files nothing on any person behalf.
