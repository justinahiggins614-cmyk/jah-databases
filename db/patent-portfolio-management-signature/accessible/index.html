The Signature Patent Portfolio Management Database holds 24 full patent portfolio management record records, in a teaching ladder: 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex. The Design/System

The design is one database per subject, with a public edition and a Signature edition side by side. Every record gives its history, its full content, its methods, its results as reported, and its skeptical analysis. Every technical term is defined in plain words where it first appears, and the glossary defines them all again. Each Signature record is a design study, labeled as a design, never presented as established fact.

The Generator

The boundless generator for this database is the file gen-patent-portfolio-management-signature.js. It is seeded and deterministic: the same seed always gives the same record, and different seeds differ. It is throttled to 100 records per call, it stops at 1,000,000,000 records per file, and every generated record is labeled generated, never presented as an archive record.

Math Proof

The counting argument is exact: 24 records, 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex, and 8 + 8 + 8 = 24. The formal statement is: portfolio(patents) = one true record per patent held, with its term, its owner, and its status, or a named gap, never a silent gap (stated, not machine-checked) The scientific statement is: portfolio completeness = patents fully recorded / all patents held (stated, not machine-checked) Both are stated, not machine-checked, and the record-level proofs are in each record's math proof.
