# The International Travel Assistance Database

The International Travel Assistance Database is the international travel assistance record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full international travel assistance record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database form-filling-assistance, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
