History
Health insurance has been sold for more than a century, and the policies that carry it have grown long and hard to read, with premiums, deductibles, copayments, networks, and exclusions written in terms that few policyholders know by heart. Assistance grew beside the policies: clerks, advisers, and public helpers who read the policy aloud, filled in the forms, and wrote down what was asked and what was answered. From the paper form filed in a drawer to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the help down whole.
