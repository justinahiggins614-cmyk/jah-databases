Text-to-Speech Database (text-to-speech)

Text-to-speech reads written words aloud with a voice, so text can be heard instead of read. Type or paste text, pick a voice, set the speed and pitch, and press Speak. The voices are the browser's own; nothing is uploaded and no sign-in is needed.

Worked example: The sentence 'Hello, world' is spoken as 2 words.
