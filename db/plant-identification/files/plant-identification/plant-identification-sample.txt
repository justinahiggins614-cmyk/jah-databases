Plant Identification Database (plant-identification)

A plant identification is a rule-based local demo: it identifies set toy plants, and counts the marks. Sample-data identification only: no cultivation how-to, no grow schedules, no yield instructions. Sample plants only, no real fields read. The identifier reads 3 plants. The plant alpha holds 180 marks. The largest holds 180 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
