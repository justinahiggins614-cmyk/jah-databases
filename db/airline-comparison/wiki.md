# The Airline Comparison Database

The Airline Comparison Database is the airline comparison record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full airline comparison record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database communication-aid-comparison, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
