AI Image Generator Database (ai-image-generator)

An AI image generator is a seeded local demo: it makes a small grid of pixels from a fixed seed, and the same seed always makes the same grid. Type a prompt and a seed, press Generate, and read the grid. A grid of 64 pixels averages 120.0. A grid of 8 by 8 holds 64 pixels. The grid averages 120.0. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
