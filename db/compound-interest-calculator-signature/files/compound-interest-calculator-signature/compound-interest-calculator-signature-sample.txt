Compound Interest Calculator Database (compound-interest-calculator-signature)

A compound interest calculator works out what savings grow to: type the figures, press Calculate, and read the result. This is an estimator with example rates, not financial advice. 100 at 5 percent for 1 year is 105.00. 100 at 5 percent for 10 years is 162.89. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
