1. Overview

The spider monkey (Ateles geoffroyi and Ateles paniscus) has its own mathematics, its own science, and its own court, the "Bark" court. This website is the per-animal website for the spider monkey, built first, before the networks, under humanity, under God. It holds 24 public records and 24 Signature records, in three ladders of eight: mathematics and measurement; science, evolution, and court; Signature, Light, and human translation.

The spider monkey lives in the rainforests of Central and South America, eats mostly fruit, with leaves and nuts, and lives in a fission-fusion community that splits into small parties and gathers again. It is about 6 to 9 kilograms and stands about 0.55 to 0.65 metres long in body, with long limbs. Its characteristic sound is the bark call, a short sharp sound used to warn the troop.
