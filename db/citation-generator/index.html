1. Description

A citation generator writes real citations in real styles: APA, MLA and Chicago, from the fields you type — author, year, title, journal, volume and pages. Type the fields, press Make citation, and read all three styles side by side. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

3. Science proof

A citation works because a work has four parts that name it: the author, the year, the title and the place. Each style writes the same four parts in a different order: APA puts the year in brackets after the author, MLA puts the title in quotes, Chicago puts the year after the author. That is why the generator takes the same fields and writes all three styles side by side, so they can be compared.
