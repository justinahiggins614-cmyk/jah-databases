The Citation Generator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A citation generator writes real citations in real styles: APA, MLA and Chicago, from the fields you type — author, year, title, journal, volume and pages. Type the fields, press Make citation, and read all three styles side by side. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A citation names the author, the year, the title and the place of a work. A citation names the author, the year, the title and the place: Lovelace, A. (1843). Notes on the Analytical Engine. Scientific Memoirs, 3, 691-731.

    Science proof: A citation works because a work has four parts that name it: the author, the year, the title and the place. Each style writes the same four parts in a different order: APA puts the year in brackets after the author, MLA puts the title in quotes, Chicago puts the year after the author. That is why the generator takes the same fields and writes all three styles side by side, so they can be compared.
