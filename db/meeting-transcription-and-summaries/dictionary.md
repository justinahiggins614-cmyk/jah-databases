# Dictionary entries — The Meeting Transcription and Summaries Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## transcript

The written record of a meeting, holding every spoken word in order, with each speaker named, and every gap marked.

## summary

A short written account of a meeting, naming what was decided, what was agreed, and what was left open, with the transcript beside it.

## speaker

A person who spoke in a meeting, named in the transcript beside every word they said, so their words can be answered for.

## minutes

The formal written record of a meeting, approved by its members, naming the decisions and the people who must act on them.

## decision

Something the meeting agreed to do, written in the summary with the names of those who must do it and the date it is due.

## action item

One piece of work agreed in a meeting, named with the person who must do it and the date it is due, kept where all can see it.

## gap

A place in a transcript where words could not be heard, marked plainly instead of being filled with a guess.

## timestamp

The time written beside a spoken word, so that any word in the transcript can be found again in the meeting's recording.

## recording

The kept sound of a meeting, held beside the transcript, so that any word can be checked against what was actually said.

## agenda

The written list of what a meeting will discuss, agreed before the meeting, so that the transcript can be read against it.

## attendee

A person who was present at a meeting, named in the record, whether or not they spoke.

## correction

A change made to a transcript after it was written, marked beside the words it changes, with the name of the person who made it.
