# The Meeting Transcription and Summaries Database

The Meeting Transcription and Summaries Database is the meeting transcription and summaries record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full meeting transcription and summaries record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database writeaid-scientific-literature-summaries, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
