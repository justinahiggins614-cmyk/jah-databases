Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A logic gate simulator runs gates on ones and zeros: pick a gate, set the inputs, press Run, and read the output. 1 AND 1 is 1. 1 XOR 1 is 0. A half adder with 1 + 1 gives sum 0 and carry 1. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
