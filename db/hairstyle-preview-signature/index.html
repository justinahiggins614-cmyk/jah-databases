1. Description and History

A hairstyle preview lets a person see a new haircut or color on their own photo before committing: the style shaped to the head, matched to the hair's texture, and shown in the photo's own light. This database designs that preview one record at a time, from a shorter cut to a bold color, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.

Trying a hairstyle once meant a mirror, a magazine picture, and a leap of faith. Software later learned to place a hairstyle on a photo of the wearer: first as a flat cut-out, then shaped to the head's angle, then matched to the hair's texture and the photo's light. The design in this database follows that path, keeps the user's photo private, and shows the style honestly, including where it would need upkeep.

Every record here is a design, never a real product. Public records are fact-checked, real, and legal-only; Signature records are the Signature original version of the same subject; speculative material is labeled design-study, never as fact. Photo tools work on the user's own photos only, never anyone else's private images.
