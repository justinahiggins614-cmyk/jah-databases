History
Schools have kept rolls of their pupils for centuries, because a school that cannot say who sits in its rooms cannot teach them in order. Enrollment grew beside the school itself: a parent or student asks for a place, the school writes the name in the roll, and the term begins with every seat matched to a name. From the hand-written admission book in a school office to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the enrollment down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every student enrollment true.
