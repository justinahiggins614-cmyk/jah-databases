# Dictionary entries — The Student Enrollment Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## student enrollment management

The recorded keeping of student enrollments from application to start of term, so that every student enrolled can be followed from the paper that asked for the place to the roll that holds the name.

## enrollment

The recorded entry of a student on the roll of a school for a stated term, kept with the date and the place, so that the taking of the place can be proved from the record.

## applicant

The person who asks for a place at a school, named in the record with the application, so that who sought the place can be read plainly.

## admission

The written grant of a place at a school to a named applicant, kept in the record with its date and its terms, so that the grant can be proved from the paper.

## registrar

The officer of a school who keeps the enrollment rolls and the records of admission, named in the record with the papers kept, so that who kept the roll can be traced to the office.

## place

A single seat offered to a student for a stated term, written in the record with its class and its year, so that the seat can be matched to the student who holds it.

## term

A stated span of teaching in a school year, written in the record with its start and its end, so that every enrollment can be matched to the term it belongs to.

## application

The written request for a place at a school, naming the applicant and the term sought, kept with the papers it carries, so that the request and its papers can be read together.

## waiting list

The ordered list of applicants who have asked for a place but have not yet been given one, kept in order so that the next place offered can be matched to the next name in line.

## transfer

The written record of a student moving from one school or class to another, kept with the date and both places, so that the change of place can be followed step by step.

## withdrawal

The written record of a student leaving a school before the term ends, kept with the date and the reason given, so that the leaving can be proved from the paper.

## class list

The ordered list of students enrolled in one class for one term, kept in name order, so that every student in the class can be counted from the list itself.
