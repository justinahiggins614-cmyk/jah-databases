The Chance of One Coin Landing Heads Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A probability calculator website: the visitor enters the chance of one event and the number of tries, and the page works out the chance of at least one success, showing the single chance, the number of tries, and the result together. Every step is shown in plain numbers, so a visitor can check the arithmetic without trusting a hidden figure. This record covers The Chance of One Coin Landing Heads.

A visitor who wants the chance of one coin landing heads needs a plain chance stated as a number: the chance of one event, the chance of several, and the arithmetic that joins them, each shown so the sum can be checked. Chances are easy to misread, and a misread chance can make a rare event look likely or a likely event look rare. This record states the design of a calculator that works chances with plain arithmetic and shows every step. One coin landing heads is a half chance, so the calculator must show the two outcomes, the one wanted, and the chance as a fraction and a percent. This is a simple record: one idea and one calculation, with nothing extra to learn.
