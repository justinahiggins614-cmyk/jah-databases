Shipping Calculator Database (shipping-calculator-signature)

A shipping calculator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy parcels, and counts the coins. The calculator reads 3 parcels. The parcel small holds 12 coins. The largest holds 12 coins. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
