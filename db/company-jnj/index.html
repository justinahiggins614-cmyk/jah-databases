Johnson & Johnson — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Johnson & Johnson is an American multinational healthcare company headquartered in New Brunswick, New Jersey, founded in 1886. It operates pharmaceutical (Janssen) and MedTech businesses; its consumer health division was spun off as Kenvue in 2023. Brands originated by J&J include Band-Aid, Tylenol, and Neutrogena (now Kenvue).
