AI Engineer Database (ai-engineer)

An AI engineer is a rule-based local demo: it sizes a beam from its load and span, and checks the result against a safe limit. A load of 100 over a span of 4 gives a bending moment of 100. A load of 200 over a span of 6 gives a bending moment of 300. A capacity of 300 over a load of 100 gives a safety factor of 3. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
