A Look to Open a Page Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A gaze guide website: a visitor picks a command, such as a look to open or a blink to confirm, and the guide shows the command in plain words, what it does, and how to practice it, with a rest mark that stops all commands. Every command is a generic study, and the guide never claims the commands steer a live computer. This record covers A Look to Open a Page.

A steady look that opens a page, with a plain sign the page is open. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a look to open a page runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
