Video Compressor Database (video-compressor-signature)

A video compressor shrinks a video file by using fewer bits: a lower bitrate, a smaller resolution, or both. The demo on this page compresses a seeded clip locally: a clip of 30 seconds, 900 frames and 30 megabytes. A full transcode needs a backend, and that is BLOCKED; this is a local partial demo, and it says so. You can also upload your own clip, which is read in the browser and never leaves the page.

Worked example: A video compressor shrinks 30 megabytes to 15 megabytes
