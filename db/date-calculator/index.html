Counting the Days Between Two Dates Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A date calculator website: the visitor picks two dates, and the page counts the days between them with calendar arithmetic, showing the start date, the end date, the day count, and the same count in weeks. Leap years and month lengths are handled by the calendar rules, and every step is shown so a visitor can check the count by hand. This record covers Counting the Days Between Two Dates.

A visitor who wants counting the days between two dates needs an exact count, not a rough guess: days counted one by one, with leap years and month lengths handled correctly. Counting by hand is slow and easy to get wrong, and a wrong count can miss a deadline or misstate an age. This record states the design of a calculator that counts dates with calendar arithmetic, shows the dates it used, and lets any visitor repeat the count. Two dates and one count is the simplest date calculation, so the calculator must show both dates and the exact day count between them, with leap days already handled. This is a simple record: one idea and one calculation, with nothing extra to learn.

Worked example: in a website built this way, counting the days between two dates runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
