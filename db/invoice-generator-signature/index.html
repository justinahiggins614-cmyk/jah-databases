1. Description

An invoice generator builds a bill: line items with quantity and price, a subtotal, tax, and a total due. Add lines, set the tax rate, and print or save the invoice. All money is computed in this browser; no payment is taken anywhere.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

Each line total is quantity times price. The subtotal is the sum of the line totals. Tax is the subtotal times the tax rate. The total due is subtotal plus tax minus discount. With lines of 80, 50 and 20, a subtotal of 150 and 10 percent tax, the total is 165.

3. Science proof

An invoice works because it is a complete record: who owes, who is owed, what for, how much, and by when. A unique number stops double payment, and a written total that can be recomputed from its lines stops disputes, because any reader can check the arithmetic.
