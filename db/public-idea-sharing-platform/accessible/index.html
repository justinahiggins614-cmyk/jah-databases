  1. An Idea Board Where Every Idea Is Public

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The public idea-sharing platform is a board where every idea is public: each card states a problem and a proposal, and the community votes the good ones to the top. An idea board where every idea is public lets anyone read every idea, so a good idea is never hidden in a private drawer. The board needs each idea written plainly, so a reader who was not there when it was written can still understand it. Without a public board, ideas are shared in private and the best idea in the room is the one nobody else ever hears. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.     History: Sharing ideas in public is an old habit: a suggestion box in a hall, a letter to a newspaper, a notice pinned to a board. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a card that separates the problem from the proposal, a vote that surfaces good ideas, and a rule that every idea stays public and credited. Public idea boards are an old habit of workshops and town halls, where a suggestion pinned to a board belonged to everyone who read it.
