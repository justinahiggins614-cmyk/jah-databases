Mecca's buildings answer a hot valley and a pilgrimage city at once: thick walls, shaded courts, lattice screens, and now tall towers around the mosque precinct. This database describes that fabric in general factual terms, with original geometric diagrams only, no human figures, and every figure labeled with its source and its measurement. Every record is the full, fully-formed file: full prose description in sentences and paragraphs, an explicit faith label (fact, tradition, or interpretation) with the distinction stated in the text, a named source with a source-quality label, and a working demo program. Everything is free forever, with no payments and no upsells.
