# Dictionary entries — The Hotel Booking Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## hotel booking

The written record of a room held at a hotel for a stated stay, naming the guest, the room, and the rate, kept as the single true record of the booking. It is a record only: no live booking and no payment happens here.

## guest

The person named on a booking as the one who will stay, recorded with the name written exactly as it stands on their travel document.

## room

The sleeping place held by a booking, named by its number and its type, so that the guest's place can be found and no room is booked twice.

## room type

The named kind of room, such as single or double, written on the booking, so that the room held is the kind the guest asked for and the rate matches it.

## stay

The nights a guest will spend at a hotel, written with the arrival and departure dates, so that the length of the booking can be read from the record.

## check-in

The act of arriving and taking the room, recorded with its time, so that the booking moves from held to occupied.

## check-out

The act of leaving and giving the room back, recorded with its time, so that the booking moves from occupied to done and the room is free again.

## nightly rate

The price stated for one night in a room, recorded with its amount and what it covers, so that the cost of a stay can be counted. No payment is taken here; the rate is a recorded figure only.

## booking reference

The one code given to a booking when it is made, written on every message about it, so that the booking can be found in the record at any moment.

## cancellation policy

The written rules about ending a booking before the stay, naming the dates and the terms, kept with the booking, so that every cancellation is judged by the same stated rules.

## occupancy

The number of guests a room holds, written on the booking, so that the room held is the room that fits the party that will stay in it.

## amenity

Something a hotel provides beyond the room, such as breakfast or a pool, named in the booking record, so that what the stay includes can be read beside the rate.
