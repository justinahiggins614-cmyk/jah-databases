AI Music Generator Database (ai-music-generator-signature)

An AI music generator is a seeded local demo: it makes a seeded note list from a fixed seed, and the same seed always makes the same notes. Type a seed, press Generate, and read the notes. A song of 8 notes starts on 69. A song holds 16 notes. 16 beats at 120 per minute last 8.0 seconds. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
