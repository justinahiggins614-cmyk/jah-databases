The Signature original past park inspired by River Country is a closed rustic swimming-hole water park on a lake shore, with a sandy beach, a rope swing, a barrel bridge, and wooden-look slides, closed after larger sister parks drew its guests away. Its riverbank, its beach, and its closure are original Signature designs, labeled Signature-original where they echo the subject.
