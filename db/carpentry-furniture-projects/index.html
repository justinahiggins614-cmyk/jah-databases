Simple Bench Rank 1 · simple · seating · beginner

Source: public-harvest

A simple bench is three seat boards over a four-leg frame with aprons and one long stretcher, built entirely from straight cuts and butt joints. The seat sits 18 inches high, a standard sitting height, and the stretcher keeps the legs from splaying when someone sits down hard. It works as an entry bench, a garden bench indoors, or extra seating at a table, and it is the first project a beginner should build after the practice table.

Project diagram

Original dimensioned project sketch for Simple Bench: a front-view diagram showing the overall 48 x 18 x 14 inch (W x H x D) form, the main cut pieces, and the overall dimensions labeled on the drawing, drawn for this record.

Specifications

Dimensions: 48 x 18 x 14 in (W x H x D)
Tolerances: Cut to within 1/16 in; check square by measuring both diagonals and matching them.
Load notes: A bench seat rated for two seated adults, about 400 pounds spread across the seat.
Arithmetic: Cut list adds up: Total pieces: 12 (3 x Seat board, 4 x Leg, 2 x Apron, long, 2 x Apron, short, 1 x Stretcher, long). Longest piece 48 in fits within the 51.264 in face diagonal of the overall form. Cut the longest pieces first so a mistake on a short piece does not waste a long board.

Materials

  • Seat boards: pine, 3/4 in
  • Leg stock: pine, 1-1/2 in

Cut list

PieceDimensionsQty
Seat board48 x 5.5 x 0.75 in3
Leg17 x 1.5 x 1.5 in4
Apron, long42 x 3 x 0.75 in2
Apron, short11 x 3 x 0.75 in2
Stretcher, long42 x 2 x 0.75 in1

Fasteners

  • Wood glue and 2-1/2 in screws, 24 total

How to build

  1. Read the cut list and the arithmetic note in full, and check every piece against the plan; measure twice before cutting.
  2. Cut the pieces in order, longest first: Seat board (48 x 5.5 x 0.75 in), Leg (17 x 1.5 x 1.5 in), Apron, long (42 x 3 x 0.75 in).
  3. Dry-fit the frame without glue and check both diagonals match so the assembly is square.
  4. Test the bench by sitting and shifting weight before finishing.
  5. Glue and fasten as listed in the specs, keeping the assembly square while fasteners go in; wipe glue squeeze-out before it dries.
  6. Sand through the grits, ease the edges, and finish as described in the record; check the finished piece for square and for its rated load before use.

Tools required

  • Tape measure
  • Combination square
  • Sharp pencil
  • Handsaw or circular saw
  • Drill driver
  • Clamps (2)
  • Sandpaper, 80/120/180 grit
  • Workbench or sawhorses

Safety gear

  • Safety glasses
  • Hearing protection
  • Dust mask
  • Work gloves for handling rough lumber (never near rotating blades)

Wear eye protection for every cut and sanding pass, hearing protection for power tools, and a dust mask for sanding. Unplug power tools or remove batteries before changing blades or bits, keep guards in place, and keep hands clear of blades. Clamp work before cutting, never hold a small piece by hand near a blade, and clear offcuts before the next cut.
