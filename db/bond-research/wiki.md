# The Bond Research Database

The Bond Research Database is the bond research record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full bond research record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database research-papers, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
