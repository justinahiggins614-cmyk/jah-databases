Equation Solver Database (equation-solver-signature)

An equation solver finds the unknown number: type an equation, and it shows the steps and the answer, then checks the answer by putting it back. Linear equations and quadratics both work, in this browser, with no sign-in.

Worked example: In x + 5 = 12, the answer is x = 7.
