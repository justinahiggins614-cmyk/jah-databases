# Dictionary entries — The Invoice Generation Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## invoice

A written bill from a seller to a buyer, naming the goods or work given, the amount owed, and the date by which it is owed, kept as the record of the debt.

## invoice number

The one number given to one invoice, never used for another, so that the invoice can be found, quoted, and paid without confusion with any other bill.

## line item

One row of an invoice, naming one thing given, how many, and at what price, so that the total can be checked by adding the rows.

## buyer

The person or business named on an invoice as the one who owes the amount, with the address where the bill is sent, so the debt has a named owner.

## seller

The person or business named on an invoice as the one who gave the goods or work, with the address where the amount is to be sent, so the payment has a named destination.

## payment terms

The written conditions of an invoice, saying by when the amount is owed and what happens if it is late, agreed before the bill is sent, not after.

## due date

The date by which an invoice amount is owed, written on the invoice itself, so that both seller and buyer can see the same deadline and count from it.

## tax line

A row of an invoice naming a tax charged on the sale, with its rate and its amount, so that the tax can be seen apart from the price of the goods.

## credit note

A written correction that reduces an amount owed, naming the invoice it corrects and the reason, so that the corrected debt is the one the buyer must pay.

## invoice copy

The seller's own kept copy of an invoice, held with the same number and the same words, so that what was sent can be proved if the buyer asks.

## outstanding invoice

An invoice whose amount has not yet been received, kept on the open list with its age counted, so that no debt waits unnoticed.

## paid invoice

An invoice whose amount has been received in full, marked paid with the date, so that the record shows the debt is settled and the buyer owes nothing on it.
