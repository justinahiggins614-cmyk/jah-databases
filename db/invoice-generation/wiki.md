# The Invoice Generation Database

The Invoice Generation Database is the invoice generation record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full invoice generation record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database code-generation-repair-library, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
