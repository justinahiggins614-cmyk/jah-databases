History
A bill for work done is one of the oldest written things: the mason chalked what he was owed on the wall, the merchant wrote what the buyer must pay. The modern invoice carries the same facts in a fixed form — who sold, who bought, what was given, how much is owed, and by when — and each invoice carries its own number, so that no two bills can be confused. This database is built on that public practice of writing the bill truly, once, and keeping a copy.
