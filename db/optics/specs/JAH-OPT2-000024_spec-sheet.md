## Spec
- **Mechanism:** This is a Signature-authored optical design study for a phase-contrast microscope at 100x 10x. Design goals address the real aberration profile: Field curvature: the focal surface curves instead of lying flat across the field. Axial chromatic aberration: colors focus at different depths in the specimen. Corrected with an apochromatic (APO) design using ED glass. Intended application: materials inspection.
