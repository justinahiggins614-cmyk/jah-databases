Program: Interactive record tour — Echelle spectrometer — 2400 l/mm grating.

Language: javascript.

The echelle spectrometer is a spectrometer specified here at 2400 l/mm grating. Astigmatism: perpendicular planes focus at different points; the classic Czerny-Turner limitation. Coma: off-axis rays in the collimator smear the spectral lines. Corrected with an apochromatic (APO) design using ED glass. Typical use: chemistry quality control.
