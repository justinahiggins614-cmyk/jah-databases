1. Description and history

The Mach-Zehnder interferometer is a interferometer specified here at 632.8 nm laser. Astigmatism: uneven curvature that splits the interference focus. Spherical aberration: wavefront error from imperfect optics under test. Corrected with a multi-element achromat. Typical use: optical surface testing.
