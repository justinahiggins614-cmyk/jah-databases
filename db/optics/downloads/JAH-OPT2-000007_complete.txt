DESCRIPTION
The long-focus refractor is a telescope specified here at 150 mm f/5. Reflecting designs are inherently free of chromatic aberration. Spherical aberration: edge rays of a spherical mirror focus closer than axial rays; a parabolic figure removes it on axis. Corrected with a coma corrector lens. Typical use: lunar mapping.
