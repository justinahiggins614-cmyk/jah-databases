DESCRIPTION
The Maksutov-Cassegrain is a telescope specified here at 150 mm f/10. Coma: off-axis point sources smear into comet-like teardrops; the classic Newtonian limitation. Spherical aberration: edge rays of a spherical mirror focus closer than axial rays; a parabolic figure removes it on axis. Corrected with aspherical lens elements. Typical use: planetary observation.
