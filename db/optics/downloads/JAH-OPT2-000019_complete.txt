DESCRIPTION
The Schmidt-Cassegrain is a telescope specified here at 152 mm f/7. Reflecting designs are inherently free of chromatic aberration. Spherical aberration: edge rays of a spherical mirror focus closer than axial rays; a parabolic figure removes it on axis. Corrected with aspherical lens elements. Typical use: amateur astronomy clubs.
