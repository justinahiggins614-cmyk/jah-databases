Review System Database (review-system)

A review system collects star ratings and shows the mean: read the reviews, add your own, and see the mean move. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it stores reviews in this browser, and posting a review to a real service is BLOCKED. 5 reviews with a mean of 4.4. 3 of 5 reviews are 5 stars: 60 percent.

Worked example: 5 reviews with a mean of 4.4

Demo output:
5 reviews with a mean of 4.4
