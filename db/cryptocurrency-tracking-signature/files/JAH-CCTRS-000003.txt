History
Cryptocurrencies are money kept as numbers in a shared public ledger, where every movement is written for all to see and no single keeper holds the book. The ledger began with the first public chain, and tracking grew beside it: counting prices by the day, reading the ledger's own entries, and writing down what moved, when, and how much. This database tracks public ledger facts only: it counts and cites published movements and prices, holds no coins, and moves none.
