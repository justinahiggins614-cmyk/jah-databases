Data Anonymizer Database (data-anonymizer)

A data anonymizer masks entered sample text and hashes identifiers for study. Type the sample, press Anonymize, and read the masked result. Masking 'Alex Rivers' gives 'A*** R***'. A mail mask turns 'alex@example.org' into 'a***@example.org'. An age of 34 generalises to 30 to 39. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: Masking 'Alex Rivers' gives 'A*** R***'
