About this data base

Archive
This database's original Signature-line draft specifications ("Draft — ready to file"), preserved as full structured records. Originals only, never reworded copies. Full record chunks are served by this database's own shard network; the archive page fetches them invisibly and one click opens the whole file.
Record schema
spec_id · title · abstract · category · cpc · era · prepared_date · inventor · owner · status · signature_tool_mapping · key_parameters · patent_draft · manufacture · demo · ai_explainer · algorithm_steps · measurements · autoread_block
Generator
jahdb-specs-1.0 — deterministic (mulberry32), builds each full draft constructively from known-valid parts, then an independent checker rebuilds and re-verifies every derived field.
Drift control
Every generated record is checked against an archive sample for exact title duplicates and re-validated by the independent checker.
Cross-database
The generator can borrow a numeric theme from any of the other 76 data bases when you tick the box.
AI
JAH Spec Librarian — archive-aware. On-device Standard 1.0, no key needed; uses the shared Signature backend automatically when configured.
