The Business-in-a-Box Generator Database (Signature) (business-in-a-box-generator-signature)

A business-in-a-box generator builds a complete starter kit for a new small business: a one-page plan, a name, a structure, a registration checklist, a startup budget, prices, a marketing plan, a sales funnel, operations, hiring, bookkeeping, tax basics, customer service, a growth plan and a risk register — all in one place, free, in the browser.

General business information only — not legal or financial advice.

Worked example: A startup budget lists everything the business must buy before it opens, with a price beside each item, and adds them up. The worked budget buys a stall table for 150 dollars, jars and labels for 120 dollars, a licence for 60 dollars and ingredients for 170 dollars, a total of 500 dollars.

Demo output:
Startup budget: 500 dollars
Profit per jar: 2 dollars; weekly profit: 80 dollars
Break even: 60 jars a week
