History
Public records have been kept since the first towns wrote their births, their marriages, and their land sales in parish books and town rolls, first in ledgers kept by clerks, then in archives of paper files, and now in indexes that hold the search, the result, the citation, and the copy side by side. Public records search is the finding of what a government has written down and made open, so that any citizen can read what was recorded. From the paper index kept in the record office to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the search down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every public records search record true.
