# Dictionary entries — The Spreadsheet Editor Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## spreadsheet

A table of cells in rows and columns, where each cell holds a number, words, or a formula, and where changing one cell changes every answer that depends on it.

## cell

One box in a spreadsheet, named by its column and row, holding one value: a number, words, or a formula.

## formula

A written rule in a cell that says how its value is made from other cells, such as adding two cells, which is recomputed whenever its inputs change.

## row

One line of cells across a spreadsheet, holding one record's values, so that a row can be read as one thing.

## column

One line of cells down a spreadsheet, holding one kind of value, so that a column can be added, averaged, or counted.

## range

A block of cells named by its corners, so that one formula can read, add, or count them all at once.

## function

A named piece of arithmetic, such as adding or averaging, which a formula can call with its inputs, and which always gives the same answer for the same inputs.

## reference

A formula's naming of another cell, by its column and row, so that the formula reads that cell's value, and follows it when the cell moves.

## recalculation

The recomputing of every formula after a change, in the right order, so that no answer is left stale and no answer is computed before its inputs.

## error

A cell whose formula cannot be computed, such as one that divides by nothing, marked plainly so that the mistake is found, not hidden.

## chart

A picture drawn from a spreadsheet's numbers, such as bars or a line, which changes when the numbers change, and which never hides the numbers it came from.

## ledger

The editor's own written record of every sheet, every formula, and every save, kept in order.
