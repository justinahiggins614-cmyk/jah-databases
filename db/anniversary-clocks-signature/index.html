Signature Yearkeeper: The Anniversary Clock of the House JAH-ANCS-000001

Specifications. The Signature Yearkeeper is an original Signature design for a household anniversary clock, standing 28 centimetres tall under a glass dome, with a four-ball torsion pendulum and a brass movement wound once a year on the household's chosen date. Its dial is cream with black Roman numerals, and its base is walnut. This is a Signature-original design, never an authentic historic record.

Mechanism. A torsion spring matched to the four-ball pendulum gives a rotation cycle of about fifteen seconds, and the mainspring barrel delivers power for over 400 days. The escapement gives the pendulum one small impulse per cycle, and the train converts the slow rotation into the hands' motion. The clock is wound through the base with a single key.

Maintenance. Wind once a year on the household date, keep the dome on, dust the dome weekly, and service the movement every seven years. Lock the pendulum before moving the clock, and level it after every move.

Build and restoration guidance. Build the walnut base with a felt seal for the dome, fit the suspension spring by its blocks only, and regulate the clock in its final spot over a fortnight before delivery.

Tools. Winding key, dome cloth, spirit level, service log.
