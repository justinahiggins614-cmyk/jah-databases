1. Overview

The gorilla (Gorilla gorilla and Gorilla beringei) has its own mathematics, its own science, and its own court, the "Wooo Woo" court. This website is the per-animal website for the gorilla, built first, before the networks, under humanity, under God. It holds 24 public records and 24 Signature records, in three ladders of eight: mathematics and measurement; science, evolution, and court; Signature, Light, and human translation.

Archive records: 48 (24 public + 24 Signature).

The gorilla lives in the rainforests of Central Africa, eats mostly leaves, stems, fruit, and other plant food, and lives in a troop led by a silverback male. It is adult males about 140 to 200 kilograms, adult females about 70 to 110 kilograms and stands about 1.4 to 1.8 metres tall when standing. Its characteristic sound is the recognizable woo and hoo calls, deep hoots and grunts.

The science is built from the same ancestor, so the gorilla is scientifically measurable in its own framework. The court, the "Wooo Woo" court, is named after the sound the gorilla makes.

The system mimics and cascades with human mathematics, human science, and human law in both directions, and connects through Signature. Humanity is the best-fit recognition and translation system. The gorilla is supreme in its own system, under humanity, under God, subject to no other animal.

5. Math Proof

The gorilla is measurable in its own system. Proof by calculation: one mouse lemur unit is 60 grams. An adult male gorilla of about 170 kilograms is about 2833 mouse lemur units. An adult female of about 90 kilograms is about 1500 mouse lemur units. A human of 70 kilograms is about 1166 mouse lemur units. The translation is checked both ways: a number translated into human units and back returns to the number it started from. The probabilistic behaviour model gives chances with counted ranges, and the evolutionary cascade measures the ancestor in the same units.
