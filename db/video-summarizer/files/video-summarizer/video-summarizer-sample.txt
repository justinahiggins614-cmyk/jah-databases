Video Summarizer Database (video-summarizer)

A video summarizer is a local demo: it picks the brightest frames as the summary, and the summary keeps only those frames. The summary keeps 2 frames: 210 and 200. The brightest frame is 210. 6 frames shrink to a summary of 2 frames. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
