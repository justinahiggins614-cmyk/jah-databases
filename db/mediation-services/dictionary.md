# Dictionary entries — The Mediation Services Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## mediation services

The recorded service of helping parties in a dispute reach an agreement with the aid of a neutral mediator, kept so that each mediation can be followed from first session to outcome.

## mediation

A meeting of parties in a dispute with a neutral mediator who helps them talk and agree, kept in the record with its date and its outcome, so that the settlement can be proved from the paper.

## mediator

A neutral person who helps parties in a dispute talk and agree, named in the record with the mediation, so that who guided the talk can be read plainly.

## party

A person or body named as a side in a dispute, written in the record so that who stood on each side can be read plainly.

## dispute

A disagreement between parties that mediation seeks to settle, named in the record with its subject, so that what was disputed can be read plainly.

## session

One meeting in a mediation, kept in the record with its date and its purpose, so that each sitting can be followed.

## agreement

The written settlement reached in a mediation, kept in the record with its terms and its date, so that what was agreed can be proved from the paper.

## confidentiality

The rule that what is said in mediation stays private, written in the record so that the limit on the record can be read plainly.

## referral

The written sending of a dispute to mediation, kept in the record with its date and its sender, so that how the dispute arrived can be followed.

## outcome

The stated end of a mediation, such as agreement or no agreement, written in the record so that how the dispute closed can be read plainly.

## ground rules

The stated rules for how a mediation will be run, written in the record so that the order of the talk can be read plainly.

## mediation centre

The office or body that arranges mediations, named in the record with the mediation, so that where the service came from can be read plainly.
