History
People have settled disputes with the help of a neutral third party for centuries, because a quarrel taken to a court costs time and coin that a talk might save. Mediation grew beside the courts: a mediator who hears each side, keeps the talk in order, and writes down what was agreed. From the paper agreement signed at a kitchen table to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the settlement down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every mediation true. It is a record of published practice only, and it gives no advice about any dispute.
