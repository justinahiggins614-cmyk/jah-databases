1. Description

A colour palette generator makes colours that go together, from one starting colour: complements, analogues and triads, with real contrast ratios for every pair. The demo on this page uses real colour maths in the browser, with no server and no connection. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

The colour #FF0000 is [255, 0, 0]: 3 numbers. Red at 0 degrees has its complement at 180 degrees. Black on white has a contrast ratio of 21 to 1. Red on white has a contrast ratio of 4.0 to 1. Two palettes: 5 colours and 5 colours, 10 colours in all.

3. Science proof

A palette generator works because a colour is three numbers and a place on the wheel: red at 0 degrees has its complement at 180 degrees, an analogous palette uses 3 colours 30 degrees apart, and a triadic palette uses 3 colours 120 degrees apart. Contrast is a ratio of light: black on white is 21 to 1, and red on white is 4.0 to 1. That is why the local demo shows the palette and every ratio together, and runs 0 generations on a server.

        c = c / 255.0
        return c / 12.92 if c <= 0.04045 else ((c + 0.055) / 1.055) ** 2.4
