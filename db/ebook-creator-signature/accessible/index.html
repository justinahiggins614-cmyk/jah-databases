  1. A First Ebook of Three Short Chapters

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The ebook creator lets a person write a short book on screen: chapters in order, a cover with a title, and a table of contents, so a reader can open the book and read it from the first chapter to the last. A first ebook of three short chapters needs plain rules a new visitor can follow, and the count is written beside each ebook so a reader can see it at a glance. Without plain rules, a visitor must guess at every step, and a wrong guess wastes the time the tool was meant to save. The rules must be checked by a second reader before they are published, because a mistake in plain words is a mistake every visitor will follow. When the rules are clear and checked, the tool does its job quietly and the visitor gets on with the work.
