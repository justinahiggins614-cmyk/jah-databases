Physics Simulator Database (physics-simulator-signature)

A physics simulator steps through motion with real equations: pick a speed, press Run, and read the result. A falling ball after 2 seconds moves at 19.6 metres per second. A pendulum of length 1 metre has period 2.01 seconds. A ball thrown at 10 metres per second at 45 degrees lands 10.2 metres away. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
