Fault-tolerant quantum computing JAH-FCU-000001 · advanced

Fault-tolerant quantum computing runs quantum algorithms on error-corrected logical qubits that survive noise..

Fault-tolerant quantum computing runs quantum algorithms on error-corrected logical qubits that survive noise. No fault-tolerant quantum computer exists yet; experiments have shown error suppression below threshold on small logical qubits. A surface-code logical qubit at distance 7 needs about 49 physical qubits, so 100 logical qubits need about 4,900 physical qubits. The open challenge is scaling from one logical qubit to the thousands needed for useful algorithms. Maturity T1 (Theoretical): supported by a mathematical or scientific model. Reversible computing performs logic without erasing information, in principle avoiding the minimum energy cost of erasing a bit..

Reversible computing performs logic without erasing information, in principle avoiding the minimum energy cost of erasing a bit.
