Record JAH-ACO-000007 — Public edition, acoustics.

## Spec
- **Mechanism:** The reference chart rates threshold of hearing at 0 dB: the faintest sound a healthy human ear can detect. All chart ratings are taken standing near the sound; distance lowers the reading fast. Used for product noise ratings.
