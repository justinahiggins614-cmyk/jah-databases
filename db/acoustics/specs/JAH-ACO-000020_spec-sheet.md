Record JAH-ACO-000020 — Public edition, acoustics.
JCC v1 code: JAHDB/acoustics#JAH-ACO-000020

## Spec
- **Mechanism:** Sound intensity falls with the square of distance from the source (inverse-square law). Field readings use a sound level meter with A-weighting, which mimics human hearing. Used when field surveys.
