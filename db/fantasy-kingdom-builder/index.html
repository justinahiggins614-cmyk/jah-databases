Section 1: Description and history

The fantasy kingdom builder helps a storyteller invent a kingdom of their own, in plain words, with the land, the people, and the laws written out first. Aldervale, a River Valley Kingdom Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A fantasy kingdom builder website: a visitor picks the parts of a kingdom, such as a river valley, a mountain pass, and a market town, and the site joins them into one invented kingdom, with a plain description of the land, the people, and the laws. Every kingdom is an invented creation written for this database, and no part of it names a real place or real people. This record covers Aldervale, a River Valley Kingdom.

Aldervale is a river valley kingdom with a slow river, green fields, and one market town, where farmers and millers live by the water. The builder must show the land, the people, and the laws in plain words, so a storyteller can see the whole kingdom at once and change any part. Without those plain words, a storyteller cannot tell what the kingdom holds, and a kingdom that cannot be seen cannot be told. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.

Worked example: in a website built this way, aldervale, a river valley kingdom runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

River valley kingdoms are an old storyteller habit, from a tale of a slow river to a kingdom built around one market town.
