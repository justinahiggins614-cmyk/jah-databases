Berkshire Hathaway — Overview and history
Public edition. Public records, fact-checked reference. Berkshire Hathaway Inc. is an American multinational conglomerate headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska, led by Warren Buffett since 1965. What began as a textile company became a holding company owning GEICO, BNSF Railway, Berkshire Hathaway Energy, Dairy Queen, and dozens of businesses, plus a huge stock portfolio including Apple. Its annual shareholder meeting in Omaha is famously called the Woodstock for Capitalists.
