The Universal Vehicle-to-Grid Coordination Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The universal vehicle-to-grid coordination network plans charge sessions in plain words, one session for a place, an hour, and a kind of day, so parked vehicles give back power when the grid needs it most. Every session is a clearly labeled design study of a generic kind of charge, never a live order to any real vehicle or grid.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
