# Dictionary entries — The Checkout System Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## checkout

The last steps of a purchase: reviewing the cart, naming the totals, giving an address, and confirming the order.

## cart

The list of things a buyer has chosen and not yet bought, held until checkout or until the buyer clears it.

## order

A confirmed purchase: the cart, the totals, the address, and the confirmation, kept together as one record.

## receipt

The written proof of a purchase, naming what was bought, what was paid, and when, given to the buyer at once.

## shipping

The carrying of bought things from seller to buyer, with its own price and its own promised time.

## discount

A reduction in price, by a code or a sale, applied before the total is named.

## tax

The share of a purchase that goes to the government, worked out from the price and the buyer's place.

## guest checkout

Buying without an account, giving only what the order needs, with nothing kept after the sale.

## confirmation

The message that an order is accepted, carrying the order number the buyer can quote if anything goes wrong.

## refund

Money returned when an order is cancelled or a thing is sent back, recorded against the original order.

## order number

The one number that names an order, used by buyer and seller alike whenever the order is discussed.

## specification

A written description of how a thing must work, used to build it and to test it, without being the thing itself.
