History
Land has been bought, sold, and passed down for centuries, and every transfer has left a paper trail: deeds signed before witnesses, entries written into bound record books, and maps drawn to show where one parcel ends and the next begins. County recorders and land registries grew up to keep that trail in order, so that any person could look up who held a parcel and on what terms. From the hand-copied deed in a courthouse vault to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the holding down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every property lookup true.
