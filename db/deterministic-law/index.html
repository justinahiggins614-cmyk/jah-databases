5. Math Proof

The standard-math facts are proved from the axioms. One is not zero: the Peano axioms say zero is a number, every number has a successor, zero is not the successor of any number, and one is defined as the successor of zero, so one cannot be zero. One is not infinity: in standard real analysis infinity is not a real number but a symbol for growing without bound, so one equals infinity is not an equation between two numbers, and division by zero is undefined rather than infinite. A proof that ends in a contradiction is rejected: proof by contradiction shows the assumption was false, and a chain that ends in one equals zero has broken a rule, most often a hidden division by zero.

The Riemann Hypothesis is the quest: the zeta function is the sum of one over n to the power s, extended by analytic continuation; its nontrivial zeros are conjectured all to have real part exactly one half, the critical line. The Euler product ties the zeta function to the primes, and the explicit formula rebuilds the count of primes from the positions of the zeros. More than ten trillion zeros have been checked, all on the critical line; checking is not proving, and the hypothesis is open since 1859. The Signature-as-one scaling is shown by calculation in the demo: one, two, four, eight, sixteen, thirty-two, sixty-four, one hundred and twenty-eight, two hundred and fifty-six, five hundred and twelve — ten rungs, each twice the rung before it. The deterministic grid is shown the same way: a ten by ten by ten grid holds one thousand cells, each measured.

6. Science Proof

The science in this pair is the science of measurement and of atoms and energy. An atom is the smallest unit of ordinary matter that keeps the properties of a chemical element, about a ten-billionth of a metre across, and a human body holds on the order of seven thousand trillion trillion atoms, roughly seven times ten to the twenty-seventh power, which is an estimate. Energy is the capacity to do work or make change happen, in forms such as heat, light, motion, and chemical bonds. A deterministic system is one with no randomness in how one state follows the next, and a grid measurement is deterministic in its method because each cell is recorded and can be re-checked by another measurer with the same grid.

The deterministic measurement itself is not performed by this pair; it is the design of the live human-body-deterministic pair, which this pair links to. The atom and energy combinations are not computed here; they are the design of the live nine-pair atom-energy program and the live atom-mixes-until-collapse pair, which this pair links to. Where the science is uncertain, the record says so and keeps the range wide; nothing is invented as fact. The court step is a legal-measurement argument, a design, not a scientific result and not a legal outcome.
