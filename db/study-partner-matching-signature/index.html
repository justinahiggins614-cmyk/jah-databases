Matching by Subject, Level, and Hours Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A study website: a learner lists a subject, a level, and the hours they can study, and the site suggests partners who match on all three. Partners meet in a public study room, share notes on a public board, and quiz each other with questions anyone can read. There are no private messages anywhere on the site, and no personal data is ever shown. This record covers Matching by Subject, Level, and Hours.

A study partner must match on three things at once, because a partner in the right subject at the wrong level will either race ahead or fall behind, and a partner at the right level with no shared hour will never meet. The match should weigh subject first, then level, then the hours both partners can study, and say plainly which of the three was the closest. Without all three, the pair spends its first weeks discovering the mismatch, and the study never starts.

Worked example, Signature design: the same study pair is rebuilt from the Signature pattern, so matching by subject, level, and hours runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
