  13. Checks That Tell a Real Local Guide From a Copied Listing

    Record 13 of 24. compounded. Feasibility: research-stage. Active public research exists on automatic checks that tell a real local guide from a copied listing, results are published, but no working system at the needed scale yet.

    The local discovery network helps people find what is near them: public places, local events, and neighborhood guides, all written by locals and open to read. Checks that tell a real local guide from a copied listing are a hard problem, because a copied listing can look real in every way a checker can see, and a real guide can look rough beside it.
