1. Description

A derivative calculator differentiates functions by the power rule, the product rule and the chain rule, and checks the answer with a numeric slope. Type a function, and it shows the rule, the substitution and the slope. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

2. Math proof

The second derivative of x to the 4 is 12x squared: at x = 2 it is 48. The numeric slope of x squared at x = 3 is 6.0, matching the symbolic 6.

3. Science proof

A derivative works because the power rule turns each term into a simpler one: x to the n becomes n times x to the n minus 1, and a constant becomes zero. Sums and differences differentiate term by term, products use the product rule, and a function of a function uses the chain rule. A numeric slope checks the symbolic answer: if the two agree, the derivative is right.

A numeric slope checks the symbolic answer: if the two agree, the derivative is right.
