Derivative Calculator Database (derivative-calculator)

A derivative calculator differentiates functions by the power rule, the product rule and the chain rule, and checks the answer with a numeric slope. Type a function, and it shows the rule, the substitution and the slope. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: The derivative of x squared at x = 3 is 6
