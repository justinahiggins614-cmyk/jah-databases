  1. What a Fraction Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A fraction is a part of a whole: a top number over a bottom number. 1/2 is one part of two

    Math proof: 1/2 + 1/3 is 5/6, and 3/4 - 1/4 is 1/2. 1/2 times 2/3 is 1/3, and 1/2 divided by 1/4 is 2. 6/8 simplifies to 3/4, and 10/15 in lowest terms is 2/3. The GCD of 12 and 18 is 6, and the LCM of 4 and 6 is 12. 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6 is 1.

    Science proof: Fractions work because a fraction is a part of a whole: adding needs a common bottom number, multiplying goes top by top and bottom by bottom, and dividing flips the second fraction and multiplies. Simplifying divides top and bottom by their greatest common divisor, so the answer is in lowest terms. That is why the calculator shows the common bottom and the simplification, not only the answer.
