About this data base

Archive
This database’s story record archive (JAH-STRY- records), preserved as structured, searchable data.
Record schema
id · title · description · category · topic · structure{} · source
Generator
jahdb-storytelling-1.0 — deterministic, every record validated by an independent checker before acceptance.
Drift control
Every generated record is checked against an archive sample for exact duplicates and re-validated by the independent checker.
AI
JAH Story Creator — archive-aware and network-aware: it knows all databases across the JAH Databases network, can discuss and route across them, and specializes in generating boundless new options in storytelling.
