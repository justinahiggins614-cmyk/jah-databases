Reading Comprehension Tester Database (reading-comprehension-tester-signature)

A reading comprehension tester asks questions about a fixed passage and scores the answers. Read the passage, answer, and press Score. A passage of 12 words with 5 questions: 4 right. 300 words at 200 words a minute take 1.5 minutes to read. 4 right out of 5 is 80.0 percent. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
