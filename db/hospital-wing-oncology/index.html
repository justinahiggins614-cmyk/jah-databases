TNM Cancer Staging

Staging describes Tumor size, Node involvement, and Metastasis — T2N1M0, for example. Stage determines treatment and prognosis more than any other factor. Every cancer plan starts with an accurate stage.

def chemo_bsa_dose(height_cm, weight_kg, mg_per_m2):
    """Body-surface-area chemo dose via Mosteller formula."""
    bsa = ((height_cm * weight_kg) / 3600) ** 0.5
    return f"BSA {bsa:.2f} m2 -> dose {round(bsa*mg_per_m2,1)} mg"

Chemotherapy Regimens

Chemotherapy drugs kill fast-dividing cells in timed cycles, often combining several agents with names like R-CHOP or FOLFOX. Cycles repeat every 2 to 3 weeks to catch cancer cells as they divide. Doses are calculated from body surface area.

Radiation Therapy

Linear accelerators aim high-energy beams at tumors from precise angles, planned from CT simulations. Treatments take minutes, given daily for weeks. Modern targeting spares healthy tissue far better than older wide fields.

Immunotherapy

Immunotherapy drugs like checkpoint inhibitors release the brakes on the patient's own immune system to attack cancer. Some patients have dramatic, durable responses. Side effects are immune-related — colitis, thyroiditis — and need quick recognition.

Targeted Therapy

Targeted drugs attack specific mutations, like EGFR or BRAF, found by tumor genetic testing. They work only when the tumor carries the target, making molecular testing essential. Pills can replace infusions for some cancers.

Neutropenia and Infection Risk

Chemotherapy wipes out white blood cells, leaving patients defenseless against infection. Fever during neutropenia is an emergency — broad antibiotics start within an hour. Hand hygiene and visitor screening protect these patients fiercely.

Port-a-Cath Devices

A port is a small disc implanted under the skin connected to a central vein, giving reliable access for months of chemo without repeated needle sticks. It is accessed with a special needle and flushed regularly. Ports spare veins and pain.

Bone Marrow Biopsy

A needle into the hip bone draws marrow to diagnose leukemias, lymphomas, and staging. Local anesthetic plus sedation keeps it tolerable. Pathologists read the sample for cancer cells and genetics.

Antiemetic Protocols

Modern anti-nausea protocols layer several drugs — ondansetron, dexamethasone, NK1 blockers — matched to the chemo's emetogenicity. Most patients now get through chemo with manageable nausea. Prevention beats rescue every time.

Cancer Pain Management

Cancer pain is treated aggressively with the WHO ladder: from anti-inflammatories to opioids to interventional blocks. Around-the-clock dosing prevents pain better than as-needed doses. Addiction fears never justify undertreated cancer pain.
