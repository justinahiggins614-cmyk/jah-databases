A Bakery Listed by the Market Square Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A business directory website: a visitor picks a generic kind of business, such as a bakery, a bookshop, or a hardware shop, and the site shows illustrative sample listings of that kind, each with a plain description of what the business offers in general words, the hours, and the distance. The site names no real business, copies no real listing, and makes no claim that any sample entry is a real business. This record covers A Bakery Listed by the Market Square.

A bakery listed by the market square gives a neighbor a plain way to find fresh bread, with what it offers, the hours, and the distance written out before the neighbor sets out. The directory must show what the bakery offers in general words, when it opens, and how far it is, so a neighbor can plan the walk and arrive while the bread is fresh. Without those plain facts, a neighbor cannot tell a morning bakery from an afternoon one, and a walk taken on a guess is a poor way to buy bread.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a bakery listed by the market square runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

History

Finding a shop nearby is an old habit: a sign on a street, a list in a town guide, a tip from a neighbor. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a search by kind of business and a plain description for every listing. Bakeries by the market square are an old habit, from a baker who sold loaves from a window to a shop with a sign over the door.

Proposed solution

Name the business listing and what a visitor does in it; Write the rules in plain sentences a new visitor can follow; Give every business listing the same fair start: the same clues, the same time, the same scoring; Let a visitor try one business listing without signing in; Check the business listing works the same way on a phone and on a computer.
