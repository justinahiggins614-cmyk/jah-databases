The Problem

A simply supported beam of span 6 m carries a central point load of 10 kN. Find the maximum bending moment in the beam.

The Worked Solution

The solution proceeds in the following steps. Step 1: The reactions at each support are equal by symmetry: R = P/2 = 10/2 = 5 kN. Step 2: The bending moment diagram is triangular with its peak at midspan, under the load. Step 3: The moment at midspan is R times half the span: M = 5 kN × 3 m = 15 kN·m. Step 4: Equivalently, M_max = P×L/4 = 10 × 6/4 = 15 kN·m. Step 5: No other point carries a larger moment, so 15 kN·m is the maximum. That completes the worked solution; the answer is 15 kN·m.
