# Dictionary entries — The Shipping Logistics Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## shipping logistics

The ordering of the carrying of goods, with their path, their handlers, and their times, so that every shipment can be followed from sender to receiver.

## shipment

One set of goods sent together from a sender to a receiver, recorded with its goods, its path, and its handlers, so that the shipment can be followed to its end.

## sender

The person or business sending goods, named in the record with their place, so that where the shipment began can be read plainly.

## receiver

The person or business goods are sent to, named in the record with their place, so that where the shipment is bound can be read plainly.

## warehouse

A building where goods are stored before they are shipped, named in the record, so that where the goods waited can be read.

## supply chain

All the steps goods pass through from their making to their delivery, named in the record, so that one shipment can be read against the whole chain it belongs to.

## container

A steel box of a standard size in which goods are carried, named in the record with its number, so that the box and the goods inside it can be followed together.

## handler

A person or business that handles goods on their path, named in the record, so that every hand the goods pass through can be read.

## tracking

The following of a shipment along its path, recorded with the places and times, so that where the goods are can be read at any moment.

## port

A place where ships load and unload goods, named in the record, so that where the goods changed from land to sea can be read plainly.

## customs

The office and the rules that govern goods crossing a border, named in the record when the shipment crosses one, so that the border the goods passed can be read.

## delivery

The handing of a shipment to its receiver at the destination, recorded with the time and the name of who received it, so that the shipment is proved done.
