# The Shipping Logistics Database

The Shipping Logistics Database is the shipping logistics record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full shipping logistics record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database transportation-logistics-networks, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
