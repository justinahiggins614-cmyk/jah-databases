The Electricity Consumption Calculator Generator

Boundless generator for the electricity consumption calculator database. Seeded and deterministic: the same seed always gives the same record. Throttled to 100 records per batch. Hard stop at 1,000,000,000 records, or when genuinely complete. Generated records are labelled src "generated" and are never presented as archive records.
