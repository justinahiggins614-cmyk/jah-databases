PLAIN WORDS: This is the database for word combinations up to seven words long. A combo is two or more words in a row, in a set order, like "joke generator" or "ai tutor". This database keeps the vocabulary those combos are built from, the rules for building them, and one record for each combo that has actually been built as a website. The full space of all combos is far too big to store, so it is kept as generator option space: any combo can be made on demand, and only the highest-demand combos are stored.

Every claim in this Signature edition is a SIGNATURE DESIGN CLAIM, labelled as design, never presented as public record. The Signature records are Manon's original versions of the same 103 combos; the vocabulary counts and the combo-space arithmetic are the same public facts as in the public edition.

The Vocabulary

The vocabulary is the 102,223 headwords of the Signature Dictionary — never 1,127,335, which is the record count of the live words database, of which only 102,223 are full headword records; the composition of the remaining roughly 1,025,112 generator and invention records is unverified. Definitions exist for 99,579 headwords: 58,223 authored, 41,356 template-filled, 2,644 pending. Authored definitions are used as written; template-filled definitions are always labelled as such; pending headwords are labelled pending and never given an invented definition. Part of speech is recorded for only about two thirds of the headwords, while 35,654 headwords (34.9 percent) carry no part of speech, so no combo rule here relies on part-of-speech tags.

The Combo Rules

A combo is an ordered list of 2 to 7 words; word order matters. The hyphenated lowercase form of the words is the combo slug, and the slug is the combo's identity. The deterministic generator ordering is the code-point ascending sort of the 102,223 headwords, index 0-based; the rank of an ordered combo of distinct headwords is its lexicographic rank in that ordering. A rank is defined only when every word is a Dictionary headword and no word repeats.

Math

Ordered combos are counted with permutations P(n,k). With n = 102,223: 2-word P(102223,2) = 10,449,439,506; 3-word P(102223,3) = 1,068,152,155,742,826; 4-word P(102223,4) = 109,186,513,360,031,673,720; 5-word P(102223,5) = 11,160,936,209,149,077,655,984,680; 6-word P(102223,6) = 1,140,848,577,426,800,419,839,442,020,240; 7-word P(102223,7) = 116,614,119,038,835,258,514,728,244,982,872,080. Total ordered combos of 2 to 7 words: 116,615,259,898,573,730,711,878,684,717,733,052. Worked example: "joke generator" has rank 5,001,555,420 and "ai tutor" has rank 236,023,061. 57 of the 103 stored combos have a computable rank; the other 46 have no rank, stated on their records. A stored page is 15 to 63 kilobytes, so the 2-word combos of the top 1,000 words alone would be 999,000 pages and 15 to 63 gigabytes — the full space is generator option space, never stored.

Science

The science here is the science of words: what the Dictionary actually holds, and what people actually search for. The demand seed is List 03, 450 authored service items, not measured search volume — no search-query source is connected and none was scraped. The demand peak is 3-word combos (293 of 450), then 2-word (103 of 450). In a 100-word sample of the vocabulary, hand-classified by the reviewer and stated as judgement, about one word in five was an everyday word and about two in three were obscure, archaic or technical. A 2-word search is usually a thing, a tool or a topic; a 3-word search is usually a specific kind of thing; a 6 to 7 word search is usually a question or a sentence, and its page is an answer page. Speculative material is labelled design-study or entertainment, never fact.

The Generator and the Router

The combo-page generator is word-combo-gen 1.0.1: any combo of 2 to 7 words is emitted as a full page with its record and two AI spec files, deterministically, byte-identical on repeat runs. Combos whose words trigger a standing boundary are refused with a recorded reason and never generated around. The Combo Router answers any combo on demand at its stable address, combos/<slug>/; stored pages and generated pages share the same address. A combo with no stored page resolves to the Router's generator page — never a dead link.
