Voice Changer Database (voice-changer-signature)

A voice changer is a seeded local demo: it shifts set sound samples by a pitch factor, and the shifted samples are counted. Shifting 3 samples by 2 gives 20, 40 and 60. Doubling 2 samples gives 20 and 40. The loudest sample is 30. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
