The Messaging Platform Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A messaging platform carries written messages between people, so they can talk without being in the same place. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it stores messages in the browser with simulated contacts, no server is involved, and no message ever leaves the page. No account, no sign-in, no payment.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/messaging-platform/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Messaging Platform Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A messaging platform carries written messages between people, so they can talk without being in the same place. A message from Ana to Ben arrives as 1 message in Ben's list.

    Math proof: A conversation is a list of messages, and its size is a count: 12 messages from Ana and 8 from Ben make 20 messages. An average is a total divided by days: 140 messages in a week is 20 a day. Deleting is subtraction: 10 messages minus 1 deleted leaves 9.

    Science proof: A messaging platform works because a short written message is quick to send, quick to read, and kept in order, so a conversation can be followed days later. Marking messages read, counting unread ones, and searching by words turn a long list into something findable. That is why the demo keeps its messages in the browser: the same order and counts, with no server at all.
