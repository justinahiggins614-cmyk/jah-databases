1. Description

A messaging platform carries written messages between people, so they can talk without being in the same place. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it stores messages in the browser with simulated contacts, no server is involved, and no message ever leaves the page. No account, no sign-in, no payment.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A conversation is a list of messages, and its size is a count: 12 messages from Ana and 8 from Ben make 20 messages. An average is a total divided by days: 140 messages in a week is 20 a day. Deleting is subtraction: 10 messages minus 1 deleted leaves 9.

3. Science proof

A messaging platform works because a short written message is quick to send, quick to read, and kept in order, so a conversation can be followed days later. Marking messages read, counting unread ones, and searching by words turn a long list into something findable. That is why the demo keeps its messages in the browser: the same order and counts, with no server at all.
