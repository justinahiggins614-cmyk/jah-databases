Numerical Simulation Database (numerical-simulation)

A numerical simulation steps through a changing system one small step at a time: pick a step, press Run, and read the result. A simulation steps y from 1.0 to 1.1 in one step of 0.1. A falling ball after 2 seconds moves at 19.6 metres per second. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
