2. Math proof

A simulation steps y from 1.0 to 1.1 in one step of 0.1. Two Euler steps: y goes 1.0 to 1.1 to 1.21. Euler with 100 steps of 0.01 gives 2.7048. A smaller step of 0.05 gives 1.05. After 5 steps of 0.2 the value is 2.49.
