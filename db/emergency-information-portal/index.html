What to Do First in a Flood Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An emergency information portal website: a visitor picks a kind of emergency, such as a flood, a power cut, or a fire alarm, and the portal shows plain first steps, what to have ready, and how to stay safe until help arrives. Every guide is a clearly labeled general-information page written for this database, never a substitute for professional help, and the portal names no real service and makes no claim about any real emergency. This record covers What to Do First in a Flood.

What to do first in a flood is the first page every household should read before the water rises: move to higher ground, avoid walking through flood water, keep clear of fast water, and call for help. The portal must put these steps first, in plain sentences, with no long introduction before them, because a person reading in a flood has no time for a long introduction. Without plain first steps, a household must search for guidance while the water is already rising, and searching while the water rises is a poor way to stay safe.
