Charging Station Finder Database (charging-station-finder)

A charging station finder is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy stations, and counts the marks. The finder reads 3 stations. The station north holds 18 marks. The largest holds 18 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
