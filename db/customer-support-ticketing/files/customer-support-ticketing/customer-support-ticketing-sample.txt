Customer Support Ticketing Database (customer-support-ticketing)

Customer support ticketing tracks help requests as tickets: read the tickets, add your own, and watch the counts. The demo on this page is a LOCAL DEMO ONLY: it stores tickets in this browser, and sending a ticket to a real service is BLOCKED. 5 tickets, 3 open. 3 of 5 tickets are open: 60 percent.

Worked example: 5 tickets, 3 open

Demo output:
5 tickets, 3 open
