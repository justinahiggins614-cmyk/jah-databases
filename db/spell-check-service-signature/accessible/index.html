The Signature Spell Check Service Database holds 24 full spell check service record records, in a teaching ladder: 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex. This edition is the Signature edition. The Design/System

The design is one database per subject, with a public edition and a Signature edition side by side. Every record gives its history, its full content, its methods, its results as reported, and its skeptical analysis. Every technical term is defined in plain words where it first appears, and the glossary defines them all again. Each Signature record is a design study, labeled as a design, never presented as established fact.

The Generator

The boundless generator for this database is the file gen-spell-check-service-signature.js. It is seeded and deterministic: the same seed always gives the same record, and different seeds differ. It is throttled to 100 records per call, it stops at 1,000,000,000 records per file, and every generated record is labeled generated, never presented as an archive record.

The AI

The archive-aware AI for this database is JAH Signature Spell Check Designer. It explains this database in plain sentences, answers questions about every record, and can generate new records in this database's family. Its full specification is downloadable: spell-check-service-signature-ai.json.

Math Proof

The counting argument is exact: 24 records, 8 simple, 8 compounded, 8 most complex, and 8 + 8 + 8 = 24. The formal statement is: correct(w) = w is in the dictionary D; else nearest(w, D) (stated, not machine-checked) The scientific statement is: accuracy = correctly judged words / all tested words (stated, not machine-checked) Both are stated, not machine-checked, and the record-level proofs are in each record's math proof.

The scientific claim is that the subject can be stated, built, and checked by the method in the records: state it in plain words, define every term, run the demo and the program as real programs, and re-verify independently.
