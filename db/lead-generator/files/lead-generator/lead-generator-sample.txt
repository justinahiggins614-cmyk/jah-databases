Lead Generator Database (lead-generator)

A lead generator is a rule-based local demo: it gathers set toy leads from set toy sources, and counts the leads and the sources. The generator gathers leads from 3 sources. The lead is called sam. The largest source gathers 45 leads. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
