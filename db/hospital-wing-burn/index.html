Parkland Resuscitation

The Parkland formula — 4 mL × kg × %TBSA of lactated Ringer's over 24 hours — replaces the plasma leaking from burned vessels. Urine output, not the formula, titrates. Under-resuscitation kills kidneys; over does lungs.

Escharotomy

Full-thickness burns form leathery eschar that tourniquets limbs and chests; surgical release restores circulation and breathing. It is emergent, bedside, and limb-saving. Chest escharotomy lets the patient breathe.
