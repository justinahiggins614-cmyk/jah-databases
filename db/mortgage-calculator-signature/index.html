1. Description

A mortgage calculator estimates mortgage payments from the home price, the deposit, the rate and the years. This page is an ESTIMATOR for education: every rate on it is a stated example rate, shown on the page, not a real mortgage rate, and it is not financial advice. Type a home, pick an example rate, and read the estimate. Everything runs in this browser.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

A mortgage of 200000 at 6 percent over 30 years pays 1199.10 a month, and 360 payments of 1199.10 total 431676.38, which is 231676.38 of interest. The same mortgage over 15 years pays 1687.71 a month. At 4 percent it pays 954.83 a month. A home of 250000 with a deposit of 50000 needs a mortgage of 200000, and the deposit is 20 percent. The first payment of 1199.10 is 1000.00 of interest and 199.10 of principal.

3. Science proof

Mortgage estimation works because the arithmetic is the same as any loan: a monthly payment is worked out from the principal, the monthly rate and the number of months, the total is the payment times the months, and the interest is the total minus the principal. Each payment is part interest and part principal, and the balance falls a little each month. The estimate is only as good as the rate, which is why every rate on this page is labelled as an example and the page never claims to be real financial advice.
