History
Registering a business means telling the public record that a new business exists, under a name, at a place, with an owner who answers for it. Long before computers, towns kept such registers in ledgers; the forms asked for the same things they ask for now: who owns it, what it is called, where it trades, and what form it takes. Assistance means walking a founder through those questions in plain words, checking each answer, and keeping a copy of what was filed, so the business starts on a true record.
