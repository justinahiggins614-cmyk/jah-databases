# Dictionary entries — The Business Registration Assistance Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## registration

The act of recording a business with the public authority that keeps the register, so the business exists on the public record with a name, an owner, and a place.

## business name

The name under which a business trades, written exactly as registered, so customers, suppliers, and the register all mean the same business by it.

## sole proprietorship

A business owned by one person, where the owner and the business are not separate in law, and the owner answers for the business debts.

## partnership

A business owned by two or more people who agree to share the work, the gains, and the responsibility, with the agreement written down.

## limited liability company

A business form that keeps the owners' personal property separate from the business, so the business answers for its own debts within the law.

## corporation

A business formed as its own legal person, owned by shareholders, run by directors, and able to own property and sign contracts in its own name.

## trade name

A name a business trades under that differs from its legal name, recorded so the public can trace the trading name back to the legal owner.

## registered agent

A named person or office that accepts official papers for a business, so the business can always be reached at a known address.

## filing receipt

The written proof that a registration was received and recorded, kept with the date and the reference number, so the filing can be shown later.

## good standing

The state of a registered business whose filings and fees are up to date, so the register shows it as active and in order.

## business license

A permission from an authority to carry on a named trade in a named place, separate from registration, and renewed as the authority requires.

## amendment

A filed change to a registration, such as a new name or a new address, recorded so the public record stays true after the change.
