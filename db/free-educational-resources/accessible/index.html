  1. A Lesson List for One Subject

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Free educational resources gather free lessons by subject: each lesson shows its level clearly, its steps in plain words, and a practice task a learner can try at once. A learner who cannot see the lessons for one subject in one place must hunt through many pages, and the lessons found are forgotten before the study begins. A lesson list for one subject writes every free lesson for that subject in one list, in plain words, so the learner can see them all at once. Without a single list, lessons live in scattered places, the learner compares from memory, and the best lesson is often the one forgotten. Worked example: in a website built this way, a lesson list for one subject runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

Lesson lists for one subject are an old habit, from library shelves grouped by subject to printed course lists pinned by the door.
