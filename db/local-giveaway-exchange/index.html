A Giveaway Listing With a Clear Pickup Time Record 1 · simple

Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

A giveaway website: each listing shows the item in plain words, its condition, the neighborhood where it can be picked up, and the pickup time, with no payment, no delivery fee, and no private messages anywhere on the site. Every listing is public, every pickup is arranged in a public place, and every item is checked against a written safety list before it is published. This record covers A Giveaway Listing With a Clear Pickup Time.

A giveaway listing without a clear pickup time wastes the time of everyone who wants the item: one neighbor arrives in the morning and the giver is out, another arrives in the evening and the item is already gone. A clear pickup time fixes the meeting point in time, so the giver waits once and the taker comes once, and the exchange is done. Without the time written down, the board fills with messages asking when, and the item that should have found a new home sits by the door for days.

Worked example: in a website built this way, a giveaway listing with a clear pickup time runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.

Pickup times were written on paper notices long before websites existed, because a neighbor who knows when can plan a visit and a neighbor who does not cannot.
