A strong biology and genetics research summaries has recognizable parts, and each part has a job. The opening orients the reader and states purpose. The middle carries the substance in a logical order — facts, arguments, narrative, or instructions depending on the form. The closing delivers the outcome: a decision, an action, a feeling, or a summary. Headings, transitions, and consistent terminology hold the parts together.
