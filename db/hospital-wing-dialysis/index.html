Hemodialysis Principles

Hemodialysis pumps blood through a dialyzer — an artificial kidney — removing toxins and fluid over 3 to 4 hours. Diffusion and ultrafiltration do the kidneys' work. It is life support, thrice weekly.

Peritoneal Dialysis

PD uses the patient's own peritoneal membrane: fluid dwells in the belly, absorbing waste, then drains — done nightly at home by cycler. Independence and gentler fluid shifts appeal to many. Training makes patients their own technicians.

Vascular Access: Fistulas

The AV fistula — vein sewn to artery, maturing over weeks — is the gold standard access: durable, low-infection. 'Fistula first' is the mantra. Needles the size of straws, three times a week, for years.

Anemia Management

Mineral and Bone Disorder

Kidney failure deranges calcium, phosphorus, and PTH — bones weaken, vessels calcify. Binders, vitamin D analogs, and calcimimetics manage it. Bones and vessels both suffer.
