# Dictionary entries — The Building Permit Management Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## building permit management

The recorded keeping of building permits from application to final inspection, so that every work permitted can be followed from the paper that allowed it to the sign-off that closed it.

## building permit

The written permission from a public office to carry out stated building work, kept in the record with its number and its conditions, so that the permission can be proved from the paper.

## building code

The published set of rules that building work must meet for safety and soundness, written in the record so that each inspection can be checked against the rule it applies.

## application

The written request for a building permit, naming the work, the place, and the applicant, kept with the plans it carries, so that the request and its papers can be read together.

## plan

The drawn design of the building work, made to scale and filed with the application, kept in the record so that what was allowed can be compared with what was built.

## inspection

The visit by a public inspector to a building site to check the work against the permit and the code, kept in the record with its date and its finding, so that the check can be followed.

## certificate of occupancy

The written statement that a building may be occupied after its work has passed inspection, kept in the record with its date, so that the permission to occupy can be proved from the paper.

## zoning

The public rules that state what may be built and used on a parcel of land, written in the record so that the permit can be checked against the use the rules allow.

## applicant

The person or body that asks for a building permit, named in the record with the application, so that who sought the permission can be read plainly.

## contractor

The person or body that carries out the building work under the permit, named in the record with the permit, so that who did the work can be traced to the paper.

## fee

The amount charged for a permit or an inspection, written in the record with its amount and its date, so that what was charged for the permission can be counted.

## stop-work order

The written order that building work must halt until a stated fault is put right, kept in the record with its date and its grounds, so that the halt can be followed to its cause.
