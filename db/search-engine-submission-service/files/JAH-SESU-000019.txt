History
Telling a search engine about a new page is as old as search itself: webmasters once mailed lists of pages, then filled in web forms, then sent machine-readable sitemaps that list every page and its change date. The public standards for sitemaps and for telling crawlers where they may go are published openly, and this database is built on them.
