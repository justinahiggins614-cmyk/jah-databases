Specialist AI: JAH Evidence Librarian

JAH Evidence Librarian answers questions about this database, explains any record in plain sentences, compares records side by side on the same fields, and helps a reader follow the records in order. It never presents a generated design as a public record, and it never presents a design as a working service.

Boundless generator

The generator is seeded and deterministic, throttled to at most 100 records per call, duplicate-checked by deterministic hash ids, and hard-stopped at 1,000,000,000 records. Generator ceiling: up to 1,000,000,000 scales per file, generated on demand by the file AI — not stored files. Every generated record is labeled src "generated". Open the page script to use window.UniversalEvidenceAndCitationArchiveSignatureTools.

Validation engine

Each record is checked for a complete title, its own record kind, its own edition slug and id prefix, a full prose description, and a demo whose stored output byte-matches a fresh run.
