# Dictionary entries — The Nutrition Planning Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## nutrition planning

The recorded planning of the food eaten by a household, holding the menus, the ingredients, the shopping lists, and the food diary together, so that the food of the household can be read as a whole.

## food group

One of the named kinds of food, such as grain, fruit, vegetable, or dairy, written in the plan so that the kinds of food in a menu can be counted.

## daily menu

The written list of the meals planned for one day, naming each dish, so that the food of the day can be read before it is cooked.

## weekly plan

The written list of the menus planned for one week, kept in order, so that the food of the week can be read as a whole.

## portion record

The written account of the amount served of a named dish, kept beside the menu so that the quantities in the plan can be counted.

## ingredient list

The written list of the ingredients in a dish, naming each one, so that what goes into the food can be read plainly.

## recipe card

The written card holding one recipe, naming its ingredients and its method, kept in the plan so that the dish can be cooked again as it was written.

## shopping list

The written list of the food to be bought for a plan, naming each item and its quantity, so that the buying can be checked against the plan.

## pantry record

The written account of the food held in store, naming each item and its quantity, so that what is on hand can be read before anything is bought.

## nutrient table

The written table of the nutrients published for a food, kept beside the ingredient it describes, so that the published values can be read without guessing.

## food diary

The written diary of the food eaten on each day, kept in order, so that what was eaten can be compared with what was planned.

## allergy note

The written note naming a food that a member of the household cannot eat, kept with the plan so that the food is left out of every menu.
