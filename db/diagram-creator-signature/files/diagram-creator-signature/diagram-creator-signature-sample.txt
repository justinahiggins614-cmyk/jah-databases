Diagram Creator Database (diagram-creator-signature)

A diagram creator draws boxes, circles and lines on a canvas: pick a shape, press Draw, and download the diagram. A diagram with 3 boxes and 2 arrows. A box 100 by 50 has area 5000. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
