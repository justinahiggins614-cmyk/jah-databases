Medical Image Viewer Database (medical-image-viewer)

A medical image viewer is a rule-based local demo: it views set toy images, and counts the marks. Reference only: general information, no specific dosing, no cure claims, no diagnosis claims. Sample images only, no real patient images or data. The viewer reads 3 images. The image alpha holds 15 marks. The largest holds 15 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
