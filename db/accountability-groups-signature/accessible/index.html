  1. Stating One Goal in Plain Words

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Accountability groups are small public circles where each member states a goal in plain words, reports progress each week, and is cheered on by the group, with no shame and no pressure. A goal stated in plain words is a goal a group can help with, because a vague goal like get better cannot be checked, cheered, or finished. The goal should name one thing, one measure, and one date, so the group knows what success looks like. Without a plain goal, the group cheers politely at nothing in particular, and the member never knows whether they have arrived.
