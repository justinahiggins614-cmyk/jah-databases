Feasibility: buildable-now The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

An accountability website: a member joins a small public circle, states one goal in plain words, and reports progress each week on a public board the whole circle can read. The group cheers progress and helps when a member is stuck, under written rules that forbid shaming, pressure, and any comment about a person's health or body. There are no private messages anywhere on the site. This record covers Stating One Goal in Plain Words.

A goal stated in plain words is a goal a group can help with, because a vague goal like get better cannot be checked, cheered, or finished. The goal should name one thing, one measure, and one date, so the group knows what success looks like. Without a plain goal, the group cheers politely at nothing in particular, and the member never knows whether they have arrived. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
