Badminton Rank 1 · simple · racquet sport · beginner

Signature badminton is taught as footwork before power. A student learns the split step, the chasse, and the lunge, then the clear, then the drop, because a smash hit from a poor position is a gift to the opponent. Our coaches feed multi-shuttle drills until movement is automatic, and deception is taught late, only when basic shots are reliable. Doubles rotation is drilled as a partnership skill, with partners learning to cover each other without words. The hall's praise goes to the player who reaches every shuttle in balance, not the player who smashes hardest.

Training

Sessions pair footwork drills, multi-shuttle feeding, shot practice, and match play.

Techniques

Signature basics are the split step, chasse, lunge, clear, and drop, in that order.

Safety

Courts are kept clear, eye protection is advised for doubles, and warm-ups precede all play.

History

The Signature school follows badminton from battledore games to Olympic competition.

Squash Rank 2 · simple · racquet sport · beginner

Signature squash is taught as length before trickery. A student learns to hit a straight rail deep to the back, then to return to the T, then to vary height, before any drop or boast is attempted, because a player who cannot hit length cannot build a rally. Our coaches drill movement to the T until it is automatic, and ghosting drills rehearse court movement without a ball. Drops are taught as a reward for good length, not as a first shot. The hall's praise goes to the player whose rails are straight and deep, rally after rally.

Training

Sessions pair rail drills, T movement, ghosting, and match play, with drops added only after length is reliable.

Techniques

Signature basics are the straight rail, the return to the T, the cross-court, and then the drop.

Safety

Eye protection is always worn, and players call lets rather than playing through interference.

History

The Signature school follows squash from Harrow School to the professional tour and the Olympics.

Working demo program

print('Daily average: %.1f minutes' % (total / len(DAYS)))
print('Longest session: %s (%d minutes)' % (best[0], best[1]))
