Worked example: a reader who wants the simplest starting point opens record 1, A Wooden Chair Offered to Neighbors, and a reader who wants the hardest problem opens record 23, A Yearly Showcase of Listings.

    History: Offering useful things to neighbors is an old habit: a notice on a board, a table at a fair, a word passed along a street. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a search by kind of thing and a plain description for every listing. Field guides and indexes are as old as books themselves: a table of contents that tells a reader where everything lives. This index follows that habit, listing the twenty-three content records of this database in the order a reader should meet them.
