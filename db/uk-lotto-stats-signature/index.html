Boundless generator

The boundless generator creates deterministic labeled variations over the 16 seed records. It is throttled, duplicate-checked, and hard-stops at 10^15 records or when the variation space is genuinely exhausted. Generated records are labeled generated and are never mixed with assistant-built records.

Validation and testing suite

The generator testing suite (runTests) checks slug, prefix, record kind, generated source, single-signature form, search, validation, and the hard stop. Every shipped record demo was re-executed with Python 3 at build time and byte-matched against its recorded output.

Audit history

Built as a package-only lane job on 2026-10-26: 16 records per edition, demos executed at build time, historical claims cited with source and date, unverified history kept methodology-only with simulated data. Nothing in this package has been merged yet.

Randomness statement

Lottery outcomes are random. No method, pattern, or number choice can predict a future draw, and no record in this database presents a predictive method.

Responsible play

Responsible play note: lottery play is entertainment with a cost. Set a spending limit in advance, never play with money needed for essentials, and seek help from a local gambling-support service if play stops feeling voluntary.
