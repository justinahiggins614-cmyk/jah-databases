The Old-Photo Colorization Database — Accessible Version

Plain version of the The Old-Photo Colorization Database. No scripts run on this page. Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Old-photo colorization adds plausible color to black-and-white photographs: skin, sky, grass, and clothes colored in tones that fit the photo's era, with every guess marked as a guess. This database designs that colorization one record at a time, from one portrait to a whole album in consistent color, and every record is a design, never a real product. It works on the user's own photos only.
