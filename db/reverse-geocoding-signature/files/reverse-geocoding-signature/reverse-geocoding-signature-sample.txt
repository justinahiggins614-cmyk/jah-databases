Reverse Geocoding Database (reverse-geocoding-signature)

Reverse geocoding is a rule-based local demo: it turns set toy coordinates into a named place, and counts the places. The tool turns 3 points. The latitude is 40.5. The largest holds 74.0 values. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
