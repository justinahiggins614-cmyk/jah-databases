The Survey Builder Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A survey builder makes surveys: write questions, choose the answer type for each, preview the survey, collect answers, and count the results. Everything runs in this browser; answers save locally and no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/survey-builder/index.jsonl.gz

  1. What a Survey Is

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. A survey is a set of questions asked of many people, so their answers can be counted. A survey asks 5 questions of 20 people.

    Math proof: A survey result is a tally: counts per choice, divided by all answers. With 12 of 20 people picking the first answer, that choice is 12 over 20, or 60 percent. An average rating is a sum divided by a count: ratings of 5, 4, 4, 5, 3, 4, 5, 4, 4, 4 add to 42, and 42 over 10 is 4.2.

    Science proof: A survey works because a short question with a clear answer type is easy to answer, and easy questions get answered. Counting answers by choice turns many voices into a pattern that can be seen at a glance. That is why short surveys with a few answer types finish more often, and why results are counted before any decision is made from them.
