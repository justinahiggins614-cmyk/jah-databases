History
Trades have taken apprentices for as long as skilled work has been taught, because a craft cannot be learned from books alone — it must be learned beside a master who practises it. Apprenticeship matching grew beside the apprenticeship itself: a written pairing of a learner who seeks a trade with a master who will teach it, so that each apprentice can be followed from the asking to the finished trade. From the hand-written indenture signed before witnesses to the record kept in a database, the honest practice has been the same — write the match down whole. This database is built on that public practice of recording every apprenticeship match true. It is a record of published practice only, and it gives no advice about any application.
