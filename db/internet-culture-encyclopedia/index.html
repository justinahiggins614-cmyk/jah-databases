This record covers what counts as internet culture in the internet culture encyclopedia. The starting point is the entries: getting that part right is what makes the whole thing work, because a internet culture encyclopedia that is sloppy about entries is sloppy everywhere else too. Without a careful approach, the result is easy to get wrong: the work looks finished while the fair part, the honest part, or the kind part is missing, and nobody notices until someone is treated unfairly or a newcomer cannot follow what happened.
