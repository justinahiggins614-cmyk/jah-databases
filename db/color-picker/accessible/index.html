The Color Picker Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A color picker picks a colour and shows its numbers: pick a colour, press Pick, and read the hex code, the three numbers, the complement and the contrast ratios. The colour #FF0000 is [255, 0, 0]: 3 numbers. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.
