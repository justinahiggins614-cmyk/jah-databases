    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The music recommendation engine suggests a kind of music a listener may like, from plain answers about mood, tempo, and instruments, with every suggestion labeled as an illustrative sample of a generic kind, never a real song by a real artist. A recommendation by mood gives a listener music that fits how they feel, from a calm tune for a quiet evening to a bright tune for a fresh morning. The engine must ask the mood in plain words, match it to a kind of music, and give a written reason for the match, so the listener can see why this tune and not another.
