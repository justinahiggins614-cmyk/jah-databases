# The Route Optimization Database

The Route Optimization Database is the route optimization record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full route optimization record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database optimization, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
