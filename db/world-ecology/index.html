Definition: Ecology is the science of the relations between living things and their surroundings: the organism and its environment, the population and its habitat, and the community and its ecosystem. The ecologist studies how living things live, grow, and die, and how they affect each other and the world around them. Ecology is the science of the whole: the individual, the population, the community, and the ecosystem, all together.

History: Ecology began as a word in 1866, when Ernst Haeckel coined it from the Greek oikos, the house, and logos, the study: the study of the house, the household of nature. In the 1960s, ecology became the public science: the environment was the issue, and the ecologist was the expert.
