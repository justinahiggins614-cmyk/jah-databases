History
Ships have carried travellers for pleasure since the days of the great ocean liners, when a crossing was itself the holiday and the ship was a floating town. The cruise grew from that crossing: a voyage that calls at several ports and returns to where it began, with cabins, meals, and days at sea all part of one booking. Booking a cruise means setting down, before sailing, which ship, which cabins, which ports, and what fare, so that the traveller and the operator agree about the voyage before it begins. This database is built on that public practice of recording every cruise booking true, whole, and in order.
