# Dictionary entries — The Cruise Booking Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## cruise booking

The written record of cabins reserved on a named cruise ship for a named voyage, with the ports, the dates, and the fare, so that the booking can be found and checked.

## cruise ship

A large passenger ship built for voyages that call at several ports, named in the booking, so that the ship the booking covers is part of the record.

## cabin

A private room on a cruise ship where travellers sleep, named in the booking with its number and its kind, so that the cabins held and the cabins free can be counted.

## voyage

One cruise from its departure port to its return, with its ports of call in order, kept with the booking, so that the journey the booking covers can be read plainly.

## port of call

A port where a cruise ship stops during a voyage, written in the booking with its name and its date, so that every stop on the voyage can be read in order.

## embarkation

The act of boarding a cruise ship at the start of a voyage, recorded with the port and the date, so that the beginning of the voyage is part of the record.

## disembarkation

The act of leaving a cruise ship at the end of a voyage, recorded with the port and the date, so that the ending of the voyage is part of the record.

## itinerary

The ordered list of ports and dates of a cruise voyage, kept with the booking, so that the whole voyage can be read from the record in order.

## fare

The price charged for a cruise booking, written in the booking with its amount and what it covers, so that what was charged for the voyage can be counted and checked.

## deck

One level of a cruise ship, named in the booking beside the cabin, so that where the cabin sits on the ship can be read from the record.

## booking reference

The one identifier given to a cruise booking when it is made, written in the record and on the papers, so that the booking can be found at any moment.

## ocean liner

A large passenger ship built mainly to carry travellers across an ocean, named in the booking when the voyage uses one, so that the kind of ship is part of the record.
