JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000001 — Plasma Computer Logic — Overview and definition

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Logic in a plasma computer is done by the plasma itself: waves, interference, and solitary pulses replace transistors. Where a silicon chip switches voltages on wires, a plasma cell switches patterns in a continuous medium — constructive interference means 1, destructive means 0, and a soliton racing down a density channel is a bit in flight. This is wave computing, a real and studied field: researchers have demonstrated logic with spin waves, surface acoustic waves, and optical interference. Plasma is simply the most extreme wave medium of all.

JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000002 — Plasma Computer Logic — Plasma waves and dispersion

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. The fundamental signal is the Langmuir wave, an electron-density oscillation with dispersion relation omega^2 = omega_pe^2 + 3 k^2 v_th^2, where omega_pe is the plasma frequency and v_th the electron thermal speed. Waves carry information at the group velocity, and their phase can be shaped by launch antennas. Understanding dispersion is the plasma equivalent of understanding a wire's resistance and capacitance: it tells you how fast signals move and how they spread.

Demo: Langmuir wave dispersion lifts frequency

JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000003 — Plasma Computer Logic — Gates from interference

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Two waves meeting in a cell add as vectors: in phase they reinforce (a 1), out of phase they cancel (a 0). Launch two input waves and one bias wave, and the interference pattern at the output point implements AND; shift the bias by half a cycle and it implements OR; a single wave against a steady reference implements NOT. This is real, demonstrable physics — optical computers have used exactly this trick for decades — and it needs no moving parts and no semiconductors.

Demo: Interference AND becomes OR on bias flip

function gate(a,b,bias,th){return (a+b+bias)>=th?1:0;}const AND=[[0,0],[0,1],[1,0],[1,1]].map(p=>gate(p[0],p[1],-0.5,1));const OR=[[0,0],[0,1],[1,0],[1,1]].map(p=>gate(p[0],p[1],0.5,1));J({AND,OR});assert(AND.join('')==='0001'&&OR.join('')==='0111','gates');

JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000004 — Plasma Computer Logic — Solitons: bits in flight

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Solitons are solitary waves that hold their shape as they travel, balancing nonlinearity against dispersion. A soliton is the ideal bit packet: it does not spread, it survives collisions with other solitons, and its presence or absence at a detector is an unambiguous 1 or 0. Solitons have been observed in real plasmas (ion-acoustic solitons are textbook phenomena). Logic pieces use them as the interconnect: solitons shuttle bits between cells along density channels without wires.

JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000005 — Plasma Computer Logic — The plasma frequency as clock

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Every plasma has a natural oscillation rate, the plasma frequency omega_pe = sqrt(n_e e^2 / (epsilon_0 m_e)). At 10^18 electrons per cubic metre it is about 5.6e10 radians per second — roughly 9 gigahertz of natural oscillation. That is the machine's heartbeat: operations synchronized to fractions of a plasma period run at gigahertz rates with no external clock tree. Raise the density and the clock speeds up; the generation stage literally sets the processor speed.

JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000006 — Plasma Computer Logic — Nonlinear mixing and universality

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Linear interference gives you AND and OR, but a universal computer needs NAND or NOR — and that needs nonlinearity. Plasmas are naturally nonlinear: strong waves mix to produce sum and difference frequencies (three-wave coupling is standard plasma physics). A mixing cell that outputs a wave only when both inputs are absent is a NOR gate, and NOR alone builds any circuit. From NOR: inverters, latches, adders, and finally a full processor, all in waves.

Demo: Hamming(7,4) corrects a wave-bit error

function enc(d){const p1=d[0]^d[1]^d[3],p2=d[0]^d[2]^d[3],p3=d[1]^d[2]^d[3];return [p1,p2,d[0],p3,d[1],d[2],d[3]];}const w=enc([1,0,1,1]);w[4]^=1;const s1=w[0]^w[2]^w[4]^w[6],s2=w[1]^w[2]^w[5]^w[6],s3=w[3]^w[4]^w[5]^w[6];const pos=s1+s2*2+s3*4;if(pos)w[pos-1]^=1;const out=[w[2],w[4],w[5],w[6]];J({corrected:out});assert(out.join('')==='1011','hamming');

JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000007 — Plasma Computer Logic — Noise, collisions, and error correction

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Real plasmas are noisy: electron-ion collisions damp waves, thermal motion jitters phases, and instabilities inject garbage. The collision frequency sets a hard deadline — logic must finish before damping eats the signal, typically within hundreds to thousands of wave periods. The engineering answer is the classic one: redundancy and error correction. Triple-modular redundancy on critical paths and Hamming-coded wave packets turn a noisy medium into a reliable computer, exactly as early core-memory machines did.

JAH-PLASMA-LOGIC-000008 — Plasma Computer Logic — Logic in the full stack

Public edition. Public physics and engineering knowledge, fact-checked reference. Speculative material is absent from this edition; anything not yet established science lives in the Signature twin. Logic consumes stable cells from containment and publishes results to memory: its interfaces are a gate map (which cells implement which gates), a timing contract (operations per plasma period), and an error budget. Beginner exercise: write the truth table of an interference AND gate with a bias wave, then flip the bias phase by pi and show the table becomes OR. Two lines of arithmetic, and you have designed your first plasma gate.
