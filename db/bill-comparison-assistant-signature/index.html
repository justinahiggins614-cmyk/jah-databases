This record covers A Bill Total Worked Out in Plain Steps.

A bill total worked out in plain steps lets a household see where the money goes: the power bill, the water bill, and the phone bill each on their own line, then the total at the end. The steps must be plain, because a total with no steps is a number nobody can check. Without the steps, the household must trust a single figure, and a single figure hides a mistake that plain steps would show. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design.
