Podcast Creator Database (podcast-creator)

A podcast creator is a local demo: it joins spoken segments into one show, and the show length is counted. 3 segments make a show of 14 minutes. The show holds 3 segments. The longest segment is talk at 10 minutes. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Worked example: 3 segments make a show of 14 minutes

Demo output:
3 segments make a show of 14 minutes
