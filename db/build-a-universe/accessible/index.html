The Build a Universe Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. Build a universe is a design study and a simulation: a visitor picks the rules of an invented universe, such as how its stars form or how its seasons turn, and the site draws the result on paper, step by step. It is never a claim that a real universe can be built, and every record here says plainly which parts are simulation and which parts have no known path at all.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

  1. A Universe Built on Paper

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    Build a universe is a design study and a simulation: a visitor picks the rules of an invented universe, such as how its stars form or how its seasons turn, and the site draws the result on paper, step by step. A universe built on paper, with its rules written out first and its stars drawn one at a time. The design for this record must state the idea in plain sentences, name what a visitor does first, and say what good looks like when it works, so a reader can judge the design without building anything. Without that plain statement, the idea stays vague, a visitor cannot tell what the design promises, and a promising idea is lost because nobody could explain it in words a newcomer can follow. A reader who can follow the design in words can also check it, and a design that can be checked is a design worth building. Worked example: in a website built this way, a universe built on paper runs as a small, complete feature a visitor can try in one sitting, with the fair-play and moderation rules of this database applied throughout.
