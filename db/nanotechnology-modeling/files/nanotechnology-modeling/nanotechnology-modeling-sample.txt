Nanotechnology Modeling Database (nanotechnology-modeling)

A nanotechnology modeling is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy models, and counts the marks. The modeling reads 3 models. The model tube holds 30 marks. The largest holds 30 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
