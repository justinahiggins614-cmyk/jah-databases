1. Overview

The orangutan (Pongo abelii, Pongo pygmaeus, and Pongo tapanuliensis) has its own mathematics, its own science, and its own court, the "Long Call" court. This website is the per-animal website for the orangutan, built first, before the networks, under humanity, under God. It holds 24 public records and 24 Signature records, in three ladders of eight: mathematics and measurement; science, evolution, and court; Signature, Light, and human translation.

Archive records: 48 (24 public + 24 Signature).

The orangutan lives in the rainforests of Borneo and Sumatra, eats mostly fruit, with leaves, bark, and insects, and lives in a mostly solitary life, mothers with young. It is adult males about 50 to 100 kilograms, adult females about 30 to 50 kilograms and stands about 1.2 to 1.5 metres tall when standing. Its characteristic sound is the male long call, a loud booming call that carries through the rainforest.
