1. Description

A report generator turns numbers into a readable report: a table, a total, an average, the biggest and smallest numbers, and a summary. Type a few numbers, press Make report, and read the report. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

The total of [120, 200, 150, 90, 210] is 770. The average of 5 numbers totalling 770 is 154. The biggest number is 210 and the smallest is 90, so the range is 120. A monthly report of 12 months totals 1860. Week 1 totals 770 and week 2 totals 820: week 2 is bigger by 50.

3. Science proof

A report works because a list of numbers has a total, an average, a biggest and a smallest, and those four numbers say what the list means: 5 rows total 770, average 154, biggest 210. A percentage column shows each row as a share of the total: 16, 26, 19, 12, 27. That is why the report shows the table first, then the summary.
