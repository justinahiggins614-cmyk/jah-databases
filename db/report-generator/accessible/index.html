The Report Generator Database (Public)

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. A report generator turns numbers into a readable report: a table, a total, an average, the biggest and smallest numbers, and a summary. Type a few numbers, press Make report, and read the report. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/report-generator/index.jsonl.gz
