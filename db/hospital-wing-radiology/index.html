MRI Principles

Magnetic resonance uses magnets and radio waves — no radiation — to image soft tissues exquisitely: brain, spine, joints. Scans take 20 to 60 minutes inside a narrow tube. Metal implants must be screened first.

Contrast Safety

IV contrast can injure kidneys or trigger allergies, so creatinine is checked and histories screened. Premedication protects allergic patients; hydration protects kidneys. Reactions are rare but the team is always ready.

ALARA — As Low As Reasonably Achievable — governs every radiation exposure: lowest dose that answers the question. Pediatric protocols slash dose for children. Shielding and collimation protect everyone else.
