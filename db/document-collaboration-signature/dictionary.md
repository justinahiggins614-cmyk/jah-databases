# Dictionary entries — The Document Collaboration Database

Key terms of this database, defined in plain words.

## document

A written work kept as one whole, with a title, an author list, and a history of every change made to it.

## author

A person who writes in a document, named beside every change they make, so that the work can be answered for.

## change

One edit made to a document, kept with its author, its time, and the words before and after, so it can be undone.

## version

One named state of a document, fixed and dated, so that the document can be read as it stood on any day.

## comment

A note written beside a document's words, by a reader, asking a question or suggesting a change, without changing the words.

## suggestion

A proposed change, shown beside the original words, which the document's keepers may accept or refuse, in writing.

## conflict

Two changes made to the same words at the same time, which must be settled by a person, never silently by the machine.

## permission

The named right of a person to read, to comment, or to write in a document, given by the document's keepers.

## revision history

The ordered list of every change ever made to a document, with author and time, kept so the document can be traced back.

## merge

Bringing two sets of changes together into one document, keeping every change that does not conflict, and naming the ones that do.

## keeper

A person trusted to keep a document's rules, who decides who may write, settles conflicts, and names the versions.

## final copy

The version of a document its keepers have named as finished, kept beside the draft history, never instead of it.
