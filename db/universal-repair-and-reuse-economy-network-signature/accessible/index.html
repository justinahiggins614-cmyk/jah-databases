The Signature Universal Repair and Reuse Economy Network Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The universal repair and reuse economy network lists mended things and the people who can mend them, so a broken chair finds a mender and a mended chair finds a new home. Every listing is a design study of a generic repair, never a real shop and never a promise that a real thing can be mended.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.
