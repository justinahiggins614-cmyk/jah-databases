# The Cartographer's Daughter

## A Fantasy Novel of Maps That Remember

Mara grew up among maps, in a shop where the charts on the wall smelled of paper and ink, and where her father taught her that a good map tells the truth about the land and about the person who drew it. When she finds a map that changes while she watches it, she discovers that some maps remember the journeys made upon them, and that remembering is both a gift and a danger. This novel follows Mara from her father's shop to the far north and back, through guilds, mountains, seas, and cities, as she learns what her gift costs and what it is for.
