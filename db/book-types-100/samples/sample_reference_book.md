# The Complete Encyclopedia of Everyday Knowledge

## A Whole Reference Book for Readers of Every Age

This encyclopedia gathers everyday knowledge into one complete book. It explains the natural world, the human body, human history, science, technology, and the arts in plain language, with entries that teach rather than merely name, and with cross-references that let a reader follow curiosity from one subject to the next.

## Preface

This book was written to be read from beginning to end and also to be used as a reference afterwards. A reader who reads it in order will find each chapter building on the last, and a reader who opens it at a single chapter will find that chapter stating what it assumes, what it teaches, and where to look for the background. The book makes no promise it does not keep: every chapter named in the table of contents is written here in full, with examples, tables, and explanations, and the back matter is written in full as well.

## How to use this book

Read the chapter introduction first, work through the subsections in order, attempt the exercises before reading the worked solutions, and use the glossary whenever a term is unfamiliar. The index at the back will take you to every major discussion of a topic, and the appendices carry supporting tables and material that would interrupt the flow of the chapters.
