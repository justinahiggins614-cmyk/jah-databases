The Global Apprenticeship Matching Network Database

Accessible version: plain text, no scripts, no sign-in, free forever. The global apprenticeship matching network matches a learner with a master of a trade in plain words, one match for a trade, a place, and a length of placement, with the terms of each match written out beside it. Every match is a clearly labeled design study of a generic kind of trade, never a promise of a place.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to compounded to complex.

Full record index: https://justinahiggins614-cmyk.github.io/jah-databases-data-2/data/global-apprenticeship-matching-network/index.jsonl.gz
