Mathematical Modeling System Database (mathematical-modeling-system)

A mathematical modeling system turns numbers into a model: fit a line, press Fit, and read the prediction. Fitting (1, 2), (2, 4) and (3, 6) gives slope 2.0 and intercept 0.0. A model y = 2x + 1 at x = 3 gives 7. A population of 1000 growing by 3 percent for 2 years becomes 1060.9. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
