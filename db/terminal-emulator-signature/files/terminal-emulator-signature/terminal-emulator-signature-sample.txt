Terminal Emulator Database (terminal-emulator-signature)

A terminal emulator is a rule-based local demo: it reads set toy commands, runs the ones it knows, and shows the answers. The emulator runs 2 commands. echo hello gives hello. mul 3 4 gives 12. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
