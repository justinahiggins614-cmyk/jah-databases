Protein Folding Simulator Database (protein-folding-simulator)

A protein folding simulator is a rule-based local demo: it simulates set toy proteins, and counts the marks. Reference display only: no pathogen content, no toxin content, no gene-drive content, no sequence synthesis; design-study only with biosafety screening. Sample proteins only, no real patient data. The simulator reads 3 proteins. The protein alpha holds 225 marks. The largest holds 225 marks. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.
