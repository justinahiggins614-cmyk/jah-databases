  1. A Goal Written in Plain Words

    Record 1 of 24. simple. Feasibility: buildable-now. The design uses only web pages, forms, and counters that exist today; only the design work stands in the way.

    The goal tracker is a plain website where a person writes a goal in their own words, breaks it into small steps, and marks each step done, with a progress bar that shows how far along the goal is. A goal that is never written down is easy to forget and hard to measure, because nobody can tell whether it is done or not. Writing the goal in plain words fixes the finish line: the words say what done looks like, when it should happen, and what counts as finished. Without plain words, a goal drifts into a wish, and a wish has no steps, no date, and no moment when a person can honestly say it is done. This record states the design in plain sentences so a reader can judge it on its own merits, and its demo checks the arithmetic behind the design. The website form grows out of that habit, adding a progress bar, a reminder that is gentle and never nagging, and a record of finished goals a visitor can look back on. Writing a goal down in plain words is an old habit, from a line in a diary to a note pinned above a desk.
