1. Description

A fraction calculator adds, subtracts, multiplies and divides fractions, and simplifies every answer to lowest terms. Type two fractions, press a button, and read the answer with its steps. Everything runs in this browser; no data leaves the page.

Twenty-four full records, ordered simple to working to advanced, each written in human-readable sentences and paragraphs, each carrying a history, a design, a problem, a proposed solution, dependencies, a feasibility label with its reason, a verification path, a science proof, a math proof, a formal statement, an equation, a proof sketch, a runnable demo program whose output is reproduced on the page, and two named specialist AIs.

2. Math proof

1/2 + 1/3 is 5/6, and 3/4 - 1/4 is 1/2. 1/2 times 2/3 is 1/3, and 1/2 divided by 1/4 is 2. 6/8 simplifies to 3/4, and 10/15 in lowest terms is 2/3. The GCD of 12 and 18 is 6, and the LCM of 4 and 6 is 12. 1/2 + 1/3 + 1/6 is 1.

3. Science proof

Fractions work because a fraction is a part of a whole: adding needs a common bottom number, multiplying goes top by top and bottom by bottom, and dividing flips the second fraction and multiplies. Simplifying divides top and bottom by their greatest common divisor, so the answer is in lowest terms. That is why the calculator shows the common bottom and the simplification, not only the answer.
