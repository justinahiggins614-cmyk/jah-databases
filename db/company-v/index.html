Visa Inc. is an American multinational financial services company headquartered in San Francisco, California, operating one of the world's largest electronic payments networks. Visa does not issue cards or extend credit itself; it provides the network that connects merchants, banks, and cardholders, processing hundreds of billions of transactions a year across more than 200 countries and territories.
