History
Enterprises have always needed to know how they stand: the merchant counted the till at night, the mill owner counted the bales at week's end. As enterprises grew, counting became reporting — regular written summaries of sales, costs, people, and stock, each figure traceable to the record it came from, so that those who run the enterprise and those who own it can see the same truth at the same time. This database is built on that public practice of honest, traceable reporting.
