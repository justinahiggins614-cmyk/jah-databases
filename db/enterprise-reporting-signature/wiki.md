# The Enterprise Reporting Database

The Enterprise Reporting Database is the enterprise reporting record database in the Signature network. It holds 24 full enterprise reporting record records in the public edition and 24 in the Signature edition, in a teaching ladder of 8 simple, 8 compounded and 8 most complex records.

It expands the live database accessibility-barrier-reporting, which it links to and never rebuilds. The database carries the specification, models, test suites and verification records; it is not a running service.
