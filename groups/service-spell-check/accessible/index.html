The Spell Check Service

The Spell Check Service is a free spelling checker that runs entirely in the browser. It checks words against 96,088 English headwords taken from the live spelling archive, plus their regular forms. Its pair of databases holds 48 full records: 24 public records of spelling rules and 24 Signature records of original memory aids and drills, and it builds on the live Signature Spell Check Database.
