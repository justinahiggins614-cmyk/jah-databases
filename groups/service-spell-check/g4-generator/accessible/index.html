Spelling-drill generator

A spelling-drill generator, alone. Pick a seed and a count and it builds a drill from real archive headwords — some words spelled right, some with one classic mistake slipped in. The generator itself needs JavaScript; use the generator page to build a drill.

How it works

  1. Pick a seed — the same seed always builds the same drill.
  2. Words come from a 723-word sample of the 96,088 archive headwords.
  3. Mistakes are rule-based: a dropped letter, swapped letters, a doubled letter undone, or ie/ei mixed up.
  4. The answer key lists every right spelling; drills download as JSON.
