How it works

1. Pick a seed. The seed is a number. The same seed always builds the same drill, so a teacher can hand out seed 21 and everyone gets the same words.

2. Words come from the archive. The generator draws from a 723-word sample of the 96,088 archive headwords — real words, nothing invented.

3. Mistakes are rule-based. A misspelled word is the right word with one classic slip: a dropped letter, two letters swapped, a doubled letter undone, or ie/ei mixed up. The answer key lists every right spelling.

4. Nothing leaves the page. The drill is built in your browser. Download it as JSON to keep or print.

What it does not do: it does not grade handwriting, and it does not invent new words — every drill word is a real archive headword.
