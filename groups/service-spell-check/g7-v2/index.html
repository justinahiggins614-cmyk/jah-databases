The Spelling Bee World

The Bee Hall. Rounds run on the same rules as the 24 public records: doubling, silent e, i before e, plurals, and the twelve classic mix-ups. Every round word is a real archive headword.

Bee levels. Rookie (short everyday words) → Detective (doubles and silent letters) → Champion (the long traps: accommodation, embarrassment, occurrence, rhythm). The three practice packs above match the three levels.

Try a round right now: press Generate drill on the drill generator (Group 4) with seed 7 for a Rookie round, 42 for Detectives, 99 for Champions — the same seeds as the packs.

Ollie's rule of the day: i before e except after c — believe, field, piece; receive, deceive. Full record: The I Before E Rule.
