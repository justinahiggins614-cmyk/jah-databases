Ollie's Word Workshop

The themed second version of Spell Check. Same word list, same rules, as a workshop world: a spelling-bee hall, word games, and practice packs. The games need JavaScript; use the workshop page to play them.

Practice packs

  • Bee Rookies pack — 20 friendly words, seed 7.
  • Word Detectives pack — 20 trickier words, seed 42.
  • Champions pack — 20 hard words, seed 99.

Spelling Bee World

Rookie, Detective, and Champion levels, matching the three packs. Rounds use the same rules as the 24 public records.
