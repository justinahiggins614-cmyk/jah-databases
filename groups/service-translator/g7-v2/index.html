The world

Lola's Language Market is a sunny market square where every stall speaks a different language. Four stalls — Spanish, French, German, Italian — each with phrase cards, a phrasebook shelf, and a friendly stall-keeper. Visitors learn by saying hello, asking the way, and ordering food, one phrase at a time.
