Guacamole

JAH-RECIPE-000001

Appetizers, section Dips & Spreads. 10 minutes in total (10 preparation, 0 cooking), serves 6, rated Easy. Ingredients: 1 cup mashed avocados; 1/3 cup diced tomatoes; 1/3 cup shredded cheddar; 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder; 1 teaspoon salt; 4 tablespoons hot sauce; 24 for serving tortilla chip. Steps: 1. In a medium bowl, combine the 1 cup mashed avocados with the 1/3 cup diced tomatoes, 1/3 cup shredded cheddar. 2. Stir in the 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder, 1 teaspoon salt, 4 tablespoons hot sauce until well blended. Taste and adjust seasoning. 3. Cover and refrigerate at least 30 minutes so the flavors meld. 4. Serve chilled with 24 for serving tortilla chip.

Chicken Noodle Soup

JAH-RECIPE-000005

Soups & Salads, section Hearty Soups. 65 minutes in total (15 preparation, 50 cooking), serves 4, rated Hard. Ingredients: 2 tablespoons butter; 1 diced yellow onion; 2 cloves minced garlic; 2 diced carrots; 1 cup diced chicken breast; 2/3 cup red lentils; 4 cups beef broth; 1/4 teaspoon salt; 1/2 teaspoon black pepper; 1 teaspoon dried thyme; 2 tablespoons cilantro. Steps: 1. Heat the 2 tablespoons butter in a large pot over medium heat. Add the diced yellow onion, minced garlic, diced carrots and cook, stirring often, until softened, about 5 minutes. 2. Pour in the 4 cups beef broth and add the 1 cup diced chicken breast, 2/3 cup red lentils. Bring to a boil over high heat. 3. Reduce the heat to low, cover partially, and simmer 40 minutes, until everything is tender. 4. Remove any bay leaf. Season with the 1/4 teaspoon salt, 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, 1 teaspoon dried thyme, tasting and adjusting as you go. 5. Ladle into warm bowls, top with 2 tablespoons cilantro, and serve hot.

Caesar Salad

JAH-RECIPE-000008

Soups & Salads, section Green Salads. 15 minutes in total (15 preparation, 0 cooking), serves 4, rated Easy. Ingredients: 4 cups chopped romaine; 2/3 cup diced bell pepper; 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes; 3/4 cup diced cucumber; 3/4 cup chickpeas; 1 tablespoon olive oil; 2 tablespoons lemon juice; 1/4 teaspoon Dijon mustard; 1/4 teaspoon honey; 1/2 teaspoon salt. Steps: 1. Wash and dry the 4 cups chopped romaine and place in a large salad bowl. 2. Add the 2/3 cup diced bell pepper, 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes, 3/4 cup diced cucumber, 3/4 cup chickpeas. 3. Spaghetti Bolognese

JAH-RECIPE-000012

Mains, section Pasta & Noodles. 30 minutes in total (10 preparation, 20 cooking), serves 4, rated Hard. Ingredients: 16 ounces spaghetti; 1 1/2 cups Italian sausage; 2 1/2 cups marinara sauce; 3 tablespoons olive oil; 4 cloves minced garlic; 1/3 cup grated Parmesan. Steps: 1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the 16 ounces spaghetti until al dente; reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain. 2. Meanwhile, heat the 3 tablespoons olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the 4 cloves minced garlic and cook 1 minute. Add the 1 1/2 cups Italian sausage and cook until browned, breaking it up. 3. Stir in the 2 1/2 cups marinara sauce and simmer 8 minutes, loosening with pasta water as needed. 4. Toss the pasta with the sauce, top with 1/3 cup grated Parmesan, and serve immediately.

Banana Bread

Breads, section Quick Breads. 75 minutes in total (15 preparation, 60 cooking), serves 10, rated Easy. Ingredients: 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour; 1/2 teaspoon baking soda; 1/4 teaspoon salt; 1 cup sugar; 2 eggs; 1 1/2 cups mashed ripe bananas; 3/4 cup shredded zucchini. Steps: 1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan. 2. Whisk together the 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour, 1/2 teaspoon baking soda, and 1/4 teaspoon salt in a large bowl. 3. In another bowl, whisk the 1 cup sugar, 2 eggs, 1 1/2 cups mashed ripe bananas until smooth. 4. Pour wet into dry and stir just until combined — a few lumps are fine. Dinner Rolls

JAH-RECIPE-000021

Breads, section Yeast Breads. 145 minutes in total (30 preparation, 115 cooking), serves 12, rated Easy. Ingredients: 4 cups bread flour; 2 teaspoons active dry yeast; 1 cup warm water; 1 tablespoon sugar; 1 1/2 teaspoons salt; 3 tablespoons olive oil. Steps: 1. Stir the 2 teaspoons active dry yeast and 1 tablespoon sugar into the 1 cup warm water; let stand 5–10 minutes until foamy. 2. Add the 4 cups bread flour, 1 1/2 teaspoons salt, and 3 tablespoons olive oil. Knead 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. 3. Cover and rise in a warm spot about 1 hour, until doubled. 4. Punch down, shape as desired, and rise 30 minutes more. 5. Bake at 375°F for 25 minutes until deep golden and hollow-sounding when tapped. Cool on a rack.

Chocolate Chip Cookies

Desserts, section Cookies. 27 minutes in total (15 preparation, 12 cooking), serves 10, rated Hard. Ingredients: 3/4 cup butter, softened; 3/4 cup brown sugar; 1/4 cup granulated sugar; 2 eggs; 1 teaspoon vanilla extract; 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour; 1 teaspoon baking soda; 1 teaspoon salt; 3/4 cup raisins. Steps: 1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Line baking sheets with parchment. 2. Beat the 3/4 cup butter, softened with the 3/4 cup brown sugar and 1/4 cup granulated sugar until creamy, about 2 minutes. 3. Scoop rounded tablespoons 2 inches apart. Bake 12 minutes until edges are golden. Cool on the sheet 5 minutes.

Vanilla Cake

JAH-RECIPE-000023

Desserts, section Cakes. 48 minutes in total (20 preparation, 28 cooking), serves 12, rated Easy. Ingredients: 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour; 1 cup granulated sugar; 2 teaspoons baking powder; 1 teaspoon salt; 2 eggs; 3/4 cup milk; 1/2 cup vegetable oil; 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract; 1/2 cup mashed banana. Steps: 1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Grease and flour two 8-inch round pans. 2. Whisk the 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour, 1 cup granulated sugar, 2 teaspoons baking powder, and 1 teaspoon salt in a large bowl. 3. Add the 2 eggs, 3/4 cup milk, 1/2 cup vegetable oil, 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract and beat 2 minutes until smooth. Fold in the 1/2 cup mashed banana. 4. Divide between pans and bake 28 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. 5. Cool 10 minutes in pans, then turn out and cool completely before frosting.
