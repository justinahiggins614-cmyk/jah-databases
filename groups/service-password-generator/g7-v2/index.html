The world

Bruno's Key Chest is a cosy workshop with a wooden chest, a brass key on the wall, and jars of word dice. Every key is cut to order: long, random, and different from every other key in the chest.

Tools

Passwords here are made with the browser's own random source, never a predictable one. The strength meter counts bits: longer and more varied means stronger. Nothing you make leaves this page.
