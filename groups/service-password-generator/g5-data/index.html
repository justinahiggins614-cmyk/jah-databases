Avoiding Names, Dates, and Common Words

Public record — JAH-PWGN-000007

The Password Generator Service Database holds the full record for Avoiding Names, Dates, and Common Words. Names, dates, and common words are the first guesses any attacker tries, because they are the passwords people…

Names, dates, and common words are the first guesses any attacker tries, because they are the passwords people choose most. A password that holds a pet's name, a birthday, or a favourite team is weak no matter how long it is, because a dictionary attack tries those words first.
