Q: What is Porphyric Insecticides?

A: Publication AP76A, titled Porphyric Insecticides, held by Univ Illinois, invented by John A Juvik, filed 1989-01-13 and granted 1990-03-07, class A22. Its abstract reads: Insecticidal compositions.

Q: How many patent records are in the catalog?

A: 77,275 records in the Globally Rejustered Patent Catalog Database, all live. The first record is AP76A, Porphyric Insecticides.

Q: What is class A44?

A: A44 is Jewellery and haberdashery, in section A, Human necessities. In the first 200 catalog records, A44 is the most common class, with 45 records.

Q: Who holds the most patents in the first 200 records?

A: Pfizer and Ethicon Inc., with 6 records each in the first 200 catalog records.

Q: What are the five classification areas the catalog harvests?

A: H04L63 network security, H04L9 cryptography, G06F21 system security, H04W12 wireless network security, and H04K secret communication, as documented in the catalog readme. The catalog also carries earlier classes such as A22, A42 and A47.
