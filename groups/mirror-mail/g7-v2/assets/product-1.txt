Letter paper set
A set of plain letter sheets with a harbor-post border, for writing letters by hand.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the sheets on plain paper. Write the recipient's name at the top, then your letter, then your name at the bottom.

3D-print directions:
Print the sheets at A4. The border leaves room for a stamp in the corner.
