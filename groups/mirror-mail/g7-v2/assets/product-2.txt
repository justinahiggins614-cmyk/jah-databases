Envelope template
A folding envelope template that fits the letter paper set.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the template on thick paper, cut along the outline, and fold the four flaps in. Glue the side flaps and write the recipient's name on the front.

3D-print directions:
Print the template at A4. One envelope fits one folded letter sheet.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
