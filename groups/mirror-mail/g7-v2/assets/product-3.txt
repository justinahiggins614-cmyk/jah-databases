Stamp sheet
A sheet of harbor-post stamps to stick on envelopes, each one showing a small boat or a lantern.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the sheet on sticker paper or plain paper with glue. Cut the stamps out along the dotted lines and stick one on each envelope.

3D-print directions:
Print the sheet at A4. Twelve stamps fit on one sheet.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
