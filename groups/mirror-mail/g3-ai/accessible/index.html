Signature Mail helper

A mirror of the mail kind of website: an address book, a subject, and a message composed in the page.

Plainly stated: the helper is a demo. It answers from an archive sample of 20 records built from this mirror's own content. It is not the full engine and it does not check the live web. Online fact-checking is not live yet: it needs the network's hosted helper, which waits on the owner's Cloudflare sign-in.

Ask about

Is Rin in the address book?

Yes. Rin is in the mirror's sample address book, and Rin's role is baker. The address book holds eight sample names.

What makes a message ready?

A message is ready only when both parts hold: the recipient is in the address book, and the subject is 1 or more characters long. Rin with the subject Harvest fair is ready: Rin is in the book and Harvest fair has 12 characters.

Who is in the address book?

Eight sample names: Rin the baker, Mara the gardener, Theo the teacher, June the sailor, Ansel the carpenter, Wren the painter, Kofi the drummer, and Lena the librarian.

What are the two AIs?

The mirror carries two named helpers: The Signature Mail website AI, which discusses the mirror and helps you pick a recipient, and Signature Mail AI, an AI version of the mail itself, which takes a recipient and a subject in plain words and composes the message with you.

What this helper knows

This mirror has no database in the live manifest — mirrors are not manifest databases. The mirror's own data is its sample catalog and its nine sections, held in Group 5 (public-reference index) and Group 6 (Signature-made content).

The seven groups

  1. Group 1 — the full website — The full website with everything — this mirror, with a wiki section added.
  2. Group 2 — static links list — A static list of every link for this subject — plain text, no JavaScript.
  3. Group 3 — AI-only website — The helper alone: ask about this subject and get answers from the mirror's own records.
  4. Group 4 — generator alone — The generator alone: make new things from the mirror's own catalog, picked by seed.
  5. Group 5 — data alone — The public data alone: the mirror's public-reference index, in plain form.
  6. Group 6 — Signature data alone — The Signature data alone: the mirror's own Signature-made content, labeled Signature.
  7. Group 7 — version 2, themed — Version 2, themed: the themed world, working tools, and designs.
