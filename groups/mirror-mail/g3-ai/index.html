Is Rin in the address book?

Yes. Rin is in the mirror's sample address book, and Rin's role is baker. The address book holds eight sample names.

What makes a message ready?

A message is ready only when both parts hold: the recipient is in the address book, and the subject is 1 or more characters long. Rin with the subject Harvest fair is ready: Rin is in the book and Harvest fair has 12 characters.

Who is in the address book?

Eight sample names: Rin the baker, Mara the gardener, Theo the teacher, June the sailor, Ansel the carpenter, Wren the painter, Kofi the drummer, and Lena the librarian.

What are the two AIs?

The mirror carries two named helpers: The Signature Mail website AI, which discusses the mirror and helps you pick a recipient, and Signature Mail AI, an AI version of the mail itself, which takes a recipient and a subject in plain words and composes the message with you.

What this helper knows

The helper answers from an archive sample of 20 records built from this mirror's own content: its sample catalog, its nine sections, its two named helpers, and its demonstration. The sample is stored with this website as archive-sample.json.

This mirror has no database in the live manifest — mirrors are not manifest databases. The mirror's own data is its sample catalog and its nine sections, held in Group 5 (public-reference index) and Group 6 (Signature-made content).

What it cannot do: it cannot check the live web, it cannot reach any other database, and it never asks for a key.
