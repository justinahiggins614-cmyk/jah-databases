The Signature Computer Chip Maker and Archive — The Silicon Foundry — product lines (printable)

Chip pin-badge sheet (Signature)
A printable sheet of chip pin badges in the Silicon Foundry style, one per chip family, ready to cut out.
Making: print the printable file on card, cut along the outlines, and pin them to a board. The sixteen families come from the site’s own catalog file.

Wafer poster (Fun)
A poster of glowing silicon wafers on a conveyor belt under the brass lamp of the foundry.
Making: print the printable file and pin it up. The glowing wafers are drawings, not real silicon.

Circuit stencil (Signature)
A stencil of circuit lines for drawing a chip layout on paper.
Making: print the printable file on card, cut out the stencil shapes, lay the stencil on paper, and trace the lines with a pen.

All designs are free printables. Nothing here is for sale.
