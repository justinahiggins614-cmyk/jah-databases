Ask about

Sample questions, with their demo answers as written text:

Q: How many chip designs are in the archive?

A: 35,601 records in The Signature Computer Chip Maker and Archive Database, all live. The site’s own catalog file records the same 35,601 designs.

Q: What is the first chip design in the archive?

A: JAH-CHIP-000001, Signature VoltCore X1 — a CPU chip design, Historic era.

Q: What chip families are there?

A: Sixteen families: CPU, GPU, NPU, SOC, MCU, FPGA, MEMC, SENSOR, PMIC, QCTRL, PHOT, NEURO, DSP, MODEM, SEC, and CHIPLET. In the first 500 records, CPU, GPU, NPU, and SOC hold 32 designs each and the other twelve hold 31 each.

Q: What eras do the chip designs come from?

A: Three eras: Historic, Modern, and Projected. The first 24 designs hold 6 Historic, 13 Modern, and 5 Projected designs.

Q: What is the Signature edition of the archive?

A: The Signature Computer Chip Maker and Archive Database (Signature Edition), 20 records, live. It is a field guide to the archive: core definitions, five principles, a glossary, and a record on the archive-aware guide.
