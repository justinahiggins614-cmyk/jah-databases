Q: How many articles does JAH Wiki hold?

A: 1,470,175 articles: 144,545 spec-derived, 77,275 patent-derived, 1,127,335 word articles, and 121,020 dossier articles.

Q: What is a word article?

A: A word article resolves live from the Signature Dictionary index. It carries an ID of the form JAH-WIKI-W-<word>, for example JAH-WIKI-W-15-deoxy-16-hydroxyprostaglandins.

Q: Are spec-derived articles granted patents?

A: No. Spec-derived articles are draft specifications, ready to review and file — not granted patents. Only patent-derived articles describe issued or granted patents, and only where the public record says so.
