The world

JAH Wiki is the encyclopedia of the JAH system. It holds no dataset of its own: spec-derived articles read the Signature Spec Catalog, patent-derived articles read the Patent Catalog, subject articles read the Wiki Leaks subject files, and word articles resolve live from the Signature Dictionary index.

Product lines

Designs, printables, and demos — free, downloadable, usable on the page. Every product is labeled Signature or Fun. Nothing here is for sale.

Library card (Signature)

A printable library card for the Library of Everything, with room for a name and a favourite article.

Making: print the printable file on card, cut it out, write a name on it, and keep it with the books.

Article outline worksheet (Signature)

A printable worksheet for drafting an encyclopedia article: title, what it is, where it comes from, related words.

Making: print the printable file, pick a word from the archive sample, and fill in the outline in plain sentences.

Bookshelf poster (Fun)

A poster of the library shelves with the floating books, in the Library of Everything style.

Making: print the printable file and pin it up. The floating books are drawings, not a promise that books float.
