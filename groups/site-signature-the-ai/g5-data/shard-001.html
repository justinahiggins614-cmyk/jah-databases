Shorten

text-shorten

Trims long text down to a set number of words without cutting mid-thought — handy when a reply runs long. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: shorten(text, maxWords), firstSentence(text).

Count

text-count

Counts words, characters and sentences, and estimates reading time — the quick health check for any text. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: count(text) -> {chars, words, sentences, readingMinutes}.

Clean

text-clean

Tidies messy text: collapses extra spaces, strips invisible control characters, and can turn any title into a URL slug. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: clean(text), slugify(text).

Keywords

text-keywords

Pulls the most important words out of any text by frequency — a quick way to see what a passage is about. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: keywords(text, n).

Summarize

text-summarize

Boils text down to its first few sentences — a fast, honest extractive summary with no guessing. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: summarize(text, n).

Template Fill

text-template

Fills {{placeholders}} in a template with real values — the easy way to build prompts and messages. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: fill(template, vars).

Diff Words

text-diff

Compares two texts word by word and lists what was added and what was removed. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: diffWords(a, b) -> {added, removed}.

Wrap

text-wrap

Wraps text to a set line width without breaking words — for clean pre-formatted output. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: wrap(text, width).

Tone Shift

Expands contractions (don't → do not) to make casual writing more formal — a small, real tone adjustment. Category: Text & Words. Version: 1.0. Functions: expandContractions(text).

Bullets
