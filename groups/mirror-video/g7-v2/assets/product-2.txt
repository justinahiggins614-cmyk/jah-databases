Product design sheet — Flicker's Film Strip Cards

A strip of cards showing how a video is a row of still pictures, one after another, with sound beside them.

Label: Signature

Making:
Print the strip, cut it out, and fold it like a concertina. Each card is one still picture; flip through them fast to see the idea of motion.
