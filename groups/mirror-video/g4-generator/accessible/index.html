Signature Video — the generator (plain version)

Signature Video is a mirror of the video kind of website: it holds videos grouped by subject and by channel, so a viewer can find a video to watch and follow a channel for more. The mirror holds 12 catalog entries made for this mirror, and it builds on the network's public video databases.

How it works

  1. Type a seed word, or keep the one shown.
  2. Pick how many videos the lineup should hold.
  3. Press Make a lineup. The same seed always makes the same lineup, and a different seed makes a different one.
  4. Copy the lineup or download it as JSON.

It generates from the mirror's own 12 entries only. Every output is labeled generated and is never presented as an archive record.

Worked example

Worked example (seed "movie-night", 3 videos): How rain falls (Sky Watch, 3:30), How ships sail (Map Walk, 5:30), How rivers flow (Map Walk, 4:35) — total 13:35 (815 seconds).
