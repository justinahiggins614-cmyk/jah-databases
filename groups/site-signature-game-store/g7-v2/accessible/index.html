The themed world, in words

The Neon Arcade Row

A street of glowing cabinets. Each cabinet is one of the store's 13 games, and Pip the Pixel Fox keeps the high scores on a chalk wall.

The Concept Corner

A worktable where new game concepts are drawn. The generator on Group 4 picks a seed and sketches a concept in the style of a real game.

The Cozy Nook

A quiet corner with a shelf of the store's paper games: tickets, stickers and stand-up cabinets to print and build.

Tools

Tonight's game picker (a seed picks one of the store's 13 games) and a score keeper (adds points to a total, checking the numbers first). The tools run on the themed page. This plain page describes them and links to them.
