What games are in the catalog?

The mirror's own sample catalog holds 10 Signature-made games: Secret Number, Lantern Maze, Starfall Catch, Word Ladder, River Stones, Comet Dodge, Garden Match, Beacon Code, Harbor Checkers, and Cloud Hop.

What is the secret number?

The secret number is 42. With seed 7, the rule is secret = (7 x 6) mod 100 = 42. The same seed always gives the same secret.

How is the score worked out?

Score = 100 minus 10 for each try after the first, down to 0. A correct guess on the first try scores 100. Guessing 30, then 50, then 42 is 3 tries, so the score is 100 minus 20 = 80.

What are the two AIs?

The mirror carries two named helpers: The Signature Arcade website AI, which discusses the mirror and helps you pick a game, and Signature Arcade AI, an AI version of the games arcade itself, which plays the guessing game with you.

What this helper knows

The helper answers from an archive sample of 22 records built from this mirror's own content: its sample catalog, its nine sections, its two named helpers, and its demonstration. The sample is stored with this website as archive-sample.json.

This mirror has no database of its own in the live manifest — mirrors are not manifest databases. A related database exists and is not this subject: the JAH Arcade Game Database (db/arcade, 5,000 records, JAH-ARC-), with its Signature edition arcade-signature (20 records). The two are kept apart by the kind-prefixed keys: this subject is mirror-arcade; the database subject would be db-arcade.

What it cannot do: it cannot check the live web, it cannot reach any other database, and it never asks for a key.
