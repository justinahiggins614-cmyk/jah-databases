Starfall Catch card set
A set of 20 star cards and 5 slip cards for playing Starfall Catch away from the screen.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the 25 cards on card, cut them out, and shuffle them. Draw one card at a time: a star is caught, a slip card is a star slipping past. 20 stars caught before 5 slips wins.

3D-print directions:
Print the cards at 63 mm by 88 mm. The backs carry a small star outline.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
