Secret Number score pad
A score pad for the secret-number game, with the score rule printed on every sheet.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the pad on plain paper. After each game, write the tries and work out the score by the rule: 100 minus 10 for each try after the first, down to 0.

3D-print directions:
Print the pad at A5. One sheet holds 10 games.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
