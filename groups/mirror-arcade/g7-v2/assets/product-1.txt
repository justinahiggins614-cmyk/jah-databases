Lantern Maze board
A printed board for the Lantern Maze game: 12 rooms, a far gate, and a lantern token to move through them.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the board on thick card. Cut out the lantern token and fold its stand. Mark the far gate with a gold pen. Play by the maze rules in Section 4 of the mirror page.

3D-print directions:
Print the board at 200 mm square. The token stand is a 20 mm folded card base.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
