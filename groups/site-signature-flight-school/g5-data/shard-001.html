Skyhopper T-1 Trainer

t1

Prop trainer, skill Student. Speed 120, range 800, crew 2. Best for: First solo flights and landing practice.. The school's friendly first airplane. Slow, stable, and forgiving — it practically wants to fly straight. Every pilot starts here.

Cloudrunner C-2

c2

Prop trainer, skill Student. Speed 140, range 1100, crew 4. Best for: Cross-country navigation lessons.. A slightly faster trainer with room for the whole family. Great for cross-country student flights.

Albatross G-1 Glider

g1

Glider, skill Student. Speed 80, range 300, crew 1. Best for: Understanding lift, stalls, and gentle landings.. No engine — just wings and wind. Teaches the purest flying: reading the air, finding lift, landing softly.

Whirlybird H-4

h4

Helicopter, skill Intermediate. Speed 150, range 600, crew 4. Best for: Hover control and rooftop landings.. Hovers, spins, and lands on a dime. Trickier than airplanes — the stick does more jobs at once.

Dart Racer R-3

r3

Sport plane, skill Intermediate. Speed 220, range 900, crew 2. Best for: Aerobatics basics and formation flying.. A zippy little sport plane for pilots who have the basics down. Quick rolls, steep climbs, big grins.

Comet Cruiser C-9

c9

Business jet, skill Intermediate. Speed 480, range 3500, crew 8. Best for: High-altitude cruise and jet handling.. Fast, smooth, and high-flying. Your first taste of jet speed and flying above the weather.

Sunseeker A-6 Amphibian

a6

Seaplane, skill Intermediate. Speed 160, range 1200, crew 6. Best for: Water landings and adventure flying.. Lands on runways AND water. Double the landing practice, double the fun.

Thunderbolt J-7

j7

Fighter jet, skill Advanced. Speed 900, range 2500, crew 1. Best for: High-speed maneuvering and energy management.. Military-style speed and power. Demands respect — everything happens twice as fast up here.

Sky Giant G-800

g800

Jumbo airliner, skill Advanced. Speed 560, range 12000, crew 400. Best for: Heavy aircraft handling and ILS landings.. The queen of the skies. Heavy, graceful, and complex — landing this smoothly is a true pilot's badge.

Stormchaser S-5

s5

Fighter jet, skill Advanced. Speed 1050, range 3000, crew 2. Best for: Advanced aerobatics and supersonic cruise.. Twin-engine thunder built for the edge of the envelope. Only for pilots with cool heads.

Night Owl N-2

n2

Stealth jet, skill Ace. Speed 700, range 5000, crew 2. Best for: Precision night flying and systems management.. Flies like a shadow. Unstable by design — the computer helps, but the pilot must stay ahead of it.

Starhopper X-1 Rocket

x1

Rocket plane, skill Ace. Speed 1500, range 800, crew 1. Best for: The final exam: rocket climb and dead-stick landing.. A rocket with wings. Climbs like an elevator to the edge of space. The ultimate graduation flight.
