Q: What is the best flight path?

A: JAH-PATH-0001, Al Canyon Carve, in the Jungle Region, 8.8 km, skill Ace. Captain Siggy’s top pick: the perfect mix of beauty, challenge, and smooth flying — great in any aircraft. Its highlight: "City lights sparkle under the wings at night."

Q: How many flight paths are there?

A: 55 flight paths in the live index, matching the manifest. The school notes it is seeding 55 and marching to 1,000,000 — new paths are added over time, and the best flight path is always pinned on top.

Q: What is the first airplane?

A: The Skyhopper T-1 Trainer, a prop trainer for Student pilots: speed 120, range 800, crew 2. The school calls it the friendly first airplane — slow, stable, and forgiving.

Q: How many aircraft does the school have?

A: 12 aircraft: Skyhopper T-1 Trainer, Cloudrunner C-2, Albatross G-1 Glider, Whirlybird H-4, Dart Racer R-3, Comet Cruiser C-9, Sunseeker A-6 Amphibian, Thunderbolt J-7, Sky Giant G-800, Stormchaser S-5, Night Owl N-2, and Starhopper X-1 Rocket.

Q: What regions do the paths fly?

A: Ten regions: Arctic, Canyon, Coastal, Desert, Island, Jungle, Lakeside, Mountain, Prairie, and Volcano. The four skill levels are Student, Intermediate, Advanced, and Ace.
