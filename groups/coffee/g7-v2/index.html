Drinks menu — house fun versions

Each drink below starts from the archive's own words and ratio, then adds a house fun twist. The twist is play, not archive — the archive line is the record.

Brewster's Espresso

Espresso is pressure-extracted intensity: 18 g in, 36 g out, 25–30 seconds.

Archive ratio: 1:2 · JAH-COF-000001

House fun twist: served in a tiny cup with a bean on the saucer. — house fun version, free recipe card below.

The Cloud Cappuccino

Cappuccino is the classic thirds: dry foam cap, drinkable art.

Archive ratio: 1/3 espresso, 1/3 milk, 1/3 foam · JAH-COF-000003

House fun twist: a foam heart, drawn fresh each time. — house fun version, free recipe card below.

Velvet Latte

Latte is the milky canvas: espresso softened under steamed milk.

Archive ratio: 1:5+ espresso to milk · JAH-COF-000004

House fun twist: extra velvet milk, latte art on request. — house fun version, free recipe card below.

Flat White Corner

Flat white is the Australian intense latte: less foam, more coffee punch.

Archive ratio: Double ristretto + velvet milk · JAH-COF-000005

House fun twist: the corner table's favourite, double shot always. — house fun version, free recipe card below.

Mocha Hearth

Mocha is dessert coffee: espresso meets chocolate.

Archive ratio: Espresso + chocolate + milk · JAH-COF-000008

House fun twist: a square of dark chocolate on the side. — house fun version, free recipe card below.

Slow Cold Brew

Cold brew steeps coarse grounds cold for 12–24 hours: smooth, sweet, low acid.

Archive ratio: 1:8, 12–24 hours · JAH-COF-000009

House fun twist: brewed overnight in the big glass jar by the window. — house fun version, free recipe card below.
