Drinks menu — house fun versions

Each drink starts from the archive's own words and ratio, then adds a house fun twist. The twist is play, not archive.

  • Brewster's Espresso — Espresso is pressure-extracted intensity: 18 g in, 36 g out, 25–30 seconds. Archive ratio: 1:2. House fun twist: served in a tiny cup with a bean on the saucer.
  • The Cloud Cappuccino — Cappuccino is the classic thirds: dry foam cap, drinkable art. Archive ratio: 1/3 espresso, 1/3 milk, 1/3 foam. House fun twist: a foam heart, drawn fresh each time.
  • Velvet Latte — Latte is the milky canvas: espresso softened under steamed milk. Archive ratio: 1:5+ espresso to milk. House fun twist: extra velvet milk, latte art on request.
  • Flat White Corner — Flat white is the Australian intense latte: less foam, more coffee punch. Archive ratio: Double ristretto + velvet milk. House fun twist: the corner table's favourite, double shot always.
  • Mocha Hearth — Mocha is dessert coffee: espresso meets chocolate. Archive ratio: Espresso + chocolate + milk. House fun twist: a square of dark chocolate on the side.
  • Slow Cold Brew — Cold brew steeps coarse grounds cold for 12–24 hours: smooth, sweet, low acid. Archive ratio: 1:8, 12–24 hours. House fun twist: brewed overnight in the big glass jar by the window.

Bean bags — the product line

  • Brazil beans — nutty, chocolate, low acid. The everyday bag — smooth and kind.
  • Ethiopia beans — floral, bergamot, bright. The bright bag — the one that smells like flowers.
  • Colombia beans — caramel, red apple, balanced. The balanced bag — nothing out of place.
  • Kenya beans — blackcurrant, winey, bright. The bold bag — bright enough to wake the street.
  • Sumatra beans — earthy, dark chocolate, heavy. The heavy bag — thick, dark, slow mornings.
  • Guatemala beans — cocoa, spice, smoky. The fireside bag — cocoa and a whisper of smoke.
