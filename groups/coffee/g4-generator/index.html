Make blends

A seed can be any word or number — try "sunday", "42" or your name. Change one letter and every blend changes.

How it works

Pick a seed. Any word or number. The seed is the only input — everything else follows from it.
Press Generate blends. The page mixes two origins, picks a roast and a method, and works out the dose, ratio and water from the archive's figures.
Read the record. Each blend is a full record: an ID, the beans and their notes, the roast, the method, the grind, the ratio, the time and the steps.
Take it with you. Copy all the blends or download them as JSON. Run the same seed again any time — you will get the same blends back.

What it does not do: it does not look up archive records, it does not taste the coffee, and it never claims a generated blend already exists in any database.
