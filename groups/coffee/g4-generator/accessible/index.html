Coffee Blend Generator

Pick a seed and this page makes new coffee blend records — beans, roast, method, ratio and steps. The same seed always makes the same blends. Free forever. No sign-in, no payment.

Open the working generator (needs JavaScript)

Generated, not archive: every blend the generator makes is newly generated on the page. It is not an archive record and never pretends to be one. Methods, grinds, ratios and roast notes follow the JAH Coffee Guide Database; bean origins are the generator's own option list.

How it works

  1. Pick a seed — any word or number. The seed is the only input.
  2. Press Generate blends. The page mixes two origins, picks a roast and a method, and works out the dose, ratio and water from the archive's figures.
  3. Read the record — ID, beans and notes, roast, method, grind, ratio, time and steps.
  4. Take it with you — copy all the blends or download them as JSON. Run the same seed again any time and you will get the same blends back.

Worked example — seed "morning-cup", blend 1 (verified)

JAH-COFGEN-652851 — 70% Brazil + 30% Kenya — Medium roast

  • Beans: 70% Arabica Brazil (nutty, chocolate, low acid), 30% Arabica Kenya (blackcurrant, winey, bright)
  • Roast: Medium roast — balances origin and caramel: the crowd-pleaser
  • Method: AeroPress · 1:13 · fine-medium grind · 2 min
  • Steps: Grind 22 g fine-medium. AeroPress at 1:13 — 286 g water at 90–96 C. Brew 2 min.

The option lists

Origins: Brazil (nutty, chocolate, low acid), Ethiopia (floral, bergamot, bright), Colombia (caramel, red apple, balanced), Kenya (blackcurrant, winey, bright), Sumatra (earthy, dark chocolate, heavy), Guatemala (cocoa, spice, smoky).

Roasts: Light roast (keeps origin character: bright, tea-like), Medium roast (balances origin and caramel), Dark roast (roast flavour forward: smoky, bold, low acid).

Methods: Pour over (V60) 1:16, Chemex 1:16, French press 1:14, AeroPress 1:13, Espresso 1:2, Cold brew 1:8.
