How it answers

You ask. Type a coffee question in plain words.
The page asks the archive library. The question is handed to the network's archive library (core/jahdb.js) with the coffee database and the 50 archive records below as context — the corrected call, library first, question second.
You get an answer in plain sentences. If the library cannot be reached, the page falls back to a plain lookup over the same 50 records and says so.
Nothing leaves the page. No key, no sign-in, no account. The answer comes from the archive sample on this page.

What it cannot do: it cannot check the live web, it cannot see databases beyond the sample below, and it never invents an archive record — if the archive does not hold an answer, it says so.

Bean storage

Store beans airtight, cool, dark — never the freezer for daily use. Buy whole, grind fresh.

Water for coffee

Water is 98 percent of coffee: filtered, 90-96 C, not distilled.

Caffeine content

Caffeine: espresso shot ~63mg, drip cup ~95mg, cold brew ~200mg. Light roasts edge darker by scoop.
