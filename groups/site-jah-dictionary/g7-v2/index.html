The Signature Dictionary — The Word Garden

Welcome to the Word Garden — every English word defined two ways, growing like books from the soil.

Meet Dexter the Dictionary Fox, the mascot of this world.

The world

The Signature Dictionary defines every English word two ways: a plain definition and a deterministic one. The archive holds 1,127,335 entries, from 102,223 headwords plus catalog terms.

Tools

Two working tools, right here on the page. Each one says what it does and what it cannot do. Neither tool reaches the live web, and neither one needs a key.

Product lines

Designs, printables, and demos — free, downloadable, usable on the page. Every product is labeled Signature or Fun. Nothing here is for sale.

Alphabet poster (Signature)

A poster of the whole alphabet, with one word per letter taken from the archive sample.

Making: print the printable file and pin it up. The words on it are real archive words.

Word-card pack (Signature)

A pack of printable word cards: the word on the front, its part of speech and definitions on the back.

Making: print the printable file on card, cut out the cards, and quiz a friend — one card, one word, one definition.

Bookmark (Fun)

A bookmark with Dexter the Dictionary Fox reading under the tree, and the words "look it up" on the back.

Making: print the printable file on card, cut it out, and keep it in a book. The fox on it is a drawing, not a librarian.
