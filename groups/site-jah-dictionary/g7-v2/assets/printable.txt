Alphabet poster (Signature)
A poster of the whole alphabet, with one word per letter taken from the archive sample.
Making: print the printable file and pin it up. The words on it are real archive words.

Word-card pack (Signature)
A pack of printable word cards: the word on the front, its part of speech and definitions on the back.
Making: print the printable file on card, cut out the cards, and quiz a friend — one card, one word, one definition.

Bookmark (Fun)

Making: print the printable file on card, cut it out, and keep it in a book. The fox on it is a drawing, not a librarian.
