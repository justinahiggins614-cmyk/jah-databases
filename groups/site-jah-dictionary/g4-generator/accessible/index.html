The practice-entry generator drafts a practice dictionary entry from a real word in the archive, picked from a seed.

Every output here is generated (simulated) — made fresh by the generator from the subject’s real data. A generated output is never an archive record.

How it works

  1. You give a seed — any word or number.
  2. The generator picks from the subject’s real data using that seed, the same way every time.
  3. Same seed, same output. A different seed gives a different output.
