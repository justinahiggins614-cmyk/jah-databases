Product lines

Sticker sheet (Signature)

A printable sheet of sticker designs in the Pixel Studio style, ready to cut out.

Making: print the printable file on sticker paper, cut along the outlines, and stick them on a notebook or a laptop lid.

Pixel-art poster (Fun)

A poster of the pixel mural from the studio wall, made of big coloured squares.

Making: print the printable file and pin it up, or copy the squares onto graph paper and colour them in.

Tote-bag stencil (Signature)

A stencil for painting a pixel design onto a plain tote bag.

Making: print the printable file on card, cut out the stencil shapes, tape the stencil to the bag, and dab paint through the holes. Let it dry fully before use.
