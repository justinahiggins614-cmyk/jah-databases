How it works

  1. You give a seed — any word or number.
  2. The generator picks from the subject’s real data using that seed, the same way every time.
  3. Same seed, same output. A different seed gives a different output.
  4. Copy the output or download it as JSON. Nothing leaves your device.

What it cannot do: it cannot fetch the live archive, it cannot check the live web, and it cannot make a real archive record.
