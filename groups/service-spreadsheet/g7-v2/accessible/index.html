Sammy's Counting Garden

Sammy's Counting Garden — the themed second version: a garden where every row is a ledger and every acorn counts.

Sammy's Counting Garden is a garden where every row is a ledger. The plants grow in neat grid rows, the acorns are counted into baskets, and the watering can keeps the numbers growing. Change one number and every total that depends on it changes at once — that is the garden's one rule.
