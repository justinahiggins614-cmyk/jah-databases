The world

Sammy's Counting Garden is a garden where every row is a ledger. The plants grow in neat grid rows, the acorns are counted into baskets, and the watering can keeps the numbers growing. Change one number and every total that depends on it changes at once — that is the garden's one rule.

Sammy the squirrel runs the world. The tools below are the same working tools as the full website, and the product lines are designs you can print and use — never goods for sale.

Tools

The Tool

Your grid — five columns (A to E) and six rows. Type a number, a word, or a formula like =SUM(A1:A3).

Formulas: =SUM(A1:A3) adds a range, =AVERAGE(A1:A3) averages it, =MAX and =MIN find the largest and smallest, =COUNT counts the numbers. You can also write plain sums like =A1+B1*2.

Signature — A budget sheet built from the worked budget record: five things you need, three columns of amounts, and a totals row.
