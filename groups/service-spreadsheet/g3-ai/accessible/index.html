What to ask it

What is a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet is a grid of rows and columns where numbers and words are kept in cells, and where formulas can add the numbers up by themselves. Change one number and every total that depends on it changes at once. It is used for budgets, lists, and accounts, anywhere that numbers must be added, compared, and kept in order.

How do I add numbers with SUM?

SUM is the function that adds up every number in a range, written as =SUM(A1:A5). It takes the five cells A1 through A5 and returns their total in one step. A writer uses SUM for weekly spending, monthly totals, and any list where the total matters more than the single numbers.

What does AVERAGE do?

AVERAGE adds up the numbers in a range and divides by how many there are, written as =AVERAGE(A1:A5). It answers the question what is usual here, such as the usual weekly spend or the usual test score. An empty cell is skipped, and a cell holding a word is skipped too, so only real numbers count.
