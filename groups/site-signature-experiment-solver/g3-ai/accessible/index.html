Q: What categories do experiments cover?

Q: Was the experiment physically performed?

A: No. A solved experiment is a complete, structured, solver-generated record — question, hypothesis, variables, materials, procedure, a three-trial matrix, findings, results, conclusion. Solved does not mean physically performed: the computer ran its model of the experiment, and nobody went into a lab. Every record carries status SOLVER-GENERATED, simulation SIMULATED, physically performed NOT-PERFORMED, and measured data none.
