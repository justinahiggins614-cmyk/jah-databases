Bookmark set
A set of six bookmarks, one for each subject shelf: stories, travel, science, craft, poems, and maps.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the bookmarks on thick card and cut them out. Each one carries the name of its shelf and a line from the mirror's own catalog.

3D-print directions:
Print the bookmarks at 50 mm by 150 mm. A hole at the top takes a ribbon.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
