Reading journal
A plain reading journal: one page per book, with lines for the title, the subjects, the page count, and what you thought of it.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the journal on plain paper and bind the pages with thread or staples. Fill in one page for each book you finish from the mirror's catalog.

3D-print directions:
Print the journal at A5. One sheet holds two journal pages.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
