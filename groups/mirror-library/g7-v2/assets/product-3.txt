Bookplate sheet
A sheet of bookplates to paste inside the front cover of a book, marking it as part of your own shelf.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the bookplates on sticker paper or plain paper with glue. Write your name on the line and paste one inside each book you keep.

3D-print directions:
Print the bookplates at 90 mm by 60 mm. Eight fit on one sheet.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
