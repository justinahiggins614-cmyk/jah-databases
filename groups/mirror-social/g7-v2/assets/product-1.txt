Postcard set
A set of postcards, one for each sample post, with the post's words on the front and room for your own note on the back.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the postcards on thick card and cut them out. Write your own note on the back and hand one to a friend.

3D-print directions:
Print the postcards at 100 mm by 150 mm. Eight fit on two sheets.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
