Badge sheet
A sheet of badges for the feed: one badge for each sample name, with a small picture of what they posted about.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the badges on sticker paper or plain paper with glue. Cut them out along the circles and wear one, or stick one on your notebook.

3D-print directions:
Print the badges at 50 mm circles. Eight fit on one sheet.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
