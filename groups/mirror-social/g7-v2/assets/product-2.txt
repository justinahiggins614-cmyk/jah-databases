Feed board game
A board game for the whole feed: move your token along the green, score points for likes and comments, and reach the notice board first.

Signature design.

Making it:
Print the board on thick card. Each player picks a sample name as a token. Roll a die, move, and score by the mirror's rule: likes plus twice the comments.

3D-print directions:
Print the board at 400 mm square. The tokens are 20 mm discs.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
