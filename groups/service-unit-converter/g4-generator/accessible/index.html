A unit converter generator, alone. a conversion worksheet: seeded measurement questions with worked answers, from the real unit ratios The generator itself needs JavaScript; use the generator page to build a set.

How it works

  1. Pick a seed — the same seed always builds the same set.
  2. The worksheet draws from the 11 unit groups and their fixed ratios; every answer is worked from those ratios.
  3. Every output is labeled generated; nothing here is an archive record.
  4. Sets download as JSON.
