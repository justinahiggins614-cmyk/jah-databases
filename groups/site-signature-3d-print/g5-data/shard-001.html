3D Benchy

JAH-3DP-000001

The 3D Benchy is the world's most-printed 3D model and the standard torture test for FDM printers. Designed by Creative Tools, this little tugboat packs overhangs, bridging, curved surfaces, small details, and symmetry checks into one 60mm print. Every new printer, filament, or slicer profile gets validated against a Benchy: stringing shows in the cabin windows, overhang droop on the bow, elephant's foot at the hull base, and layer consistency along the hull. It is the universal benchmark print — if your Benchy is clean, your printer is dialed in. Designer: Creative Tools. License: CC BY. Group: calibration. Year: 2015. Parts: 1. Materials: PLA (standard); PETG for functional tests.

20mm XYZ Calibration Cube

JAH-3DP-000002

The 20mm XYZ calibration cube is the simplest dimensional-accuracy test in 3D printing. A hollow-free 20x20x20mm cube with embossed X, Y, and Z letters lets you verify each axis with calipers: every face should measure 20.00mm within 0.1mm. Skewed dimensions reveal belt tension problems, wrong steps-per-mm, or frame squareness issues. Print it in 20 minutes, measure all three axes, and adjust before committing to long prints. Every printer owner should keep a reference cube from each machine. Designer: Open community design. License: Open license (community). Group: calibration. Year: 2016. Parts: 1. Materials: PLA.

Cali Cat

JAH-3DP-000003

Cali Cat is a cute calibration cat that tests overhangs, bridging, stringing, and fine detail in one friendly 35mm print. Its tail is a steep overhang, the ears test small-feature cooling, and the whisker gaps reveal stringing. Faster and more fun than a cube, it is the go-to first print for dialing in a new filament: one cat per temperature step builds a visual temperature tower of quality. The model prints without supports and finishes in under an hour. Designer: Dezign. License: Open license (community). Group: calibration. Year: 2017. Parts: 1. Materials: PLA.

Temperature Tower

JAH-3DP-000004

A temperature tower prints the same test geometry at stepped temperatures (e.g., 220C down to 185C in 5C blocks) so you can read the optimal nozzle temperature for any filament off the finished print. Each block is labeled with its temperature; look for the cleanest bridging, sharpest overhangs, and least stringing. This single 2-hour print replaces days of trial and error and is the correct first step with every new spool. G-code post-processing scripts in most slicers automate the temperature changes. Designer: Open community design. License: Open license (community). Group: calibration. Year: 2016. Parts: 1. Materials: Any filament under test.

Retraction Test (Twin Towers)

JAH-3DP-000005

The twin-tower retraction test prints two thin pillars with a gap between them, forcing dozens of travel moves. Stringing, blobs, or wisps between the towers reveal retraction distance and speed problems. Tune retraction in 0.5mm steps until the gap is clean. This 15-minute print saves hours of post-processing on real models and is essential when switching between PLA, PETG (stringier), and TPU (minimal retraction). Designer: Open community design. License: Open license (community). Group: calibration. Year: 2017. Parts: 1. Materials: Filament under test.

Voron 2.4 Printed Parts Set

JAH-3DP-000006

The Voron 2.4 is a flagship open-source CoreXY 3D printer, and its entire printed-parts set — 87 components including the StealthBurner toolhead, TAP probe mount, gantry joints, and skirt pieces — is released under the GPL. These are functional engineering parts, not decorations: they must be printed in ABS or ASA with high infill for heat resistance inside the enclosed chamber (60C+ ambient). This set is the definitive advanced print project: 60+ hours of printing that ends with a 350mm^3 high-speed printer. The Voron project proves open hardware can outperform commercial machines. Designer: VoronDesign. License: GPL. Group: mechanical. Year: 2020. Parts: 87. Materials: ABS or ASA (enclosure required).

Lithophane Photo Panel

JAH-3DP-000013

Free lithophane makers convert any photo to a curved or flat STL in seconds. Print in white PLA at 0.1mm layers, 100% infill, standing vertical for best resolution. The result looks like magic — a blank white slab that reveals a portrait in window light. Popular for nightlights, ornaments, and memorial pieces. Designer: Open community technique.
