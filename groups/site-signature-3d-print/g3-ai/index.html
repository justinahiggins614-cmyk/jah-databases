Ask about

Sample questions, with their demo answers as written text:

Q: How many public print designs are there?

A: 62 designs in the Public 3D Print Archive Database, all live. The live index holds 62 rows, the same number as the manifest.

Q: What is the first design?

A: JAH-3DP-000001, "3D Benchy", by Creative Tools, from 2015, in the calibration group, license CC BY. It is the world’s most-printed 3D model and the standard test for printers.

Q: What groups do the designs fall into?

A: 9 groups: art, assistive, calibration, database-types, educational, household, mechanical, toys, and workshop.

Q: What is the Signature 3D Print Database?

A: 60 records with IDs beginning JAH-S3DP-, Signature originals by Justin Addam Higgins. The first is the Signature Hex Vault, a hex-cell storage vault.

Q: What is the Signature edition of the public archive?

A: public-3d-print-archive-signature, 20 records with IDs beginning JAH-3DPS2-. They are Signature originals: a field guide, five principles, a glossary, a teaching ladder, and method guides.
