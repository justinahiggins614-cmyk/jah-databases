Ask about — sample questions and their answers

Four questions the helper can answer, with the answers written out, so you can see what it knows before you type.

Q: What is a word processor?

A: A word processor is a program for writing and changing text on a computer. The writer types words, fixes mistakes, moves sentences around, and prints or saves the finished page. Unlike a typewriter, it lets the writer change any word at any time without retyping the whole page, and it keeps a count of the words as the writer goes.

Q: How do I make text bold?

A: Bold makes letters thick and dark, italic makes them lean to the right, and underline draws a line beneath them. A writer uses bold for words that must stand out, italic for titles and emphasis, and underline for headings or links. The three can be mixed, and each can be switched off again with the same button.

Q: What does the word count do?

A: The word count is the number of words in a document, counted live as the writer types. It tells the writer how long the text is, which matters for school work, letters, and books, where a length is often asked for. The count also counts characters and sentences, and it never counts the blank space between words.
