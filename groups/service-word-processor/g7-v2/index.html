The world

Penny's Writing Desk is a paper workshop where every page begins blank. Stacks of paper wait on the shelves, pencils stand in a jar, and the lamp over the desk is always on. Penny types at the big keyboard, and every document in the workshop starts the same way: one sentence, then another.

Penny the penguin runs the world. The tools below are the same working tools as the full website, and the product lines are designs you can print and use — never goods for sale.
