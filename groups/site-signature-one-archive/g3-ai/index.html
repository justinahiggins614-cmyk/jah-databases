Q: How many specs are in the catalog?

A: 908,995 records in the Signature Spec Catalog Pending Patents Database, all live. The live index holds 908,995 rows, the same number as the manifest.

Q: What is the first spec?

A: JAH-SPEC-000001, "API gateway for batch ETL using WebAssembly sandboxing", category APIs, in chunk specs-c00001.jsonl.gz.

Q: What categories do the first specs fall into?

A: 22 categories in the first 24 records, among them APIs, Wearables, Cameras, Lighting, Pumps, Valves, 3D Printers, and Cloud Infrastructure.

Q: What is a spec, exactly?

A: A spec is the author’s own draft specification, marked Draft and ready to file. It is not a granted patent, and it is never a reworded copy of another patent.

Q: What is the Signature edition?

A: specs-signature, 20 records with IDs beginning JAH-SPECS-. They are Signature originals: a field guide, five principles, a glossary, a teaching ladder, and method guides.
