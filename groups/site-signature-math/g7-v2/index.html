The themed world

Gridley's Geometry Garden is a geometry garden. Walk it in words:

  • The Triangle Hedge — hedges cut into triangles, each one a different size.
  • The Circle Lawn — a round lawn for sitting inside a circle and measuring it.
  • The Number Stones — stepping stones numbered 1 to 12, in order, across the garden.
  • The Compass Corner — a corner with a compass, a ruler, and a pencil, for drawing what the garden shows.

Tools

Three working tools, plain JavaScript, no backend, no key.

Change a number's base

Enter a whole number and pick a base. The tool cannot do fractions, negatives, or bases other than 2, 8 and 16.

Triangle area

Enter a base and a height. The area is base times height, halved.

Quiz — which group?

A quiz drawn from the site's own 32 conversion records. It is a game, not a record.

Labeled Signature. A poster of the geometry garden: triangle hedges, circle lawns, and Gridley on the number stones.

The Conversion Card Set

Labeled Signature. A set of 32 printable cards, one for each conversion record in the site's own data file, with the record's name and group on each card.

Making: Print the cards on stiff card and cut along the lines. Draw a card and say what the conversion does before turning it over. A 3D-print version: print a card box, 100 mm by 70 mm, with a lid.

The Grid Notebook

Labeled Fun. A notebook ruled with 12 by 12 grids, for drawing your own Signature grids. Labeled fun: it is a notebook design, not a record.

Making: Print the grid pages and bind them with staples or rings. Draw one grid per page and mark the cells that hold geometry.

Fun stuff

Fun, labeled fun — not a fact. Gridley's riddle, made up for the garden: I have three sides and three corners, and my corners always add up to the same number. What am I? Answer: a triangle — its three corners add up to 180 degrees.
