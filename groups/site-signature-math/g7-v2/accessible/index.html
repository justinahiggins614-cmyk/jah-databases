The themed world

Gridley's Geometry Garden is a geometry garden.

  • The Triangle Hedge — hedges cut into triangles, each one a different size.
  • The Circle Lawn — a round lawn for sitting inside a circle and measuring it.
  • The Number Stones — stepping stones numbered 1 to 12, in order, across the garden.
  • The Compass Corner — a corner with a compass, a ruler, and a pencil, for drawing what the garden shows.

Tools

The tools on the main page work in the browser: a base changer, a triangle area tool, and a group quiz.
