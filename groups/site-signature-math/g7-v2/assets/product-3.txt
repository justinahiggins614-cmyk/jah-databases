Product design sheet — The Grid Notebook

A notebook ruled with 12 by 12 grids, for drawing your own Signature grids. Labeled fun: it is a notebook design, not a record.

Label: Fun

Making:
Print the grid pages and bind them with staples or rings. Draw one grid per page and mark the cells that hold geometry.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
