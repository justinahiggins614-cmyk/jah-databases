Product design sheet — The Conversion Card Set

A set of 32 printable cards, one for each conversion record in the site's own data file, with the record's name and group on each card.

Label: Signature

Making:
Print the cards on stiff card and cut along the lines. Draw a card and say what the conversion does before turning it over. A 3D-print version: print a card box, 100 mm by 70 mm, with a lid.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
