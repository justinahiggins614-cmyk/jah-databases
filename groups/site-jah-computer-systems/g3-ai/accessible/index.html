Q: How many records does the depository hold?

A: 23,044 records in The Signature PC System Depository Database, all live: 109 public seed systems, 135 Signature originals, and 22,800 generated models.

Q: What is the oldest system in the depository?

A: JAH-PC-90000001, the Abacus, c. 2700–2300 BCE. Its description reads: Rows of beads on rods are slid by hand to add, subtract, multiply, and divide.

Q: What is the Signature edition of the depository?

A: The Signature PC System Depository Database (Signature Edition), 20 records, live, IDs JAH-PCS2-000001 to JAH-PCS2-000020. It is a field guide to the depository: core definitions, five principles, a glossary, and the teaching ladder.

Q: How does the on-demand generator work?

A: GEN/2.0 is a mixed-radix lattice over architecture, material, ALU width, registers, cores, era, and form factor. A model number from 1 to 1,000,000 decodes to one generated model, the same number always giving the same model.

Q: What is a generated model?

A: A deterministically generated possible model from the combinatorial space. It is not evidence the machine exists or was built. Generated models carry IDs from JAH-PC-00000001 (on-demand) or JAH-PC-80000001 and up (drip records).
