Sample questions, with their demo answers as written text:

Q: What is Signature Space Mapping?

A: Signature Space Mapping turns room photos, video, or live camera into top-down maps with real measurements — plus robot packs future robots can navigate with. That is the site’s own front-door description.

Q: How many maps are there?

A: Three counts, all stated. The manifest lists 13,700 records for the Signature Space Mapping Database. The archive map file holds 11,500 room maps. The site’s own map file holds 175,500 maps. The three counts come from three different files and do not agree; none was edited.

Q: What is the first room map?

A: JAH-MAP-000001, "West One-Bedroom 3": a one-bedroom sample layout with a living room, a bedroom, a kitchen, and a bath. Its rooms carry real measurements in feet.

Q: What is the Maps Database?

A: The Maps Database holds 55 records, IDs beginning JAH-MAP, kept by the JAH Maps Guide. Its first record is "Scale Bars (overview)".

Q: What is a robot pack?

A: A robot pack is a pack of maps and measurements that future robots can navigate with — stated on the site’s own front door. Robot track mode watches through your camera; it is not connected to any vacuum’s sensors and integrates no vendor program.
