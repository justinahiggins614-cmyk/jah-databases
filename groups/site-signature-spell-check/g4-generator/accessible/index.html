The Signature Spell Check — the generator (plain version)

The Signature Spell Check checks a word, checks a whole document, and learns the writer's own patterns. Its word archive holds 99,227 words from A to Z, and its Signature field guide holds 20 records on the rules of spelling.

How it works

  1. Type a seed word, or keep the one shown.
  2. Pick how many words the drill should hold.
  3. Press Make a drill. The same seed always makes the same drill.
  4. Spell the words, fix the misspellings, then copy the drill or download it as JSON.

It generates from the first 200 words of the A list and a short list of common misspellings. Every output is labeled generated and is never presented as an archive record.

Worked example

Worked example (seed "first-word", 5 words): abearance, abhorrent, abbreviated, abdication, abhominal; fix these: wierd becomes weird; goverment becomes government; enviroment becomes environment.
