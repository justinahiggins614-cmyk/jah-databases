Product design sheet — Wren's Word Garden Flash Cards

A set of printable flash cards from the first 200 words of the A list, with the word on the front and its letter on the back.

Label: Signature

Making:
Print the cards on stiff card and cut along the lines. Read the word aloud, then spell it letter by letter before turning the card over.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
