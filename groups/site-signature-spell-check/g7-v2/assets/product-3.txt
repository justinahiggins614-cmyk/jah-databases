Product design sheet — The Letter Stone Set

A set of stepping stones lettered A to Z, for the garden path. Labeled fun: it is a garden design, not a record.

Label: Fun

Making:
Cut the letters from paper and lay them on the floor as a path, or paint them on flat stones for a real garden path. A 3D-print version: print a stone 150 mm across with a raised letter.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
