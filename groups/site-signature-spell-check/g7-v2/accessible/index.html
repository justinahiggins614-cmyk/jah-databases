The themed world

Wren's Word Garden is a word garden.

  • The Letter Hedges — hedges cut into the letters W, O, R and D.
  • The Quill Path — stepping stones lettered A, B, C and D, in order, across the garden.
  • The Word Pond — a pond where words float on leaves, one word per leaf.
  • The Reading Nook — a quiet corner with a bench and a stack of word cards.

Tools

The tools on the main page work in the browser: a word checker over the shipped sample, and a missing-letter quiz.
