The themed world

Wren's Word Garden is a word garden. Walk it in words:

  • The Letter Hedges — hedges cut into the letters W, O, R and D.
  • The Quill Path — stepping stones lettered A, B, C and D, in order, across the garden.
  • The Word Pond — a pond where words float on leaves, one word per leaf.
  • The Reading Nook — a quiet corner with a bench and a stack of word cards.

Tools

Two working tools, plain JavaScript, no backend, no key. The checker works on the shipped sample of the first 200 words of the A list only — it does not hold the whole 99,227-word archive.

Check one word

Type a word. The tool says whether it is in the shipped sample, whether it is a common misspelling with a known fix, or whether it is not in the sample.

Quiz — fill the missing letter

A quiz drawn from the first 200 words of the A list. It is a game, not a record.

Product lines

Three design lines. Every one is labeled Signature or Fun, and none is for sale: designs, printables, and demos only.

Wren's Word Garden Flash Cards

Labeled Signature. A set of printable flash cards from the first 200 words of the A list, with the word on the front and its letter on the back.

Making: Print the cards on stiff card and cut along the lines. Read the word aloud, then spell it letter by letter before turning the card over.

The Quill Pen Guide

Labeled Signature. A guide to holding a quill and forming letters, written for Wren's garden, with practice rows for A, B, C and D.

Making: Print the guide and work through one row a day. Use any pen; the quill in the picture is a drawing, not a tool you must own.

Download the design sheet

The Letter Stone Set

Labeled Fun. A set of stepping stones lettered A to Z, for the garden path. Labeled fun: it is a garden design, not a record.

Making: Cut the letters from paper and lay them on the floor as a path, or paint them on flat stones for a real garden path. A 3D-print version: print a stone 150 mm across with a raised letter.

Fun stuff

Fun, labeled fun — not a fact. Wren's tongue-twister, made up for the garden: Wren wrote the wrong word on the wrong wall, / then wrote it right, and read it all. Say it three times fast, then check every word against the word list.
