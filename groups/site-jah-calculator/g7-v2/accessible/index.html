Addy's Answer Arcade — the themed second version: an arcade where every machine is a calculator and every game ends in an answer.

Addy the ant is the mascot.

The themed world

Addy's Answer Arcade is an answer arcade.

  • The Sum Machines — a row of machines shaped like calculators, each one for a kind of sum.
  • The Solver's Booth — a booth where equations are solved step by step, with the check at the end.
  • The Check Desk — a desk for putting every answer back into its equation to prove it.
  • The Arcade Awning — a striped awning over the whole arcade, with Addy the ant at the door.

Tools

The tools on the main page work in the browser: a sum tool, a linear equation solver, and a solve-it quiz.

Fun stuff

Fun, labeled fun — not a fact. Addy's lucky sums, a game for the arcade: pick any number, double it, add 6, halve it, and take away the number you first thought of. The answer is always 3. Try it: with 7, double is 14, add 6 is 20, halve is 10, take away 7 is 3.
