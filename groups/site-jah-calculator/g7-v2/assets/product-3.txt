Product design sheet — The Solver's Notebook

A notebook for working sums by hand, with ruled pages for steps and a box for the check. Labeled fun: it is a notebook design, not a record.

Label: Fun

Making:
Print the pages and bind them with staples or rings. Write each step on its own line, then put the answer back into the equation to check it.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
