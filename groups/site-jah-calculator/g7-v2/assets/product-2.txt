Product design sheet — The Equation Card Deck

A deck of 20 printable cards, one for each of the first 20 solved equations in the archive, with the steps, the answer and the check on each card.

Label: Signature

Making:
Print the cards on stiff card and cut along the lines. Draw a card, cover the answer, work the steps, and check your answer against the card.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
