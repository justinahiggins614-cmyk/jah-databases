Product design sheet — The Answer Arcade Token Set

A set of printable tokens for the answer arcade, one for each kind of sum: adding, taking away, multiplying, dividing, and solving.

Label: Signature

Making:
Print the tokens on stiff card and cut them out. Spend a token each time the arcade solves a sum for you. A 3D-print version: print a token 30 mm across and 3 mm thick.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
