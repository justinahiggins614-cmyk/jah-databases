The themed world

Addy's Answer Arcade is an answer arcade. Walk it in words:

  • The Sum Machines — a row of machines shaped like calculators, each one for a kind of sum.
  • The Solver's Booth — a booth where equations are solved step by step, with the check at the end.
  • The Check Desk — a desk for putting every answer back into its equation to prove it.
  • The Arcade Awning — a striped awning over the whole arcade, with Addy the ant at the door.

Tools

Two working tools, plain JavaScript, no backend, no key.

Work out a sum

Type a sum with digits and + - * / ( ). The tool reads only those marks; anything else is refused.

Solve a linear equation

Enter a and b for ax + b = 0. The tool cannot solve it when a is 0.

Quiz — solve it

A quiz of linear equations with whole-number answers. It is a game, not a record.

Product lines

Three design lines. Every one is labeled Signature or Fun, and none is for sale: designs, printables, and demos only.

The Answer Arcade Token Set

Labeled Signature. A set of printable tokens for the answer arcade, one for each kind of sum: adding, taking away, multiplying, dividing, and solving.

Making: Print the tokens on stiff card and cut them out. Spend a token each time the arcade solves a sum for you. A 3D-print version: print a token 30 mm across and 3 mm thick.

The Equation Card Deck

Labeled Signature. A deck of 20 printable cards, one for each of the first 20 solved equations in the archive, with the steps, the answer and the check on each card.

Making: Print the cards on stiff card and cut along the lines. Draw a card, cover the answer, work the steps, and check your answer against the card.

Download the design sheet

The Solver's Notebook

Labeled Fun. A notebook for working sums by hand, with ruled pages for steps and a box for the check. Labeled fun: it is a notebook design, not a record.

Making: Print the pages and bind them with staples or rings. Write each step on its own line, then put the answer back into the equation to check it.

Download the design sheet

Fun stuff

Fun, labeled fun — not a fact. Addy's lucky sums, a game for the arcade: pick any number, double it, add 6, halve it, and take away the number you first thought of. The answer is always 3. Try it: with 7, double is 14, add 6 is 20, halve is 10, take away 7 is 3.
