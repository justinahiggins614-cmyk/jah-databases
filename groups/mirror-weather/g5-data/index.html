The feels-like rule

ID: weather-rule-feels-like

Feels-like equals temperature minus wind divided by 2. Worked example: Silver Bay, 18 degrees and wind 6, feels like 15 degrees.

The temperature-band rule

ID: weather-rule-band

25 degrees or more is warm; 15 to 24 degrees is mild; 5 to 14 degrees is cool; below 5 degrees is cold.
