The themed world

Four-Seasons Hill is a green hill where all four seasons sit side by side. The warm garden holds Sunfield and Willow Creek; the cold garden holds Frost Hollow; and Willa the wren walks between them with her yellow umbrella.

Tools — Feels-like checker

This tool works out the demonstration forecast from the mirror's own sample table: feels-like equals temperature minus wind divided by 2. It cannot give a real forecast; every condition is a sample condition.

Product lines

Designs, printables, and demos only — free, downloadable, usable on this page. Nothing here is for sale.

Forecast cards

Signature line. Printable forecast cards: one card per sample place, with its condition, temperature, wind, band, and feels-like temperature.

Making: Print the cards from the download below; each card shows the working, not just the answer.

Season garden signs

Signature line. Signs for the four season gardens — warm, mild, cool, cold — each naming the sample places that belong to it.

Making: Print the signs and place one in each garden bed.

Willa's umbrella

Fun line. A fun paper umbrella in Willa's yellow, with the feels-like rule printed on the handle: feels-like equals temperature minus wind divided by two.

Making: Fold it from the printable sheet; it is a toy, not a real umbrella.
