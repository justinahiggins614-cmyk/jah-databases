The themed world, in words

Four-Seasons Hill is a green hill where all four seasons sit side by side. The warm garden holds Sunfield and Willow Creek; the cold garden holds Frost Hollow; and Willa the wren walks between them with her yellow umbrella.

Tools

Feels-like checker — Pick a sample place and see its temperature, its band, and its feels-like temperature, worked out step by step. It runs in the browser with no backend and no key.

Product lines

  • Forecast cards (Signature line): Printable forecast cards: one card per sample place, with its condition, temperature, wind, band, and feels-like temperature.
  • Season garden signs (Signature line): Signs for the four season gardens — warm, mild, cool, cold — each naming the sample places that belong to it.
  • Willa's umbrella (Fun line): A fun paper umbrella in Willa's yellow, with the feels-like rule printed on the handle: feels-like equals temperature minus wind divided by two.
