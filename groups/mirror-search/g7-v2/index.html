A search mirror: it takes a question in plain words and returns the network's own websites, ranked best first. Free forever — no sign-in, no payment

The themed world

Search Trail Town is a little town where every door is a website. Sable the fox walks the trails with her magnifying glass, and every signpost shows the way to the next door. The town square holds the score board, and the library of doors never closes.

Tools — Score checker

This tool scores the mirror's own data with the mirror's own rule: 3 points for a word in a name or title, 1 point for a word in keywords. It cannot search the outside web; it only scores what is already on this page.

Product lines

Designs, printables, and demos only — free, downloadable, usable on this page. Nothing here is for sale.

Trail cards

Signature line. A printable set of search-trail cards: one query per card with its top result and score. Print them, cut them out, and lay a trail across a table.

Making: Print the cards from the download below; each card carries its query, its top result, and its score.

Score board

Signature line. A wall board that shows the scoring rule as a picture: 3 points for a word in a name, 1 point for a word in keywords, with worked examples from the mirror.

Making: Build it from the printable sheet: fold the board, pin the worked examples beside it.

Door signs

Fun line. Little door signs for the town: one sign per website door, each with the website's name and keywords. A fun way to label a shelf or a notebook.

Making: Print the signs, cut them out, and stick one on each door of your own little town.
