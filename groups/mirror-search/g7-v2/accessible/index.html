The themed world, in words

Search Trail Town is a little town where every door is a website. Sable the fox walks the trails with her magnifying glass, and every signpost shows the way to the next door. The town square holds the score board, and the library of doors never closes.

Tools

Score checker — Type a query and see every matching entry with its score, worked out by the mirror's own rule. It runs in the browser with no backend and no key.

Product lines

  • Trail cards (Signature line): A printable set of search-trail cards: one query per card with its top result and score. Print them, cut them out, and lay a trail across a table.
  • Score board (Signature line): A wall board that shows the scoring rule as a picture: 3 points for a word in a name, 1 point for a word in keywords, with worked examples from the mirror.
  • Door signs (Fun line): Little door signs for the town: one sign per website door, each with the website's name and keywords. A fun way to label a shelf or a notebook.

Fun stuff

Entertainment and fun — never fact.

  • Sable's favourite query is 'songs' — it always leads her to the music door first.
  • The town's shortest trail is one word long: ask for 'maps' and the map door opens.
