Q: How many places does the site itself hold?

A: 69,763 real places, counted in the site’s own data file (data/index/stats.json, built 2026-10-04). The 17,300 archive addresses and the 69,763 site places are two different counts from two different files, and both are stated here.

Q: Where does the place data come from?

A: GeoNames cities5000 plus countryInfo, under CC-BY 4.0 — the source named in the site’s own data file. The site’s imagery is NASA Blue Marble, public domain, transformed by the Signature-binary transform.
