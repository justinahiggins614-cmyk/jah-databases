Signature Tunes — the generator (plain version)

Signature Tunes is a mirror of the music-streaming kind of website: it plays songs and albums on demand from its own sample catalog, with a search by mood and a playlist builder. The mirror holds 12 Signature-made tracks, and it builds on the network's public music databases.

How it works

  1. Type a seed word, or keep the one shown.
  2. Pick how many tracks the playlist should hold.
  3. Press Make a playlist. The same seed always makes the same playlist, and a different seed makes a different one.
  4. Copy the playlist or download it as JSON.

It generates from the mirror's own 12 tracks only. Every output is labeled generated and is never presented as an archive record.

Worked example

Worked example (seed "morning-cup", 3 tracks): Sunny Day Chorus (2:48), Quiet River Song (3:21), Festival Drum Dance (3:12) — total 9:21 (561 seconds).
