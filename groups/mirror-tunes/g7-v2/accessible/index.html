Melody the mockingbird is the mascot.

The themed world

Melody's Listening Garden is a listening garden.

  • The Gate — where Melody sings and every visitor is greeted by a tune.
  • The Note-Flower Beds — flowers shaped like notes, one bed for each mood: calm, bright, evening, lively.
  • The Listening Lawn — a wide lawn for sitting and listening to a whole playlist through.
  • The Playlist Pond — still water that holds the total play time of the day's playlist.

Tools

The tools on the main page work in the browser: a mood playlist builder, a play-time adder, and a mood quiz.
