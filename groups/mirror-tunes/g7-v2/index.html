The themed world

Melody's Listening Garden is a listening garden. Walk it in words:

  • The Gate — where Melody sings and every visitor is greeted by a tune.
  • The Note-Flower Beds — flowers shaped like notes, one bed for each mood: calm, bright, evening, lively.
  • The Listening Lawn — a wide lawn for sitting and listening to a whole playlist through.
  • The Playlist Pond — still water that holds the total play time of the day's playlist.

Tools

Two working tools, plain JavaScript, no backend, no key. They work on the mirror's own 12 tracks only.

Build a playlist by mood

Type a mood word — calm, bright, evening, lively, gentle, quiet, soft or fast — and the builder lists every track that matches, with the total play time.

Add up play times

Enter track lengths in seconds, separated by commas, and the adder gives the total as minutes and seconds.

Quiz — which mood fits?

A quiz drawn from the sample catalog. It is a game, not a record.

Product lines

Three design lines. Every one is labeled Signature or Fun, and none is for sale: designs, printables, and demos only.

Melody's Song Card Set

Labeled Signature. A set of 12 printable cards, one for each track in the sample catalog, with the track's mood words and length on each card.

Making: Print the cards on stiff card. Cut along the lines. Shuffle the deck and deal three cards to make a playlist, then add the lengths. A 3D-print version: print a small card stand, 90 mm wide, with a 2 mm slot, from the stand sheet.

The Listening Garden Poster

Labeled Signature. A poster of the listening garden: the gate, the note-flowers, and Melody on the branch.

Making: Print the poster at A3 on heavy paper. Frame it, or pin it to a board. The poster is a picture only; the garden itself is a place in words on this page.

The Playlist Wheel

Labeled Fun. A spinning wheel that picks a mood word for the next playlist. Labeled fun: it is a game, not a record.

Making: Print the wheel and the arrow on stiff card. Cut both out, pin the arrow through the centre, and spin. Whatever mood it lands on, build a playlist from that mood word.
