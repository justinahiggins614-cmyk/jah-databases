Product design sheet — Melody's Song Card Set

A set of 12 printable cards, one for each track in the sample catalog, with the track's mood words and length on each card.

Label: Signature

Making:
Print the cards on stiff card. Cut along the lines. Shuffle the deck and deal three cards to make a playlist, then add the lengths. A 3D-print version: print a small card stand, 90 mm wide, with a 2 mm slot, from the stand sheet.
