Product design sheet — The Playlist Wheel

A spinning wheel that picks a mood word for the next playlist. Labeled fun: it is a game, not a record.

Label: Fun

Making:
Print the wheel and the arrow on stiff card. Cut both out, pin the arrow through the centre, and spin. Whatever mood it lands on, build a playlist from that mood word.

This is a design, a printable, and a demo. It is free, downloadable, and usable on this page. It is not a good for sale: nothing to order, nothing to pay, no checkout.
