Lantern making sheet
A making sheet for a paper lantern in the shape of the Signature Lantern.

Signature design.

Making it:
Cut the paper by the sheet, fold the panels, and glue the edges. Place a small battery light inside, never a flame.

3D-print directions:
Cut the paper at 200 mm by 300 mm. The finished lantern is 120 mm tall.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
