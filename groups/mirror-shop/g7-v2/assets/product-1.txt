Market tote design
A design sheet for a market tote: a strong cloth bag with a wide opening and a long handle.

Signature design.

Making it:
Cut the cloth by the sheet, sew the side seams, fold the top hem, and sew the handle on. The tote holds the goods from the mirror's catalog.

3D-print directions:
Cut the cloth at 400 mm by 450 mm. The handle is 600 mm long.
