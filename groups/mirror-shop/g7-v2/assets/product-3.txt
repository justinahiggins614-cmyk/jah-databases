Mug design
A design sheet for a mug in the shape of the Signature Mug, with a handle and a wide rim.

Signature design.

Making it:
Shape the clay by the sheet, attach the handle, and let it dry before firing. Glaze the inside so it holds hot drinks.

3D-print directions:
Shape the mug 90 mm tall and 80 mm wide. The handle is a 20 mm loop.

Label: Signature design — a design, not a good for sale. Free to download and make.
