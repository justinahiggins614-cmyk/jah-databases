The world, in words

Signature Shop is a mirror of the shopping kind of website: a website where a visitor browses goods, reads prices, and builds a cart. Shopping websites began as plain lists of goods with prices, then grew catalogs you could search, carts that kept a running total, and checkout pages. This mirror follows the same shape — a catalog, a cart, and a total — rebuilt with Signature branding and the network's own data.

Fun stuff

Fun, labeled fun — not a fact. The market-day tales are made-up stories from the stall: Bramble the badger once traded a blanket for a song, the lanterns stayed lit through a whole rainy market day, and the clock on the stall has never once agreed with the harbor bell. None of this happened; it is a story for market day.

Bramble the badger is the mascot of this themed version. Questions about the subject go to Group 3.
