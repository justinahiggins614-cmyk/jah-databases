Signature Shop helper

A mirror of the shopping kind of website: a catalog, a basket, and a total worked out in the page.

Plainly stated: the helper is a demo. It answers from an archive sample of 22 records built from this mirror's own content. It is not the full engine and it does not check the live web. Online fact-checking is not live yet: it needs the network's hosted helper, which waits on the owner's Cloudflare sign-in.

Ask about

How much is 3 lanterns?

Three Signature Lanterns come to 36. The lantern's catalog number is 12, and 12 x 3 = 36.

What is in the catalog?

The mirror's sample catalog holds 10 Signature-made goods: Signature Lantern, Signature Mug, Signature Blanket, Signature Kettle, Signature Scarf, Signature Notebook, Signature Candle, Signature Backpack, Signature Water Bottle, and Signature Clock.

How is a basket total worked out?

Each line is the catalog number times the quantity, and the basket total is the sum of every line. Three lanterns give 12 x 3 = 36, two mugs give 8 x 2 = 16, and the basket total is 36 + 16 = 52.

What are the two AIs?

The mirror carries two named helpers: The Signature Shop website AI, which discusses the mirror and helps you pick a good from the catalog, and Signature Shop AI, an AI version of the shopping itself, which works out basket totals with you.

What this helper knows

This mirror has no database in the live manifest — mirrors are not manifest databases. The mirror's own data is its sample catalog and its nine sections, held in Group 5 (public-reference index) and Group 6 (Signature-made content).
