The themed world

The Winding Path crosses a green valley with one river and one bridge. Eight stops stand along the path — North Gate, Harbor, Market Square, Old Mill, Hilltop, River Bridge, Library, Station — and Milo the mole knows every leg by heart.

Tools — Route finder

This tool finds the shortest route in the mirror's own sample network. It cannot give real directions; every stop is a sample stop.

Route cards

Signature line. Printable route cards: one card per pair of stops, with the shortest route, every stop along the way, and the total distance.

Making: Print the cards from the download below; each card shows the working, leg by leg.

Valley map poster

Signature line. A poster of the sample valley: all eight stops, all ten legs with their distances, and the river bridge.

Making: Print the poster and pin it up; the distances on it are the mirror's own.

Milo's compass

Fun line. A fun paper compass like Milo's, with the eight stops written around the rim instead of north and south.

Making: Cut it out from the printable sheet and spin it to pick your next stop.
