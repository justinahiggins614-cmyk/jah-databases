The themed world, in words

The Winding Path crosses a green valley with one river and one bridge. Eight stops stand along the path — North Gate, Harbor, Market Square, Old Mill, Hilltop, River Bridge, Library, Station — and Milo the mole knows every leg by heart.

Tools

Route finder — Pick two stops and see the shortest route between them, every stop along the way, and the total distance. It runs in the browser with no backend and no key.

Product lines

  • Milo's compass (Fun line): A fun paper compass like Milo's, with the eight stops written around the rim instead of north and south.
