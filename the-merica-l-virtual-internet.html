What is the virtual internet?

The 'Merica-L Virtual Internet is The 'Merica-L Internet browsed as your own internet. The network already is an internet of websites — 39 websites, 2,700 per-artifact websites, and the database hub — and this page is the browser front door over all of it: one address bar, one search, one directory, and one place to find the mirrors. You do not need to remember where anything lives. You type what you want, in plain words or as a virtual address, and the front door takes you there.

A virtual address starts with merica-l:// and names what you want — a subject, a tool, a kind of thing — instead of a server somewhere else. merica-l://dictionary is The Signature Dictionary. merica-l://songs is Signature Music Studio. merica-l://hub is the database hub. The addresses are virtual: they point inside this network, not to the outside web.

The virtual internet is a vast amount of linked websites — not a list. Every website leads onward to the next: the address bar takes you from one website to another, the search shows you where to go next, and the linked trails below chain websites together by what you are doing — making something, learning something, reading and playing, finding and filing. Follow a trail and you travel the network the way you travel any internet: one link at a time.

What the virtual internet contains

  • The 39 websites — the network's own websites for subjects, tools, books, music, maps, games, and more, listed in the directory below.
  • The per-artifact websites — a full website for every database, every file, and every mix, each a full rundown (description and history, sourced data, math proof, science proof, spec and 3D-print version, demos, two AIs, and a use section).
  • The database hub — 63,047 databases, each with its own AI and its own boundless generator, counted 2026-11-09.
  • Signature mirrors of real websites online — the network's own versions of the kinds of websites people use every day, built as Signature-branded equivalents. See the mirrors section below.
  • The accessible version of every page: a plain version you can read without JavaScript.

What the virtual internet is not

  • It is not a shop. Nothing here is for sale.
  • It is not a social network. It does not ask for your personal data.
  • It is not a copy of the outside web. It is the network's own internet, built from its own databases and websites, plus Signature mirrors of the kinds of websites people use — never a copy of anyone else's site, brand, or content.

Mirrors of real websites

The virtual internet also holds Signature mirrors of real websites online — the network's own versions of the kinds of websites people use every day.

What a mirror is

A mirror is a Signature-branded website of the same kind as a real website online: a search mirror works like a search website, an encyclopedia mirror works like an encyclopedia website, a news mirror works like a news website — built from the network's own data, with the network's own products, names, and content. A mirror is never a copy of the real website it mirrors.

The rule every mirror follows

A mirror takes layout inspiration only. It never uses the real website's brand name, logos, trademarks, product names, or copyrighted images. It never presents itself as the real website, and it never reuses the real website's content. What it reuses is the kind of website: the layout shape, the sections a visitor expects, and the job the website does — rebuilt with Signature branding and the network's own data.

Mirror a website yourself

The Signature Website Creator has a mirror tool: paste any address and it rebuilds the layout shape with your own brand. Open the mirror tool (Signature Website Creator).

The mirror program

The network's mirror program builds Signature mirrors by kind of website — most-used kinds first. Wave 1 is live: 12 mirrors, each with its own working demo, two AIs, and an accessible version. Signature Search, Signature Encyclopedia, Signature News, Signature Video, Signature Maps, Signature Shop, Signature Weather, Signature Mail, Signature Social, Signature Tunes, Signature Arcade, and Signature Library. Tiers 2 and 3 of the seed list follow in later waves.

Every mirrored real website gets its AI version too: two AIs per mirror, the same as the per-artifact websites — one AI that discusses the website and helps you use it, and one AI that IS the website itself — both downloadable with their specs.
