Combo Router — Signature Word Combos (accessible version)

No JavaScript is used on this page. Full version: combos/index.html.

What this site does

Every word combination of 2 to 7 words has one stable URL: combos/<combo-slug>/ — the hyphenated lowercase combo. Stored combos (689 in Waves 1, 2, 3, 4, S1 and S2) serve a pre-rendered page; every other combo is built on demand at the same URL by the Combo Router. Never a dead link.

Total 2–7 words: 116,615,259,898,573,730,711,878,684,717,733,052. Wave 1 stores 103 combo pages, Wave 2 stores 347 more (293 three-word, 49 four-word, 5 five-word), Wave 3 stores 48 more (20 two/three-word and 28 six/seven-word List 04 items), Wave 4 stores 143 more (99 four-word and 44 five-word List 04 items), S1 stores 24 more (24 two-word Dictionary-attested headwords), and S2 stores 24 more (24 two-word Dictionary-attested headwords); together 689 demand-list combos are stored, and all 191 List 04 items of 2 to 7 words are now stored. Every other combo resolves on demand; the full space is generator option space, never stored.
