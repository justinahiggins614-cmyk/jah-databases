What this site does

Every word combination of 2 to 7 words has one stable URL: combos/<combo-slug>/ — the hyphenated lowercase combo. Stored combos (103 in Wave 1) serve a pre-rendered page; every other combo is built on demand at the same URL by the Combo Router. Never a dead link.
