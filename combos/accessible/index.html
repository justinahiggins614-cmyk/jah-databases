Combo Router — Signature Word Combos (accessible version)

No JavaScript is used on this page. Full version: combos/index.html.

What this site does

Every word combination of 2 to 7 words has one stable URL: combos/<combo-slug>/ — the hyphenated lowercase combo. Stored combos (450 in Waves 1 and 2) serve a pre-rendered page; every other combo is built on demand at the same URL by the Combo Router. Never a dead link.

Total 2–7 words: 116,615,259,898,573,730,711,878,684,717,733,052. Wave 1 stores 103 combo pages and Wave 2 stores 347 more (293 three-word, 49 four-word, 5 five-word); together all 450 List 03 combos are stored. The List 04 items and every other combo resolve on demand; the full space is generator option space, never stored.
