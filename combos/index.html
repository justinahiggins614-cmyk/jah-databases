Combo Router — Signature Word Combos

One router for every word combo up to 7 words — so any search lands on an active Signature website. Site slug: signature-word-combos (verified free against the live manifest, 63,826 databases, read 2026-11-23). Home of the combos/ namespace.

1. What this site does

Every word combination of 2 to 7 words has one stable URL: combos/<combo-slug>/ — the hyphenated lowercase combo, for example combos/joke-generator/ or combos/ai-meme-generator/. Type any combo in the search box below and this Router takes you to its page. If the combo is one of the 103 stored Wave 1 pages, you get the pre-rendered page. If it is not stored, the same URL builds the combo's full page on demand in your browser — never a dead link, never a "not built yet" page.

2. Find a combo (working demo — the search box itself)

The remaining 347 List 03 items (293 three-word, 49 four-word, 5 five-word) resolve on demand through this Router, so all 450 demand combos have a working URL. Of the 103 stored combos, 57 consist entirely of Dictionary headwords and have a computable deterministic rank; the other 46 contain at least one word that is not a Dictionary headword (for example meme, podcast, marketplace) and their records say so.

4. Science proof — what the words are

Word linguistics come from the Signature Dictionary record for each word. The Dictionary holds 102,223 headwords: noun 36,592 · no part of speech recorded 35,654 · adjective 17,223 · verb 7,804 · adverb 2,723 · phrase 1,497 · prefix 270 · interjection 107 · suffix 95 · preposition 93 · abbreviation 65 · pronoun 62 · conjunction 32 · article 6. Definitions: 99,579 headwords defined — 58,223 authored, 41,356 template-filled (always labelled as such), 2,644 still pending (always labelled pending, never faked). Roughly one word in five in the Dictionary is an everyday word an ordinary adult knows; the rest are uncommon, archaic or technical — which is why Wave 1 stores only the combos people actually search for, from the demand lists, and leaves the rest to on-demand generation.

8. Two AIs

Combo Librarian answers what any combo means, finds related combos and live sites. Combo Builder generates a combo's page, spec and demo from the template. Both ship as downloadable specs with this Router:

  • combo-librarian.spec.md
  • combo-builder.spec.md

Every stored combo page also carries its own pair of specs, grounded in that combo.

9. Use

Search any combo of 2 to 7 words, share its stable URL (combos/<slug>/), or build on it: the Combo Builder spec describes how to generate a page for any related combo. A combo with no stored page yet still has a working page at its URL — open it, and the Router builds it on the spot.
